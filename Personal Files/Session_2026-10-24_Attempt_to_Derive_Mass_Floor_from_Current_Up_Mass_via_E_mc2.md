## Plain-language summary

The floor is an effective dressing scale of order 300 MeV that is inserted by the nucleon-third or neutron-dressing protocol; it is not generated by the rest energy of the current quark.  

The operation therefore fails to derive the floor. It reproduces a known external input at the wrong scale.

\[
E_u \ne \Delta_{\rm Tav}.
