## Reading
The proposal is a grid (or lattice) of Riemann spheres that overlap, with the overlaps visible, after stereographic projection, as an overlapping-circles pattern in the plane.

A single Riemann sphere is \(\mathbb{CP}^1\simeq S^2\), the one-point compactification of the complex plane. Stereographic projection from any point sends circles on the sphere to circles or straight lines in the plane. Consequently any arrangement of overlapping spherical caps or circles on a collection of Riemann spheres can be drawn as a planar overlapping-circles diagram.
