## What the live structure already supplies

The Superblock already contains the following elements that realize the external isolating shell:

1. **Colour-singlet construction.**  
   A baryon is the colour singlet built from the six-phase occupancy sourced by the Mirron. The hadronic glob is the coherent bound set of three valence lines sharing one residual-\(L\) occupancy.

2. **TEP cost as the isolation mechanism.**  
   Forcing a bound colour line through a local \(\Phi_7\to\Phi_8\) reset overlap costs \(E_{\rm violation}\ge\Delta\). That cost is precisely the barrier that prevents free coloured flux from escaping the singlet. The external shell is the surface at which this cost is paid.

3. **Floor residual \(L\) inside the shell.**  
   Inside the glob the colour rebound is stopped at the local Present (\(T_{\rm priv}\) of the containing glob). The leftover angular momentum is the inertia of the valence line (printed floor \(\approx 309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}\)).

4. **Short-range residual colour outside the shell.**  
   Between colour singlets the residual colour force is short-range and attractive (nuclear binding). Hierarchical residual \(L\) at larger scales is the EM-type stopped-rebound account of laboratory-matching attractive gravity, not a long-range colour repulsion.

**Key.** External shell = boundary of the hadronic glob. Isolation = TEP barrier \(E_{\rm violation}\ge\Delta\). No new operator is required for ordinary confinement.

**Plain language.** Yes. The Superblock already has a “skin” around each three-quark packet. The skin is the edge of the lowest bound system. Crossing that edge costs extra energy (the TEP barrier), so colour cannot freely leak from one proton to the next. Inside the skin the colour bounce is stopped and leaves the mass we measure. Outside the skin the leftover colour force is weak and short-range. That is exactly the isolation that keeps ordinary matter from melting into quark soup.

## What the live structure does not supply

The structure does not contain:

- a long-range repulsive component of the external shell whose strength grows with distance,
- an automatic hierarchical globbing of that shell out to the hard wall that sources \(\rho_\Lambda\),
- a derivation of the dark-energy density from the confinement surface.

Any such extension remains free phenomenology: it requires a free coupling, a screening length that protects the residual-\(L\) match to laboratory \(G\), and a sign or range change that is not present in the locked colour residual. It sits in tension with the 2026-10-07 author cut if the floor colour sector is promoted to a dynamical source of \(\rho_\Lambda\). The existing non-locking entropic wall potential already supplies a fitted large-scale repulsion attracted to lack of mass without reinterpreting the confinement shell.

**Key.** Hierarchical globbing of residual \(L\) is already attractive and matches laboratory \(G\). Extending the external shell into a cosmological source adds free parameters and does not follow from the locks.

**Plain language.** The Superblock already has the right structure for the ordinary isolating skin that keeps protons from melting into quark soup. It does not already have the right structure for turning that skin into a long-range repulsion that gets stronger with distance and then accounts for dark energy at the edge of the universe. That extra step needs new free knobs and is not forced by what is already locked.
