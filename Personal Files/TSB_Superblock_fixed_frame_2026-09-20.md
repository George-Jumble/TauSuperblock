## 5. D4 — Kernel and the far-zone Poisson limit

### 5.1 Operator

Each domain \(D_i\) contributes a Yukawa Green function of range \(\lambda_{m_0}\), Lorentz-boosted by \(\beta_i\) along its internal velocity and time-averaged over the internal period \(T_i\) in the Prime chart.

\[
K(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{x}')
=
\sum_{i=1}^{6}
\gamma_i\,
\overline{G}_i(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}';\beta_i,\lambda_{m_0}),
\]

\[
\overline{G}_i(\mathbf{r};\beta_i,\lambda_{m_0})
=
\lim_{T\to\infty}
\frac{1}{T}
\int_0^{T}
\frac{\mathrm{e}^{-s_i(\mathbf{r},t)/\lambda_{m_0}}}{4\pi\, s_i(\mathbf{r},t)}\,dt.
\]

Here \(s_i(\mathbf{r},t)\) is the spatial interval in the instantaneous rest frame of \(D_i\), pulled back to Prime. For the purpose of the far-zone theorem the only properties required are: \(s_i(\mathbf{r},t)\sim |\mathbf{r}|\) as \(|\mathbf{r}|\to\infty\), uniformly in \(t\), and \(\lambda_{m_0}>0\).

**Key.** \(K\): 4D averaging kernel. \(\overline{G}_i\): period average of the Yukawa propagator of \(D_i\). \(s_i\): boosted spatial interval. \(T_i\): internal period. The integral kernel acts on \(\sum_j T^{(j)}_{00}\) to produce a Prime effective density.

Working hypothesis, not used as a derivation: one may *speak* of an IR end of \(K\) at a catalogue web scale only if \(K\) is not \(S_5\) and \(m_0\) is not derived from that scale. This paper does not introduce that scale.
