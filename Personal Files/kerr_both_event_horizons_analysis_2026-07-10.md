## Kerr Horizons — Explicit Equations with Complete Variable/Function Keys

The locations of the two horizons are the roots of Δ(r) = 0:

\[
r_\pm = M \pm \sqrt{M^2 - a^2}
\]

**Complete key to all variables and functions**:
- \( r_+ \): Radius of the **outer event horizon** (relevant for external observers; photon "bounce" surface in paper's heuristic; where Δ=0 and the Killing horizon is null for future-directed observers).
- \( r_- \): Radius of the **inner Cauchy horizon** (future boundary of the domain of dependence; beyond it, predictability fails due to blue-shifted radiation/mass inflation; still a null surface but unstable).
- \( M \): ADM mass parameter of the black hole (geometric units where G = c = 1).
- \( a \): Specific angular momentum parameter (\( a = J/M \), with \( |a| \leq M \) for black-hole solutions; a encodes the Kerr ring radius in the equatorial plane).
- \( \pm \): The two solutions; when a = 0 these coincide at the Schwarzschild horizon r = 2M.
- The function whose roots define them is the horizon polynomial:
  \[
  \Delta(r) = r^2 - 2 M r + a^2
  \]
  **Key for Δ**:
  - Δ(r) > 0 outside horizons (allowed radial motion for null geodesics in some regimes).
  - Δ(r) < 0 between r_- and r_+ (forbidden region for certain trajectories).
  - Δ(r) = 0 exactly at the horizons (null generators of the horizon surfaces).

**Shape confirmation**:
Both horizons are at **constant r** (no θ or φ dependence in the defining equation). Therefore:
- Coordinate shape: perfect spheres.

- No elliptical deformation from the ring singularity (which is interior to r_-).

**Ergosphere** (for contrast, as referenced in paper):

**Ring singularity** ("Kerr ring"):
Located at r = 0, θ = π/2 (a ring of coordinate radius |a| in the equatorial plane). Curvature invariants diverge there. It is **inside** r_- and does **not** affect the sphericity of either horizon surface.
