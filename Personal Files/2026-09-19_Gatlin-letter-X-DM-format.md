### DM 2

What I am not saying.

I’m not saying the cosmic web has no few-h⁻¹-Mpc scale. It does.

I’m not saying a 7-fold clock, a resonance graph, or a geometric ambition for dark matter and the vacuum is worthless.

I’m not saying you were wrong to test k = 0.142857 h Mpc⁻¹ in JADES and DESI.

I am saying that identifying that scale with a metric extra dimension that gravity wraps conflicts with older, tighter measurements than any galaxy spectrum — and that several of the “outputs” of tau were observed numbers written back in.

### DM 3

The published Tau construction isn’t a metaphor. It’s 5D Einstein–Hilbert on a circle, with G₄ = G₅ / (2π τ) and τ ≈ 10 Mpc.

If gravity can go around that circle, torsion-balance tests already require one flat extra dimension to be smaller than about 30 microns. That’s a mismatch of order 10²⁸ in radius. The first gravitational KK mass would be around 10⁻³⁰ eV: an almost continuous tower of light spin-2 modes.

This doesn’t depend on believing in gravitons as particles. Those experiments measure a force between masses. If flux can spread into a fifth spatial direction of size τ, Newton’s law changes below that size. It doesn’t.

---

### DM 4

Your own prediction sheet lists “no sub-mm gravity deviation” among the things that must not be seen. I agree with that null. I don’t see how it can coexist with a metric circle of ten megaparsecs that gravity wraps.

Resonances on the circle don’t warp the circle. They are the tower. Selecting n = 7 changes the integer, not the radius.

Fork: if τ is only a 4D coherence length or a phase label — something gravity does not wrap — then this paragraph doesn’t apply. In that case the 5D Einstein–Hilbert writing and G₄ = G₅/(2π τ) should be retired as a picture of laboratory gravity. Both sentences cannot stay.

---

### DM 5

Ordinary Casimir energy on a compact circle scales as 1/τ⁴. At 10 Mpc that’s about 10⁻¹²¹ eV⁴. Observed dark energy is about 10⁻¹¹ eV⁴. Gap of about 10¹¹⁰.

A formula that goes as (π/τ)⁴ cannot be made to yield Ω_Λ = 0.694 by rearranging π. If a prefactor is chosen so the observed density appears, the observed density was an input. That’s allowed. It isn’t a derivation, and it isn’t zero parameters.

Classical reduction of the 5D action also doesn’t, by itself, produce a Casimir cosmological constant. Sign and magnitude both have to be earned. I haven’t found that calculation at the required size in the Tau corpus.

---

### DM 6

If the same circle is asked to supply dark matter as KK modes, the masses in the public notes wander among 10⁻³⁰ eV, 10⁻³⁴ eV, and the 10⁻²² eV fuzzy-DM window. Those aren’t one particle.

A Compton wavelength of order 10 Mpc is not how galaxies virialize. Fuzzy DM at 10⁻²² eV is a different literature.

That’s separate from a hidden-sector idea: matter in another phase or domain that sources curvature and not photons. That idea doesn’t need the circle. I think it’s the better home for a geometric dark-matter story. I don’t think the circle is doing that job.

---

### DM 7

November 2025 papers announced an 8.1σ line at exactly k = 1/7 h Mpc⁻¹ and said, in substance, that the fifth dimension had been measured. Some of the same texts said the mode lay just beyond the then-available k-reach. Those two sentences can’t both be true of the same dataset.

The feature was described as appearing only after filters prescribed by the same model. A public script injects a Gaussian at 0.142857 and then prints a discovery sentence. I mention that only because it matters for the word “measurement.” A template plot isn’t a detection.

---

### DM 8

In March 2026 you tested the prediction on 7,775 JADES galaxies and DESI DR1 BGS. No Berard excess, no Rayleigh phase clustering, no KK comb. You published that. That was the right act.

What I can’t follow is keeping τ unchanged as a measured radius after the flagship didn’t reproduce. A null may still leave a target. It withdraws a discovery. Euclid and Rubin can test a target. They can’t ratify a measurement that already failed its first controlled look.

1/7 = 0.142857… Choosing that k and inverting it to 7 Mpc is an identification, not an independent length.

---

### DM 9

Nothing’s wrong with taking an observable as a given. GR doesn’t derive G. A geometric theory may take c, ħ, even one cosmological length from the world, and then predict something else.

What goes wrong is moving a given into the “derived” column without a calculation that would have produced that number if we hadn’t already seen it. Ω_Λ, H₀, Ω_DM h², and τ = 7 h⁻¹ Mpc can’t all be outputs of one radius if that radius was set to a catalogue scale and the densities were set to the observed ones.

A short public list of inputs would make the theory stronger, not weaker. I’d rather have three named givens and two real predictions than a zero-parameter sentence that covers every leftover constant.

---

### DM 10

What I still think is worth keeping.

A discrete harmonic structure that isn’t itself a field on expanding spacetime — a menu of allowed modes, with occupations that can change — is useful. It doesn’t have to be a metric circle. It doesn’t have to be “non-computable” in order to exist.

A 7-fold clock as an internal schedule is compatible with ordinary causality if time is a helix: the phase label may cycle; the tick is never the same spacetime event six turns later. That’s not a compact-time machine.

The web scale can stay a question: is there a coherence length in whatever kernel couples hidden stress to visible curvature? That question doesn’t need the 5D action. A first estimate using only a Hubble-scale carrier and a subluminal boost does not fracture at 7 Mpc. So even that milder reading isn’t a derivation yet.

I’m not embedding any of this into a joint axiom tonight. I’m telling you what I can no longer defend if someone asks whether Tau, as written, holds water on its own.

My present answer is no: not as extra-dimensional gravity, not as Casimir dark energy, not as a completed measurement of a fifth dimension.

Grateful for what you’ve already put in the open.
