## Analysis

As the hard wall expands under the entropic force (attracted to the lack of mass), unbound Mirrons of the eighth phase are created. The expansion of the wall into the void-state is accompanied by the production of new Mirron entities on the Φ₈ seam.

This supplies a concrete microphysical carrier for the previously abstract entropic term: the outward motion of the wall is the macroscopic consequence of Mirron creation events at the expanding edge.

### Compatibility
- Mirrons are already unbound topological defects associated with slot 8 / the bounce. Creating additional unbound Mirrons at the expanding wall does not alter the residual-\(L\) account of laboratory \(G\), provided the creation process is localized to the wall and does not inject residual angular momentum into interior bound globs.
- The kinematic stamp on already-present floor excitations remains unchanged. Mirron creation is an additional process tied to the wall’s radial degree of freedom.
- The entropic force stays attracted to the lack of mass; Mirron production is the mechanism that realizes the expansion into that void-state. No reversal of the residual-\(L\) arrow inside the chamber is required.
- Casimir is not used as the source. The 7 Mpc Tau cylinder remains rejected.
- One-Mirron lock: the locked census is one Mirron. Continuous creation of new Mirrons as the wall expands either (a) treats the new entities as transient or virtual unbound defects that do not enlarge the permanent census, or (b) relaxes the “one Mirron” statement to “one Mirron per chamber at each epoch, with new chambers or new wall segments acquiring their own.” The former preserves the lock; the latter is a non-locking extension.
