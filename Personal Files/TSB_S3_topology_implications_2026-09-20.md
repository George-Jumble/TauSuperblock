# \(S^3\) topology implications (working note)

**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Object:** internal three-sphere of the six domains.  
**Not:** a KK circle, not a megaparsec, not live-frame law.  
**Live pieces used:** F7 (radius slot frozen), B_lin (relative \(\beta_i\)), six occupancy points.

---

## 1. What \(S^3\) is

\[
S^3
=
\{(u,v)\in\mathbb{C}^2:|u|^2+|v|^2=1\}
=
\mathrm{SU}(2)
=
\mathrm{unit\ quaternions}.
\]

Simply connected: \(\pi_1(S^3)=0\). No closed curve on the sphere is a hole. You cannot store a Tau-style Wilson loop that is topologically forced.

Hopf invariant: \(\pi_3(S^3)=\mathbb{Z}\). Maps \(S^3\to S^3\) have an integer winding. A Mirron reflection of an aeon into a child can carry a winding \(n\in\mathbb{Z}\). That is topology, not a length.

**Key.** \(S^3_{\rm int}\): internal three-sphere. \(\pi_1=0\): no forced large circle. \(\pi_3=\mathbb{Z}\): integer winding of maps \(S^3\to S^3\).

## 2. Parallelizable: Prime can sit at rest

\(S^3\) is parallelizable. It admits three nowhere-vanishing vector fields (left-invariant on \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\)). A global rest chart for Prime is allowed. That is why B_lin may set \(\beta_1=0\) without a hairy-ball obstruction.

Hairy ball would have blocked a rest frame on \(S^2\). Internal space is not \(S^2\).

The three left-invariant fields \(e_1,e_2,e_3\) are a global frame. Envelope rotation of the *whole* occupancy is a left (or right) action: one number, an angular velocity along one of those fields, shared by all six points.

**Key.** \(e_a\): left-invariant frame. Envelope rotation: \(\omega_{\rm env}\,e_a\), not a relative \(\beta_i\).

## 3. Hopf fibration — the only natural circles

\[
S^1\longrightarrow S^3\longrightarrow S^2.
\]

Each Hopf fibre is an \(S^1\). They are linked once. This is the *only* family of circles that \(S^3\) hands you for free. They are not a fifth spacetime dimension and they are not \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\).

If the Prime envelope rotates, the least extra structure is: the six occupancy points ride Hopf fibres, or ride a left-invariant flow that *is* the Hopf flow in a suitable frame.

Linking number \(1\) means two domain world-lines on distinct fibres cannot be unlinked without leaving \(S^3\). Binding \(B_{7\to i}\) that used a Hopf fibre would inherit that linking. That is a topological conservation law for phase attachment, not a mass.

**Key.** Hopf fibre: \(S^1\subset S^3\). Linking number: \(1\). Base: \(S^2\).

## 4. Isometry group and why we froze the continuum

\[
\mathrm{Isom}(S^3)
=
\mathrm{SO}(4)
=
\bigl(\mathrm{SU}(2)_L\times\mathrm{SU}(2)_R\bigr)/\mathbb{Z}_2.
\]

If \(S^3\) were used as a smooth KK fibre, 4D would see \(SU(2)\times SU(2)\) gauge fields. Live Superblock does **not** do that. Occupancy is six discrete eigenplanes, isometry broken on purpose. Topology remains; the gauge continuum is off.

What survives the breaking: the global frame, the Hopf fibration, \(\pi_3=\mathbb{Z}\), volume form.

**Key.** \(SU(2)_L\times SU(2)_R\): would-be KK group. Off. Discrete six-point occupancy: on.

## 5. Volume and the frozen radius

F7 froze exponent \(1\) as the radius slot. Call that radius \(R_{\rm int}\). Volume

\[
\mathrm{Vol}(S^3)
=
2\pi^2 R_{\rm int}^3.
\]

\(R_{\rm int}\) is not derived from exponent 7 and is not \(\tau_{\rm web}\). If \(R_{\rm int}\sim\ell_{\rm Pl}\), the sphere is Planckian and Hopf fibres are Planckian. If \(R_{\rm int}\) is left unnamed, volume is unnamed. Do not mint it from \(H_\Lambda\) to cook \(\rho_\Lambda\).

Six points on \(S^3\) have a typical geodesic spacing \(\sim R_{\rm int}\). Relative \(\beta_i\) are speeds *along* those geodesics (or along Hopf fibres), not spacings.

**Key.** \(R_{\rm int}\): frozen radius slot. \(2\pi^2 R_{\rm int}^3\): volume. Spacing: geometry, not \(\beta\).

## 6. What this does to the last calculations

- Hubble note: relative \(\beta_i\) only. Envelope \(\omega_{\rm env}\) is a left-invariant Hopf flow, *not included*. Adding it is one extra function of time (or one constant). Topology allows it; B_lin does not fix it.
- Conformal density: \(\Omega_A\) lives on 4D, not on \(S^3\). \(S^3\) topology does not set \(\dot\Omega/\Omega=H_\Lambda\).
- Binding \(7\to 6\): slot 7 is clock algebra. It may be represented as a function on \(S^3\) (a phase). Hopf linking can constrain which domain phases a slot-7 Mirron may attach to. Not computed here.
- Child superblock: a map \(S^3_{\rm parent}\to S^3_{\rm child}\) has winding \(n\in\pi_3(S^3)\). \(n=1\) is the simplest motherhood. \(n=0\) is no topological copy. \(|n|>1\) is a multiply-wound child. Ontology, not a rate.
