## 1. What Superblock Theory is now

A **multi-domain constitutive theory**.

Six co-located 3+1D spacetimes sit on an internal supersphere, with staggered embedding velocities. Each domain has its own metric and its own Maxwell field. Photons stay on their domain’s null cone. Other-domain stress reaches us only through a four-dimensional impulse kernel on the embedding. A discrete Mirron / 8-phase cell supplies the electromagnetic vacuum as a constitutive law, not as a resistor already equal to \(376.73\,\Omega\). A seventh, non-prime sector can source a near-vacuum floor if its coupling stays below threshold.

It is **not** 5D Einstein–Hilbert on a metric circle of radius \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\).  
It is **not** Gatlin’s Tau Universe.  
It is **not** a completed derivation of \(\alpha_{\rm fs}\), of \(\Omega_{\rm DM}\), or of \(\Omega_\Lambda\).

## 2. Architecture that stands

**Domains.**  
Six 3+1D manifolds, co-located via an internal supersphere. Three future-oriented and three past-oriented remains an allowed SBT reading for matter / antimatter addressing. Values of \(V_{\rm orb}^{(i)}\) are not fixed in this session.

**Clock.**  
Round-robin turns: \((n,i)\to(n+1,\,i+1\bmod 6)\). Phase is cyclic. The tick \(n\) is never identified with \(n+6\) at the same event. That is a helix, not a tamed CTC. Compact metric time is refused.

**Electromagnetism.**  
No shared \(U(1)\) across domains. Confinement is tilted cones / failed pull-back of \(F^{(j)}\) onto domain \(i\). Horizon language is withdrawn and is not part of the theory.

**Gravity.**  
Stochastic / impulse kernel \(\mathcal K_{ij}\) on the embedding. Inside one domain, gravitational waves and light share that domain’s cone (GW170817). Between domains there is no superluminal wave and no graviton wrapping an extra circle. Other-domain matter appears as \(\mathcal K_{ij}\langle T^{(j)}\rangle\) — geometric hidden sector, not KK modes.

**Mirron cell.**  
8-phase discrete geometry. Target:

\[
\text{cell}\;\longrightarrow\;B_F(f;\mathcal G)\;\longrightarrow\;\Phi(f),\;\xi
\]

plus suppression of birefringence. Use “Mirron,” not “soliton.”

**Phase-7.**  
Non-prime sector. Candidate late-time floor: vacuum-like pressure from Mirrons that almost couple to a prime domain, amplitude \(\varepsilon_{7\to p}\ll 1\). Not ordinary Casimir energy of a megaparsec cavity. Call it Phase-7 vacuum pressure until a mode sum is actually performed.

**TEP.**  
Selection rule on allowed phases, embeddings, and the refresh map. Forbids identifying a full six-turn as the same event. Does *not* by itself prove finite curvature or geodesic completeness. Does *not* live on Tau-cylinder windings.

## 9. One-paragraph public form

Superblock Theory describes six co-located 3+1D domains on an internal supersphere. Electromagnetism is domain-intrinsic. Gravity is a four-dimensional impulse kernel on the embedding, so other-domain matter is dark to our photons and not dark to curvature. Time in the architecture is a helix over a six-cycle, not a compact-time loop. The electromagnetic vacuum is to be read from an 8-phase Mirron cell; the fine-structure constant is a target, not a trophy. A seventh sector may source a near-vacuum floor. The few-\(h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) cosmic web is a possible coherence scale of that kernel, not a fifth-dimensional radius. Metric Kaluza–Klein at cosmological size is not part of the theory.
