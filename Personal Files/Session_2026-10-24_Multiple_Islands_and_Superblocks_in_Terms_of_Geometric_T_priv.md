## Plain-language summary

Each causally disconnected island of bound energy possesses its own dominant glob and therefore its own local geometric \(T_{\rm priv}\). Because the islands no longer share causal contact, there is no cross-island correspondence that could contract their local labels into a single universal \(T_{\rm priv}\).  

Likewise, each superblock (a set of six co-located domains that form one aeon or one causally sealed region) carries its own universal \(T_{\rm priv}\) obtained by contracting the six matched local labels inside that superblock. Distinct superblocks, being causally disconnected, carry distinct and unsynchronized \(T_{\rm priv}\) structures. The construction remains purely kinematic: it organizes the hierarchy of simultaneity slices without adding forces or changing residual-L matching to laboratory \(G\).

## Analytical reading

**Key to symbols**  
- \(T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}\): geometric UPS of the dominant containing glob of event \(p\).  
- Universal \(T_{\rm priv}^{\rm univ}\): kinematic contraction \(\mathcal{C}\) of the six matched local labels belonging to one superblock.  
- Bound island: causally disconnected hierarchical glob (or set of globs) with its own residual-L center of gravity.  
- Superblock: one set of six co-located domains sharing a single Mirron and a single universal relative label.  
- Local light-cone: the null cone belonging to a given \(T_{\rm priv}\).

For multiple islands:  
\[
T_{\rm priv}^{(I_k)} \;\text{is independent for each island}\; I_k.
\]  
No shared contraction exists across islands because the hierarchy labels cannot be matched once causal disconnection is complete. Each island therefore owns a private simultaneity skeleton and a private light-cone structure.

For multiple superblocks:  
\[
T_{\rm priv}^{\rm univ,(S_j)} = \mathcal{C}\bigl(T_{\rm priv}^{(p_a;a)}\bigr)_{a=1\dots 6}\quad\text{for each superblock}\; S_j.
\]  
Different superblocks possess different contractions. The universal relative label of one superblock does not extend to another.

The earlier statement that every point creates a local hierarchy-relative slice \(\Sigma_p\) continues to hold inside each island and inside each superblock separately. No global absolute block-universe foliation is generated.
