**Date**: 2026-10-24

## Plain-language summary

The \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) index does not change the numerical values of mass or energy. It only tags which side of an already-existing distinction a given stamp belongs to.  

If the index marks domain versus antipodal partner, the energy cost of moving from one to the other is the antipodal domain-cross energy already recorded (\(\pi\Delta_{\rm Tav}\)); both members of the pair carry the same mass floor. If the index marks bound versus unbound, the bound side carries residual angular momentum (and therefore residual-L compression and effective mass) while the unbound side carries none. In both readings the index is bookkeeping only.

- \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) index \(\varepsilon=\pm 1\): binary label.

Two consistent assignments:

1. Domain / antipodal partner  
   \[
   E(\varepsilon=-1)-E(\varepsilon=+1)=E_\times^{\rm ant}=\pi\Delta_{\rm Tav}.
   \]  
   The mass floor itself is identical for both partners.

2. Bound / unbound

   Effective mass appears only on the bound side through residual-L compression.

In neither case does the index supply a new source term. Residual-L matching to laboratory \(G\) and the printed mass floor remain exactly as locked.
