## 3. Examination

### 3.1 The curve is a metric fact

In the vacuum Kerr extension, sufficiently near the ring with \(r<0\),

\[
g_{\phi\phi}<0.
\]

**Key.** \(g_{\phi\phi}\): coefficient of \(d\phi^2\). \(\phi\): azimuthal angle, period \(2\pi\). If this coefficient is negative, \(\partial_\phi\) is timelike and the orbit of \(\phi\) is a closed timelike curve. No energy-flow condition enters that sign. Harmony with another chart does not flip it.

A CTC returns to the same event. “Moves forward in time” is the opposite local order. A forward-moving hole in a conjugate copy can be perfectly causal and still leave the \(r<0\) curve closed. The two statements do not cancel.

### 3.2 Antimatter copy is not that partner

The conjugate domain shares the Prime chart. Its stress, under ansatz A, is seen through the kernel. It is not an exterior whose black hole runs forward while ours runs closed. Pairing \(D_a\) with \(D_{\bar a}\) costs antipodal domain-cross work. It does not identify \(r<0\) of one Kerr extension with the exterior of the other.

White-hole repulsion remains the sign flip of expansion on one engine, already examined. It is not an antimatter universe.

### 3.3 What would actually have to be shown

Not shown, and not granted by the charge:

- a metric in which \(g_{\phi\phi}\) does not go negative;
- or a cut before \(r_-\), which is the Kruskal note’s honest option, and which removes the CTC region rather than licensing it;

Chronology protection is a conjecture about semiclassical backreaction. It is not this charge, and it is not a Superblock lock.
