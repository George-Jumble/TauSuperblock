## The assumption and the postulate

The query assumes that ordinary electrodynamic forces (everything except the residual-L account of gravity) do not interact across the six co-located domains. Virtual-particle interactions inside each domain are nevertheless allowed to impart an inertial push that is collected, via a geometric torsion associated with the privileged observer, and the energy of interest is the energy released when that virtual mass is re-absorbed.

In symbols the collected energy is

\[
E_{\rm push}
=
\sum_{a=1}^{6}
E_{\rm reabs}^{(a)}
\cdot
f_{\rm torsion}^{(a)},
\]

where \(f_{\rm torsion}^{(a)}\le 1\) is a transmission factor through the privileged-observer torsion.

**Key.** \(E_{\rm reabs}^{(a)}\): energy returned to the vacuum by re-absorption inside domain \(D_a\). \(f_{\rm torsion}^{(a)}\): fraction of that energy (or of the associated momentum) that is routed to the universal +1. Both the per-domain virtual energy and the transmission factor are unfixed.
