## Laboratory strong force (brief)

Colour is exactly conserved. Asymptotic freedom: coupling small at short distance, large at long distance. Isolated coloured states are not in the spectrum; detectors see singlets. Nucleon mass is dominated by the non-perturbative gluon/quark kinetic energy, chiral condensate, and trace anomaly, not by current quark masses (\(\sim\) few MeV). Lattice glueball masses \(\sim 1.5\)–\(1.8\,\mathrm{GeV}\); string tension \(\sqrt{\sigma}\approx 440\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Neither is the Superblock floor. The Yang–Mills mass gap remains unproved.

**Plain language.** The strong force is what keeps three quarks locked inside a proton so tightly that you never see a single coloured quark free. Almost all of the proton’s mass comes from that locking energy, not from the tiny bare masses of the quarks themselves.

## Superblock reading of the strong force

A colour line that is bound completes its bounce to Absolute Past. The rebound toward Absolute Future is stopped at the Present. The leftover angular momentum is the residual \(L\) of that line — its inertia.

\[
\text{bound colour rebound truncated at Present}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\text{residual }L
\;=\;
\text{inertia of the line}.
\]

An unbound line completes the full Past \(\leftrightarrow\) Future cycle and leaves zero residual \(L\).

The quark cloud is the six-phase occupancy sourced by the Mirron. Dynamic Refresh rewrites quarks, gluons, colour, and emergent electromagnetic flux at that floor only. A hadron is the colour singlet built from that occupancy. Nuclear binding is residual colour between singlets (tens of MeV).

**Key.** Residual \(L\) here is the floor inertia carried by the stopped colour rebound. It is not the hierarchical residual-\(L\) that accounts for laboratory \(G\) (that is the EM residual-\(L\) of higher globs). The two share the same stop-at-Present language but act at different hierarchical levels.

**Plain language.** In this framework the “glue” of the strong force is the fact that the colour bounce is interrupted at “now.” The leftover twist of that interrupted bounce is what we measure as the mass of the quark line. If the bounce finished all the way to the future, there would be no leftover mass.

## Where \(T_{\rm priv}\) enters

The Present that truncates the rebound is not an absolute external clock. It is the local geometric simultaneity label \(T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}\) of the event at which the colour line is evaluated.

From the existing cascade:

\[
T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}
=
\text{POS}\bigl(\mathbf{R}_{\rm dom}(p),\,\text{UPS stamp}\bigr),
\]

where \(\mathbf{R}_{\rm dom}(p)\) is the center of gravity of the dominant containing glob of \(p\), and the UPS stamp is the kinematic \(\Phi_8\) phase advance.

The stop surface for a colour line belonging to event \(p\) is therefore the level set of that label:

\[
\Sigma_{\rm stop}(p)
=
\bigl\{q
\;\big|\;
T_{\rm priv}^{(q)}=T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}
\bigr\}.
\]

The residual \(L\) is the angular momentum of the Future-directed half that fails to complete once the trajectory reaches \(\Sigma_{\rm stop}\).

**Key.** \(T_{\rm priv}\) marks the geometric cut. It does not deposit the angular momentum; the residual \(L\) is generated by the truncation of an already-present bound colour bounce. The stamp remains kinematic (\(\Delta\phi_{\rm tick}\) only).

Because colour lives at the floor, the relevant containing glob for a valence line is the hadronic (or nuclear) glob. Higher globs supply the hierarchical residual-\(L\) that sources laboratory \(G\), but they do not rewrite the colour floor (DR is floor-only).

**Plain language.** The “now” that cuts the colour bounce short is the local privileged time of whatever bound system contains that quark line. That privileged time is just a geometric label obtained by looking up the nesting of bound objects until you reach the biggest one that still acts as a single unit. The label itself does not push or create mass; it only tells the bounce where to stop.

- **Floor vs higher.** Colour residual \(L\) is the inertia of the stopped colour line (floor). Both use the same stop-at-\(T_{\rm priv}\) language; the effective \(G\) of a set belongs to the dominant glob’s COM.

- **TEP.** Forcing a bound colour line through a local \(\Phi_7\to\Phi_8\) reset overlap still costs \(E_{\rm violation}\ge\Delta\). The digits of \(\Delta\) remain the protocol insertion; TEP supplies only the inequality.
- **No Tau restoration.** Identifying the stop surface with \(T_{\rm priv}\) does not reintroduce a metric fifth dimension, a Casimir \(\rho_\Lambda=\hbar c/\tau^4\), or \(n_{\rm hier}\). The cylinder is not licensed.

**Plain language.** The strong force operates at the bottom of the nesting of bound systems. Bigger bound systems (atoms, molecules, planets, galaxies) have their own “nows,” and those bigger “nows” are what produce ordinary gravity in this framework. The six co-located copies of the universe all share a common geometric clock label, but that label only keeps their floor clocks in step; it does not glue them together with a new force.

## Equations of the stop

Let a colour line carry a bounce amplitude that would otherwise complete to Absolute Future. The truncation at the local Present yields

\[
L_{\rm residual}
=
L_{\rm Future-directed half}\Big|_{\text{truncated at }\Sigma_{\rm stop}(T_{\rm priv}^{(p)})}.
\]

The inertial energy associated with that residual for one valence line is the protocol insertion

\[
E_g
\approx
\frac{M_N - (m_u^{\rm cur}+m_d^{\rm cur}+\cdots)}{3}
\approx
309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}

(printed working value; current masses removed). This is bookkeeping, not a clock derivation.

**Key.** \(L_{\rm residual}\): leftover angular momentum. \(\Sigma_{\rm stop}\): level set of \(T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}\). \(E_g\): per-line dressing share. \(M_N\): nucleon mass. The numerical value is inserted by the nucleon protocol, not derived from \(T_{\rm priv}\) or the G2 clock.

**Plain language.** When the colour bounce is cut at the local “now,” the leftover twist is the mass we assign to that quark line. The number we currently print (about 309 MeV) is simply the proton or neutron mass minus the tiny bare quark masses, divided by three. It is a convenient bookkeeping choice, not something calculated from the clock or from \(T_{\rm priv}\).
