## 0. Verdict

**Not a material. A causal edge of occupancy.**

The wall is the hypersurface where a chamber’s signals stop. It is not made of quarks, photons, a Casimir plate, or the retired circle. Calling it matter would put a signal source on the boundary that the disconnect forbids.

## 2. Composition

A chamber is a region of the one \(3+1\) manifold with a clock and a set of bound fields. The wall is the boundary of that region. Across it, the occupancy changes: interior clock versus complement. The complement is the high-Weyl side in the recovery reading. The wall itself is the jump, not a slab of substance.

Nothing in the notes assigns it a stress-energy of its own. A surface stress would be extra matter. Not introduced. The Higgs-depth Casimir was a plate gap at \(1.89\times 10^{-18}\,\mathrm{m}\), a floor-scale separation, not a constituent of this wall.
