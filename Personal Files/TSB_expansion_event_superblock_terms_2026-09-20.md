## 1. The Superblock event (native terms)

As the aeon runs:

1. Voids empty. Matter rides to the walls.
2. For an observer who stays in the void, walls pass the cosmological event horizon. No further EM arrives from the rest of Prime.
3. Leftover radiation in the patch is the redshifting CMB plus a negligible horizon glow. Vacuum pressure already dominates that radiation; isolation makes the domination absolute.
4. Volume of the patch keeps growing at constant \(\rho_{\rm vac}\). Integrated vacuum energy in the patch grows.
5. Quantum fields are already there. Occupation of those fields is another question.
6. Mirron refresh does not wait for a global bang. It is the local engine that has been running all along.
7. Hidden copies still sit on the same 4-chart. EM from Prime walls is gone. The kernel \(K\) is not EM. Shadow coupling can remain after the sky goes dark.

That list is Superblock. Penrose is used only where a conformal 3-surface and Weyl vanishing are the right language for step 2–3.

**Key.** Event: late isolated void, Prime observer. \(P_{\rm vac}=-\rho_{\rm vac}\). \(P_{\rm rad}=\rho_{\rm CMB}/3\). \(K\): 4D average, still allowed after EM cutoff. Mirron: refresh engine, not a soliton.

## 5. Vacuum energy and the word “Higgs”

Superblock-from-the-beginning talk of a potential that expands is kept as **vacuum bookkeeping**:

\[
E_{\rm vac}(\Sigma)
=
\rho_{\rm vac}\,
\mathrm{Vol}(\Sigma),
\qquad
\rho_{\rm vac}\ \mathrm{constant},\quad
\mathrm{Vol}(\Sigma)\uparrow.
\]

Call \(\rho_{\rm vac}\) the true-vacuum energy density of this aeon. If that density is identified with the electroweak Higgs potential height, say so as an identification and accept that \(H_\Lambda^{2}/v^{2}\) is negligible for the *shape* of \(V(\Phi)\). Growing \(E_{\rm vac}\) is volume, not a running \(v\).

New *occupation* of fields (particles) is not implied by growing \(E_{\rm vac}\). Growing \(E_{\rm vac}\) is implied by GR plus constant density.

**Key.** \(\Sigma\): spatial slice of the isolated patch. \(E_{\rm vac}\): integrated vacuum energy on \(\Sigma\). \(v\): EW VEV, not automatically \(v(a)\).
