## Corpus Status
- The Gatlin Tau Universe / Tav Topology layer (\(\tau = 7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) compact \(S^1\), 142857 KK comb, RGC-as-cosmological-backend) is treated as falsified.
- Live analyses use the Superblock / Mirron corpus only.
- The 2026-09-21 7 Mpc 5D / seventh-phase aside line is rejected from the corpus and is not a candidate for inclusion.

## What Is Not Preserved
- Global 7 Mpc compact circle and its monodromy.
- The 142857 KK comb and spectral features derived from it.
- Flux-choking (or related suppression/enhancement) mechanisms whose scale and geometry are set by that circle.
- Any large-scale periodicity, preferred direction, or flux modulation that required the global Tau circle as its source.
- RGC operating as a cosmological backend on that circle.

These effects do not reappear automatically inside local Tau cells, residual-\(L\) gravity, or gravitic-twistor extensions. The microscopic KK cylinders and the local hard-walls do not generate a 7 Mpc global structure.

## What Remains Active
- Local Tau cells, each with its own hard-wall \(\mathcal{W}\).
- Residual-\(L\) gravity matching laboratory \(G\) via the hierarchical rule.
- Gravitic twistors of each KK cylinder extending only to the hard-wall of its own cell.
- Bound/unbound split (\(L_{\rm res}=0\) at the local hard-wall).
- Scale separation that keeps heavy modes decoupled and laboratory gravity Newtonian.
- Absence of a global repeating circle.

Any phenomenological signature that depended on the global 7 Mpc circle must be re-derived from these local geometric elements if it is to be retained. No such re-derivation is currently in the corpus.

## Verdict
The phenomenology associated with the 7 Mpc Tau circle (flux choking and related effects) is not preserved in the current Superblock / Mirron framework. It remains excluded along with the circle itself.
