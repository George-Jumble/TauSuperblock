## 2. What the Baker text is actually claiming

Baker is not saying “dark energy has been measured to be thermodynamic.” She is listing the three classes that remain after \(\Lambda\) as a bare constant starts to crack (DESI \(w(z)\) hints, Hubble tension, \(S_8\), possible supernova re-analyses):

1. **Hidden dimensions.** Extra-dimensional gravity (DGP, braneworlds, large extra dimensions) can leak gravitons or induce a self-accelerating branch. Effective late-time \(H_\infty \sim 1/r_c\).
2. **Elusive fields.** Quintessence, k-essence, rolling Galileons, Horndeski / DHOST scalars. A field \(\phi(t)\) with potential \(V(\phi)\) gives \(w(z)\neq -1\). This is the industry default if DESI’s evolving-\(w\) hint holds.
3. **Thermodynamic spacetime (her favourite, undeveloped).** Einstein’s equation is an *equation of state* for an underlying microscopic theory, the way \(PV = Nk_BT\) is an equation of state for molecules. Dark energy would then be an *average* property of those micro-degrees of freedom, not a new fluid and not a new particle.

The observational lever she actually owns is gravitational waves:

- GW170817 already killed a huge slab of modified-gravity DE (any model with \(c_T \neq c\) at late times).
- Dark and bright sirens give an \(H_0\) and a gravity-law test that does not go through the cosmic distance ladder or the CMB.
- LISA + Einstein Telescope extend that to cosmological-baseline wavelengths.

That is a clean experimental program. It does not, by itself, pick among (1)–(3).
