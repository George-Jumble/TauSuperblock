## 1. Project Overview (Plain Language)

The Tau-Superblock Universe (TSB / Tav-Superblock) project is an ongoing independent theoretical framework that attempts to unify particle physics, gravity, and cosmology through geometric and topological structures rather than fundamental fields or particles. It posits six co-located 3+1-dimensional domains, an 8-phase octonionic (G₂) clockwork, a central Mirron (formerly called a soliton or Planck-Kerr mirror), and a residual-angular-momentum account of gravity. 

Early versions (mid-2026) included a macroscopic compact fifth dimension (the Tau cylinder, \(\tau \approx 7\,h^{-1}\) Mpc) as a Kaluza-Klein substrate that was supposed to generate the 1/7 resonance, the 142857 cycle, a parameter-free mass gap of ≈313.1 MeV, and a Casimir-like dark energy. By late September–October 2026, that Tau package was hard-rejected. The live frame is strictly 4-dimensional. The mass floor is an observational insertion (nucleon-third protocol), not a derived theorem. Gravity is residual angular momentum from stopped bound rebounds, matching laboratory \(G\) via the dominant glob’s center of gravity. Dark energy is handled by a dual-channel residual-L construction (time-like compression = lab \(G\); space-like push on a non-metric hard wall = phenomenological \(\rho_\Lambda\) with free efficiency \(\eta\)).

The project is highly iterative. Many early claims (zero free parameters, Vălean harmonics, 313 MeV glueball, planted research-tool signals) have been audited and retired or demoted. The corpus emphasizes falsifiability, author cuts, and clear separation between essential locks and relaxable annotations.

**Analytical status**: The framework is a coherent geometric re-expression of known physics plus a set of non-locking phenomenological extensions for large-scale repulsion. It does not yet derive the mass floor or \(\rho_\Lambda\) from locked structure alone.
