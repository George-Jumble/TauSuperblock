## 3. Examination

### 3.1 One seed, not one per domain

The overview puts a PK hole at the center of each domain. The live engine is one ring. A domain is a face, a sector of \(\chi\), or a co-located copy read through the kernel. Multiplying the seed by six multiplies geometries the Kruskal note already refused.

### 3.2 Ergosphere is not local \(v>c\)

Static observers fail where the time-translation becomes spacelike. A particle in that region must co-rotate. Relative to a local orthonormal frame its speed is still at most \(c\). “Superliminal relative to a distant observer” is a statement about coordinate angular velocity \(\Omega_{\rm drag}\). “\(v>c\) relative to the local phase domain” is the sentence that is wrong. Progenitor fermions do not outrun local light.

### 3.3 CTCs do not drive Dynamic Refresh

The factory slogan is real in the 21 July note, and it is the slogan the 30 September lock cut down. Floor occupancy can be intermittent. That is not orbital entrapment on a CTC, and it is not regeneration of atoms or COM. Using the CTC as the driver contradicts the 2 October line that the bounce is not a CTC.

### 3.4 Eight slots are real; the axiom is not in the notes

Six domains, slot 7, slot 8: live table. “Quark emergence moves the clock one unit” and “a full passage advances \(\Delta t_{\rm Superblock}=8\)” were not found in the taming section, the TEP recall, the \(142857\) note, or the 2 October summary. The taming that was written is a change of internal phase, not a guaranteed advance of eight time units.

If a return is \((t+8,x)\) rather than \((t,x)\), the curve is not closed. That would tame it. The overview asserts the advance. The notes do not derive it. The previous progression’s written signs summed to \(0\), which is the opposite bookkeeping.

### 3.5 The angle and the algebra

\[
\delta_{\rm overview}=\frac{2\pi}{8}=\frac{\pi}{4},
\qquad
\theta_{\rm slip}=\frac{2\pi}{7}.
\]

**Key.** First: the overview’s phase shift. Second: the slip in the derivation that actually discusses the ergosphere. They differ. The order-6 cycle and the empty residue \(0\bmod 7\) are why the corpus slip is sevenths, not eighths. Slot 8 is the reset, not the step of the active cycle.

\(\mathrm{Cl}(8)\) has dimension \(256\). \(\mathbb{O}\) has dimension \(8\). Bott periodicity of Clifford algebras is 8, which is why the 19 September note can say “Cl(8) fibre” for an eight-phase clock. That is not the same object as the octonion algebra, and it is not \(G_2\). The July taming uses \(G_2\) and \(e_k\), \(k=1,\ldots,7\).
