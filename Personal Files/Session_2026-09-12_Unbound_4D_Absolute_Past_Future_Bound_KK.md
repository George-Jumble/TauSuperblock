## 4. Absolute Future: separated nodes vs event horizon

On a late slice \(\mathcal{F}\), nodes are isolated at comoving sites \(\{x_n\}\). Physical separation grows as \(a(t)\,d_{\rm com}\).

\[
R_{\rm EH}(t) = a(t)\int_t^{t_{\max}}\frac{c\,dt'}{a(t')}.
\]

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| \(R_{\rm EH}(t)\) | Proper radius of the future event horizon at time \(t\) |
| \(t_{\max}\) | Conformal future infinity (or end of this Superblock tick) |
| \(c\) | Speed of light in the Prime metric |
| \(a(t')\) | Scale factor along the future integral |

A node at comoving coordinate \(\chi_n\) **leaves** the unbound 4D causal diamond when

\[

That is “disappearing over the cosmic event horizon.” The bound \(S^1_\tau\) on that node does not un-compactify; it leaves **causal contact** with the Prime observer. The fifth circle is still there; the unbound 4D circle of this observer no longer contains that node.
