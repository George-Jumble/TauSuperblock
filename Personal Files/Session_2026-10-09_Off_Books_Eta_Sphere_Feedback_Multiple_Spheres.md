## Extended Sketch

### Single η-sphere
Let the fifth sector be coordinatized by a radial parameter η ≥ 0 and an angular coordinate χ ∈ [0, 2π). The geometric object is the sphere of radius R_η(η):

\[
S^2_\eta = \{ (\eta, \chi) \mid \eta = R_\eta \}
\]

or, more generally, a 2-sphere embedded in the fifth-dimensional parameter space. The lapse is now a functional of the sphere:

\[
N = N\bigl(t,\mathbf{x};\, R_\eta, \chi\bigr).
\]

**Key to variables and functions**
- \( N \): lapse function (dimensionless).  
- \( t \): coordinate time.  
- \( \mathbf{x} \): spatial coordinates.  
- \( R_\eta \): radius of the η-sphere (dimension of length or of time × c, by convention).  
- \( \chi \): angular coordinate on the sphere (radians).  
- \( \eta \): fifth radial coordinate (same dimension as \( R_\eta \)).  
- \( S^2_\eta \): the η-sphere.

Proper time:
\[
d\tau_{\rm proper} = N\bigl(t,\mathbf{x};\, R_\eta, \chi\bigr)\, dt.
\]

### Feedback from universal-field actions
Residual-L gravity arises from residual angular momentum of stopped rebounds. EM and colour processes set frequencies and bounce rates. Both change the local rate of proper time. We let that rate feed back onto the radius (or shape) of the η-sphere:

\[
\frac{\delta R_\eta}{R_\eta} = \kappa\, \frac{\delta\tau_{\rm proper}}{\tau_{\rm proper}},
\]

or, equivalently, a functional dependence

\[
R_\eta = R_\eta\bigl[\, L_{\rm residual}(\mathbf{x}),\, \omega_{\rm EM}(\mathbf{x}),\, \omega_{\rm colour}(\mathbf{x})\, \bigr].
\]

**Key**
- \( \delta R_\eta / R_\eta \): fractional change in sphere radius.  
- \( \kappa \): dimensionless coupling constant of the feedback (unfixed; not a lock).  
- \( \delta\tau_{\rm proper}/\tau_{\rm proper} \): fractional change in proper-time rate induced by the fields.  
- \( L_{\rm residual} \): residual angular momentum (source of spatial compression).  
- \( \omega_{\rm EM} \), \( \omega_{\rm colour} \): characteristic frequencies of EM and colour processes (rad / proper time).  

Because the universal field has no direct charge under η, the only channel is the time rate. Spatial compression and frequency shifts therefore appear as deformations of the η-sphere, which then re-modulate the lapse, closing a feedback loop that remains purely temporal in origin.

### Multiple spheres
Allow a discrete family of η-spheres, one per sector or per hierarchical depth:

\[
\{ S^2_{\eta,a} \}_{a=1}^{N_\eta},\qquad R_{\eta,a} = R_{\eta,a}\bigl[\, \text{local field rates} \bigr].
\]

Possible readings (all optional, none locked):
- one sphere per co-located domain (six copies),  
- one sphere per globbing generation,  
- one sphere for the floor (colour + inverted colour) and another for residual-L gravity,  
- a continuous family parametrized by a hierarchical index (still not the retired \( n_{\rm hier} \)).

Each sphere contributes its own lapse factor; the total proper-time rate is a product or a sum according to the chosen composition rule:

\[
N_{\rm total} = \prod_a N_a(R_{\eta,a})\qquad\text{or}\qquad N_{\rm total} = \sum_a w_a N_a(R_{\eta,a}).
\]

**Key**
- \( a \): sphere label.  
- \( N_\eta \): number of spheres.  
- \( w_a \): optional weights (dimensionless).  
- All other symbols as above.
