## On the mass remark, then stop

In GR the exterior field of a stationary black hole is vacuum. What instruments outside measure as mass is a global charge of that spacetime (\(M_{\rm ADM}\), or a Komar integral on a large sphere), not a Newtonian potential being radiated from a point inside \(\mathcal H\). The horizon is the causal boundary of the interior; it is not a membrane that lets gravity out and keeps photons in.

That is why the picture does not translate to domains. Other-domain matter is not “inside our horizon.” It is on another cone. Photons fail to mix because there is no shared \(U(1)\). Gravity mixes only if \(\mathcal K_{ij}\) exists on the embedding.

Agreed: the analogy is off-subject. Do not use it in the mixing paragraph.
