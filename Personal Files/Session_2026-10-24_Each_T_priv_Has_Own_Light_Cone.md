## Geometric implications

1. **One cone per local label.**  
   Each \(T_{\rm priv}^{(p;a)}\) generates its own null cone. Inside a domain the cones of nested globs follow the containment partial order (nested or overlapping); they are not identified.

2. **Universal label has no private cone.**  
   \(T_{\rm priv}^{\rm univ}\) matches six local stamps. It selects which cones count as simultaneous; it does not fuse them into one null surface. Null geodesics remain domain-wise.

3. **Expansion and weakening remain cone-wise.**  
   Each individual cone expands toward the hard wall of causal separation. Cross-domain link strength dilutes with the null volumes of the participating cones or with hierarchical depth. Dilution is now explicitly a sum or product over distinct cones.

4. **Co-location preserved.**  
   The six domains occupy the same 4D locus. Distinct light-cones do not imply spatial separation; they imply distinct null structures on that shared locus (sectors on \(\chi\)).
