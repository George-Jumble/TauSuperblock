# Session Log: Macroscopic Kerr Ring as Conformal Copy of a Macroscopic Supersphere
**Date:** 2026-10-25  
**Project:** Tau-Superblock Universe  
**Topic:** What if we assume the macroscopic Kerr ring is just a conformal copy of the macroscopic supersphere? How would that work?

---

## Assumption
The macroscopic Kerr ring is the conformal image, in the spacetime of a local Tau cell, of a macroscopic supersphere belonging to the internal conformal structure of the framework.

---

## 1. Conformal Identification
Let \(\mathcal{S}\) be a macroscopic supersphere (internal \(S^n\) or projective quadric carrying the conformal data of the cell). A conformal map
\[
\phi:\mathcal{S}\to\Sigma_{\rm ring}
\]
sends \(\mathcal{S}\) to the macroscopic Kerr ring \(\Sigma_{\rm ring}\) of radius \(R_{\rm ring}\).

Because the map is conformal:
- null generators and the causal boundary are preserved;
- the light-cone hard-wall \(\mathcal{W}\) of the cell’s \(T_{\rm priv}\) locality remains the image of the corresponding conformal boundary on \(\mathcal{S}\);
- the monodromy phase \(\phi_{\rm mon}\) is unchanged.

The ring inherits its causal placement from the supersphere without becoming a new hard-wall.

---

## 2. Interaction with Residual-\(L\) and the Dual Channels
The supersphere and its conformal image carry only existing conformal/twistor data. They do not lock residual angular momentum.

- **Bound channel (\(+L\))**: \(\langle L_{\rm res}\rangle\) originates exclusively at microscopic Mirrons. The conformal copy may redistribute the null congruences that transport this residual, but
  \[
  \kappa\langle L_{\rm res}\rangle=\frac{G M_{\rm eff}}{c^2}
  \]
  is unaltered. The ring can still dominate \(M_{\rm eff}\).
- **Unbound channel**: generators that unwind at \(\mathcal{W}\) remain unwind trajectories. Shear and Weyl scalar still vanish on the light-cone wall. No new monodromy is generated.

Channel mixing remains forbidden. The incidence relation and optical-scalar expressions are unchanged.

---

## 3. Locality
The supersphere is macroscopic yet internal to the conformal compactification of a single cell (or nested family of cells). Its image—the Kerr ring—lies entirely inside the light cone of the enclosing \(T_{\rm priv}\).

No global extra dimension or universal circle is introduced. Nested light cones continue to nest by inclusion; each cell keeps its own hard-wall \(\mathcal{W}\).

---

## 4. Laboratory Gravity and Floor
The conformal factor drops out of the time-averaged curvature that sources \(G_{\rm eff}\), so
\[
G_{\rm eff}\big|_{\rm inside\ glob}=G\bigl(T_{\rm priv}^{\rm dominant}\bigr)
\]
continues to hold. The microscopic floor \(\Delta=\hbar c/r_M=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) is untouched.

---

## 5. Points of Care
- An explicit conformal factor and the embedding of \(\mathcal{S}\) into the cell’s twistor space are needed only for quantitative lensing or shadow calculations.
- The construction supplies no new residual-\(L\) source.
- Rejected elements (universal-circle hard-wall, 7 Mpc global circle, Vălean TEP generators) remain excluded.

## Verdict
The assumption is consistent with the hard corpus. A macroscopic Kerr ring can be realised as the conformal image of a macroscopic supersphere internal to the cell. The map preserves the light-cone hard-wall, does not generate or mix residual-\(L\), and leaves the hierarchical recovery of laboratory \(G\) and the microscopic floor unchanged. All previously closed structures remain intact.

---

## Plain-Language Summary
The big Kerr ring can be thought of as a stretched, angle-preserving copy of a large internal sphere that already belongs to the cell. Because the copying preserves light rays and the outer light-cone edge, the ring does not create new residual spin, does not mix the two gravity channels, and does not move the cell’s outer wall. Ordinary gravity and the 313.1 MeV floor stay exactly as they were.
