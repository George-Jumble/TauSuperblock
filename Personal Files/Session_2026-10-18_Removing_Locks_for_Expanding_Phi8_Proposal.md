Operating rule (2026-10-09): Reject only the mathematically impossible or core-breaking. Otherwise give the mathematics and the repercussions.

## Analysis of Removing Individual Locks

### Removing the kinematic-only character of Φ8
Allowing Φ8 to become a continuously expanding atemporal surface that fills lower-energy states does not immediately contradict laboratory physics. The stamp relation
\[
\phi_{\rm floor}\big|_{\rm after}=\phi_{\rm floor}\big|_{\rm before}+\Delta\phi_{\rm tick}
\]
can be retained as a discrete update while an additional continuous expansion is superimposed. Mathematically possible. Repercussions: the simultaneity seam acquires a dynamical role it does not currently have; residual \(L\) is no longer the sole account of compression; a free expansion law must be supplied. Does not break known physics by itself, but conflicts with residual-\(L\) matching to laboratory \(G\) if the expansion reverses the compression arrow.

### Removing the residual-\(L\) direction lock (compression toward high concentration)
This is core-breaking. The locked thesis is that bound fields tend toward the highest concentration of the bound fields via residual angular momentum of stopped rebounds. Reversing the arrow so that an expanding Φ8 fills lower states would mean residual \(L\) no longer produces the observed attractive laboratory gravity. Mathematically one can write the opposite sign, but then the account of laboratory \(G\) is lost and no replacement is supplied that matches the measured value without new free parameters. This breaks the core lock that residual-\(L\) matches laboratory \(G\).

### Removing the Casimir-as-floor / no-source-for-\(\rho_\Lambda\) cut
Casimir-sourced dark energy is a known (minority) proposal in the literature (Lifshitz renormalization yielding a cosmological term of the observed order after specific cutoffs). It does not break laboratory Casimir physics. Mathematically possible if a renormalization scale is chosen to match \(\rho_\Lambda\). Repercussions inside Superblock: restores a source the 2026-10-07 author cut refused; requires an additional free scale (the matching length \(\sim0.086\) mm for pure Casimir, or another cutoff); does not derive the density from residual \(L\) or the clock; leaves the laboratory force unchanged only if the cosmological term is cleanly separated from the local Lifshitz pressure. Not mathematically impossible, but reintroduces the cosmological-constant tuning problem and conflicts with the explicit cut.

### Removing the void-wall-as-causal-edge lock
Allowing the wall to become an expanding sink that draws energy toward lower states is mathematically possible (assign a surface stress or a radial velocity). It does not immediately contradict local laboratory physics. Repercussions: residual-\(L\) coupling is no longer merely co-located; a crossing flux appears; the second-law accounting must be rewritten so that entropy increase is driven by transport into the wall rather than by internal clumping. Conflicts with residual-\(L\) direction if the wall expansion empties dense regions. Not impossible, but core-adjacent because it alters the bound/unbound distinction and the compression arrow.

### Removing the non-reversal of the crossover integral
The evaluated integral is a definite calculation under stated assumptions (observed \(\rho_{\rm vac}\), laboratory \(G\), direction from floor density outward). Reversing it requires different boundary conditions or a different equation of state. Mathematically possible if those are changed. Repercussions: the numerical result \(t_\times=4.62\) Gyr and the \(10^{44}\) dominance no longer hold; a new free function must be introduced to produce a pressure inversion that expands the wall. Does not break known physics, but the conformal identification with a specific sphere still requires an unfixed radius.

### Restoring a metric expansion scale or \(\tau\)
Hard-rejected. Reintroducing a compact fifth coordinate or a hoop radius fixed by the crossover restores the Tau metric package (\(\rho_\Lambda=\hbar c/\tau^4\), \(n_{\rm hier}\), etc.). This is core-breaking relative to the live 4D frame. Mathematically the old cylinder can be written down, but it has already been falsified inside the project and conflicts with residual-\(L\) as the sole account of laboratory \(G\).
