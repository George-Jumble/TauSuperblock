## Analysis

### Dual-Channel Gravity in the Framework
Dual-channel gravity consists of:
- **Bound residual-\( L \) channel**: residual angular momentum locked by the compact circle of each KK cylinder at its Mirron. This sources the effective inverse-square gravity that matches laboratory \( G \) through the hierarchical assignment above.
- **Unbound / free channel**: completed bounces at the hard-wall / ultimate glob where \( L_{\rm res}=0 \). Supplies the outermost \( T_{\rm priv} \) and carries no residual spin for free null rays.

The gravitic twistor of each KK cylinder extends from the microscopic scale (\( r_M \approx 0.630\,\mathrm{fm} \)) out to the hard-wall, transporting the residual-\( L \) information across the local Tau cell.

### Prior Corpus Limits on Vălean Math
Established decisions already fix the scope:
- The informal Vălean weight-6 pointer is retired as a generator of the TEP floor.
- Near-miss numerical maps (\( e^{45} \approx 349.5\,\mathrm{MeV} \), \( 2^{65} \approx 330.9\,\mathrm{MeV} \)) are not IN.
- Protocol step 1 remains \( \Delta = \hbar c / r_M = 313.1\,\mathrm{MeV} \). It is not derived from any Vălean identity.

### Permitted Application
Vălean Euler-sum / weight-6 techniques may be used strictly as a **spectral-weight / residue diagnostic** on the residual-\( L \) channel carried by the gravitic twistors.

Procedure consistent with the corpus:
1. Associate a spectral density with the residual-\( L \) data on the compact circle of a KK cylinder.
2. Form the corresponding multiple-zeta / Euler-sum generating function of weight ≤ 6.
3. Extract the finite residues that remain after the unbound sector unwinds at the hard-wall (\( L_{\rm res}=0 \)).
4. Compare those residues against the hierarchical \( T_{\rm priv} \) assignment to test consistency of dominant-glob collection. Do not use them to generate a new mass scale or replace the geometric residual-\( L \) source.

No new dynamical equation is introduced. The hierarchical rule, the bound/unbound split, the microscopic size of the KK cylinders, and the absence of a global extra dimension remain unchanged.

### What Remains Unaffected
- The independent floor-number discrepancy (309.3 vs 313.1 MeV).
- The status of the TEP floor as protocol step 1.
- The gravitic-twistor extension to the hard-wall established in the preceding session.

### Verdict
**Yes, the application is consistent and useful within the stated limits.**  
Vălean Euler math can serve as a residue diagnostic on the dual-channel (specifically residual-\( L \)) gravity carried by the gravitic twistors. It does not reopen retired generator lines, does not alter the hierarchical \( T_{\rm priv} \) rule, and preserves the geometric core of the Superblock/Mirron framework.
