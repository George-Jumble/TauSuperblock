**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Answer:** yes. Use Penrose’s bridging factor on each womb. Do not use his single global \(\mathcal{X}\).

## 1. What is actually global in CCC

The *form* is

\[
\hat g_{\mu\nu}=\hat\Omega^{2}g_{\mu\nu},
\qquad
\check g_{\mu\nu}=\check\Omega^{2}g_{\mu\nu},
\qquad
\hat\Omega\,\check\Omega=-1
\]

on a bandage. Penrose writes that on one 3-surface for the whole cosmos. Nothing in the tensor algebra requires the surface to be unique or the factor to be constant on all space.

**Key.** \(g\): bridge. \(\hat\Omega\): parent-side factor (late interior). \(\check\Omega\): child-side factor (early occupancy). Reciprocal chart: a convenient pair, not a fifth dimension.

## 2. Per node of the tree

Each isolated void of a parent is its own bandage patch \(\mathcal{X}_A\).

On \(\mathcal{X}_A\):

\[
\hat\Omega_A\,\check\Omega_A=-1,
\qquad
\hat\Omega_A\to 0
\text{ toward the parent interior’s }\mathscr{I}^+_{\rm void},
\qquad
\check\Omega_A\to\infty
\text{ toward the child’s first occupancy.}
\]

Another void \(B\) of the same parent, or a void of the child, gets \(\Omega_B\), independent until the regions meet.

The tree of superblocks is then a tree of conformal factors. Same algebra, many patches.

**Key.** \(\mathcal{X}_A\): womb 3-surface of node \(A\). \(\Omega_A\): that node’s pair \((\hat\Omega_A,\check\Omega_A)\).

## 3. The only extra job (walls)

CCC never had to join a bang-factor to a still-living wall. You do.

On the walls of the parent, rest mass and Weyl are still on. There \(\Omega\) must stay ordinary (no bang, no \(\mathscr{I}^+\)). So \(\Omega\) is **localized**:

\[
\Omega(x)
\to
\Omega_A(x)
\quad\text{in womb }A,
\qquad
\Omega(x)
\to
1
\quad\text{on living walls.}
\]

A smooth interpolation across the wall-side of the event horizon is a gradient \(\nabla\Omega\). That gradient is new structure. Global CCC set it to zero by having no living walls on \(\mathcal{X}\).

If \(\nabla\Omega\) is too steep you have written a thin-shell conformal shock, not a bandage. Keep the interpolation on the horizon scale \(c/H_\Lambda\), not on a minted 7 Mpc.
