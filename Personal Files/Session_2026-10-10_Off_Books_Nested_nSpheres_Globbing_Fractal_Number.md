## User Question (verbatim)
“How about nested n-spheres based on globbing? Will that give the fractal number?”

## Short Answer
A nested family of spheres whose radii (or time-contrasts) scale with the globbing hierarchy can be arranged to reproduce the numerical value of the live anomalous dimension \(\alpha\). The arrangement is a choice of scaling ratio; it does not extract \(\alpha\) from an observable and does not turn the off-books 5D sketch into a derivation.

## Setup
Let each globbing generation \(g=0,1,2,\dots\) carry an associated sphere \(S^{n_g}\) (the dimension \(n_g\) may be fixed or may itself step with \(g\)). In the time-only 5D language these may be the \(\eta\)-spheres already introduced; in the ordinary 4D language they are the geometric loci of the successive globs (hadronic singlet, nuclear residual colour, atomic EM, molecular/condensed family, \ldots).

Assign a radius (or a proper-time contrast) that scales geometrically:
\[
R_g = R_0\,\lambda^g.
\]

**Complete key**
- \(R_g\): radius (or contrast) of the sphere belonging to globbing generation \(g\).
- \(R_0\): reference radius at generation 0 (input).
- \(\lambda\): dimensionless scaling ratio between successive generations.
- \(g\): integer generation index.
- \(S^{n_g}\): the sphere of dimension \(n_g\) at generation \(g\).

## Recovering the fractal (anomalous) dimension
The live anomalous dimension extracted from the 142857 cycle is
\[
\alpha=1-\log_7 6\approx 0.079218.
\]

**Complete key**
- \(\alpha\): anomalous dimension.
- \(\log_7\): logarithm base 7.
- 6: period length of the cycle / number of co-located domains.

Choose the scaling ratio to be the base of that logarithm:
\[
\lambda=6\qquad\text{or}\qquad\lambda=7.
\]
Then the logarithmic derivative that defines a fractal scaling dimension is
\[
d_f=\frac{\log N}{\log(1/\lambda)}\qquad\text{or simply}\qquad d_f=\log_7 6=1-\alpha,
\]
where \(N\) is the number of sub-spheres per generation (here \(N=6\) for the domains, or \(N=7\) for the phase count). Setting \(N=6\) and \(\lambda=7\) yields exactly
\[
d_f=\log_7 6=1-\alpha\approx 0.920782,
\]
so that the complementary quantity is the live \(\alpha\).

**Complete key**
- \(d_f\): fractal (scaling) dimension obtained from the nested radii.
- \(N\): branching number per generation.
- The equality \(d_f=1-\alpha\) holds by the choice \(\lambda=7\), \(N=6\).

Equivalently, one may set the successive time-contrasts of the envelopes to
\[
\xi_g=\lambda^{g\alpha}=7^{g\alpha}
\]
and recover \(\alpha\) as the exponent that linearises the log-radius versus generation plot.

## What is obtained and what is not
**Obtained (by construction)**
- A nested sequence of spheres whose logarithmic scaling reproduces the numerical value of \(\alpha\) (or of \(1-\alpha\)).
- A possible geometric home for the earlier envelope contrasts: each globbing shell supplies one sphere, and the contrast between shells is fixed by the same ratio that defines \(\alpha\).

**Not obtained**
- An extraction of \(\alpha\) from a measured spectrum or from a laboratory observable. The value remains the arithmetic consequence of the 142857 cycle.
- A derivation of the mass-floor digits, of \(\rho_\Lambda\), or of laboratory \(G\). Residual-\(L\) gravity and the 309.3 MeV protocol are untouched.
- Restoration of the Tau metric package. The spheres are either ordinary 4D loci of the globs or the optional \(\eta\)-spheres of the time-only sketch; they are not a compact fifth-dimensional circle of radius 7 Mpc.

## Status
Nested n-spheres indexed by globbing generation can be given a scaling that numerically matches the fractal number \(\alpha\). The match is a choice of the ratio \(\lambda\) and the branching \(N\), not a new derivation. Live frame remains 4D.
