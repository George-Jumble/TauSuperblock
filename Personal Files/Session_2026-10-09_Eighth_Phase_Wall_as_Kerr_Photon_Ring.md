## 2. The orbit

\[
r_{\rm ph}=3M=\frac{3}{2}r_s.
\]

Prograde and retrograde roots of the equatorial effective-potential condition; extremal limits \(M\) and \(4M\). The orbit is null. Its history is a world-tube \(S^1\times\mathbb{R}\) in the existing \(3+1\), which is the cylindrical wall allowed yesterday.
