## Terms Imported

The following scoped reading is now admitted as an allowed annotation of the existing \(\Phi_8\) simultaneity tick in the main Superblock Cosmology framework:

At the \(\Phi_8\) reset seam the harmonic stamp performs a discrete one-tick phase advance of every already-present floor excitation (colour and inverted-colour EM/electroweak modes). The advance is kinematic: it multiplies the local phase by the prescribed clock step and may multiply the lapse by a harmonic product of contrasts. It does not create, re-bind, or refresh higher-level structure (atoms, molecules, or isolated centre-of-mass packets).

The defining relation is
\[
\phi_{\rm floor}\big|_{\rm after\ \Phi_8}
=
\phi_{\rm floor}\big|_{\rm before\ \Phi_8}
+
\Delta\phi_{\rm tick}.
\]

**Complete key**
- \(\phi_{\rm floor}\): phase already carried by a colour or inverted-colour EM/electroweak excitation that exists at the binding floor.
- \(\Delta\phi_{\rm tick}\): the discrete phase increment fixed by one letter of the eight-letter clock or by one cyclic step of the 142857 labelling.
- \(\Phi_8\): the bound-sector reset seam (point of simultaneity for the floor).
- Floor excitations: colour + inverted-colour EM/electroweak modes only.
- Optional: \(N\big|_{\Phi_8}\) the lapse multiplier constructed from the harmonic product of envelope contrasts (annotation only; not required for the phase advance).

## Compatibility with Live Locks (restated)

- Dynamic Refresh remains floor-only. The stamp does not constitute a second refresh of atoms or of isolated centre-of-mass packets.
- The Pedalino-type constraint is preserved: classical Dynamic Refresh of an isolated COM is excluded.
- Residual-\(L\) gravity is unchanged. The phase advance may modulate the instantaneous proper-time rate at which stopped rebounds are evaluated, but it does not generate new residual angular momentum. Matching to laboratory \(G\) is unaffected.
- The unbound photon (completed bounce, \(L=0\)) experiences the same stamp; no residual contrast accumulates on the null generator. The photon-ring world-tube remains four-dimensional.
- Six co-located copies, one Mirron, printed working floor 309.3 MeV (neutron dressing protocol), empty residue \(0\bmod 7\), and the hard rejection of the Tau/Tav metric package are all preserved.
- No fifth coordinate is introduced. Any Recovery-project language about a time-only fifth sector or \(\eta\)-spheres stays outside the main frame.

## What Was Not Imported

- The 2026-06-28 Tau Cylinder geometric arena, \(n_{\rm hier}\), \(\lambda_{313.1}\), and the associated Vălean-action evaluation. These remain hard-rejected archive material.
- Any claim that the stamp re-initialises or re-binds structure above the floor.
- Any derivation of the mass-floor digits, of \(\rho_\Lambda\), or of laboratory \(G\).

## Operational Consequence

Universal ticks continue to be points of simultaneity. The content of that simultaneity for the binding floor is now explicitly allowed to include the one-tick phase advance of existing excitations. Higher globs continue to inherit their centres of mass from the composition of their children; the stamp does not rewrite those centres.

## Summary

The compatible stamp-propagation reading has been imported into the main Superblock framework as a kinematic annotation of the \(\Phi_8\) tick. Core locks are unchanged. The construction supplies a precise meaning for “propagate existing floor excitations by one tick” without expanding Dynamic Refresh beyond the floor or reopening the Tau package.
