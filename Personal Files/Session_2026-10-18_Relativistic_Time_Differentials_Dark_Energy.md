## Analysis

### Relativistic time differentials
The proper-time interval along a world-line is
\[
d\tau = \frac{1}{c}\sqrt{-g_{\mu\nu}\,dx^\mu dx^\nu},
\]
where \( g_{\mu\nu} \) is the metric, \( x^\mu \) are coordinates, and \( c \) is the speed of light.  

In special relativity (flat space, velocity \( v \)):
\[
d\tau = dt\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}.
\]
In a weak gravitational field (approximate Schwarzschild):
\[
\frac{d\tau}{dt} \approx \sqrt{1 - \frac{r_s}{r}}, \qquad r_s = \frac{2GM}{c^2}.
\]
These are local. “Cells of differential time” means finite regions (domains, hierarchical globs, voids vs filaments) carrying integrated \( \Delta\tau_i \) relative to a reference.

### Points of simultaneity
A point of simultaneity is a spacelike hypersurface \( \Sigma \) on which events share a common coordinate time \( t = t_{\rm sim} \) (or a common UPS tick). In the Superblock, UPS ticks are the geometric instantiation of the universal clock; they do not dynamically refresh higher-level objects.

### External analogues (not locks)
Literature contains models that re-interpret apparent acceleration via time differentials:

1. **Timescape cosmology** (Wiltshire 2007 and later works, e.g. Seifert et al.). Gravitational time dilation makes voids “older” (more expansion time) than clusters. Volume-weighted averages over inhomogeneous regions can mimic accelerated expansion without a cosmological constant. The required age differentials are large (billions of years in some claims); consensus GR estimates are much smaller (hundreds to thousands of years).  
2. **Quantum uncertainty of simultaneity** (Luo, arXiv:1401.2488). Synchronization of distant quantum clocks cannot be exact, yielding an effective
   \[
   \rho_{\rm de} = \frac{6}{\pi}\, L_P^{-2} L_H^{-2},
   \]
   with \( \Omega_{\rm de} = 2/\pi \approx 0.637 \) (order-of-magnitude comparable to observed ~0.7).  
3. Dual-time or absolute-simultaneity constructions also appear, but remain outside standard GR+ΛCDM and outside Superblock locks.

These show that differential proper time + a careful choice of simultaneity/averaging can produce an *apparent* dark-energy effect. They do not supply a derivation inside the Superblock locks, nor do they identify residual-\( L \) with \( \rho_\Lambda \).
