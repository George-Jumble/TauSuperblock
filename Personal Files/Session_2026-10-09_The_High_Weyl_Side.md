## 0. Verdict

**The complement of a chamber, read as a region of large conformal curvature.**

Weyl curvature is the tidal, trace-free part of the Riemann tensor. “High-Weyl side” means the outside of the wall, where that tidal field is the thing the recovery uses to name the void, and where this chamber’s signals do not go. It is not a substance and not \(\tau\).

## 2. The split

Riemann curvature splits into a trace piece, fixed by local matter through Einstein’s equation, and a trace-free piece, the Weyl tensor, which carries tidal field and gravitational waves. Inside a chamber the bound fields source the Ricci piece, and residual \(L\) is the live account of the compression. Across the wall the signals stop. What the recovery still names on that side is a large Weyl field: tidal structure without this chamber’s clock and without this chamber’s light.

No invariant has been computed that says how large. “High” is a label of the complement, not a measured number. Catalogue voids are underdensities in the survey. They are not automatically this side.
