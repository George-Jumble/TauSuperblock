## 2. Arithmetic

\[
\Delta_n
=
\frac{M_n-m_u^{\rm cur}-2m_d^{\rm cur}}{3}
=
309.34\,\mathrm{MeV},
\qquad
\Delta_p
=
\frac{M_p-2m_u^{\rm cur}-m_d^{\rm cur}}{3}
=
309.75\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]

Printed working value: neutron protocol, one decimal, \(309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Current-mass uncertainties of order \(0.07\,\mathrm{MeV}\) move the share by a few hundredths of an MeV. The equal-share assumption is the larger model choice.

Relative shift from the old print: \((309.3-313.1)/313.1=-1.21\times 10^{-2}\). Lengths that scale as \(1/\Delta\) lengthen by \(1.23\times 10^{-2}\). \(S_E/\hbar=2\) does not move.
