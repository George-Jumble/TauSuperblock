# Phase-7 Mirrons / quarks — EM neutrality on all six domains

**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Question:** how do 7th-phase Mirrons and the quarks they produce stay electromagnetically neutral as seen from every active copy?  
**Live fact:** \(J_{\rm Mirron}\) is a *stress* current. Maxwell current \(j^\mu_{\rm EM}\) is separate. Isolation set incoming wall photons to zero; it did not license net charge.

---

## 1. The condition

For every domain \(i=1,\ldots,6\),

\[
Q_i\!\left(\text{7th-phase products}\right)
=
0
\]

as a set (pair, bag, or singlet), at each secular tick after \(\mathcal{P}_8\).

“Relative to all six” is stronger than “neutral in Prime.” A state that is Prime-neutral but charged in \(D_3\) is not allowed.

**Key.** \(Q_i\): electric charge as read by copy \(i\). Set-neutrality: the produced collection, not each parton.

---

## 2. Two Maxwell architectures

**One shared photon.** One \(U(1)\) for all copies (the optional Planckian KK circle, not live). Then \(Q_i=Q\) for all \(i\), and it is enough that the set has \(Q=0\): \(q\bar q\), \(qqq\) with total \(0\), or a charge-zero Mirron. Ordinary pair production.

**Six photons.** Each copy has its own \(U(1)_i\). Then the object must be a singlet under \(U(1)_1\times\cdots\times U(1)_6\). A quark that carries \(Q_1\neq 0\) must carry compensating charges on the other five, or come with partners that cancel every \(Q_i\). This is the reading “neutral relative to *all* six” in the strict sense.

Live frame derived neither photon. Neutrality is a constraint on whatever Maxwell is later installed.

---

## 3. Slot 7 is the empty EM class

Clock algebra: residue / slot 7 is the fabric slot, not a seventh spacetime. Old occupancy: residues \(1\)–\(6\) filled, \(0\) empty. Read that as: slot 7 carries **no** \(U(1)\) charge of its own. A bare 7th-phase Mirron has

\[
Q_i(\mu_7)=0\qquad\text{for all }i.
\]

Charge appears only when binding \(B_{7\to i}\) attaches the fabric to a domain face and partons are written on that face.

So the Mirron itself is neutral on all six. The danger is the quarks.

**Key.** \(\mu_7\): slot-7 Mirron amplitude. Bare \(\mu_7\) is a \(U(1)^6\) singlet.

---

## 4. Quarks on a face

Old Superblock: fractional charge is a *footprint of domain orientation* (future-face \(+2/3\), past-face \(-1/3\), double-negative through the PK ring). That is orientation talk, not a second Maxwell.

On a single face \(i\), a produced quark may carry \(Q_i\neq 0\). Neutrality on *that* face is standard: produce \(q\bar q\) or a colour-and-charge singlet. Binding that writes a lone \(u\) on Prime and nothing else violates \(Q_1=0\).

Neutrality on the *other* faces: if Maxwell is shared, \(Q_j=Q_1\), and the same pair cancels everywhere. If Maxwell is sixfold, a pair written only on face \(1\) is invisible to \(U(1)_3\) only if \(U(1)_3\) does not see face-1 partons. Then “neutral on domain 3” is automatic (charge not in that algebra). The dangerous case is a parton charged under several \(U(1)_i\) at once. Forbid that: a quark bound to face \(i\) carries at most \(Q_i\).

Rule:

\[
Q_j(q_i)=\delta_{ij}Q(q_i).
\]

Then a Prime-neutral pair is automatically neutral on \(D_2,\ldots,D_6\). Trans-domain is the *ring*, not the charge.

---

## 5. What enforces it

Not \(K\). The kernel is gravitational.  
Not \(\mathcal{P}_8\) by itself. Eight-tick average can cancel an oscillating dipole and still leave a secular charge if production is unbalanced.

Enforcement is a selection rule on \(B_{7\to i}\):

\[
B_{7\to i}
\quad\text{creates only }U(1)_i\text{-singlets (or }q\bar q\text{ on face }i\text{)}.
\]

Plus the slot-7 rule \(Q_i(\mu_7)=0\). Plus no cross-charges \(Q_j(q_i)=0\) for \(j\neq i\).

That is an axiom on the binding, not a theorem from F7. Without it, a 7th-phase quark can ionize Prime while the other copies stay dark, and “neutral relative to all six” is false.

---

## 6. Double-negative does not replace this

Prime-Past transit through the ring can flip a footprint (\(+\leftrightarrow-\)). That can *pair* a face-charge with its opposite on the time-reversed reading of the same face. It does not cancel a charge on a *different* domain unless that domain is identified with the Past copy of the same face. Only Prime-Past is that pair in the old notes. The other four copies still need the selection rule.

---

## Decision

Bare 7th-phase Mirrons are neutral on all six by being in the empty slot. Quarks stay neutral on all six only if (i) they are produced in singlets on the face they bind to, and (ii) they do not carry the other five \(U(1)\)s. That pair of rules is required and not derived. The stress current does not enforce them.
