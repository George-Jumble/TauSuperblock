Micro-gravity in this context is simply the residual-\(L\) compression evaluated on a small glob. It remains compressive. Imparting residual angular momentum or inertia from a stopped rebound is the definition of residual-\(L\) gravity; it does not flip sign by itself.

The Mirron is the topological source of the quark cloud and the locus of the phase-inversion bounce. Any “imparting of inertia to the Mirron” must be read as a kinematic or topological effect at the bounce, not as a continuous residual-\(L\) accumulation that sources \(\rho_\Lambda\).
