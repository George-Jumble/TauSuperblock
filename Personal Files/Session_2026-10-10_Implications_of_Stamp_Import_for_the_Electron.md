## Status of the Electron
The electron is a charged lepton. In the live framework it is not a floor excitation in the same sense as a quark or gluon line. Its electromagnetic coupling is to the inverted-colour EM sector that originates at the floor and is transmitted outward by the W and Z bosons (accepted 2026-10-08 wording: W/Z carry inverted-colour excitation from the floor toward atomic EM). The electron itself appears as a stable particle that can occupy:
- a free (unbound-packet) coordinate, or
- an atomic or molecular envelope in which residual \(L\) lives in the bound EM degrees of freedom.

From the 2026-09-30 COM notes, electrons and metallic cohesion are internals of a higher glob; residual angular momentum resides in those envelopes. The isolated centre-of-mass packet of a composite object can remain a wave; the stamp does not rewrite it.

## What the Stamp Does for the Electron
- The inverted-colour EM flux (virtual photon cloud, Coulomb field, etc.) that the electron sources or couples to is floor-derived. That flux, where it exists as an excitation already present at the floor, receives the one-tick phase advance at \(\Phi_8\).
- The electron’s own identity as a lepton, its rest mass, and the phase of any isolated COM wave packet it may form are not independently advanced by the stamp. Those are higher-generation quantities inherited through composition.
- Atomic binding (electron–nucleus Coulomb envelope) is an EM glob above the floor. The stamp does not re-bind or refresh that envelope; it only advances the floor modes that feed the inverted-colour EM.

## Residual-\(L\) and Gravity
Any residual angular momentum associated with a bound electron (for example inside an atom or a metallic lattice) continues to arise from stopped rebounds of the relevant EM lines. The stamp may modulate the proper-time sampling of those rebounds but does not generate new residual \(L\). Laboratory \(G\) matching is unaffected. A free electron (unbound packet) carries no residual from the stamp itself.

## What Is Not Claimed
- The stamp does not constitute a Dynamic Refresh of the electron or of any atomic orbital.
- It does not alter the electron mass, charge, or magnetic moment.
- It does not place the electron on the same ontological footing as a quark line at the floor.
- It does not reopen any Tau-package element.

## Summary
The electron couples to the inverted-colour EM sector that the stamp advances at the floor. The electron particle itself, and any higher glob of which it is a constituent, is not directly stamped. Dynamic Refresh remains floor-only; residual-\(L\) gravity and all other core locks are unchanged.
