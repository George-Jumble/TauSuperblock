  Improved entangled photon source (two-photon laser excitation). Key innovation: time-varying analyzers with fast acousto-optic switches to close communication loophole. Strong CHSH violations (e.g., S = 2.697 ± 0.015 in 1982 time-varying run, ~5σ violation).
