## Exploratory integration

Suppose the relative-now label \(t_N\) is not perfectly synchronized across regions. Define a local contrast

\[
\delta t_N(x)
=
t_N(x)
-
\langle t_N\rangle,
\]

where the average may be taken over a glob, a domain, or a larger averaging volume.

**Key.** \(\delta t_N(x)\): local excess or deficit of the Present stop locus relative to a reference. \(\langle t_N\rangle\): spatial or hierarchical average.

Possible thermodynamic readings (all non-locking):

1. **Modulation of completion fraction**  
   A local advance or lag in \(t_N\) changes the fraction of quanta that complete an unbound bounce versus those that stop and leave residual \(L\). The local contribution to the wall potential then acquires a correction
   \[
   \delta V_{\rm wall}
   \propto
   f(\delta t_N)\,E_{\rm unbound}.
   \]
   The gradient of this correction supplies an additional piece of \(\mathbf{F}_{\rm ent}\).

2. **Entropy production from relative-time gradients**  
   Spatial gradients of \(t_N\) (or of proper-time rate) can be read as a source of irreversible entropy production, analogous to the thermodynamic extension in Desré’s ΛCDM+S or to Timescape-style backreaction. An effective
   \[
   \Lambda_{\rm thermo}
   \propto
   \bigl|\nabla t_N\bigr|^p
   \]
   (power \(p\) unfixed) then appears as a fitted phenomenological term. No derivation of the observed \(\rho_\Lambda\) is claimed.

3. **Differential dispersion along the wall**  
   Regions with different \(t_N\) see different stages of the atemporal photonic dispersion already discussed in the 2026-10-21/22 notes. Local time lags modulate how rapidly unbound energy spreads relative to the wall, altering the local slope of \(V_{\rm wall}\).
