## 1. Definition and Basic Structure

\(G_2\) is the smallest of the five exceptional simple Lie groups. It is defined as the automorphism group of the octonion algebra:

\[
G_2 \;\cong\; \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{O}).
\]

### Complete key
- \(G_2\): exceptional simple Lie group of dimension 14 and rank 2.  
- \(\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{O})\): group of algebra automorphisms of the octonions (field automorphisms that preserve the multiplication).  
- \(\mathbb{O}\): 8-dimensional real division algebra of octonions (Cayley numbers).

An element \(g\in G_2\) is a linear map \(g:\mathbb{O}\to\mathbb{O}\) satisfying

\[
g(xy) = g(x)\,g(y) \qquad \forall\, x,y\in\mathbb{O}
\]

and preserving the Euclidean inner product on \(\mathbb{R}^8\simeq\mathbb{O}\).

The Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{g}_2\) consists of all derivations of \(\mathbb{O}\):

\[
\mathfrak{g}_2 = \mathrm{Der}(\mathbb{O}) = \{ D\in\mathfrak{gl}(8,\mathbb{R}) \mid D(xy)=D(x)y + x\,D(y) \}.
\]

### Complete key
- \(\mathfrak{g}_2\): 14-dimensional simple Lie algebra of type \(G_2\).  
- \(\mathrm{Der}(\mathbb{O})\): derivation algebra of the octonions.  
- \(\mathfrak{gl}(8,\mathbb{R})\): general linear Lie algebra on the underlying 8-dimensional vector space.

## 2. Octonion Multiplication and the Fano Plane

The octonions admit a basis \(\{1,e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4,e_5,e_6,e_7\}\) with

\[
e_i^2 = -1, \qquad e_i e_j = -e_j e_i \quad (i\neq j),
\]

and the multiplication of distinct imaginary units given by the Fano plane (or its oriented lines):

\[
\begin{align*}
e_1 e_2 &= e_4, &
e_2 e_4 &= e_1, &
e_4 e_1 &= e_2, \\
e_1 e_3 &= e_5, &
e_3 e_5 &= e_1, &
e_5 e_1 &= e_3, \\
e_1 e_6 &= e_7, &
e_6 e_7 &= e_1, &
e_7 e_1 &= e_6, \\
e_2 e_3 &= e_6, &
e_3 e_6 &= e_2, &
e_6 e_2 &= e_3, \\
e_2 e_5 &= e_7, &
e_5 e_7 &= e_2, &
e_7 e_2 &= e_5, \\
e_3 e_4 &= e_7, &
e_4 e_7 &= e_3, &
e_7 e_3 &= e_4, \\
e_4 e_5 &= e_6, &
e_5 e_6 &= e_4, &
e_6 e_4 &= e_5.
\end{align*}
\]

Any automorphism in \(G_2\) must map this oriented Fano configuration to itself (up to even permutation of the lines consistent with the multiplication table).

### Complete key
- \(\{e_i\}_{i=1}^7\): standard imaginary octonion units.  
- Fano plane: combinatorial design that encodes the 7 lines of three mutually associative imaginary units.
