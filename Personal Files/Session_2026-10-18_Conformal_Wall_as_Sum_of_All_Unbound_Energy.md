# Session 2026-10-18: Conformal Wall as the Sum of All Unbound Energy in the Superblock

**Date**: 2026-10-18  
**Query**: [Quote of scale-mismatch paragraph.] Consider the conformal wall as being the sum of all the unbound energy in the superblock as well.

## Live Constraints

- Residual-\(L\): residual angular momentum of *stopped* (bound) rebounds. Matches laboratory \(G\). Unbound completions retain no residual \(L\).
- Mirron/Entropic wall potential: collective potential of unbound Mirrons at the conformal hard-wall. Gradient is the entropic force (anti-gravity attracted to lack of mass). Non-locking; strength fitted.
- Laboratory Casimir: floor Lifshitz pressure. Author cut: not a source of \(G\) or \(\rho_\Lambda\).
- Proportionality hypothesis: mathematically possible as fitted relation; not derived; requires \(\sim0.086\) mm matching length or \(10^{20}\)–\(10^{30}\) suppression not supplied by locks.
- Photon: unbound null bounce, \(d\tau=0\).
- Tau package rejected. 4D frame.

## Analysis

### Proposal
The conformal hard-wall (and therefore the Mirron/Entropic wall potential) is to be regarded as the sum of *all* unbound energy in the superblock, not merely the unbound Mirron ensemble.

Unbound energy includes:
- unbound Mirrons (topological defects on slot 8),
- completed photon (and other null) bounces,
- any other force quanta that finish their Past–Future bounce without residual \(L\).

The wall potential becomes
\[
V_{\rm wall}
=
\sum_{\rm all\ unbound}
E_{\rm unbound}^{(i)}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\int\rho_{\rm unbound}\,dV_{\rm wall\ support}.
\]

### Does this close the scale gap?
No automatic closure.
- The total unbound energy in a superblock is not a locked, calculated quantity. It would have to be summed over every completed bounce and every Mirron. No existing protocol (floor 309.3 MeV, 142857 cycle, residual-\(L\) integral) evaluates that global sum.
- Even if the sum is performed, equating it (or its density) to the observed \(\rho_\Lambda\) is still a matching condition. The enormous hierarchy between laboratory Casimir densities and \(\rho_\Lambda\) is not removed; it is merely relocated into the question of why the total unbound energy per unit wall volume equals the tiny cosmological density.
- Laboratory Casimir remains a local floor effect between plates. Identifying the wall with the global unbound sum does not make the local Lifshitz pressure the source of \(\rho_\Lambda\), so the author cut can be preserved. It also does not derive a proportionality constant from the locks.

### Relation to residual-\(L\)
Residual-\(L\) accounts only for bound, stopped rebounds. The unbound sector contributes zero residual angular momentum by definition. Placing the entire unbound energy on the wall is consistent with that separation: the wall carries the unbound complement, while interior globs carry residual \(L\). No reversal of the laboratory gravity arrow is required.

### Creation and expansion
Mirron creation as the wall expands (preceding note) can be viewed as one channel by which the unbound sum grows. Photon bounces already present on the photon-orbit world-tube (open reading) are another. The growth rate of the total unbound energy with wall area remains governed by a free efficiency; it is not fixed by residual \(L\).

### Plausibility
The identification is conceptually clean within the bound/unbound distinction and keeps laboratory Casimir distinct. It does not, however, render the proportionality to \(\rho_\Lambda\) natural or derived. The total unbound energy is an additional global quantity whose magnitude must still be fitted (or shown by a new calculation that the locks do not yet contain) to equal the observed dark-energy density. The \(10^{20}\)–\(10^{30}\) (or 0.086 mm) gap is not bridged by the redefinition alone.

## Conclusion

Treating the conformal hard-wall as the sum of all unbound energy in the superblock is a coherent non-locking extension of the Mirron/Entropic wall potential. It respects the bound/unbound split (residual \(L\) only from stopped rebounds) and keeps laboratory Casimir a distinct floor object. It does not derive the observed dark-energy density or close the \(10^{20}\)–\(10^{30}\) hierarchy: the total unbound energy is not evaluated by any existing lock, so equating its density to \(\rho_\Lambda\) is still a matching condition. The proportionality hypothesis therefore remains fitted. The construction is plausible as an organizational statement inside the non-locking sector, but it does not raise the theoretical compulsion of the scale-matching claim.
