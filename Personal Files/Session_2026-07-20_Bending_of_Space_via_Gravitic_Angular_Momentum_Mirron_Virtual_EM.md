## 3. Bending of Space (Weyl Path-Length Deposit)

The local contribution to the Weyl scalar (or the equivalent conformal curvature density) is

\[

\]

where \(R_{\rm M}\) is the characteristic radius of the Mirron’s 6-phase cloud (or the dominant coherent set that contains it).

More precisely, the differential path-length deposit per hierarchical step \(ds\) is

\[
\frac{d\ell_{\rm path}}{ds} = \kappa_{\rm dep} \cdot \frac{G_{\rm eff}(S^*)\, L_{\rm grav}^{\rm tot}}{c^3 R_{\rm M}^2}
\]

**Complete key**  
- \(\delta C_{\rm Weyl}\) / \(d\ell_{\rm path}/ds\): local bending measure (Weyl curvature density or path-length accumulation rate).  
- \(G_{\rm eff}(S^*)\): effective Newton constant of the dominant coherent bound set \(S^*\) that contains the Mirron (evaluated at its center-of-gravity).  
- \(L_{\rm grav}^{\rm tot}\): total gravitic angular momentum defined above.  
- \(R_{\rm M}\): Mirron 6-phase cloud radius (or hierarchical radius of the dominant set).  
- \(\kappa_{\rm dep}\): dimensionless deposition efficiency fixed by the twistor measure on the Tau cylinder and the 1/7 resonance projector (order-1 geometric factor).  
- \(c\): speed of light (restored for dimensional clarity; set to 1 in natural units).

This expression is the direct continuum limit of the photonic-bounce + virtual-bounce deposition mechanism already identified in the framework: every bounce (virtual or photonic) advances the Tau-circle coordinate by a discrete amount and simultaneously writes a path-length quantum into the Weyl tensor.
