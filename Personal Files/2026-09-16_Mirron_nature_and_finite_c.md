There are three different claims that look similar and are not the same.

\[

\]
If either vacuum constant vanished, that formula would give \(c\to\infty\). Special relativity then *postulates* that this \(c\) is the causal speed for all interactions, not just light.

Remove that engine and there is no remaining geometric reason for a finite dwell time per unit path, so the only consistent limit is \(c\to\infty\).

The question is Claim C. Claim C can be *proved as a theorem inside TSB*. It cannot be proved as a theorem of physics independent of those axioms. Both statements should stay on the page.

## 2. Standard physics: what *is* proven, and what is not

### 2.1 Maxwell identity

From the source-free Maxwell equations in SI vacuum,
\[
\nabla\times\mathbf{E}=-\partial_t\mathbf{B},\qquad

**Limit.**
\[

That is the closest textbook analogue of “without vacuum structure, \(c\) is infinite.” It does **not** name a mirror. It names two phenomenological constants.

### 2.2 Relativity does not derive finite \(c\) from mirrors

Minkowski spacetime has a finite invariant speed because the metric signature is \((+,-,-,-)\) (or the opposite). Null geodesics satisfy
\[
\mathrm{d}s^2=0\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\biggl|\frac{\mathrm{d}\mathbf{x}}{\mathrm{d}t}\biggr|=c
\]
in inertial coordinates. There is no reflecting 2-surface in that derivation. Light cones exist in empty Minkowski space.

Newtonian gravity and pre-Maxwell electrostatics *do* have \(c=\infty\): Poisson’s equation
\[

updates \(\Phi\) instantaneously when \(\rho\) changes. The historical replacement of that instantaneous law by a retarded Green function is exactly the statement “the vacuum now has a finite signal speed.”

### 2.3 What standard physics therefore does *not* prove

Standard physics does **not** prove: “if spacetime is not a Mirron, then \(c=\infty\).”

It proves a weaker statement: if the vacuum has no electromagnetic inertia (\(\varepsilon_0\mu_0=0\)), Maxwell waves are instantaneous. Why \(\varepsilon_0\mu_0\) is nonzero is left open.
