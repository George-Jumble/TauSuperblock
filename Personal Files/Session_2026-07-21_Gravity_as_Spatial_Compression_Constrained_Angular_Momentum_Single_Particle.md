**Complete key**  
- \(\delta V/V\): fractional spatial compression.

\[
\frac{\delta V}{V}
=
-
\frac{\kappa_{\rm comp}}{c^3}
\cdot
G_{\rm eff}

Because \(\mathcal{C}_{\rm constr}=\rho_m=m/V\) and \(G_{\rm eff}\) itself contains a factor of \(\mathcal{C}_{\rm constr}\), the compression is quadratic in the constraint (and therefore quadratic in the mass density).
