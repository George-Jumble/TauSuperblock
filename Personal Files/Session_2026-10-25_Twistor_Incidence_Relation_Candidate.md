## Candidate Incidence Relation

Let \(Z^\alpha=(\omega^A,\pi_{A'})\) be the projective twistor of a null ray belonging to the gravitic congruence. On the microscopic KK cylinder the residual-\(L\) data are carried by the monodromy phase \(\phi_{\rm mon}\) around the compact circle and by the spin connection induced by the Mirron reflection.

The incidence map is
\[
\omega^A = i\,x^{AA'}\pi_{A'} + \kappa\,L_{\rm res}\,e^{i\phi_{\rm mon}}\,o^A,
\]
where
- \(x^{AA'}\) is the spacetime point inside the local Tau cell,
- \(o^A\) is a normalised spinor fixed by the orientation of the KK circle,
- \(\kappa\) is a dimensionless coupling fixed by the requirement that the time-averaged curvature reproduce laboratory \(G\) via the hierarchical rule (not a free parameter).

The null congruence is the set of rays obeying this incidence. Its optical scalars are governed by the Sachs equations with the residual-\(L\) term acting as the source for the Weyl curvature that survives Mirron averaging.

At the light-cone hard-wall \(\mathcal{W}\) the unbound sector satisfies
\[
\phi_{\rm mon}\to 0\quad\Rightarrow\quad L_{\rm res}\to 0
\]
on the generators that reach the wall. The bound residual remains available for the hierarchical \(T_{\rm priv}\) assignment inside the cell.
