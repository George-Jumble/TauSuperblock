## 2. The tree

Accepted as an axiom, and already the picture: one manifold, chambers born in the high-Weyl complement of a parent, growing, no last generation required. Infinite space is the ambient \(3+1\), not a bulk. Eternal growth does not need a metric fifth and does not need \(r<0\) to be another universe. The wormhole, if used, is the disk passage in a Kerr extension inside that same manifold. The user’s refusal of a second world is the core, kept.

Repercussion. “Infinitely many superblocks” is a tree of chambers, not infinitely many Kruskal blocks. The vacuum Kerr ladder remains a chart of one hole, not the census of the tree.

## 3. Why a CTC is not a battery

On a closed timelike curve the identification is \((t,x)\sim(t,x)\). A field on that loop must already match itself. Novikov consistency is a constraint. It is not a source term.

In a stationary metric the Killing energy \(E=-u_\mu\xi^\mu\) is conserved along a geodesic. A loop brings the tangent back to itself, so \(\Delta E=0\) around the loop. Perpetual motion that recreates energy needs \(\Delta E>0\) on a cycle with no inlet. The closed curve does not supply the inlet. Hawking’s chronology protection is the conjecture that quantum back-reaction cuts such curves off. It is not a theorem that they are engines.

The recreation the framework already has is the bounce: a stopped rebound flips sign and the white stroke is outgoing. That is a reflection on one congruence. It does not return the particle to its earlier event. Using the Kerr \(g_{\phi\phi}<0\) band as the battery identifies the bounce with the extension pathology. That step is the one that fails. Harmony with a co-located antipodal copy does not change the conservation: the copy is a sector on \(\chi\), not an energy inlet.
