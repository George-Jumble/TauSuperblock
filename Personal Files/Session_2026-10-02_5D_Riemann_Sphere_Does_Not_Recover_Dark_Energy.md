## 2. The three tempting routes, and why each fails

### 2.1 Casimir of the Hopf fibre

Retired route, written so it is not reused:

\[
\rho_\Lambda=\frac{\hbar c}{\tau^4}.
\]

The fibre coordinate satisfies \(z\sim e^{i\vartheta}z\) at fixed point of \(\mathbb{CP}^2\). The phase \(\vartheta\) is quotiented out. A Casimir sum needs a length \(\tau\) (or a radius \(R\)) to set the mode gap \( \hbar c/R\). The unit sphere fixes no length. Putting the retired \(\tau\approx 7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) back in is the move already withdrawn, not a consequence of \(S^5\).

### 2.2 Volume of the unit 5-sphere

The round unit \(S^n\) has volume

\[
\mathrm{Vol}(S^n)=\frac{2\pi^{(n+1)/2}}{\Gamma\!\left(\frac{n+1}{2}\right)}.
\]

For \(n=5\), \(\Gamma(3)=2!\!=2\), so

\[
\mathrm{Vol}(S^5)=\frac{2\pi^{3}}{2}=\pi^3.
\]

That is a pure number, about \(31.0063\). A density needs a scale to the fourth power in the denominator. \(\pi^3\) has none. Multiplying by \(1/\ell_P^4\) or by \(1/\lambda_{m_0}^4\) inserts a scale the construct did not provide. Either insertion is bookkeeping of an input, not a recovery.

### 2.3 Conformal density already refused

The live frame already declined a nearby rewrite: identifying a conformal density built from \(\dot\Omega/\Omega=H_\Lambda\) with \(\rho_\Lambda\) restates the input. The same refusal applies here. Charting unbound rays on a CR 5-sphere does not turn ray labels into vacuum energy. Unbound completed bounces leave no residual \(L\) and pay no TEP cost. Dark energy, if it is a bound-sector effect at all, is not this chart.

## 3. What would have to be added, and is not added

To get a density out of \(S^5\) one would have to postulate at least one of:

- a radius \(R\) with dimensions of length, then a formula \(\rho\sim\hbar c/R^4\);
- a product metric \(M_4\times S^5\) or \(M_4\times S^1\) and an Einstein equation on it;
- an identification of \(\mathrm{Vol}(S^5)\) with \(\Omega_\Lambda\) after inserting \(H_0\) or \(\rho_{\mathrm{crit}}\) by hand.

Each of those is a new postulate. None is implied by the Hopf map. None is adopted here. The Tau circle, \(G_5\), \(n_{\mathrm{hier}}\), and \(\Lambda_\Omega=7/R_\tau\) stay retired.

Numerical coincidence is not available either. \(\pi^3\approx 31.0063\) is not \(\Omega_\Lambda\) (order \(0.7\) of critical density) and not the shadow ratio \(5.401514\) under ansatz A.
