## 3. Examination

### 3.1 Larger than the universe, and infinite

A Schwarzschild (or Kerr) chart is an exterior plus an interior. “Larger than the universe” and “holds all the aeons” delete the exterior. The chart the radius belongs to is then gone. That is the same refusal already recorded against isolated Kerr-at-infinity.

The numerical temptation is the critical-density identity, not a container. For a sphere of radius \(R=c/H\) filled at \(\rho_{\rm crit}=3H^2/(8\pi G)\),

\[
r_s
=
\frac{2G}{c^2}\cdot\frac{4\pi}{3}R^3\rho_{\rm crit}
=
R.
\]

**Key.** \(r_s\): Schwarzschild radius of the mass inside the sphere. \(G\): Newton constant. \(c\): speed of light. \(R\): sphere radius, here the Hubble radius. \(\rho_{\rm crit}\): critical density at that \(H\). The equality is the definition of critical density. It does not mean the observable universe is the interior of a black hole.

de Sitter’s event horizon is a different null surface. It is observer-dependent. Its physical radius is constant if \(\Lambda\) is constant. It does not grow forever, and it is not infinite. The particle horizon grows. Calling the growing one a black hole swaps the two horizons.

### 3.2 Ever-growing, holding every aeon

Live picture is a tree of wombs, each with its own \(r_{\rm eh}\) and its own \(\Omega\). One infinite hole holding all aeons collapses that tree into a single interior. The 20 September expansion note already split “one global crossover after the whole aeon dies” from “local isolation of a void while walls still exist elsewhere,” and kept the second.

Vacuum inversion / expansion, as written on 26 July and repaired on 21 September, is proper 3-volume growth at frozen dressing length, with new Mirrons booked as transfer \(J_{\rm Mirron}=-\Delta_K\), not minted on a hoop and not minted behind a horizon. \(\rho_{\rm vac}\) stays an input until a local \(\Omega\) is integrated.
