**Framework**: SBT / TSB. Mirron, never soliton. Standard gravity not retouched.

## 0. Verdict

**Accepted, with the invariant kept sharp.** Local \(c\) is the same in every phase. What differs is the orbital speed of the envelope relative to Prime. That relative motion Doppler-shifts and aberrates signals read across copies. It does not assign a domain its own \(c\).

This retracts the reading, in the previous audit, that a phase \(a_n\) changes the local speed of light. The \(300{,}000\,\mathrm{km}\) Higgs benchmark remains a different sentence and stays out.

## 3. What the orbital velocity actually changes

A signal emitted in copy \(i\) and read in Prime is a relative-motion problem, not a changed invariant. For a receding envelope the frequency shift has the Doppler form

\[
1+z=\gamma_i\left(1+\beta_i\cos\theta\right),
\]

special-relativistic, line of sight \(\theta\). **Key.** \(z\): redshift of the received signal. \(\gamma_i,\beta_i\): as above. \(\theta\): angle between the envelope velocity and the line of sight, in the Prime chart. Arrival rate and apparent direction change. The local speed used to emit the signal was \(c\). The local speed used to receive it is \(c\).

Calling that an illusion of variable light speed is fair as a description of a cross-envelope reading. It is not fair as a second \(c\). Nothing here touches laboratory \(G\).

The fastest envelope in this map is \(\beta_6=3/5\), so \(V_{\rm orb}^{(6)}=0.6c\approx 180{,}000\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}\) at the rounded \(c\). That is an orbital speed of a whole copy relative to Prime. It is not the local speed of light in that copy.

## 4. What this does not revive

The \(300{,}000\,\mathrm{km}\) figure in the cosmogenesis summary was a length, a claimed Higgs-confinement radius. This \(c\) is a speed. They share a rounded \(300{,}000\) and are not the same claim. The length stays out.

Ansatz A still uses these \(\gamma_i\). Their sum \(5.401514\) is still not the catalogue dark-matter ratio.
