## What the locked structure already supplies

- Residual-\(L\) attractive compression toward the highest concentration of bound fields, with effective \(G\) belonging to the dominant glob’s center of gravity. Matches laboratory \(G\).
- Floor colour residual \(L\) and the TEP barrier that realize the external isolating shell of each colour singlet.
- Six co-located domain copies, one Mirron, kinematic UPS, floor-only DR, Tau rejection.
- A non-locking candidate for large-scale repulsion: the entropic/Mirron wall potential (gradient of the collective unbound-Mirron potential at the conformal hard-wall, attracted to lack of mass). Its magnitude is fitted to the observed \(\rho_\Lambda\), not derived.

**Plain language.** The framework already has ordinary gravity that pulls bound systems together and matches laboratory measurements, and it already has the “skin” that keeps protons from melting into quark soup. It also has a place-holder for a large-scale push that prefers empty regions (the entropic wall potential), but the strength of that push is adjusted by hand to match the dark energy we see; it is not calculated from the locks.

---

## What is still missing

Nothing in the present locks derives \(\rho_\Lambda\) or its equation of state. To have a Superblock account of dark energy one of the following is still required:

1. **An external phenomenological input.**  
   Accept a cosmological constant or a \(w_0 w_a\) parametrization as an observational boundary condition. No new structure inside the Superblock is needed.

2. **A fitted large-scale term with free coefficients.**  
   Retain the entropic/Mirron wall potential (or an equivalent empty-fraction, hierarchical-depth, or light-cone-volume normalization) and calibrate its free efficiency, screening length, or power-law index to early-galaxy growth, DESI-type residuals, or other large-scale data. The coefficients remain unfixed by locked structure.

3. **A parameter-free derivation that does not yet exist.**  
   A mechanism that (a) produces the observed density from residual-\(L\), the clock, the floor protocol, or the six-domain occupancy without free suppression factors, (b) preserves the residual-\(L\) match to laboratory \(G\) (via automatic screening or localization), and (c) respects the floor-object cut (colour, Casimir, and virtual sectors remain floor objects). No such mechanism is currently locked.

Any promotion of the external isolating shell, hierarchical colour residual, Casimir sum, virtual torsion, or UPS-cascade energy into a dynamical source of \(\rho_\Lambda\) reintroduces free parameters and sits in tension with the 2026-10-07 author cut. Domain orbital kinetic energy can be accepted as one observational data point that narrows free coefficients; it does not close the derivation.

**Key.** Fitted coefficients (\(\eta\), \(\kappa\), screening length, duty cycle, etc.) remain free. Residual-\(L\) laboratory match is preserved only if any new repulsive term is screened or localized at the wall. Author cut remains in force for floor objects.

**Plain language.** To account for dark energy the Superblock still needs either “we take the observed density as an input” or “we add a free knob that we turn until the large-scale push matches the data.” There is not yet a calculation that starts from the locked residual-\(L\) gravity, the floor shell, and the six domains and ends with the measured dark-energy density without any adjustable numbers. Turning the proton’s isolating skin into that source would require new free knobs and would conflict with the existing rule that floor objects do not source dark energy.

## Observational anchors that can calibrate free terms (but do not derive)

- Early-galaxy proper-distance growth (\(\sim 2\times\)) can set free coefficients of non-locking wall-potential normalizations.
- Domain orbital under equal-mass CPT assignment can be treated as one data point.
- DESI-type indications of evolving \(w\) can constrain the time dependence of a free term.
- Precision gravity and equivalence-principle bounds supply upper limits on any unscreened long-range repulsion.

None of these supplies the missing derivation.

**Plain language.** We can use galaxy surveys, the statistical “axis of evil,” and laboratory gravity tests to limit how strong any extra push is allowed to be. Those limits help us set the free knobs; they do not replace the knobs with a calculation.

---

## Consistency requirements for any addition

- Residual-\(L\) laboratory match to \(G\) must be preserved (screening or wall localization).
- Floor-only DR and kinematic UPS remain.
- Tau package stays rejected.
- Floor objects (colour residual, Casimir, virtuals) are not promoted to dynamical sources of \(\rho_\Lambda\) without violating the author cut.
- No new continuous free parameter is introduced if the goal is a derivation rather than a fit.

---

## Verdict

The Superblock already has laboratory-matching attractive gravity and the external isolating shell of ordinary confinement. For dark energy it still needs either an external phenomenological input or a free fitted large-scale term (the existing non-locking entropic/Mirron wall potential or an equivalent). Nothing in the present locks derives the observed density. Additional structure is possible only as non-locking phenomenology that preserves residual-\(L\) matching via screening and respects the floor-object cut. No further locked ingredient is required for the ordinary isolating shell; the missing piece for dark energy remains a derivation or an accepted free fit.
