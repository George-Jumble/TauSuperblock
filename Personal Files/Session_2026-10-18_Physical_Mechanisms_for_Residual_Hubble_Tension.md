## Candidate Mechanisms

### 1. Late-time dynamical dark energy (standard)
A departure from \(w=-1\) (CPL \(w_0w_a\), quintessence, or other evolving equation of state) can raise the late-time expansion rate enough to close the remaining gap. DESI DR2 already shows mild-to-moderate preference for such evolution. This is the most direct phenomenological fix and does not conflict with the early-universe PMF solution.

### 2. Additional early-universe physics
Further modifications to recombination, extra relativistic species, or a small early dark-energy component can shrink the sound horizon more. These are constrained by CMB damping-tail and polarization data; the PMF paper already uses the most detailed recombination treatment available.

### 3. Mirron/Entropic wall potential (Superblock, non-locking)
The expanding hard-wall / Mirron-Entropic wall potential already examined in this project supplies a late-time anti-gravity term attracted to the lack of mass (void-state). Its fitted strength can be chosen to furnish precisely the residual \(\Delta H_0\):
\[
\rho_{\rm ent}\sim\frac{3(\Delta H_0)^2}{8\pi G}\quad\text{(order-of-magnitude)}.
\]
- Localized to the wall and screened inside bound globs, it leaves residual-\(L\) matching to laboratory \(G\) intact.
- Mirron creation as the wall expands, or the wall as the sum of all unbound energy, provides a carrier.
- Inter-aeon chasing of the true vacuum is a possible temporal setting for the same term.
- Magnitude remains fitted, not derived from residual \(L\) or the clock.
- Does not restore Tau or promote laboratory Casimir to a source of \(\rho_\Lambda\).

### 4. Timescape / inhomogeneous backreaction
Apparent acceleration from averaging over hierarchical residual-\(L\) contrasts (dense globs versus voids) can mimic a residual late-time term without a new fluid. Compatible with the Superblock bound/unbound split.

### 5. Interacting dark sector or modified gravity
Energy exchange between dark matter and dark energy, or a scale-dependent modification of \(G\), can shift \(H_0\). Must be checked against residual-\(L\) laboratory matching if the modification reaches glob scales.
