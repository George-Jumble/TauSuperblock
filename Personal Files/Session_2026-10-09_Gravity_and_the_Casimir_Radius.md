## 0. Verdict

**By changing the separation, or by changing the mode sum. Not by replacing \(\hbar c/L^4\).**

Three channels. Tides stretch \(L\). Curvature changes the spectrum at fixed coordinate \(L\). Residual \(L\) in the framework is the continuum name for the first two, and it does not assign a new radius to the root wall.

## 2. Channels

Tidal. A mass \(M\) a distance \(R\) away stretches a plate gap by a fractional amount of order \((r_s/R)(L/R)\). The density then moves as \(\delta\rho/\rho\sim -4\,\delta L/L\), because of the fourth power. For laboratory plates this is negligible. For a gap next to a horizon it is not.

Spectral. At fixed coordinate separation, a curved background changes the eigenfrequencies of the vacuum modes. That is a gravitational correction to the Casimir sum, still scaled by \(1/L^4\), with a factor that depends on \(L/r_s\) or on the Weyl field. No closed form is in the notes.

Framework. Residual-\(L\) compression is the live account of the tide. It can shrink or stretch a floor-scale gap. It does not pick the Higgs-depth value \(1.89\times 10^{-18}\,\mathrm{m}\) out of \(G\), and it does not set the root wall equal to \(0.086\,\mathrm{mm}\) or to \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\).
