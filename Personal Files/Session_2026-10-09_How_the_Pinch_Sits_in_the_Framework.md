## 2. Effects

Gravity. The pinch is residual \(L\) acting on a Casimir gap. Same coupling as the fluid reading. No graviton. No wave across the void wall. Laboratory \(G\) stays the laboratory value. Ansatz A is untouched: the pinch is not a dark-matter ratio.

Casimir radii. The Higgs-depth gap, the \(0.086\,\mathrm{mm}\) matching length, and the retired \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) are not redefined. A \(10^{-44}\) density shift does not select among them.

Exclusion and the floor. A bound forced overlap still costs \(\ge\Delta\). The pinch does not evaluate \(\Delta\). The protocol print stays \(309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}\).

Tree geometry. Root chamber, interior descendants, photon-ring reset tube, throat as a place the white stroke may be read: none of these radii is the plate gap. The pinch does not merge them.

What it adds. A direction. Mass of a node is an input. Closure of a gap is an output. Reversing that arrow is withdrawn.
