## 0. The observation, not a derivation

Cosmic voids expand, shed matter onto walls, and internally approach a thin, \(\Lambda\)-dominated fluid. Catalogue sizes run tens of megaparsecs; that is survey data, not a theorem of F7.

Penrose’s late aeon is the same process written globally: rest mass fades or is locked in holes that later evaporate, Weyl is washed out of the volume, \(\mathscr{I}^+\) is spacelike, the bandage begins.

So: a mature void is a **local patch of future conformal infinity**, not yet the whole \(\mathcal{X}\).

## 1. Two entropies (Penrose)

Matter / radiation entropy \(S_{\rm m}\) can be high at the bang (thermal fireball) while gravitational entropy \(S_{\rm g}\) is low. Penrose locates \(S_{\rm g}\) in clumping, i.e. in Weyl curvature. Schematic, not a unique theorem:

\[
S_{\rm g}
\;\sim\;
\int
\frac{|C|^2}{\rho}\,\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x
\quad\text{(order-of-magnitude; several Weyl-squared proposals exist).}
\]

Exact de Sitter has \(C_{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}=0\). A void interior that becomes de Sitter-like therefore drives **local** \(S_{\rm g}\to 0\) while **matter** entropy in that interior saturates (nothing left to burn). The walls carry the Weyl and the locked entropy (galaxies, holes).

**Key.** \(S_{\rm m}\): matter/radiation entropy. \(S_{\rm g}\): gravitational entropy, Weyl-sector. \(C_{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}\): Weyl tensor. \(\rho\): a local density scale in the chosen proposal. Walls: the complementary high-\(C\) set.

Maximum entropy *of the void interior* means: \(S_{\rm m}\) done, \(C\to 0\) inside, further work inside the void is impossible. That is heat death of the patch, not heat death of the aeon.

## 2. Local metric of an emptying void

Treat the interior as an open FLRW patch plus \(\Lambda\), matter density falling as the void expands.

\[
\mathrm{d}s^2
=
-\mathrm{d}t^2
+
a(t)^2
\bigl(\mathrm{d}\chi^2+\sinh^2\chi\,d\Omega^2\bigr).
\]

Friedmann:

\[
H^2
=
\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho_{\rm m}
+
\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}
-
\frac{kc^2}{a^2},
\qquad
k=-1
\text{ in this chart.}
\]

As \(\rho_{\rm m}\to 0\) and curvature dilutes relative to \(\Lambda\),

\[
H
\to
H_\Lambda
=
c\sqrt{\frac{\Lambda}{3}},
\qquad
C_{\rho\sigma\mu\nu}
\to
0.
\]

The patch approaches de Sitter. Conformal time to the future boundary is finite:

\[
\eta_\infty-\eta
=
\int_t^\infty\frac{c\,dt'}{a(t')}
<
\infty.
\]

That finite conformal remainder is Tod’s reason \(\mathscr{I}^+\) can be a 3-surface. The void interior inherits it locally.

**Key.** \(a(t)\): scale factor of the patch. \(H=\dot a/a\). \(H_\Lambda\): de Sitter rate. \(\eta\): conformal time. \(k=-1\): open chart for an underdense interior.

## 3. Bandage test inside the void

Penrose bandage at global \(\mathcal{X}\): \(T^\mu{}_\mu=0\), no rest-mass clocks, reciprocal conformal factors.

Inside a mature void the same test can be asked *locally*:

- Rest-mass density \(\rho_{\rm m}\) negligible against \(\rho_\Lambda= \Lambda c^2/(8\pi G)\).
- Residual radiation \(T^\mu{}_\mu=0\) already.
- Weyl inside \(\approx 0\).
- Lightcones those of de Sitter.

Then a local conformal factor \(\Omega_{\rm void}\) can be drawn so that the void’s future boundary is a piece of a bridging metric. It is **not** yet global \(\mathcal{X}\). Walls still have rest mass and Weyl; clocks still exist on the walls. The bandage is patchy.

Reciprocal chart on that patch, same form as the global one:

\[
\hat\Omega\,\check\Omega=-1
\quad\text{on the local future boundary of the void.}
\]

Here \(\hat\Omega\to 0\) walking toward the void’s own \(\mathscr{I}^+_{\rm void}\). There is no next-aeon bang inside the void until the walls have also faded. So this is a **rehearsal**, not a second cosmology.

**Key.** \(\mathscr{I}^+_{\rm void}\): future conformal boundary of the patch. \(\Omega_{\rm void}\): local conformal factor. Patchy bandage: \(\mathcal{X}\) not yet global.

## 4. Horizon entropy of the patch

A de Sitter patch has a cosmological horizon of radius \(c/H_\Lambda\) and Bekenstein–Hawking entropy

\[
S_\Lambda
=
\frac{k_{\rm B}\,c^3}{4G\hbar}\,
4\pi
\Bigl(\frac{c}{H_\Lambda}\Bigr)^2
=
\frac{3\pi k_{\rm B}c^3}{G\hbar\Lambda}.
\]

That number is the **ceiling** for the interior. Expanding the void does not raise \(S_\Lambda\); it only lets the interior approach it. Extra entropy sits in the walls (ordinary \(S_{\rm m}\) plus hole area plus Weyl).

When people say “void at maximum entropy,” the precise statement is:

\[
S_{\rm interior}
\to
S_\Lambda
\quad\text{and}\quad
C_{\rm interior}\to 0.
\]

The aeon is not done. Global crossover still waits on rest-mass fade everywhere (option 1 in the previous note) or it is not CCC (option 2).

**Key.** \(S_\Lambda\): de Sitter horizon entropy. \(k_{\rm B}\): Boltzmann constant. This is the interior cap, not the entropy of the walls.

## 6. What this does *not* prove

- That voids *are* previous-aeon bangs. They are not; the bang is the other side of global \(\mathcal{X}\).
- That entropy is maximized in the universe when voids are empty. Walls and holes still hold \(S_{\rm g}\) and area entropy.
- A length from exponent 7.
- Hawking points in the CMB.

A void that has emptied to de Sitter is a 4D, local, conformal dress rehearsal of Penrose’s future boundary: interior Weyl dead, interior entropy at \(S_\Lambda\), lightcones ready. The actual crossover still waits for the walls.
