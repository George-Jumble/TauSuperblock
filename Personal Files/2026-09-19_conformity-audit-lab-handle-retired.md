- Laboratory gravitational tension of the \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) cell is gone. That matches “drop gravity from the circle” and the Cavendish exemption.
- The old handle really did treat a cosmological cell and a Planck-scale dual as one \(S^1\) written twice. Retiring the *premise*, not only the 21.83 GHz resonator, matches the session: two sizes of one metric circle are out.
- \(d/\tau\sim 10^{-23}\) for a one-metre gap is the right order of magnitude (\(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) at \(h=0.7\) is \(\sim 3\times 10^{23}\,\mathrm{m}\)). It is a good reason the cosmological pole does not ring a bench as variable \(G\).
- “Variable \(G\) of the cell” rewritten as conformal exhaustion of *large-scale binding*, not a force between plates, matches the exhaustion / \(\ell_C^{\rm web}\) reading. Not a torsion-balance effect.
- Bench physics, if any, is plate-scale Lifshitz corrections from domain averaging, the 7-fold *clock*, and a hadronic Compton — not a gravimeter of the cell. That is the session split: cell cosmological, clock and cell-constitution local.
- RGC as a silicon 7-ring analog of topology, not a gravimeter: allowed. Gatlin’s graph as bookkeeping, not as extra-dimensional gravity.
- Resonator already retired for dimensional analysis: consistent with not reviving a GHz gadget as “the cell in the room.”

## 2. What does not yet conform, or will slide

**Name collision on \(\ell_C\).**  
This session used \(\ell_C\) for the candidate *web* coherence length of \(\mathcal K_{ij}\). The note uses \(\ell_C\) for \(\hbar/(m_0 c)\approx 0.63\,\mathrm{fm}\). Those are not the same object. In a joint draft this will glue the megaparsec pole to the femtometre pole by vocabulary. Rename the Compton length \(\lambda_{m_0}\) (or \(\ell_{m_0}\)). Keep \(\ell_C\) for the IR / web scale if you keep that symbol at all.

**“The circle is now the IR pole of \(K\).”**  
If \(K\) is a 4D conformal averaging kernel, the IR feature is a *pole* or a coherence scale, not a circle gravity can wrap. The word “circle” is how the metric \(S^1\) keeps walking back in. Write “IR pole of \(K\)” or “IR scale \(\tau_{\rm web}\).” Do not write “the circle” unless you mean an internal phase \(\phi\sim\phi+2\pi\), and then say so.

**Coxeter 7 / residue \(0\bmod 7\) collapsed onto \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\).**  
A frozen exponent 7 in the clock algebra is allowed. Using that 7 as if it *produced* the catalogue length 7 is the Given→Derived slide. Two sentences:

- The clock has Coxeter exponent 7.  
- \(K\) has an IR scale of order a few \(h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\), taken as a given or computed from \(\mathcal K\), not minted by the exponent.

If those stay two sentences, the note conforms. If they become one sentence, it does not.

**One operator \(K\) with UV pole \(\lambda_{m_0}\) and IR pole \(\tau_{\rm web}\).**  
That is a *candidate* architecture (two ends of one kernel). This session did not lock it. It is compatible only if \(K\) is the 4D average, not \(S_5\) on a metric circle, and only if \(m_0\) is not derived from \(\tau_{\rm web}\). Label it as a working hypothesis, not as the new axiom.

**Flavor echoes and CMS/ALICE 7-fold residues.**  
Allowed as *proposed tests* of the 7×8 clock and of \(m_0\). Not allowed as evidence already in hand. Session put CMS/LHCb sevenfold in the hypothesis queue. Keep the verbs in the future tense.

**Lifshitz \(P(d)\) from “real \(\ell_C\).”**  
Once \(\ell_C\) means Compton of \(m_0\), a plate-gap correction with no \(7\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) period is consistent. If someone reads \(\ell_C\) as the web scale, they will look for a 7 Mpc period in \(P(d)\) and you have revived the lab handle. The rename fixes this.
