# Session 2026-10-25: Testable Predictions from the Current Tau-Superblock Corpus

**Date**: 2026-10-25  
**Status**: Inventory of predictions that follow from the live framework after dual-channel residual-L, bounce-back reflection, local Tau glob, nested KK cylinders, T_priv = COM, and optional 5D rotational overlay.

## 1. Laboratory and solar-system gravity
**Prediction**: The time-like residual-L channel (bounce-back reflection from the hard wall collapsing onto the dominant-glob COM) reproduces the inverse-square law and the measured value of G to existing precision. No additional long-range force appears inside solar-system scales unless the free screening length of the space-like or 5D-rotational channel is violated.

**Test**: Precision torsion-balance, lunar laser ranging, and planetary ephemeris data.

**Plain language**: Ordinary gravity in the lab and solar system should look exactly like Newton/Einstein. If a new force shows up at those distances, the screening we assumed is wrong.

## 2. Hierarchical dominance
**Prediction**: Inside the volume of any glob (including a local Tau-scale glob or a nested KK cylinder) the effective gravitational parameter is that of the dominant glob’s center of gravity (T_priv). Sub-dominant globs do not set G_eff while the larger one is coherent.

**Test**: Rotation curves and velocity dispersions (SPARC, cluster dynamics).

**Plain language**: The biggest clump in a region should set the gravity there. Smaller clumps inside it should not add extra pull beyond what the big one already accounts for.

## 3. Dynamical dark energy (optional 5D rotational overlay)
**Prediction**: If the user-directed 5D rotational/centrifugal overlay is active, the space-like channel acquires a time-dependent piece
\[
\frac{d\Lambda_{\rm eff}}{dt}\propto\cos\Bigl(\frac{\pi t}{T_{\rm cycle}}\Bigr)

that can decline after an inflection point, adjustable to the DESI 2024 preference.

**Test**: Continued DESI, Euclid, and Roman measurements of w(z).

**Plain language**: If we turn on the spinning-5D option, dark energy should eventually slow down. Future surveys can check whether the slowdown matches the shape we wrote down.

## 4. Hard-wall reflection and gravity waves
**Prediction**: Residual-L that sources laboratory G arrives as compressive modes reflected from the hard wall. No additional propagating scalar or vector mode is required beyond standard gravitational waves.

**Test**: LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA and pulsar-timing arrays.

**Plain language**: Gravity waves should look like the ones Einstein predicted. Extra kinds of waves would mean the reflection picture needs more pieces.

## 5. Mass-floor insertion
**Prediction**: The printed working floor Δ ≈ 309.3 MeV does not appear as a new particle pole. It remains a bookkeeping scale.

**Test**: Hadron spectroscopy and lattice QCD.

**Plain language**: We do not expect a new particle sitting at 309 MeV. If one shows up there, the “insertion” story needs revision.

## 6. Nested cylinders and local Tau glob
**Prediction**: No universal macroscopic compact fifth dimension. Any KK-like scale is local to a glob. The former τ ≈ 7 h⁻¹ Mpc appears only as the characteristic size of one hierarchical level.

**Test**: Large-scale structure, void catalogs, and CMB spectral distortions.

**Plain language**: We should not see one giant repeating circle the size of the old Tau radius stamped on the entire universe. Local clumps of that size are allowed; a universal one is not.

## 7. Unbound sector
**Prediction**: Completed unbound bounces leave residual L = 0 and do not source either gravity channel.

**Test**: Photon mass bounds and neutrino gravitational coupling.

**Plain language**: Light and neutrinos should stay as light as the Standard Model already allows and should not feel extra gravity.

## Summary of falsifiability
The framework is falsifiable in the laboratory, in the solar system, in galactic dynamics, in gravitational-wave catalogs, and in the time evolution of dark energy (if the 5D overlay is kept). It is not falsifiable on the existence of the Mirron, the precise geometric details of Φ7/Φ8, or the reality of co-located domains.
