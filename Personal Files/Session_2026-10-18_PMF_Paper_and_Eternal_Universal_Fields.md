# Session 2026-10-18: Jedamzik et al. PMF Paper and Support for Continual/Eternal Universal Fields

**Date**: 2026-10-18  
**Query**: It supports to continual and eternal existence of the universal fields. (referring to Jedamzik, Pogosian & Abel, Nat. Astron. 10, 317, 2026 on primordial magnetic fields).

## Paper Recap

Jedamzik, Pogosian & Abel show that non-helical primordial magnetic fields (PMFs) of present-day strength \(\approx5\)–10 pG, evolved with full MHD simulations and Lyman-α transfer, accelerate recombination. The resulting bΛCDM model fits Planck+DESI as well as or better than ΛCDM while raising \(H_0\), easing the Hubble tension. The same field strengths can account for cluster magnetic fields without extra dynamo amplification. PMFs are treated as relics generated in the early Universe that persist through recombination to the present day.

## Evaluation of the Claim

The paper supports the *persistence* of a primordial magnetic field from early epochs to the present. It does not, by itself, establish the continual and eternal existence of the universal fields in the Superblock sense.

### What the paper actually shows
- A stochastic magnetic field can be present before recombination, induce baryon clumping, survive to \(z=0\) at picogauss strength, and remain compatible with CMB, BAO and supernova data.
- This is evidence that at least one relativistic field (the PMF) can have a long cosmic lifetime without being erased.
- It is silent on colour fields, residual-\(L\) bound fields, unbound Mirron ensembles, the 8-phase clock, or any mechanism that would make fields eternal rather than merely long-lived relics of a hot early phase.

### Superblock reading

- bound-sector excitations whose stopped rebounds supply residual \(L\) (laboratory \(G\));
- unbound completions (null bounces, \(d\tau=0\)) that retain no residual \(L\);
- the Mirron as the unbound topological defect on slot 8.

A persisting PMF can be annotated as one realization of an unbound or weakly coupled electromagnetic field that has existed since early times. That annotation is compatible with the bound/unbound split and does not alter residual-\(L\) matching to laboratory \(G\). It does not, however, demonstrate:
- that the fields are eternal (existing independently of any initial hot phase or continuing through arbitrary future aeons);
- that they are continually regenerated by the \(\Phi_8\) stamp or by Mirron creation at an expanding hard wall;
- that their energy density sources or is proportional to \(\rho_\Lambda\).

The paper’s PMFs are still relics whose amplitude is set by early-universe generation and subsequent MHD decay/inverse cascade. Eternal existence in the Superblock sense would require a maintenance or regeneration mechanism (for example ongoing Mirron creation or floor-stamp replenishment) that the data do not address.

### Impact on prior non-locking constructions
- The result is consistent with the existence of long-lived unbound electromagnetic degrees of freedom.
- It supplies no new constraint on the Mirron/Entropic wall potential, the fitted entropic force, or the Casimir proportionality hypothesis.
- It leaves the author cut on Casimir, the Tau rejection, and floor-only Dynamic Refresh unchanged.

## Conclusion

The Jedamzik et al. result supports the persistence of primordial magnetic fields from the early Universe to the present and is compatible with the existence of long-lived unbound electromagnetic degrees of freedom. It does not establish the continual and eternal existence of the universal fields in the Superblock sense, nor does it supply a regeneration mechanism, a derivation of \(\rho_\Lambda\), or a modification of residual-\(L\). The support is therefore limited to longevity of one class of relic field; stronger claims of eternality remain non-locking interpretations outside the paper’s scope.
