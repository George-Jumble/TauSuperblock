## The Imported Relation (restated)

**Complete key**
- \(\phi_{\rm floor}\): phase already carried by a colour or inverted-colour EM/electroweak excitation present at the binding floor.
- \(\Delta\phi_{\rm tick}\): discrete phase increment fixed by one letter of the eight-letter clock or by one cyclic step of the 142857 labelling.
- \(\Phi_8\): bound-sector reset seam (point of simultaneity for the floor).
- Floor excitations: colour + inverted-colour EM/electroweak modes only.

## Implications for Dynamic Refresh
Dynamic Refresh remains strictly floor-only. The stamp supplies the content of the \(\Phi_8\) simultaneity tick for those floor modes: a phase advance of whatever excitations already exist. It does not re-initialise, re-bind, or rewrite atoms, molecules, or isolated centre-of-mass packets. The Pedalino constraint continues to hold: classical Dynamic Refresh of an isolated COM is excluded. Hierarchical binding continues to align with COM composition; each generation is a glob whose centre of mass is inherited from its children.

## Implications for Residual-\(L\) Gravity
Residual angular momentum arises from stopped rebounds of bound lines. The one-tick phase advance can modulate the instantaneous proper-time rate at which those rebounds are sampled, but it does not generate additional residual angular momentum. Consequently the matching of effective \(G\) to the laboratory value is unchanged. Gravity remains spatial compression via constrained angular momentum; no fundamental graviton is introduced.

## Implications for the Unbound Sector
The unbound photon (completed P\(\leftrightarrow\)F bounce, residual \(L=0\)) experiences the same stamp. Because it carries no residual, the phase advance does not accumulate a contrast on the null generator. The photon-ring world-tube remains a four-dimensional object. Light-like propagation continues to be unconstrained.

## Implications for the Mass Floor and TEP
The printed working floor stays the 309.3 MeV neutron-dressing protocol. The stamp does not derive the digits, does not convert the protocol into a theorem, and does not alter the TEP cost \(E_{\rm violation}\ge\Delta\). TEP continues to supply only the inequality \(\Delta>0\); the numerical scale remains an insertion.

## Implications for the Clock and Simultaneity
Universal ticks remain points of simultaneity realised geometrically (UPS / shared \(\chi\)). The imported reading makes the floor content of that simultaneity explicit: every existing floor excitation is advanced by exactly one clock step. Higher-level packets inherit their phases only through the composition of their floor constituents; they are not independently stamped.

## Implications for Six Copies, Mirron, and the Arena
Six co-located copies on shared \(\chi\), one Mirron, and the four-dimensional arena are unaffected. The stamp is local to the floor excitations inside each copy; it does not open a metric fifth coordinate, does not restore the Tau cylinder, and does not reintroduce \(n_{\rm hier}\) or \(\rho_\Lambda=\hbar c/\tau^4\).

## What the Import Does Not Imply
- It does not expand Dynamic Refresh beyond the floor.
- It does not license a classical rewrite of an isolated centre-of-mass wave packet.
- It does not derive laboratory \(G\), the mass-floor digits, or dark-energy density.
- It does not rehabilitate the 2026-06-28 Tau Cylinder note or any other retired metric package.
- It does not promote Recovery-project side-notes (time-only fifth coordinate, \(\eta\)-spheres, nested globbing spheres) into the main frame.

## Operational Summary
The import supplies a precise kinematic meaning for the statement that the harmonic stamp propagates existing universal-field excitations by one tick, while leaving every core lock of the live Superblock framework intact. The construction is an annotation of the existing \(\Phi_8\) seam, not an enlargement of the ontology.
