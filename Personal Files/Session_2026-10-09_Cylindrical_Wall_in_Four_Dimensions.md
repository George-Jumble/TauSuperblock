## 0. Verdict

**Yes, as a surface in the existing \(3+1\). No, as a fifth coordinate.**

A wall with cylindrical topology embedded in the one universe is an ordinary submanifold. The retired cylinder was an extra arena \(S^1_\phi\times\mathbb{R}_s\), with a radius that did Casimir work. Shape in four dimensions does not restore that arena.

## 2. What is possible

A timelike or null world-tube in \(3+1\) is a cylinder: spherical cross-section, generators along the 4th coordinate. The apparent seam is already that kind of object. A void-wall crossover can be given the same topology without leaving the manifold. The reset helix is evaluated on that tube. Internal phase \(\Phi\) advances. No extra dimension is required for the tube to be cylindrical.

Repercussion. “Cylindrical in the 4th dimension” names the embedding. It does not assign a circumference, a Casimir density, or a level count. Those were the cylinder’s retired jobs. \(\mathscr{I}^+\) remains a 3-surface of the conformal completion, not a hoop of radius \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\).
