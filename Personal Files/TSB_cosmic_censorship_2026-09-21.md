# Cosmic censorship — investigation

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Context:** six-face extremal ring, attempted overspin from white faces.  
**Status:** conjecture, not a theorem. Superblock does not prove it.

---

## 1. Two conjectures

**Weak (Penrose 1969).** In generic gravitational collapse with reasonable matter, singularities are hidden behind event horizons. Distant observers never see a naked singularity.

**Strong.** Generically, the maximal Cauchy development is inextendible as a suitably regular Lorentzian manifold. No observer — including one who fell in — gets a non-unique future. Cauchy horizons are unstable (this is the mass-inflation story).

They are independent. Strong does not imply weak. “Strong” / “weak” is a bad pair of names. Both remain unproved in 3+1 vacuum GR with reasonable matter.

**Key.** WCC: no naked singularities for distant observers. SCC: no generic Cauchy horizons / unique evolution.

---

## 2. What is known

- Weak is consistent with spherical collapse (Oppenheimer–Snyder, Christodoulou for dust/scalar in spherical symmetry — with exceptions at fine-tuned critical collapse that produce naked singularities of measure zero).
- Overspin / overcharge gedanken tests (Wald; Hubeny; Jacobson–Sotiriou; Sorce–Wald): test particles that would push a near-extremal Kerr–Newman hole over the line either miss the horizon or, at next order in backreaction, fail. Some test-*field* papers claim a neutrino loophole; those typically violate the area theorem at the same stroke and are not settled as real counterexamples.
- Kerr Cauchy horizon is classically unstable (mass inflation). That is evidence *for* strong censorship in the interior, not a proof.
- 2024–2026: holographic “quantum censors,” cryptographic-censorship ideas, quantum Kerr–dS tests — still not a 4D proof.

Counterexamples exist in special matter models, lower dimensions, or fine-tuned initial data. Generic 4D vacuum-plus-ordinary-matter remains open.

---

## 3. What it does to the engine

White-face emission that tries to raise \(J\) above \(GM^2/c\) is exactly an overspin attempt. WCC-as-conjecture says: that quantum does not leave a naked ring; backreaction or the face’s kinematics refuse it. SCC-as-conjecture says: if the hole instead slips *under* the line, the inner horizon does not become a usable door (mass inflation).

Neither conjecture is a pairing rule. They are reasons the generic six-face process will *not* produce a naked Planck ring sitting in Prime. They do not keep \(\kappa=0\).

A naked ring in Prime would be a visible singularity — the thing WCC forbids. So if you believe WCC, you do not get to use “overspun shared ring” as a Superblock object. If you deny WCC, you have left GR’s standard package and must say so.

---

## 4. Decision

Do not write “Superblock implies cosmic censorship” or the converse. Use WCC as the *reason* an overspun ring is not a live object. Use SCC as the *reason* \(r_-\) is not a motherhood gate. Both remain conjectures. The pairing axiom that would hold extremality is independent of both.
