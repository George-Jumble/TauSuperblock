# Session 2026-10-24: Possible Falsifications of an Entropic Force Complementary to Residual-L and Equal to Residual Lambda

**Date**: 2026-10-24  
**Framework**: Tau-Superblock (TSB / Superblock) — live 4D frame. Residual-L is attractive compression toward high concentration of bound fields and matches laboratory \(G\) via the dominant-glob rule. Entropic force is the non-locking complementary term attracted to lack of mass (void/high-Weyl complement), supplied by the Mirron/entropic wall potential and set equal to the residual (non-rotational) portion of \(\rho_\Lambda\).  
**Status**: List of potential falsifiers. The entropic term remains phenomenological; its magnitude is fitted, not derived.

## Plain-language summary

If an entropic force exists that is complementary to residual-L gravity and that accounts for the residual dark-energy density (the bulk of \(\Lambda\) after the small rotational orbital piece is subtracted), several clear observational failures would falsify the picture.  

These include: a detection of repulsive acceleration on scales where residual-L screening should still hold; a precise confirmation that \(\rho_\Lambda\) is exactly constant with no late-time diminution once rotational dilution is accounted for; an equivalence-principle or laboratory-gravity violation that cannot be absorbed by the dominant-glob rule; or the absence of any preferred large-scale directional residual consistent with domain rotation once the rotational percentage is removed. Because the entropic piece is fitted rather than derived, any of these outcomes would force the residual-\(\Lambda\) assignment to be abandoned or re-normalized.

## Analytical list of falsifiers

**Key to symbols**  
- Residual-L: stopped-rebound angular-momentum compression matching laboratory \(G\).  
- Entropic force \(F_{\rm ent}\): gradient of the Mirron/entropic wall potential, attracted to low bound-field density, magnitude set equal to residual \(\rho_\Lambda\).  
- Residual \(\Lambda\): total observed dark-energy density minus the rotational orbital contribution.  
- Screening length: scale beyond which the entropic term becomes dominant.

Possible falsifications:

1. **Laboratory or solar-system repulsion**.  
   Detection of a net repulsive acceleration at distances \(\ll\) the screening length (e.g., lunar laser ranging, Cassini, or torsion-balance tests) that cannot be absorbed into the residual-L dominant-glob accounting. This would violate the complementary assignment.

2. **Exact constancy of \(\rho_\Lambda\) after rotational subtraction**.  
   If future DESI/Euclid/Roman data confirm \(w = -1\) to high precision once the expected dilution of the rotational kinetic term is removed, the need for a residual entropic piece of the observed magnitude disappears.

3. **Wrong sign or wrong scale of the large-scale residual**.  
   Void-wall or supercluster measurements that show residual-L still dominant in under-dense regions, or an entropic acceleration whose magnitude fails to match the residual \(\rho_\Lambda\) by more than the free-coefficient tolerance.

4. **Equivalence-principle or fifth-force bounds**.  
   Any composition-dependent or long-range fifth force that couples differently to bound versus unbound sectors and exceeds current MICROSCOPE / STEP / torsion-balance limits after residual-L is fixed to laboratory \(G\).

5. **Absence of the expected rotational residual signature**.  
   Once the axis-of-evil (or other preferred-axis) contribution is subtracted at the previously estimated percentage, no remaining large-scale anisotropy consistent with domain orbital motion. A pure isotropic residual \(\Lambda\) with no directional cue would weaken the rotational/entropic split.

6. **Growth-rate or \(f\sigma_8\) mismatch**.  
   Structure-growth measurements that require a repulsive term whose redshift evolution differs sharply from the dilution-plus-constant residual predicted by the rotational + entropic split.

Any one of these, established at high significance, would falsify the specific assignment “entropic force = residual lambda not imparted by rotational/orbital inertia” while leaving residual-L itself intact.

## Repercussions and locks

- The list is diagnostic for the non-locking entropic annotation only.  
- Residual-L matching laboratory \(G\), kinematic UPS, six co-located copies, and Tau rejection remain untouched.
