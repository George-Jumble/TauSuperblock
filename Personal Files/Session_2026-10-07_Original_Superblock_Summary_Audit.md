## 2. Item by item

### 2.1 Cosmogenesis

Match: one ever-expanding world; voids of a parent mother child superblocks; not one global Penrose crossover. The 20 September fractal-tree note is this picture. Child is new occupancy of the same manifold, not a disconnected universe. Inheritance is the Mirron reflection, parent into child. Conformal factor on a bandage is auxiliary.

Not in the notes: each universe begins as an informationally complete Planck bubble that expands to about \(300{,}000\,\mathrm{km}\), where radiation pressure confines the Higgs. No session derives that radius. \(c\times 1\,\mathrm{s}=2.998\times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m}\). A light-second is not a Higgs confinement scale in the corpus. Higgs confinement in the 26 July note is the pressure condition \(P_{\rm EM}>P_{\rm vac}\) at the floor, not a kilometre benchmark.

Non-inflationary is the original picture’s refusal of an inflaton. It is not a derivation that inflation is false.

### 2.2 Six domains

Match: six co-located \(3+1\) copies on \(S^3_{\rm int}\). Each copy is rest in its own frame. Prime is rest in \(B_{\rm lin}\).

Not the live geometry: a tesseract. Phase \(a_n\) modulating the local speed of light conflicts with one laboratory \(c\) and with the instruction not to touch standard gravity. Internal phase modulates which copy is being read. It does not assign each domain its own \(c\).

### 2.3 Leakage and dark matter

Match, under ansatz A only: hidden copies stress Prime through the kernel. The sum of their Lorentz factors is \(5.401514\), not \(5\). Equal mass alone would be \(5\). The extra \(0.401514\) is \(\gamma_i=(1-\beta_i^2)^{-1/2}\) on the moving exponents. Catalogue \(\Omega_{\rm DM}/\Omega_b\) is not this number. Ansatz A is not a detection.

Conformal Weyl gravity as a second coupling is an analogue (Mannheim, 2 October). It is not a lock. Author cut: do not touch standard gravity. Residual \(L\) remains the Superblock account of laboratory \(G\). Leakage is how hidden stress is seen. It is not a second force law.

### 2.4 Quarks

Match: quarks are not inserted as fundamental point particles. They are floor occupancy of the six-phase cloud the Mirron sources.

Does not match: photon–graviton systems. No fundamental graviton is postulated. The agent is the Mirron, a Planck–Kerr mirror, plus colour constraint. Three Standard-Model generations are not the six domains. The old 3+3 wording is matter and antimatter copies, not \(e,\mu,\tau\) or \(u,c,t\).

### 2.5 Absolute Past and the radius

Match: domains share a reset. Slot 8 is Absolute Past. Parent and child are split by the interior observer’s future horizon, not by a naked singularity left in place. The Mirron replaces that singularity with a boundary operator. Extremal-or-cut is not derived.

The displayed radius is the photon sphere, not the gravitational radius:

\[
r_{\rm ph}=\frac{3GM}{c^2}=\frac{3}{2}r_s,
\qquad
r_s=\frac{2GM}{c^2}.
\]

**Key.** \(G\): Newton constant, untouched. \(M\): mass parameter. \(c\): speed of light. \(r_s\): Schwarzschild radius, where \(g_{tt}=0\) in that chart. \(r_{\rm ph}\): unstable photon orbit. Calling \(3GM/c^2\) the gravitational radius of the origin singularity misnames the locus. Neither radius is the Absolute Past seam.
