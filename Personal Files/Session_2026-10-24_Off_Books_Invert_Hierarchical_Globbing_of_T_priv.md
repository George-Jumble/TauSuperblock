## Previous direction (for contrast)

Local points of simultaneity were collected toward the privileged observer:

\[
\Delta t_{\rm glob}
=
\mathcal{G}\bigl(\{t_{\rm sim}^{(i)}\}\bigr)
-
T_{\rm priv}.
\]

**Key.** The privileged observer is the target (the universal reference). Globbing reduces many local labels to one contrast.

## Inverted role

Invert the arrow. \(T_{\rm priv}\) becomes the primitive locus that is hierarchically unfolded:

\[
t_{\rm sim}^{(i)}
=
\mathcal{G}^{-1}\bigl(T_{\rm priv};\,R^{(g)},\,\chi^{(g)}\bigr),
\]

or, equivalently, the privileged-observer time is itself the result of an inverse globbing over the hierarchy:

\[
T_{\rm priv}
=
\mathcal{G}_{\rm inv}\bigl(\{t_{\rm local}^{(g)}\}\bigr).
\]

**Key.** \(\mathcal{G}^{-1}\): operation that distributes or refines \(T_{\rm priv}\) into local simultaneity labels according to glob radius \(R^{(g)}\) or occupancy \(\chi^{(g)}\). \(\mathcal{G}_{\rm inv}\): the reverse collection (for example, weighting by inverse hierarchical depth, or letting sub-dominant globs set the reference).

Two concrete readings:

1. **Distribution from the universal POS**  
   The privileged observer emits or stamps a single \(T_{\rm priv}\). Hierarchical globs modulate that stamp into local proper times or local points of simultaneity. Domain rotation, residual-L compression, and virtual torsion then act as the modulation operators. The “push” of earlier notes becomes a differential drag on the outward-propagating stamp rather than an inward collection.

2. **Inverse dominance**  
   Instead of the largest glob’s center of gravity fixing effective \(G\) and the reference time, the inverse hierarchy lets the smallest (floor or virtual) globs determine \(T_{\rm priv}\). This directly engages the virtual sector. If those floor globs are allowed to source a dynamical term, the construction inherits all the costs already listed for lifting the 2026-10-07 author cut (free suppression, no derivation of \(\rho_\Lambda\), conflict with the cut, but no core breakage of laboratory \(G\)).

In both readings the kinematic character of any UPS stamp can be kept: the inversion changes which locus is called primitive, not the fact that the stamp advances phase without depositing residual \(L\).

## Consequences for the lambda attempt

The inverted globbing can be coupled to the earlier push:

\[
E_{\rm push}
=
\kappa\,
\bigl|T_{\rm priv}
-
\mathcal{G}_{\rm inv}(\{t_{\rm local}\})\bigr|
\,
E_{\rm carrier}.
\]

**Key.** \(E_{\rm carrier}\): whichever energy is admitted (wall potential, radiation pressure, or virtual density). \(\kappa\): still unfixed.

- If the carrier remains the unbound wall potential or radiation pressure, the inversion merely re-labels the contrast; the coefficient stays free and \(\rho_\Lambda\) is not derived.
- If the carrier is the virtual/Casimir sector (inverse dominance reading), the hierarchy problem reappears exactly as in the preceding note: radiation pressure or another mechanism must still supply a free suppression factor \(\sim 10^{120}\). No new locked scale evaluates it.
- Residual-L matching to laboratory \(G\) is preserved so long as the dominant-glob rule for gravitational \(G_{\rm eff}\) is not itself inverted. Inverting only the time-label globbing leaves the gravity rule intact. Inverting the gravity rule as well would be a deeper change and would need a separate check against laboratory tests.
