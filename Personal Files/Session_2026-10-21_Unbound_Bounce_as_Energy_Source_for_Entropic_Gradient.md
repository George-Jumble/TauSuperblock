## Live Definitions (bound vs unbound)

From the locked distinction:

- **Unbound bounce**: completed Past–Future (or \(\Phi_8\) reset) cycle. Light-like / null, \(d\tau=0\), constraint \(\mathcal{C}_{\rm constr}=0\). No residual angular momentum left on the Present slice. Ordinary free photons and other completed null quanta.
- **Bound bounce**: reaches Absolute Past / reset but rebound toward Absolute Future is stopped at the Present. Residual angular momentum \(L_{\rm res}\) obtained from the stopped rebound. This residual \(L\) sources hierarchical spatial compression (gravity) matching laboratory \(G\).

**Key (bound/unbound):**  
- Unbound: full completion, \(L_{\rm res}=0\), pure Weyl contribution, no isotropic compression.  
- Bound: stopped rebound, \(L_{\rm res}\neq0\), sources residual-\(L\) gravity.

Identify the energy carried by unbound bounces (completed null quanta) as the source that supplies the Mirron/Entropic wall potential, whose spatial or phase gradient is the entropic force (directed toward the high-Weyl / lack-of-mass side).

## What it does not do
