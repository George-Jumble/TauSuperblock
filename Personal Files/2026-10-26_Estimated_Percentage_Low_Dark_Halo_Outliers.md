# Session 2026-10-26: Estimated percentage of low dark-halo outliers

**Date:** 2026-10-26  
**Context:** Extension of the modulated Poisson randomizing set (hierarchical residual-L + domain-phase orbital modulation + prior-aeon remnant seeds).

## Query
What is the estimated percentage?

## Analytical estimate

From the previous session:
\[
P_{\rm low} \approx e^{-\lambda_0 \mathcal{V}_g \langle\alpha_0\rangle_{\rm rem}} \times P\bigl(\mathcal{M}(\phi_a,\boldsymbol{\omega}) < 1+\delta\bigr).
\]
Sparse prior-aeon remnants keep the Poisson void factor near unity for most galaxy-scale volumes; the six discrete domain-phase offsets supply an additional factor of order \(1/6\)–\(1/3\). The product is naturally a few percent or less.

Observational anchors (NGC 1052-DF2, DF4, FCC 224, DF9 and a handful of other candidates) show such systems constitute roughly 1 % or fewer of ultra-diffuse / dwarf samples and far less of the general galaxy population. The modulated Poisson process reproduces this order of magnitude.

**Estimated percentage: of order 1 % (roughly 0.5–2 % in UDG / dwarf samples).**

### Variable key
- \(P_{\rm low}\): probability (fraction) of low dark-halo outliers
- \(\lambda_0 \mathcal{V}_g \langle\alpha_0\rangle_{\rm rem}\): expected number of remnant seeds inside a galaxy-scale volume
- \(\mathcal{M}(\phi_a,\boldsymbol{\omega})\): domain-phase orbital modulation factor
- \(\delta\): tolerance still permitting only weak residual-L growth
- All symbols from prior sessions retain their definitions

## Plain language
Most patches of the early gas either catch a remnant seed from the previous aeon or sit in a domain-phase offset that packs material early; those become ordinary residual-L galaxies. A small leftover fraction catch neither. That leftover fraction is about one percent of the dwarf and ultra-diffuse population—rare enough to match the handful of known “missing dark matter” galaxies, yet not so rare that the same random process fails to produce the majority of normal residual-L systems.

## Implications and caveats
Order-of-magnitude only. Consistent with dual-channel residual-L (time-like channel = laboratory \(G\); space-like screened) and direct remnant incorporation. Quantitative refinement requires an N-body realization of the modulated Poisson process. Selection effects in current surveys affect the precise observed percentage.
