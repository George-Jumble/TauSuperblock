## Possible Recovery Mechanism (speculative)

### Setup
Let the supersphere carry a set of spacetime envelopes E_b (b = 1 … 6 for the co-located domains, or more generally for hierarchical shells). Each envelope is assigned one or more η-spheres whose radii respond to the local field rates inside that envelope.

The proper-time rate on envelope E_b is
\[
d\tau_b = N_b(R_{\eta,b})\, dt,
\]
where N_b is the lapse contributed by the η-sphere(s) belonging to that envelope.

**Key**
- \( E_b \): spacetime envelope b of the supersphere.  
- \( \tau_b \): proper time on envelope b.  
- \( N_b \): lapse on envelope b (dimensionless).  
- \( R_{\eta,b} \): radius of the η-sphere associated with envelope b.  
- \( t \): common coordinate time.

### Time-mediated scale separation
Define an effective hierarchical contrast between two envelopes by the ratio of their proper-time rates:
\[
\xi_{ab} = \frac{\tau_a}{\tau_b} = \frac{N_a(R_{\eta,a})}{N_b(R_{\eta,b})}.
\]

**Key**
- \( \xi_{ab} \): dimensionless time-contrast factor between envelopes a and b.  
- All other symbols as above.

In the old Tau construction the hierarchical index was
\[
n_{\rm hier} = \log_7\Bigl(\frac{\tau}{\lambda_{m_0}}\Bigr)\approx 45.8
\]
(with τ the compact radius). Here the same numerical role can be played by a product of time contrasts accumulated across the nested envelopes:
\[
n_{\rm eff} = \sum_b \log_7\bigl(\xi_{b,b+1}\bigr).
\]

**Key**
- \( n_{\rm eff} \): effective hierarchical index recovered by time mediation.  
- \( \xi_{b,b+1} \): successive time-contrast factors.  
- The base-7 logarithm is retained only as a phenomenological match to the old 142857 cycle; it is not derived from a metric circle.

If the feedback loop (field rates → R_η → N → proper-time rate → field rates) stabilizes at values such that the product of contrasts reproduces the old scale ratio τ / λ_m0, then the hierarchical counting formerly supplied by the Tau circle is recovered as a pure time-mediation effect among the envelopes.

### Vacuum-energy analogue
The old Casimir identification ρ_Λ ~ ħc / τ⁴ is refused as a metric source. A time-mediated substitute is the differential proper-time density across envelopes:
\[
\rho_{\rm eff} \propto \frac{\hbar c}{L_{\rm env}^4}\Bigl(\frac{N_{\rm in}}{N_{\rm out}}\Bigr)^p,
\]
where L_env is a characteristic envelope separation (an input, not a derived τ) and p is an unfixed power.

**Key**
- \( \rho_{\rm eff} \): effective energy density arising from time contrast.  
- \( L_{\rm env} \): envelope separation scale (length).  
- \( N_{\rm in}/N_{\rm out} \): ratio of interior to exterior lapses.  
- \( p \): unfixed exponent.  
- \( \hbar \), \( c \): standard constants.

This does not restore ρ_Λ = ħc/τ⁴; it only notes that a time contrast can generate an effective density contrast of similar functional form.
