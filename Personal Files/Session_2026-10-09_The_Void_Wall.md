## 2. What it is

A chamber in the fractal tree is born in the complement of a parent and expands. The wall is the edge of that chamber. Outside it, no timelike or null curve reaches in: light, colour, neutrinos, and gravitational waves stop. The complement is the high-Weyl region, open as a place and closed as a signal path. It is not a conformal copy of one infinite expanding wall, and it is not a container hole holding all aeons.

The eighth-phase wall read today as the Kerr photon orbit is a null ring outside a horizon, inside a chamber. Different surface. Different job: the reset helix is evaluated there. The void wall is the chamber’s edge.

## 3. What crosses

Nothing on the null cone. The exception is residual \(L\): compression from stopped rebounds, shared by co-located sectors of the same manifold, effective \(G\) taken from the dominant glob. That coupling does not travel across the wall as news. Ansatz A remains an ansatz.

No Casimir \(\hbar c/L^4\) is assigned to this wall. The Higgs-depth evaluation fixed a plate gap \(1.89\times 10^{-18}\,\mathrm{m}\), a floor-scale separation, not a void radius. \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) stays retired.
