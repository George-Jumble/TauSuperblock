\begin{center}\textbf{Abstract}\end{center}

\begin{quote}\small

## 1. Derivation protocol *(revised)*

Work in this order. Do not invert it.

1. **Lock the floor.** $\Delta=313.1$ MeV, set by the Mirron's double reduced-Compton length: the Mirron diameter is $2\hbar c/\Delta$, so $r_M=\hbar c/\Delta$. TEP supplies only $\Delta>0$; the number is a locked geometric input, not a $\chi^2$ minimum and not a derived theorem. The earlier neutron-dressing print is excluded. Do not retune $\Delta$.
2. **Write four-dimensional physics on six co-located domains.** The frame is 3+1 only; no extra dimension is used.
3. **Write gravity as dual-channel $L_{\rm res}$.** Normalize the time-like channel to laboratory $G$ through the dominant-glob rule before anything else is compared.
4. **Clock the engine.** Eight-phase $G_2$ clock; the stamp is kinematic and deposits no $L_{\rm res}$.
5. **Match the Standard Model at low energy.** Maxwell in each domain; electroweak and QCD are used as measured.
6. **Confront data last.** SPARC, DESI, JWST high-$z$ black holes, neutron lifetime, precision gravity, CMB, LHC open data. A lock in steps 1--5 that fails at $>3\sigma$ and can be saved only by a new continuous parameter is retired.

"Parameter-free" is withdrawn as a description. The live free parameters are named: the space-like efficiency $\eta$, an optional screening length $R_{\rm screen}$, one core radius $R_{\rm core}$ per galaxy in the SPARC confrontation, and the friction coefficients that replace v5's rotational dilution. ## 2. Geometry of the six domains *(revised)*

The universe is not a single 3+1-dimensional manifold. It consists of six exactly co-located spacetime domains sharing the same macroscopic coordinates. The domains sit on the $\pm$ axes of an octahedron; matter and antimatter partners are antipodal. They do not share electromagnetic or colour current: unbound EM and colour are domain-local. $L_{\rm res}$ is not domain-local; it interacts through all six domains (author ruling, 2026-10-31). Each domain is a full copy of the Prime domain: the same physics, its own stars, gas and baryons, and its own up quark, which is prime in that domain. The three antimatter orientations of the octahedron are the three domains that run in reversed time relative to Prime; each runs forward relative to itself and to the other reversed domains, and the converse holds from their side. All domains are equidistant from the Absolute Past (apart from a slight time dilation) and lie on the same $T_{\rm priv}$ slice, so the direction of time does not change any inter-domain interaction (author rulings, 2026-10-31). It does matter elsewhere, for example to CPT bookkeeping and the arrow of time. No two domains interact on any scale beyond the half-bounce: only gravity ($L_{\rm res}$) crosses the phase boundary (author ruling, 1 November 2026). A particle native to another domain can appear in Prime only by being shaken out of the Mirrons, when Mirron inertia collides with bound energy (author ruling, 1 November 2026). The six domain envelopes co-orbit by purely gravitational motion in the four-dimensional supersphere (author ruling, 1 November 2026). Everything complies with CPT, gravity included (author ruling, 1 November 2026).

**Every domain is Prime; gravity on $T_{\rm priv}$** *(author rulings, 3--4 November 2026)*. Every domain is Prime to its own observer and sees itself at rest (author ruling, 3 November 2026). Three domains run backwards in time (anti-prime, $-T$, $-B$) and three forward (Prime, $T$, $B$); the absolute past and the absolute future are identical geometrically and physically, so only the size of a domain's time offset matters, not its sign (author ruling, 4 November 2026). Trans-domain gravity is each domain's own local gravity, carried atemporally on $T_{\rm priv}$ by the same mechanism as the conformal half-bounce, so the quark-ladder $\gamma_q$ drops out of it; within a domain, local gravity interacts with time as the quarter-bounce rebound at $c$ in $-t$ (author rulings, 3 November 2026). All inter-domain gravitation is on $T_{\rm priv}$: the envelopes move on discrete orbital planes, which $T_{\rm priv}$ globs into one universal rotation, and each envelope contributes $\gamma Mc^2$ at the moment of action (author ruling, 3 November 2026). Only gravity propagates along $T_{\rm priv}$: intra-EM pressure waves act only on each domain's own spacetime sphere, so each domain has its own BAO, and thermal state is an EM variant that is not shared between domains (author rulings, 4 November 2026).

Moving a bound colour line from domain $a$ to domain $b$ costs
$$E_\times(a\to b)=\theta_{ab}\,\Delta,\qquad \theta_{\rm adj}=\pi/2,\quad \theta_{\rm anti}=\pi,$$
with $E_\times=0$ for unbound light and an extra $E_{\rm violation}\ge\Delta$ if the target cell is occupied. The form is derived; the MeV unit is inserted. These are not meson poles.

*Symbol key.* $E_\times(a\to b)$: energy to move a bound colour line from domain $a$ to domain $b$; $\theta_{ab}$: angle between the two domain axes of the octahedron; $\theta_{\rm adj}=\pi/2$: adjacent axes; $\theta_{\rm anti}=\pi$: antipodal axes; $\Delta$: mass floor; $E_{\rm violation}$: extra cost of double occupancy.

Apart from $L_{\rm res}$ gravity, the domains are correlated kinematically. Each event $p$ carries the label $T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}$ of its dominant containing glob, and the universal relative label is a contraction
$$T_{\rm priv}^{\rm univ}=\mathcal{C}\bigl(\{T_{\rm priv}^{(p_a;a)}\}_{a=1}^{6}\bigr).$$
*Symbol key.* $T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}$: private simultaneity label of event $p$, set by its dominant containing glob; $p_a$: the co-located event in domain $a$; $\mathcal C$: the contraction over the six co-located labels; $T_{\rm priv}^{\rm univ}$: the universal relative label.

Each local label owns its own null cone; the universal label has no cone of its own. Causally disconnected islands keep independent labels. $T_{\rm priv}$ is the atemporal layer of the moment of action: every point in space and time at the observer's local event, linked to every other observer's $t_{\rm priv}$ through a hierarchy of local $t_{\rm priv}$ values that share one point of simultaneity (author rulings, 3--4 November 2026). Relaying gravity through the $T_{\rm priv}$ clocks of $T$, $B$, $-T$ and $-B$ is therefore consistent with domain isolation (author ruling, 4 November 2026). Each quark flavour has its own domain ($u$ = Prime; $d,s,c,b,t$ hidden), and the domains interact hierarchically via the bounce. The $d$ domain is its own domain; its link to Prime is $(-1,+1,-1)$, three half-bounce steps across three domains, half the six-domain photonic bounce, so it is never adjacent to Prime (author ruling, 4 November 2026).

Because $L_{\rm res}$ interacts through all six domains, every domain feels the $L_{\rm res}$ of all the others, and the time-like channel of every glob $g$ draws on the six-domain sum. This fixes who feels the force; where each domain's contribution is centred in a merger is set observationally (Section 7). This is the standard budget, not a special case for black holes:
$$M^{(g)}_{\rm grav}=\sum_{a=1}^{6}w_a\,r\,M^{(a,g)}_{\rm b},\qquad M^{(a,g)}_{\rm b}=M^{(1,g)}_{\rm b}\ \Rightarrow\ \frac{M^{(g)}_{\rm grav}}{M^{(1,g)}_{\rm b}}=r\sum_a w_a,\qquad M_{\rm BH}\le f_{\rm acc}\,M^{(g)}_{\rm grav}.$$
Each domain's $L_{\rm res}$ is sourced only by that domain's ordinary matter $M^{(a,g)}_{\rm b}$; $r$ is the $L_{\rm res}$ mass per unit of own ordinary matter ($r=1$ for no extra halo); $w_a=1$ (equal) or $w_a=\gamma_a$ (Ansatz A, Section 18). There is no budget beyond this sum. A measured total mass (lensing, dynamics, or $\Omega_m$) already *is* this sum and must never be multiplied by six again.

*Symbol key.* $M^{(g)}_{\rm grav}$: gravitating mass of glob $g$; $a=1\ldots6$: domain index ($a=1$ is Prime); $w_a$: weight of domain $a$; $r$: $L_{\rm res}$ mass per unit of own ordinary matter; $M^{(a,g)}_{\rm b}$: ordinary (baryonic) matter of domain $a$ in glob $g$; $M_{\rm BH}$: black-hole mass; $f_{\rm acc}$: fraction of the glob's gravitating mass available for accretion.

The early-black-hole budget (Section 6) and the cluster budget (Section 7) are the same sum. The weights are Ansatz A, $w_a=\gamma_a$ on the $B_{\rm lin}$ speed map, giving $\sum_a w_a=6.401514$: the dark-to-ordinary ratio in our domain is 5.4015, our draw from a seeded distribution (T4, ruling of 2026-10-31 reworded by the author on 11 November 2026; Section 18, item 6). The base value 5 comes from the five non-Prime domains of nearly equal mass; the excess of about 0.4 is random variation, Poisson in where mass sits and power-law in how much, seeded like voids. Equal weights would give 6. Scheme A is kinematic (author ruling, 1 November 2026): the $\gamma_q=m_q/m_u$ are real kinematic factors, with Prime $=u$ and the five non-prime domains $=d,s,c,b,t$. Seen from Prime, light in non-prime domain $q$ runs at $c/\gamma_q$, the light-clock component of a ray whose envelope-drift component is $\beta_q c$, with $(c/\gamma_q)^2+(\beta_q c)^2=c^2$; every domain still measures $c$ locally. The quark ladder sets each domain's light speed and clock rate; the $B_{\rm lin}$ co-orbit map sets its gravitating weight (the 5.4015 draw). The two maps coexist. Each domain is locally the $u$ domain (author rulings, 3 November 2026): pure-number couplings ($\alpha$, $\alpha_s$) are standard in every domain, and only dimensionful readings (light speed, clock rate, mass) differ between domains. ### 15.3.3 Cosmological friction

*Author rulings (11 November 2026).* Cosmological friction is never omitted from expansion, $H_0$ or dark-energy work. It acts in two ways: through $\eta$, as the spin-down of dark-energy efficiency, and as a direct brake on expansion. The direct brake is sourced by the envelope-orbit budget, not by the ordinary rotation of matter. Galaxy and halo rotation amount to only $\Omega\approx9\times10^{-9}$ (*estimate*). The envelope-orbit budget from the 20 September 2026 corpus note [13] is $\Omega_{\rm orb}\approx0.02$, range 0.0182--0.0199 (*estimate*).

The spin-down share and the two candidate brake forms are
$$\rho_\eta(a)=F_\eta\,\rho_{\rm DE0}\,\frac{E_{\rm rot}(a)}{E_{\rm rot,0}},\qquad E_{\rm rot}=\frac{L^2}{2I}\propto L(a)^2a^{-2},$$
$$\text{(S)}\ \ +\Omega_{\rm orb}(1+z)^3,\qquad \text{(R)}\ \ -\Omega_{\rm orb}\Bigl(\frac{L}{L_0}\Bigr)^2(1+z)^4 .$$
*Symbol key.* $\rho_\eta$: friction-set (spin-down) share of dark energy; $F_\eta$: that share today; $\rho_{\rm DE0}$: dark-energy density today; $E_{\rm rot}$: rotational energy per glob, with present value $E_{\rm rot,0}$; $L$: angular momentum per glob, grown by tidal torque until collapse ($z_{\rm coll}$) and then conserved, with present value $L_0$; $I\propto a^2$: moment of inertia; $\Omega_{\rm orb}$: envelope-orbit budget in units of the critical density; $z$: redshift. Form (S) enters $H^2/H_0^2$ as positive dust; form (R) enters as a negative term.

**The brake form is open** (Section 18, item 24). Form (S) is the 20 September reading: positive dust, diluting as $a^{-3}$, with locked $\beta_i$ and no collapse epoch. Form (R) is the ruling-12 reading: a negative $L^2a^{-4}$ brake in which orbit speeds evolve (the Heckmann--Schücking rotating-cosmology term [38]). The corpus also conflicts on spin-down itself. A working note of 11 November (rev2, §9) says expansion cannot spin bound orbits down, while an author ruling of the same day gives $\omega\propto a^{-2}$. This version follows the ruling and reports the conflict (Section 18, item 29). The budget cannot be a net rotation of space. Read that way, $\Omega_{\rm orb}\approx0.019$ means $\omega/H\approx0.17$, about $3\times10^{9}$ times the Planck vorticity bound of Saadeh et al. [35]. Only a direction-free, isotropic term is allowed, for example one in which the six orbits cancel any net axis.

*Plain language.* Spin acts as a drag on cosmic expansion. As space grows, the spin that sets the walls' efficiency winds down, so dark energy fades a little. The orbital motion of the six copies may also act as a brake. Whether that brake behaves like extra matter or like a fading negative term is not yet decided. Either way it must have no preferred direction, or the CMB would show it.

## 16. Falsification protocol *(updated 2026-11-11)*

The following are independently lethal.

- **F1. Floor.** An infrared hadronic gap or TEP cost that cannot be written as $\hbar c/r_M$ after continuum and scheme conversion; a genuine particle pole at exactly 313.1 MeV; or a drift of the floor over cosmic time (ruling 10: 313 MeV is a constant).
- **F2. Laboratory gravity.** Any inverse-square or equivalence-principle violation inside laboratory or Solar-System scales (torsion balance, MICROSCOPE, lunar laser ranging, ephemerides) not removable by the declared screening, or a best-fit $\eta$ that predicts an EP violation already excluded.
- **F3. Colour confinement.** Extra Prime-domain jets from hidden-domain colour.
- **F4. Electromagnetic isolation.** Prime detection of a hidden-domain Maxwell wave that is not a Mirron flux swap.
- **F5. Information.** Loss at a Planck--Kerr ring that cannot be written as a missed inversion costing $\ge\Delta$.
- **F6. Annihilation accounting.** Two-photon or two-jet energy that cannot be written from the two occupancies plus Standard-Model kinematics, respecting the Dirac/constituent distinction.
- **F7. Binding horizon.** A nested seed whose horizon cannot be written as $\Omega(s)\,r_M$.
- **F8. Galactic dynamics.** On independent rotation-curve data with M/L marginalized, the hierarchical $L_{\rm res}$ model underperforming standard baselines, or requiring a new interpolating function of acceleration.
- **F9. Unbound sector.** A nonzero photon mass, or $L_{\rm res}$ gravity sourced by free EM waves.
- **F10. Gravitational waves.** A propagating scalar or vector gravitational mode beyond standard tensor waves.
- **F11. Six-domain budget.** High-$z$ black holes that exceed even the $6\times$ budget, or a $6\times$ factor that over-produces local black-hole mass density.
- **F12. Cluster mergers** (Section 7). A clean post-core-passage merger whose lensing peak tracks the X-ray gas rather than the galaxies. A significant lag of the lensing mass behind the galaxies, toward the gas, beyond the gas-fraction skew. A complete baryon census of a merger giving $M_{\rm tot}/M_{\rm b}$ clearly outside $N_{\rm eff}=6.40$ (our draw). Lensing mass at the stripped-gas peaks well above the gas mass itself (the domain-own-matter placement predicts about $6\times$). If the R7 placement rule is adopted: a merger whose lensing peak is significantly offset from its member-galaxy number centroid.
- **F13. Dark-matter conservation** (ruling 4, new). A robust late-time decrease of the comoving cold-dark-matter density, of the size that the retired conditional model needed to pay for the late dark-energy rise (about 10--12 % of $\Omega_c$, estimate), or a dark-matter component with pressure, would contradict the rest-energy Weyl condensate that thins only with volume.
- **F14. Base wall at $w=-1$** (ruling 3, new). A dark-energy history in which the component that persists to the far future is not a cosmological constant, once the gated bump and friction are allowed for, would contradict the base wall fed by new Mirrons at fixed density.
- **F15. Redshift kinematics** (proposal, new). Any departure from $1+z=a_{\rm obs}/a_{\rm emit}$ kinematics, such as a supernova time-dilation exponent different from 1, a non-blackbody CMB, or $T_{\rm CMB}(z)\ne T_0(1+z)$, would falsify redshift as dispersion. It would equally falsify standard expansion.
- **F16. Isotropy of friction** (ruling 12, new). Any reading of the envelope-orbit budget that requires a net rotation or shear of space is excluded by the CMB isotropy bound [35]. If the framework can be made to fit only with such a reading, the brake is falsified.
- **F17. Protocol.** Any repair of F1--F16 that introduces a new continuous parameter. That repair retires the lock.

*Plain language.* Each item names an observation that would sink the framework on its own: a drifting 313 MeV floor, a breakdown of ordinary gravity in the laboratory, coloured jets from hidden copies, light from hidden copies, lost information, mis-accounted annihilation, galaxies or clusters behaving the wrong way, dark matter that is used up or has pressure, a far-future dark energy that is not constant, redshift that does not behave like stretching, or a cosmic spin. Patching any of these with a new adjustable number counts as failure.

**Data confrontations, not locks:** SPARC (Section 6); the dark-energy history of DESI and supernovae, reported as $\Delta\chi^2$ with the free shares and never forced to zero residual (Section 15.3.4: best gated case $\Delta\chi^2=-9.5$ / $-14.85$ against $\Lambda$CDM, *estimate*); the Hubble tension (no relief as ruled, *estimate*); $S_8$ (raised by $\eta$ friction to 1.025--1.047 times $\Lambda$CDM, *estimate*); the late ISW cross-correlation; early-dark-energy limits on the equality hand-back ($f_{\rm EDE}=0.111$, *estimate*, against ACT DR6 and Poulin et al. ## 17. What this manuscript does not claim *(updated 2026-11-11)*

It does not derive $\rho_\Lambda$. It does not derive the rate law of the gated transfer $Q$, the release window of the $\Phi_7$ store, the growth exponent $p$, or the form of the friction brake. These come from background-only geometric fits with a heuristic calibration, with no perturbations and no CMB power spectrum, and none is an observational claim of the theory. It does not adopt redshift as dispersion or neutrinos in the photonic half-bounce; both are proposals. It does not adopt a $w\approx1/2$ decay of the held energy at equality. It does not derive the mass floor or the Yang--Mills gap; 313 MeV is a locked constant. It does not derive the size of each hidden domain's thermal offset: the CMB requires at least about $50\times$, and Prime's standard sound horizon follows only once that is granted (resolved in principle, pending author confirmation); the DESI factor $\gamma=10.74$ stays retired. It does not derive the $B_{\rm lin}$ weights or the rule that every domain sees the same pattern of weights and ladder factors. It does not replace PMNS parameters, $G_F$, or PDG pole masses by geometric identities. It does not use any extra dimension or a graviton; the frame is strictly four-dimensional. It does not derive general relativity from the compression of the $\Phi_7$ fabric. It does not derive why the six-domain $L_{\rm res}$ halo rides with the collisionless component in a merger, or the halo's radial profile (Section 7); under ruling R7 the placement is measured from the lensing. It does not count planted research-tool outputs as detections. It does not treat consciousness, metrological numerology, or external ontological files as modules of the geometry.

*Plain language.* The paper is explicit about its limits. Dark energy, the 313 MeV floor and the 5.4 ratio are described, not derived. The expansion-rate numbers are working estimates. Two of the newest ideas are only proposals, and the Hubble tension is not solved.
