The resulting geometric suction forces a conformal crossover: the old entanglement network is analytically continued through the white-hole interiors of Planck–Kerr Mirron interfaces while entropy is preserved to high precision (\(dS\approx 0\)) by superlubrication (\(\zeta\to 1\)). Six extremal Planck–Kerr Mirron seeds nucleate synchronously, establishing the hexametric architecture of the new aeon. All scales are fixed by the geometry of the \(E_8\times E_8\) root system, the macroscopic \(\tau\)-circle, and the topological exclusion principle. Eight concrete, observationally testable predictions are preregistered so that forthcoming void surveys, CMB polarization maps, high-redshift abundance measurements, and gravitational-wave backgrounds can deliver definitive tests.

## 1. Introduction: Replacing the Singular Big Bang

In the classical cosmological narrative the universe begins in a singularity of infinite density and temperature—an event that remains mathematically incomplete and physically opaque. The Unified Tav-Superblock framework replaces that singularity with a deterministic geometric process. The new cycle does not emerge from nothing; it is the inevitable thermodynamic and topological consequence of the terminal state of a prior aeon. This paper traces the precise sequence by which an ultra-dilute, maximum-entropy void of a dying parent cosmos crosses a critical pressure threshold and becomes the nursery of a fresh multi-domain universe.

The framework rests on four interlocking geometric elements: (i) a macroscopic 5D Einstein–Kaluza–Klein cylinder of radius \(\tau\approx 7\,h^{-1}\) Mpc; (ii) six co-located 3+1D domains orbiting on an internal \(S^3\) supersphere; (iii) an 8-phase octonionic clockwork whose automorphism group is \(G_2\); and (iv) the Topological Exclusion Principle that enforces fermionic statistics and causal structure. Gravity is realized as stochastic fractal globbing of hierarchical binding quanta across Planck–Kerr Mirron interfaces; no fundamental graviton is postulated.

## 2. The Terminal Landscape of the Parent Aeon

Imagine a cosmos that has already lived for many trillions of years. Bound structures—stars, galaxies, clusters—have long since exhausted their nuclear fuel. Cosmological friction against the ubiquitous Planck–Kerr Mirron surfaces that lace the multi-domain geometry has ground every macroscopic bound system into its most elementary constituents. What remains is an almost pure radiation field whose equation of state approaches the superlubricated limit \(\zeta\approx 1\). In this regime the radiation streams past the mirrors with vanishing frictional resistance; entropy production is essentially frozen.

Within this sea of ultra-dilute radiation, certain regions expand faster than their surroundings. These are the deep cosmic voids—structural analogs of the Boötes Void but grown to cosmological maturity. Because the voids contain almost no residual matter, they evolve as nearly isolated thermodynamic systems. Their interiors approach a localized maximum-entropy state while the denser walls that once separated them recede beyond causal contact. Each such void therefore becomes an independent nursery, sealed off from the residual dynamics of the parent aeon.

The key geometric fact is that these voids are not empty in the absolute sense. They sit inside the five-dimensional Einstein–Kaluza–Klein substrate whose compact \(S^1_\tau\) circle has a macroscopic radius of order \(7\,h^{-1}\) Mpc. The void interiors therefore retain a residual coupling to the higher-dimensional geometry even after all ordinary matter has been diluted away.
