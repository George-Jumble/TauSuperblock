## Mathematical status of the Kerr CTC

In the vacuum Kerr extension, sufficiently near the ring with \(r<0\),

\[
g_{\phi\phi}<0.
\]

**Key**
- \(g_{\phi\phi}\): coefficient of \(d\phi^2\) in Boyer–Lindquist form.
- \(\phi\): azimuthal angle, period \(2\pi\).
- When \(g_{\phi\phi}<0\), the Killing vector \(\partial_\phi\) is timelike, so the \(\phi\)-orbit is a closed timelike curve.
- No phase factor, octonion multiply, or domain-cross angle enters that sign. The condition is local to the metric component.

An 8-phase clock (six domains + Φ7 scrape + Φ8 reset) acts on internal occupancy and on the bound/unbound distinction. Φ8 is the bound reset (reflection). It does not alter the sign of \(g_{\phi\phi}\) in the vacuum extension, nor does it convert the disk passage into a non-closed world-tube that returns the ring to its own past while preserving energy accounting.
