## 0. Verdict

**A continuum reading of residual \(L\), not a fluid that carries news across the wall.**

Spacetime in this framework is compressed by the residual angular momentum of stopped rebounds. That compression is shared by co-located copies without a ray crossing the void wall. Calling it a fluid names the continuum. It does not add a graviton, and it does not let a gravitational wave through the causal boundary.

## 2. The reading

A bound electromagnetic phase completes its bounce toward the Absolute Past and is stopped on the rebound toward the Absolute Future. The leftover angular momentum compresses the neighborhood. Unbound light is not stopped and leaves no residual. The thesis already on the books: gravity is the tendency of bound fields toward the highest concentration of bound fields. Positive feedback. Proportional to local bound-field density.

“Fluid dynamics” means this compression is a continuum field on the one \(3+1\) manifold, shared by the six co-located copies. The effective \(G\) of a rock, a planet, or a star is the center of gravity of the dominant glob in that set. No exchange particle crosses the void wall. Co-located copies contribute because they are the same manifold read in another sector, not because a wave left their chamber.

---

## 3. What it is not

Not an acoustic metric, and not a material ether. Gravitational waves in the laboratory are ripples of the metric and travel on the null cone. They stop at the wall with the light. The exception named yesterday is the static, or slowly varying, coupling of residual \(L\), not news. Laboratory \(G\) is matched, not replaced. \(r_s=2GM/c^2\) remains the saturation test for a glob, not a generation.
