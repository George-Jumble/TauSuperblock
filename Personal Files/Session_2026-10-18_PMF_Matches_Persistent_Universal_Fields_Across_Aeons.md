## Match Assessment

### Within one aeon / one universe

A long-lived electromagnetic field that persists from early epochs to the present is consistent with the Superblock picture of universal fields that are not erased on cosmic timescales. The PMF can be read as one unbound (or weakly coupled) electromagnetic realization that carries \(L_{\rm res}=0\), leaving residual-\(L\) gravity intact.

### Across and between aeons
No direct match. The paper is formulated entirely inside a single standard-cosmology timeline (inflation or early generation → recombination → today). It contains:
- no bounce,
- no white-stroke sign flip,
- no transition between aeons,
- no statement that the field content is preserved through a phase inversion or passed from parent to child superblock.

Persistence “between aeons” is a Superblock-specific claim about the fractal tree and the one-engine cycle. The PMF result does not test or confirm that claim; it only shows longevity inside one conventional cosmic history.

### Compatible annotation
The result can still be annotated as supportive of the *intra-aeon* persistence assumption. Extending the annotation to inter-aeon persistence remains non-locking speculation: one may posit that the same unbound electromagnetic degrees of freedom (or their Mirron analogues) are re-instantiated or carried across the bounce, but the paper supplies no evidence for that step.
