## Mapping onto existing Superblock language

The three items already appear in the corpus without being promoted to independent dimensions:

- Unbound EM (or photonic) force completes its bounce between Absolute Past and Absolute Future. The transit is light-like; no residual \(L\) remains.
- Bound EM force completes the bounce toward Absolute Past, but the rebound toward Absolute Future is stopped at the Present. Residual angular momentum is obtained from the stopped rebound and sources residual-L gravity (spatial compression proportional to local bound-field density).
- The relative “now” is therefore already the dynamical locus of the stop. In the live reading it is realized geometrically by the \(\Phi_8\) seam / UPS: a kinematic phase advance delivered to already-present floor excitations, not a dynamical push that itself deposits residual \(L\).

The proposal can therefore be read as a re-labeling of this existing split rather than an immediate addition of two new metric time coordinates.

## Exploratory formalization (off-books)

Suppose one introduces three time-like labels:

\[
(t_P,\, t_F,\, t_N)
\]

with the ordinary laboratory time \(t\) recovered as a combination, for example

\[
t = t_N,\qquad
\text{or}\qquad
t = \frac{t_F - t_P}{2}
\]

(the precise embedding is unfixed).

**Key.** \(t_P\): coordinate increasing toward Absolute Past. \(t_F\): coordinate increasing toward Absolute Future. \(t_N\): the continually changing relative now (location of the Present stop).  

A completed unbound bounce would be a null or light-like curve that reaches both the Past and Future boundaries. A bound rebound would be a curve that reaches the Past boundary but terminates (or reflects) on the surface \(t_N =\) constant, leaving residual \(L\).

Because the live frame is strictly 3+1, any such triple must be auxiliary or emergent:

- They may be coordinates on the conformal boundary or on the photon-orbit world-tube already discussed in earlier notes.
- They may label different segments of a single ordinary time coordinate (Past ray, Future ray, and the instantaneous Present hypersurface).
- They must not be promoted to three independent metric time dimensions inside the bulk; three time dimensions generically produce closed timelike curves, ghost modes, or loss of a well-posed Cauchy problem unless additional constraints (not supplied here) are imposed.

## Consistency checks against live locks

- **Residual-L gravity.** The stop at the relative now remains the sole source of residual \(L\). Laboratory \(G\) matching is preserved provided the stop mechanism is unchanged. No new force is introduced.
- **Floor-only Dynamic Refresh.** The relative now may deliver the kinematic \(\Phi_8\) stamp, but it does not expand DR beyond the floor (colour + inverted-colour EM/electroweak).
- **Tau package.** No circumference, no \(n_{\rm hier}\), no \(\rho_\Lambda = \hbar c/\tau^4\) is restored. The three labels are not a metric fifth (or sixth) dimension.
- **Causality / no-signaling.** Prefer readings in which \(t_N\) is a derived, advancing hypersurface rather than an independent propagating degree of freedom. An independent dynamical \(t_N\) that can be influenced from the Future would risk acausal signaling; that reading is disfavored.
- **One Mirron, six co-located domains.** Unchanged. The three time labels do not multiply the Mirron census or spatially separate the domains.

## Possible repercussions (non-locking)

- The relative now becomes an explicit dynamical variable whose advance rate could, in principle, be modulated by bound-field density (positive feedback already present in residual-L). This remains a rephrasing until a concrete operator is written.
- Absolute Past and Absolute Future could be identified with the two asymptotic ends of the conformal cylinder or with the white-stroke / black-stroke sides of a single bounce, again without new dimensions.
- If \(t_N\) is allowed to vary spatially, one recovers a position-dependent simultaneity already latent in the UPS language; this does not require three metric times.
- Adding genuine extra time dimensions would conflict with the live 4D frame and with standard causality unless the extra directions are pure gauge or confined to a boundary. That stronger reading is refused for the present.
