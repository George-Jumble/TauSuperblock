## 3. Examination

### 3.1 First aeon as mother of mothers

Accepted as a root, with the rule unchanged. A first chamber has no parent occupancy to reflect. Its seed is the first stable \(\{\psi_i\}_{i=1}^{6}\), not a horizon. Later mothers are the same rule with a parent. “Mother of mothers” is that root. It does not need a different force, a different horizon, or a restored \(\tau\).

The ambient manifold is still the one ever-expanding 4-world. The root does not sit outside it. Children are not born into a second universe.

### 3.2 That root is not an infinite black hole

Carried forward from the previous session, and not repaired by calling the hole the first aeon:

- A Schwarzschild chart needs an exterior. An infinite hole holding the tree deletes it.
- \(r_s=R\) for a critically filled Hubble sphere is the definition of \(\rho_{\rm crit}\), not a seed.
- The isolating surface in the tree note is the future event horizon of the interior observer, \(r_{\rm eh}=c/H_\Lambda\). Observer-dependent. Constant if \(\Lambda\) is constant. Not ever-growing, not infinite, not \(r_s\).
- Physical locus remains an apparent seam \(\Sigma\). A static event horizon is not the seed.
- Birth is \(J_{\rm sec}\) standing as a new occupancy. The current is written and not integrated. Naming the seed a hole does not compute it.

### 3.3 Walls as conformal reflections of the infinite expanding wall

Split the sentence.

The reflection that is already in the corpus:

\[
\mathrm{Mirron}:\quad
\text{parent aeon}\longrightarrow\text{child occupancy}.
\]

**Key.** Mirron: parent-to-child reflection at every depth, including from the root if a root is granted. It is a bounce / reset map, not a copy of a material wall.

The conformal relation already in the corpus, on a bandage:

\[
\hat\Omega\,\check\Omega=-1,
\qquad
\Omega\to 1
\quad\text{on living walls}.
\]

**Key.** \(\hat\Omega\): conformal factor on the parent side of the join. \(\check\Omega\): factor on the child side. Reciprocal \(-1\) on the bandage. \(\Omega\to 1\): living-wall value, away from the quiet interior. This relates two conformal metrics across one womb’s join. It does not stamp every later void wall as a reflected image of one infinite wall.

What the walls are in the notes: the high-\(C\) complement. Galaxies, holes, leftover matter. \(F_{\rm wall}=0\) means that complement no longer fluxes into the isolating interior. The walls remain in the parent. They are not hard skins, and they are not in one-to-one reflection with a single expanding boundary.

An “infinite expanding wall” is the wrong limit of \(r_{\rm eh}\). de Sitter’s event horizon does not expand without bound at constant \(\Lambda\). \(\mathscr{I}^+\) is future conformal infinity, spacelike, not a wall that later voids reflect.
