## Domain-cross energy (already derived)

The elementary transit work is

\[
E_{\times}(a\to b)
=
\theta_{ab}\,\Delta_{\rm Tav}.
\]

**Key.** \(\theta_{ab}\): angle between domain frames. For the antipodal matter/antimatter pair \(\theta_{a\bar a}=\pi\), so

\[
E_{\times}^{\rm ant}
=
\pi\,\Delta_{\rm Tav}.
\]

At the older print \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) this is \(983.633\,\mathrm{MeV}\). At the working protocol \(309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}\) it scales to \(\approx 971.7\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Adjacent hops are half that value. The shared wall is the \(\Phi_7\) scrape, distinct from the \(\Phi_8\) reset and from the Mirron world-tube.

## Adding the flux to the energy budget

In the off-books UPS-cascade setting the matter/antimatter domain flux supplies an additional contribution to the energy that the cascade is asked to match:

\[
E_{\rm flux}
=
N_{\rm hops}
\cdot
E_{\times}^{\rm ant}
\cdot
f_{\rm duty},
\]

where \(N_{\rm hops}\) is the number of antipodal transits per tick (or per averaging volume) and \(f_{\rm duty}\le 1\) is a duty-cycle or occupancy factor.

**Key.** \(E_{\rm flux}\): net matter/antimatter domain-flux energy. \(N_{\rm hops}\): number of antipodal crossings. \(f_{\rm duty}\): fraction of the tick during which the hop is active.

This term is added to the previous phenomenological target:

\[
E_{\rm target}
=
E(\Lambda_{\rm thermo})
+
E_{\rm rotation}
+
E_{\rm revolution}
+
E_{\rm flux}.
\]

**Key.** \(E(\Lambda_{\rm thermo})\): energy scale associated with the fitted thermodynamic lambda. \(E_{\rm rotation}\): domain-rotation (envelope \(\beta_i\)) kinetic contribution. \(E_{\rm revolution}\): residual-L binding energy about the common center of gravity. \(E_{\rm flux}\): the new matter/antimatter term.

Because \(E_\times\) is already fixed by the protocol angle and the floor, the only free parameters introduced by the flux are the hop count and the duty cycle. Both remain unfixed; they can be chosen so that \(E_{\rm flux}\) sits at any desired fraction of the observed dark-energy density or of the rotational budget. No evaluation of the actual hop rate from locked structure is supplied.
