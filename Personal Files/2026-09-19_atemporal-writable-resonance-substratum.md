## 1. The idea, stripped of the circle

What is worth keeping from Gatlin is not \(\tau=7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) and not “non-computable.” It is this:

There is a discrete harmonic structure that is not a field *on* expanding spacetime. 4D physics is how that structure is *read*. Modes can be occupied, emptied, or rewritten. The law that says which modes exist does not itself age with \(a(t)\).

That is a substratum. Superblock already wants one: the Mirron cell, the 8-phase set, the allowed domain addresses. Gatlin’s Resonance Graph is the same instinct with a different marketing layer.

---

## 2. Atemporal and writable fight unless you split the object

If \(\mathcal S\) is frozen in every sense, nothing writes.  
If writes happen in ordinary domain time \(t\), \(\mathcal S\) is just another 4D field and is not atemporal.

The only split that works:

- **Law (atemporal):** the list of allowed resonances, incidence rules, exclusion rules. No \(a(t)\), no Hubble drift of the *menu*.
- **Occupation (writable):** amplitudes \(N_\lambda\), graph edge weights, Phase-7 filling. These change along the helix tick \(n\), or at a PK refresh, not by sliding along a CTC.

Then “atemporal relative to the rest of the universe” means: the *spectrum* does not know about cosmic time. Galaxies, photons, and \(a(t)\) know about the *occupations* and about how the domain metric reads them.

That is close to a frozen configuration space plus a relational clock. It is not a second metric time, and it is not compact \(t\sim t+T\).

---

## 3. Why I think this one is useful

It gives the cell paper a place to live that is not 4D hydro and not KK.

- \(B_F(f;\mathcal G)\) is a functional of occupations on a fixed harmonic set \(\mathcal G\subset\mathcal S\).
- TEP is a statement about which vertices \(\mathcal S\) does not contain (no slot for a singular / CTC identification).
- Other-domain matter is occupation of modes that our Maxwell cannot read, which our kernel \(\mathcal K_{ij}\) can.
- Phase-7 is occupation of a sector that almost, but not quite, couples to a prime-domain read-out.
- The helix \(n\) is the only write-clock you need. Domain time \(t=n\Delta t\) is how a 4D observer parametrizes those writes.

It also blocks a failure mode from today: if the *law* of \(\mathcal S\) ran with \(n_M\propto a^{-3}\), you would be back at Branch B and the clocks. Atemporal law + writable occupation is exactly the split “normalization vs susceptibility” in substrate language. \(\Phi\) comes from the law plus today’s occupations. \(\xi\) comes from how occupations change.

## 4. What I would not import from Gatlin

- The substratum *is* a metric \(S^1\) of cosmological size.
- Undecidability / halting as a stand-in for a missing magnitude.
- 142857 as a measured wavenumber in JADES.
- A write that is “non-computable” and therefore untestable.
- Casimir energy of the graph automatically equal to \(\Omega_\Lambda\).

A graph can be the bookkeeping for \(\mathcal S\). It does not get to be extra-dimensional gravity.

---

## 5. The real risk

“Atemporal” is a word people use to hide a second time. If writes are ordered, there is a clock. You already have it: \(n\). Call \(n\) an internal step. Do not call \(\mathcal S\) timeless in every sense and also say the PK engine rewrites it.

The discipline is:

\[
\frac{\partial\mathcal S_{\rm law}}{\partial t}=0,
\qquad
N_\lambda=N_\lambda(n).
\]

If a later draft writes \(\partial\mathcal S_{\rm law}/\partial t\neq 0\), the substratum has become a 4D field and the clocks constrain it again.

No: do not embed hierarchical 5D twistor space today. Do not identify \(\mathcal S\) with a metric fifth dimension. Do not declare \(\mathcal S\) atemporal and writable in the same breath without the law/occupation split.
