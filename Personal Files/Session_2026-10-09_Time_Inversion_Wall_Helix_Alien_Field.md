## 2. Inversion plus advance

A closed curve identifies the event with itself, so the clock cannot have moved. The charge wants both: time order reverses on the orbit, and the superblock clock advances. Those two together are

\[
(t,x,\Phi)\;\longrightarrow\;(t,x,\Phi+\delta),
\]

with \(\delta\) one reset step. **Key.** \(t,x\): the spacetime event. \(\Phi\): internal phase. \(\delta\): the slot advance, a discrete \(G_2\) step onto \(\Phi_8\). The spacetime projection looks closed. The extended point is not. That is an open helix. It is the old taming, with the cylinder removed. It does not need \(g_{\phi\phi}<0\), and it does not recreate Killing energy.

## 3. Wall in place of the cylinder

Possible use: the helix is evaluated where a chamber meets the conformal crossover, the hard wall of the void-driven tree. The wall is where the reset is read. It is not the metric that carries the loop.

Repercussion. Eighth-phase loops of energy become a sequence of resets at the crossover, indexed by the clock, not a hoop of radius \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\). The tree axiom is untouched. One \(3+1\) manifold.
