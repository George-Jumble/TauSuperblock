## Analysis

- The present non-locking entropic force is carried by unbound completions (\(L_{\rm res}=0\)) on the wall. Moving the carrier to Phase 7 places it inside the floor sector.
- Floor objects are already refused as sources of \(\rho_\Lambda\). Making the Higgs / Φ7 the carrier of the cosmic entropic force reopens that refusal and requires either (i) an exception to the author cut or (ii) an additional free function that promotes floor excitations into a cosmological term.
- Residual-\(L\) matching to laboratory \(G\) can be preserved only if the new Higgs-mediated term is screened or wall-localized and does not alter the bound-sector residual angular momentum on glob scales. That screening is an extra assumption, not a lock.
- The Higgs vacuum expectation and potential depth are already fixed by electroweak data (and, in one reading, related to a microscopic Casimir length). Promoting them to a source of the observed \(\rho_\Lambda\sim5\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}\) reintroduces the hierarchy problem the floor cut was meant to avoid; no locked protocol converts the Higgs scale into the cosmological density.
- Unbound photonic energy on the wall remains available as the original carrier. The Phase-7 proposal does not evaluate \(E_{\rm shell}\) or remove the free conversion constants; it merely relocates the adjustable piece into the floor.

## Conclusion

Carrying the entropic force by the seventh phase / Higgs field is possible only as a non-locking speculation that conflicts with the author cut treating Phase 7 as a floor object refused as a source of \(\rho_\Lambda\). It adds free parameters or an exception, does not derive the observed density, and risks mixing the floor sector with the cosmological term while residual-\(L\) gravity must still match laboratory \(G\). The cleaner non-locking carrier remains the unbound completions on the wall. All live locks can be preserved only by keeping the proposal explicitly non-locking and screened.

**Plain language.** Phase 7 is the Higgs “holding” layer that sits at the floor of the binding hierarchy. The rules already say this floor layer (and the ordinary Casimir effect) should not be the source of either ordinary gravity or cosmic acceleration. Moving the adjustable outward push onto the Higgs field would put a floor object in charge of dark energy, which the current cuts forbid unless one adds extra adjustable pieces or carves out an exception. It does not calculate the observed acceleration from the core rules, and it makes the separation between ordinary gravity and the cosmic push less clean. The version that keeps free light finishing on the distant wall is still the simpler fit inside the existing locks.
