## Answer

\(\lambda\) is the dimensionless quartic coupling. \(V\) is the potential it shapes.

\[
V(\phi)
=
\frac{\lambda}{4}\left(\phi^2-v^2\right)^2.
\]

**Key.** \(V\): Higgs potential, energy density. \(\phi\): the field. \(v=246.22\,\mathrm{GeV}\): vacuum value, the minimum. \(\lambda\): quartic coupling. \(V(v)=0\) by this choice of zero. \(V(0)=\lambda v^4/4\), the depth used in the wall evaluation.

\(\lambda\) is fixed by the measured Higgs mass:

\[
\lambda
=
\frac{m_H^2}{2v^2}
=
0.129,
\]

with \(m_H=125.10\,\mathrm{GeV}\). **Key.** \(m_H\): pole mass of the radial excitation about \(v\). Factor \(2\): from the second derivative of \(V\) at the minimum, \(V''(v)=2\lambda v^2\), identified with \(m_H^2\).

Depth used yesterday:

\[
\frac{\lambda v^4}{4}
=
1.186\times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{GeV}^4.
\]
