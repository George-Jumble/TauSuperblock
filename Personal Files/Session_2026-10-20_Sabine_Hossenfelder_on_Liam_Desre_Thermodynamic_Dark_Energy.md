## The Project

Liam Desré (14, Kingston, Ontario) won one of four second prizes (€5,000) at the 37th European Union Contest for Young Scientists (EUCYS 2026, Kiel) for *ΛCDM+S: Evaluating a Thermodynamic Extension to Standard Cosmology*.

- Adds a thermodynamic layer “S” (entropy) to ΛCDM.
- Inspired by black-hole thermodynamics (from reading Susskind’s *The Black Hole War*).
- Derives new solutions to the Friedmann equations.
- Generates >3.5 million data points via Monte Carlo simulations.
- Uses Bayesian inference.
- Claims the model closely matches ΛCDM expansion history without requiring dark energy.
- Reproduces key features of cosmic acceleration via entropy-driven effects.
- Earlier won Best Project–Discovery at the 2026 Canada-Wide Science Fair.

Sabine notes that the submission “ticks all the boxes of a typical journal paper” in the field and that the student’s presentation would pass as that of a working researcher. Her interest is more in the maturity of the work than in any claim that the theory solves dark energy.

\[
H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho-\frac{kc^2}{a^2}+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}

- \(a\): scale factor.

## Conclusion

It is interesting as an existence proof that such phenomenological alternatives can be formulated and simulated at a high level by a 14-year-old, and it overlaps conceptually with Superblock’s non-locking entropic-force ideas. It does not derive the observed dark-energy density, does not replace residual-\(L\), and leaves all core locks unchanged. The work remains external context.
