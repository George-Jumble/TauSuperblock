# A colleague’s note on the Tau proposition

**To:** Ernest C. Gatlin III  
**From:** William Brown  
**Date:** 19 September 2026  
**Subject:** Problems in the compact-fifth-dimension formulation, written so we can keep what is useful

Clay,

I am writing this as a collaborator, not as a referee. You have put more of this framework into public, numbered, pre-registered form than most independent programs ever do. You posted nulls when the tests failed. That is the part I want to keep. What follows is about the *load-bearing object* in the Tau papers: a compact spatial circle of radius \(\tau=7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) treated as the fifth dimension of Einstein–Kaluza–Klein gravity. I no longer think that object can carry the theory. I think some of the instincts around it can.

I will be plain, and I will try to be careful.

---

## 1. What I am not saying

I am not saying the cosmic web has no few-\(h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) scale. It does.

I am not saying a discrete 7-fold clock, a resonance graph, or a geometric ambition for dark matter and the vacuum is worthless.

I am not saying you were wrong to test \(k=0.142857\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}\) in JADES and DESI.

I am saying that identifying that scale with a *metric extra dimension that gravity wraps* conflicts with measurements that are older and tighter than any galaxy power spectrum, and that several of the “outputs” of \(\tau\) were the observed numbers written back in.

---

## 2. The circle as extra-dimensional gravity

The published Tau construction is not a metaphor. It is five-dimensional Einstein–Hilbert gravity on \(\mathbb{R}^{3}\times S^{1}\), with

\[
S_5=\frac{1}{16\pi G_5}\int d^5x\sqrt{-g_5}\,R_5,
\qquad
G_4=\frac{G_5}{2\pi\tau},
\qquad
\tau=7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\approx 10\,\mathrm{Mpc}.
\]

If gravity can propagate on that circle, laboratory inverse-square-law tests already require a single flat extra dimension to be smaller than about thirty microns. The mismatch is of order \(10^{28}\) in radius. The first gravitational Kaluza–Klein mass would sit near \(10^{-30}\,\mathrm{eV}\): an almost continuous tower of light spin-2 modes.

This does not depend on anyone believing in “gravitons” as particles. Torsion balances measure a force between masses. If flux can spread into a fifth spatial direction of size \(\tau\), Newton’s law changes below that size. It does not.

Your own prediction sheet lists “no sub-millimeter gravity deviation” among the things that must not be seen. I agree with that null. I do not see how it can coexist with a metric circle of ten megaparsecs that gravity wraps.

Resonances on the circle do not warp the circle. They *are* the tower. Selecting \(n=7\) changes the integer, not the radius.

If \(\tau\) is only a 4D coherence length or a phase label — something gravity does not wrap — then this paragraph does not apply. In that case the 5D Einstein–Hilbert writing and \(G_4=G_5/(2\pi\tau)\) should be retired as a picture of laboratory gravity. That is the fork. Both sentences cannot stay.

---

## 3. Casimir energy of that circle is not dark energy

Ordinary compactification Casimir energy scales as \(\tau^{-4}\). At ten megaparsecs that density is about \(10^{-121}\,\mathrm{eV}^4\). Observed dark energy is about \(10^{-11}\,\mathrm{eV}^4\). The gap is about \(10^{110}\).

A formula of the form \(\rho_\Lambda\propto(\pi/\tau)^4\) cannot be made to yield \(\Omega_\Lambda=0.694\) by rearranging \(\pi\). If a prefactor is chosen so that the observed density appears, the observed density was an input. That is allowed. It is not a derivation, and it is not “zero parameters.”

Classical dimensional reduction of \(S_5\) also does not, by itself, produce a Casimir cosmological constant. Casimir energy appears after a spectrum is quantized and a potential for the radius is stabilized. Sign and magnitude both have to be earned. I have not found that calculation at the required magnitude in the Tau corpus.

---

## 4. Dark matter as ultralight modes of the same circle

If the same \(\tau\) is asked to supply dark matter as Kaluza–Klein excitations, the masses wander, in the public notes, among \(10^{-30}\,\mathrm{eV}\), \(10^{-34}\,\mathrm{eV}\), and the \(10^{-22}\,\mathrm{eV}\) fuzzy-dark-matter window. Those are not one particle.

A Compton wavelength of order ten megaparsecs is not how galaxies are observed to virialize. Fuzzy dark matter at \(10^{-22}\,\mathrm{eV}\) is a different literature, with a different length.

This is separate from a *hidden-sector* idea — matter in another phase or domain that sources curvature and not photons. That idea does not need the circle. I think it is the better home for a geometric dark-matter story. I do not think the circle is doing that job.

---

## 5. The JADES line, and the later null

In November 2025 the Tau papers announced an \(8.1\sigma\) monochromatic feature at exactly \(k=1/7\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}\) and said, in substance, that the fifth dimension had been measured. Some texts also said the mode lay just beyond the then-available \(k\)-reach and would be tested when the full catalog arrived. Those two sentences cannot both be true of the same dataset.

The feature was described as appearing only after filters prescribed by the same model. A public analysis script injects a Gaussian at \(k=0.142857\) and then prints a discovery sentence. I mention this only because it matters for how we talk about “measurement.” A template plot is not a detection.

In March 2026 you tested the prediction on 7,775 JADES galaxies and on the DESI DR1 Bright Galaxy Sample. You found no Berard excess, no Rayleigh phase clustering, and no Kaluza–Klein comb. You published that. That was the right act.

What I cannot follow is keeping \(\tau\) unchanged as a *measured radius* after the empirical flagship did not reproduce. A null may still leave a *target*. It withdraws a discovery. Euclid and Rubin can test a target. They cannot ratify a measurement that has already failed its first controlled look.

\(1/7=0.142857\ldots\). Choosing that wavenumber and inverting it to seven megaparsecs is an identification, not an independent length.

---

## 6. On “zero parameters” and on anchors

There is nothing wrong with taking an observable as a given. General relativity does not derive \(G\). A geometric theory may take \(c\), \(\hbar\), and even one cosmological length from the world, and then predict something else.

What goes wrong is moving a given into the “derived” column without a calculation that would have produced that number if we had not already seen it. \(\Omega_\Lambda=0.694\), \(H_0=69.8\), \(\Omega_{\rm DM}h^2=0.120\), and \(\tau=7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) cannot all be outputs of one radius if that radius was set to a catalogue scale and the densities were set to the observed ones.

A short, public list of inputs would make the theory stronger, not weaker. I would rather have three named givens and two real predictions than a zero-parameter sentence that covers every leftover constant.

---

## 7. What I still think is worth keeping

A discrete harmonic structure that is not itself a field on expanding spacetime — a menu of allowed modes, with occupations that can change — is a useful idea. It does not have to be a metric circle. It does not have to be non-computable in order to exist.

A 7-fold clock as an *internal* schedule is compatible with ordinary causality if time is a helix: the phase label may cycle; the tick count is never identified with the same spacetime event six turns later. That is not a compact-time machine.

The web scale can remain a *question*: is there a coherence length in whatever kernel couples hidden stress to visible curvature? That question does not need \(S_5\). A first estimate that uses only a Hubble-scale carrier and a subluminal relative boost does *not* fracture at seven megaparsecs. So even that milder reading is not yet a derivation. It is a calculation still to be done.

I am not embedding any of this into a joint axiom tonight. I am telling you what I can no longer defend if someone asks me whether Tau, as written, holds water on its own. My present answer is no: not as extra-dimensional gravity, not as Casimir dark energy, not as a completed measurement of a fifth dimension.

---

## 8. What I hope we can do next

If you want \(\tau\) to remain a 4D coherence or clock-fossil scale, I will help write the kernel that would make that precise, and I will not call it a compact fifth dimension.

If you want to keep a real fifth metric direction, it has to sit inside existing laboratory bounds, or it has to come with an explicit decoupling mechanism that is not a resonance slogan. I do not see a path that keeps ten megaparsecs as that direction.

If the March 2026 nulls are the scientific result of the JADES/DESI tests, I think the public abstracts should say so in the first paragraph, and the November discovery sentence should be withdrawn or marked historical.

None of this is meant to end the correspondence. It is meant to stop us from asking one illegal object to do every job in the unification. Superblock can live without that object. I would rather have a smaller joint program that survives Cavendish than a larger one that does not.

I am grateful for the work you have already put in the open.

With respect,  
William Brown
