# Session 2026-10-24: Free Parameters Added by the Information-Carrying Harmonic Force

**Date**: 2026-10-24  
**Framework**: Tau-Superblock (TSB / Superblock) — live 4D frame. Proposal: harmonic resonance acts as a force that carries information for the octonionic configuration of new islands and synchronizes all Mirrons on a 1/6 tick. Residual-L matches laboratory \(G\). UPS stamps kinematic.  
**Question**: What free parameters are added?  
**Answer**: At least one free coupling function of the phase sequence, plus optional screening and synchronization strengths.

## Plain-language summary

The proposal adds at least one free function that converts the kinematic phase sequence into a force or into configuration information. It can also add a screening length (so the force does not spoil laboratory residual-L) and a synchronization strength (how tightly the Mirrons are locked to the common 1/6 tick). None of these numbers or functions is fixed by residual-L, the mass-floor protocol, or the kinematic cascade. They remain free phenomenological parameters.

## Analytical list

**Key to symbols**  
- \(\{\phi_n\}\): kinematic phase sequence on the cascade.  
- \(f\): free coupling function.  
- \(\lambda_{\rm screen}\): optional screening length.  
- \(\kappa_{\rm sync}\): optional synchronization strength.

Free parameters introduced by the proposal:

1. Coupling function  
   \[
   F_{\rm info}=f(\{\phi_n\})
   \]  
   (or its discrete coefficients if \(f\) is expanded). No locked operator fixes the form or the coefficients of \(f\).

2. Screening length (optional but required for laboratory consistency)  
   \[
   F_{\rm lab}=F_{\rm info}\cdot e^{-r/\lambda_{\rm screen}}
   \]  
   or any equivalent suppression. \(\lambda_{\rm screen}\) is free.

3. Synchronization strength (optional)  
   \[
   \Delta\phi_{\rm lock}\le\kappa_{\rm sync}\cdot\frac{2\pi}{6}.
   \]  
   \(\kappa_{\rm sync}\) is free.

All three remain unfixed by the live locks. Residual-L laboratory matching is recovered only by choice of the screening term.

## Repercussions and locks

- Free parameters are phenomenological only.  
- Does not derive \(\rho_\Lambda\) or the mass floor.  
- Residual-L laboratory \(G\) match preserved under screening.  
- Conflicts with kinematic-only UPS if the force is unscreened and promoted.  
- Does not restore the rejected Tau package.
