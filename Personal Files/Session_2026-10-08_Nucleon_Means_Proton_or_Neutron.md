## Answer

Yes. Nucleon means either member of the isospin doublet: proton or neutron. Not a generic baryon. Not a nucleus.

The printed working floor used the neutron only.

\[
\Delta
=
\frac{E_{\rm dress}^{(n)}}{3}
=
309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]

**Key.** \(\Delta\): printed working floor. \(E_{\rm dress}^{(n)}=M_n-m_u^{\rm cur}-2m_d^{\rm cur}\): neutron mass minus one current up and two current downs. Factor \(3\): valence count. Proton share is the other nucleon, \(309.75\,\mathrm{MeV}\), not the print.
