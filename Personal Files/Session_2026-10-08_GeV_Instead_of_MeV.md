## Answer

No. A GeV is \(1000\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Same dimension. The working print is

\[
\Delta=309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}=0.3093\,\mathrm{GeV}.
\]

**Key.** \(\Delta\): working floor, neutron dressing protocol. \(\mathrm{GeV}\): gigaelectronvolt. \(\mathrm{MeV}\): megaelectronvolt. Neither is the anchor. Hadron tables often use GeV. Light-quark tables use MeV. Either print is fine. The clock still does not produce the number.
