**Date**: 2026-10-22  
**Query**: What about the entropic force of dark energy?  
**Context**: Live 4D Superblock. Residual-L gravity matches laboratory \(G\) (bound sector). Entropic force is the non-locking candidate for dark energy.

## Definition in the live frame

The entropic force of dark energy is the gradient of the Mirron/Entropic wall potential. The wall is the locus of completed unbound bounces (\(L_{\rm res}=0\)). Unbound photonic (and other null) energy that finishes its bounce deposits on that locus and supplies the potential; the gradient points toward the high-Weyl / lack-of-mass side.

\[
V_{\rm wall}
=
\sum_{\rm completions\ on\ wall}
E_{\rm unbound}^{(i)},
\qquad
\mathbf{F}_{\rm ent}
=
-\nabla V_{\rm wall}.
\]

**Variable key:**  
- \(V_{\rm wall}\): Mirron/Entropic wall potential.  
- \(E_{\rm unbound}^{(i)}\): energy of the \(i\)-th completed unbound bounce.  
- \(\mathbf{F}_{\rm ent}\): entropic force (anti-gravity attracted to lack of mass).  
- Wall: locus of completed unbound bounces (\(L_{\rm res}=0\)).  
- \(\nabla\): gradient transverse to the wall.

This force is distinct from residual-\(L\) gravity. Residual \(L\) is sourced only by stopped bound rebounds and matches laboratory \(G\). The entropic force is carried by the unbound sector and does not source ordinary gravity.

## Qualitative behavior

- The force is larger the farther the photonic shell is from the wall and weakens as the atemporal (\(d\tau=0\)) photonic energy disperses along the expanding wall.  
- Weakening becomes pronounced as the universe approaches Penrose CCC crossover conditions (\(\mathcal{I}^+\) joined to the next aeon across a spacelike 3-surface).  
- Early residual-\(L\) focusing (ordinary \(G\)) can modulate local null-ray arrival but does not generate an early lambda term or alter the global unbound reservoir.  
- Envelope orbital speeds \(\beta_i\) can modulate effective dispersion only in a non-locking extension.

## Conclusion

The entropic force of dark energy is the coherent non-locking candidate already under discussion: the gradient of unbound photonic energy resident on the wall (locus of completed bounces). It organizes an outward push toward the lack-of-mass side whose qualitative drop-off with dispersion and approach to CCC crossover is consistent with the locks. Its magnitude is not derived from residual-\(L\), the floor protocol, or any other locked structure; it remains fitted to the observed acceleration. Phenomenological \(\Lambda\) continues to be available as an external input.

**Plain language.** The entropic force we have been examining is precisely the Superblock sketch for dark energy: free light finishes its journey on a distant wall and the slope of that energy pile pushes the universe outward toward emptier regions. The push gets weaker as the light spreads out and as an aeon nears its conformal end. Ordinary gravity stays the separate leftover-spin effect that matches laboratory measurements. The size of the push can be adjusted to fit the observed acceleration, but the core rules do not yet calculate that size from first principles.
