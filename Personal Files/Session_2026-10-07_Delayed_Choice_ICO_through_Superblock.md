## 1. What the labs actually did

### 1.1 Wheeler delayed choice

A later setting chooses which-path vs interference. The photon is not assigned a classical path that is then edited. The paradox appears only if one forces a hidden trajectory onto a state that was never a trajectory.

### 1.2 Delayed-choice entanglement swapping

Peres (J. Mod. Opt. 47, 139, 2000) proposed the protocol. Ma, Zotter, Kofler, Ursin, Jennewein, Brukner, Zeilinger, Nature Physics 8, 479–484 (2012), doi:10.1038/nphys2294, realized it.

- Two entangled pairs. Photons 1 and 4 go to Alice and Bob and are registered first.
- Photons 2 and 3 go to a bipartite-state analyser (BiSA). The choice — Bell projection vs separable measurement — is delayed into the time-like future of the Alice/Bob clicks, set by a high-speed electro-optic modulator and a quantum random-number generator.
- Conditioning on the later Bell outcome, the already-registered 1–4 data show quantum correlations. Conditioning on the separable setting, those correlations vanish into classical (or null) correlations.
- Reported correlation functions of photons 1 and 4 in the (H/V, R/L, +/−) bases: case (a) 0.511 ± 0.089, 0.603 ± 0.071, −0.611 ± 0.074; case (b) 0.632 ± 0.059, 0.01 ± 0.072, −0.045 ± 0.070.
- Zeilinger’s own phrasing was that a naïve classical worldview can “mimic an influence of future actions on past events,” and the abstract calls the projection “quantum steering into the past.” That is a statement about the post-selected ensemble, not a claim that a force quantum traveled backward.

### 1.3 Quantum switch and the causal witness

Chiribella, D’Ariano, Perinotti, Valiron, Phys. Rev. A 88, 022318 (2013): the quantum switch puts two black-box operations into a superposition of orders, controlled by a qubit. Simulating that in a fixed-order circuit needs post-selection or an extra query. It is not a closed timelike curve.

Rubino et al., Science Advances 3, e1602589 (2017), doi:10.1126/sciadv.1602589: first decisive photonic demonstration. A causal witness was negative by almost 7 standard deviations, so the process is not a classical mixture of “A before B” and “B before A.”

Local observers still have an ordinary time order inside their own labs. No-signaling and the light-cone bound are intact. Later device-independent certificates of the switch still assume relativistic causality (no superluminal and no retrocausal influences) as an input, not as something the switch violates.

## 4. Equations (complete key under each block)

### 4.1 No-signaling on the earlier wing

\[
\rho_A(s_V) = \mathrm{Tr}_{B,V}\!\left[(M_A \otimes I_B \otimes E_{s_V})\,\rho_{ABV}\right]
= \rho_A
\]

Key:

- ρ_ABV: joint state of Alice, Bob, and Victor (the later BiSA party) before Victor’s setting.
- M_A: Alice’s local measurement map (or POVM element, absorbed into the partial trace convention used here).
- I_B: identity on Bob.
- E_{s_V}: Victor’s instrument element for setting s_V.
- ρ_A(s_V): Alice’s reduced state conditional on Victor’s setting.
- ρ_A: the same reduced state with Victor traced out and no setting dependence.
- Tr: partial trace.
- Equality is the no-signaling condition. It is what forbids a usable message into the past.

### 4.2 Post-selected swapping correlation vs marginal

\[
P(a,b \mid \Phi)
= \frac{\mathrm{Tr}\!\left[(P_a \otimes P_b \otimes |\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|)\,\rho\right]}
{\mathrm{Tr}\!\left[(I \otimes I \otimes |\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|)\,\rho\right]}
\]

\[
P(a) = \sum_b P(a,b) = \mathrm{Tr}(P_a\,\rho_A)
\]

Key:

- a, b: Alice’s and Bob’s earlier outcomes.
- Φ: a Bell outcome on Victor’s photons 2 and 3 (one of the four Bell projectors).

- P(a): marginal. Independent of whether Victor later chooses a Bell setting or a separable setting.

### 4.3 Quantum switch

\[
\mathcal{S}(U,V)\,|\psi\rangle_t\,|c\rangle
= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left( UV|\psi\rangle_t\,|0\rangle_c + VU|\psi\rangle_t\,|1\rangle_c \right)
\]

when |c⟩ = (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2. For a general control, the two branches are weighted by the control amplitudes.

Key:

- S(U,V): quantum switch of two operations.
- U, V: the two black-box maps (unitaries in the ideal switch).
- |ψ⟩_t: target system.
- |c⟩: control qubit.
- |0⟩_c: branch in which U acts after V (order V then U on the target).
- |1⟩_c: branch in which V acts after U.
- The control becomes entangled with the order. Reading the control later sorts the orders. It does not reverse the laboratory clock.

### 4.4 Causal witness

\[
\langle C \rangle = \mathrm{Tr}(C\,W)
\]

Definite causal order (convex mixture of A ≺ B and B ≺ A, under the witness normalization used by Rubino et al.) gives ⟨C⟩ ≥ 0. A negative value certifies that W is not such a mixture.

Key:

- W: process matrix of the experiment (the higher-order map from the two local operations to the output probabilities).
- C: causal witness operator, built so that its expectation is non-negative on every definite-order process and can be negative on an indefinite-order process.
- Tr: trace on the process-matrix space.
- Rubino et al. reported a negative witness at almost 7 standard deviations. That number is theirs, not a Superblock prediction.
