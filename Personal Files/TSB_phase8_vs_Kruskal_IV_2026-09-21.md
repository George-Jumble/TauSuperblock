# Phase-8 \(=\) Kruskal IV or not

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Objects.** Phase 8: Mirron reset tick; map \(M\) on \(\mathcal{A}_8\) / on the Kerr ring. Kruskal IV: white-hole *interior* of one eternal vacuum 4-metric.

---

## 1. What matches (the rhyme)

| Phase 8 | Kruskal IV |
|---|---|
| outgoing / past-to-future bounce | white hole: matter can leave, not enter |
| antipode / \(\hat\Omega\check\Omega=-1\) | time-reverse of block II |
| “white” faces on the six-stroke ring | “white” in the analytic extension |

Both are white-going in ordinary language.

---

## 2. What does not match

**Dimension of the object.** Phase 8 is a *tick* and a *map* of a 3-sphere (or of the ring). Kruskal IV is an open 4-region \(\{r<2M\}\cap\) past interior.

**How many metrics.** Phase 8 acts on the shared engine of six copies. Kruskal IV is one block of one metric.

**Existence in collapse.** A hole that forms from collapse has no IV. Phase 8 is supposed to run in the real engine. Identifying them gives every astrophysical hole a white-hole interior it does not have.

**Mass inflation / inner horizon.** Kerr’s path to a later white region goes through \(r_-\), which inflates. Phase 8 as a cut *before* \(r_-\) is the honest option. That cut is not IV.

**Degree.** Phase-8 \(M\in\mathrm{Conf}(S^3)\) has \(n=\pm 1\). Kruskal IV is not a map of \(S^3\).

---

## 3. Decision

**Not.** Phase 8 is not Kruskal IV.

Keep the words: white, outgoing, time-reverse. Drop the identity. Phase 8 is the bounce map on \(\mathcal{A}_8\) and the colour-step on the six-face ring. Kruskal IV stays the vacuum block of the eternal diagram, cut off by collapse and by inflation.

A weaker sentence that is allowed: phase 8 *plays the role* white-hole language played in the extension cartoon, without being that region.
