## 2. The write

\[
N_\ell(t+\delta)
=
N_\ell(t)
+
c_\ell\,A(x).
\]

**Key.** \(N_\ell\): occupation of harmonic \(\ell\) on the conformal circle. \(A(x)\): the action at the spatial point during the tick. \(c_\ell\): a coupling of that point’s contribution onto mode \(\ell\). Unspecified. The resonance is the statement that every point and every energy action has some \(c_\ell\), so the tube’s spectrum is a running record of the tick.

This matches the 19 September substratum opinion: modes can be written, and the law of which modes exist does not age with the scale factor. Today’s circle supplies the place those modes live — the photon-ring world-tube — without a fifth coordinate.

## 3. Repercussions

Compatible with exclusion. A same-slot meeting is still forbidden. The write records the tick. It does not license the meeting. An unbound photon, \(d\tau=0\) on the orbit, is the carrier of the record, not a payer of \(\Delta\). A bound line forced through scrape and reset still pays.

Refused extension: identifying the circle with \(S^1_\tau\), assigning \(R_\tau\approx 7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\), or setting \(\rho=\hbar c/R^4\). The Casimir evaluation against the Higgs depth already fixed a microscopic plate gap, \(1.89\times 10^{-18}\,\mathrm{m}\), which is not this tube’s radius unless a particle-scale hole is chosen. Not chosen.

No MeV falls out. \(c_\ell\) is a new unspecified coupling. Writing it so that \(\sum N_\ell\) equals \(309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}\) would be a fit.
