## 2. What “product” can mean as an equation

Three honest products. Pick one and freeze it.

**Light-travel product**

\[

\]

For \(\tau_{\rm phys}\approx 10\,\mathrm{Mpc}\),

\[

**Conformal-time product**

Then \(\tau_{\rm com}\) is the comoving reach of signals since some TSB event at \(t_*\). That can lock a web scale to a *when*, not to a compact radius. Specify \(t_*\).

Let \(\Phi(x,t)\) be the phase or impulse field. Define

\[
\mathcal C(x,x')=\bigl\langle\Phi(x,t)\,\Phi(x',t)\bigr\rangle_t

with the average over the internal clock. The correlation length \(\ell_C\) is defined by the first zero or the half-width of \(\mathcal C\). The claim is

\[
\ell_C=\tau_{\rm com}=7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}.
\]

This is the cleanest “product of the passage of time”: time-averaging *produces* a spatial kernel. The circle is in \(\phi\). The megaparsecs are in \(\mathcal C\).

The third version is the one that matches “Tav is a phase label, the web scale is a fossil.”

## 3. Why the scale is comoving, if time keeps passing

Web papers quote \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) as a *comoving* length. Physical megaparsecs grow with \(a(t)\). If the imprint were a fixed physical yardstick, it would not stay at 7 in catalogue units.

So a time-product model must explain a frozen *comoving* number. Typical ways:

- the process is stretched by expansion and then *exhausts* (your conformal-exhaustion idea): binding or phase-lock fails at a fixed comoving reach,
- the correlator is defined on comoving coordinates from the start, because the clock is tied to conformal time,
- nucleation / domain-orbit is locked to \(H(t)\), so the only invariant length the process can name is a multiple of the Hubble / web scale in \(h^{-1}\) units.

Without one of those locks, “product of time” produces a length that keeps growing, and the number 7 is a present-day snapshot, not a constant of the theory.
