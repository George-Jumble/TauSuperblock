## 2. What the corpus already says, by unit

### 2.1 Unit 1 — Dynamic Refresh is the floor, and it is not a glob

Locked 2026-09-30 (author intent, mechanism notes): DR acts directly only on quarks, gluons, colour, and emergent EM flux. There is no coordinated DR of atoms, molecules, clusters, or isolated COM. Higher structure is a consequence of floor occupancy, not a second refresh engine.

The 26 July 2026 capture note is the same grain: the force that binds when electromagnetic pressure contains Phase 7 is the strong (colour) force; the particles in active DR manifestation are the mass caught out. Duty cycle is a fraction of *quark content*, not of molecules.

The 21 July 2021 revision (dated 2026-07-21 in the corpus) is the EM half of the user’s sentence: inverted colour excites local EM / electroweak fields; the other two quarks in a stable three-quark configuration bolster that excitation (associator locking). W and Z are an example of that excitation, not a separate globbing engine.

So the user’s “colour charge and EM excitation delivered to the quarks produced by the colour-flux interactions” matches the floor. Two fences:

- EM excitation is the *image* of inverted colour, \(j_{\rm EW}^{\rm local}=T_{\rm alien\to local}\,j_{\rm colour}^{\rm alien}\). It is not a second parcel handed to quarks that colour has already finished making.
- DR restocks the first row of the hierarchy table. It does not move a child COM into a parent. Globbing starts at the next arrow.

\[
\mathrm{DR}:\quad \text{floor lines on/off}.
\qquad
\mathrm{Hierarchy}:\quad
\{\text{on lines}\}
\longrightarrow
\{\mathcal{B}^{(g)},\mathbf{R}^{(g)},L^{(g)}_{\rm res}\}.
\]

**Key.** DR: occupancy protocol at colour / quark / gluon / emergent EM. Hierarchy: composition of already-stopped lines into a glob with one packet address. UPS is not in either arrow.

### 2.2 Unit 2 — “atomic = strong + weak” merges three seats

The 30 September hierarchy table, which is the live alignment, splits what the outline calls one atomic unit:

| Corpus seat | What binds it | COM status |
|-------------|---------------|------------|
| Floor | colour / DR | no lab COM |
| Hadronic | three-line colour singlet (strong) | \(\mathbf{R}^{N}\) bound inside a nucleus if the nucleus is coherent |
| Nuclear | residual colour between nucleons, plus Coulomb | \(\mathbf{R}^{\rm nuc}\) bound inside the atom if the atom is coherent |
| Atomic / metallic | EM (Coulomb) envelope | \(\mathbf{R}^{\rm atom}\) bound inside a molecule or cluster |

Textbook names agree with that split and disagree with the outline’s label: atomic binding is electromagnetic; nuclear binding is residual strong; the weak interaction changes flavour and does not hold the nucleus together as a binding force.

The *bridge* claim is the part that matches. Corpus seat of that bridge is the floor, not the atom:

\[
j_{\rm EW}^{\rm local}
=
T_{\rm alien\to local}
\,
j_{\rm colour}^{\rm alien}.
\]

**Key.** \(j_{\rm colour}^{\rm alien}\): colour current being contained. \(T_{\rm alien\to local}\): inversion / containment map (domain transit, phase, \(G_2\) factor). \(j_{\rm EW}^{\rm local}\): local electroweak current that the atom later uses as charge, magnetic moment, and photon coupling. The bridge feeds the EM properties of atoms. It is not the generation called “atomic binding.”

The 20 July ladder put electroweak *above* atomic (nuclear → atomic → electroweak → EM). That is the opposite order from this outline, and it is not live. Both the old ladder and this outline mis-seat the weak channel. Live seat: floor protocol, W/Z as example, \(\mathcal{F}_{\rm 3q}=3/4\).

### 2.3 Unit 3 — molecular EM is a family of globs, not one glob

Discrete EM bindings from nano-scale to macroscopic scale match the corpus family: atomic/metallic droplet, molecule, cluster, bench object. Pedalino’s sodium nanoparticle is one row of that family (cluster COM isolated; atomic COMs internal).

What the single label hides is the COM cut. A coordinate is bound when a parent has absorbed it into \(\chi\). It is unbound when it is still a packet address. Nano-to-macro is many such cuts, not one binding unit.

\[
\mathcal{B}^{(g)}
=
\bigl\{\mathcal{B}^{(g+1)}_k\bigr\}_{k}
\quad\text{held as one glob},
\qquad
\mathbf{R}^{(g)}
=
\frac{1}{m^{(g)}}
\sum_k m^{(g+1)}_k\,\mathbf{R}^{(g+1)}_k.
\]

**Key.** \(\mathcal{B}^{(g)}\): parent glob. \(\mathcal{B}^{(g+1)}_k\): child envelope \(k\). \(\mathbf{R}^{(g)}\): mass-weighted center of mass of the children. \(m^{(g)}=\sum_k m^{(g+1)}_k\) when the parent is just the coherent sum (no extra mass postulate). Parent coherence means the child packet addresses are no longer independent isolated packets.

Binding of children does not pin the parent packet. That is why a molecular or metallic glob can be isolated and still interfere, while its atomic internals stay bound.

### 2.4 Unit 4 — gravity is a cross-cut, and that is the match

This is the sentence the corpus has been trying to keep since 20 July, and the 30 September note restated it without the Tau node:

> Effective \(G\) belongs to the center of gravity of the largest coherent bound set among groups of sets where that \(G\) is dominant.

User’s clause “present in all levels … specific gravity of the glob … dominant center of gravity of any set of bound globs (a rock, a planet, a star)” is that sentence. It is a better reading than the 20 July ladder’s top rung “gravitic node at \(\tau\).”

Formal live assignment, without the retired scale factor \((R_{\rm max}/R_{\rm Pl})^{2\alpha}\):

\[
\mathcal{B}^{\star}(S)
=
\arg\max_{\mathcal{B}\subset S,\;\mathrm{coherent}}
G(\mathcal{B}),
\qquad
G_{\rm eff}(S)
=
G\bigl(\mathcal{B}^{\star}(S)\bigr),
\qquad
\mathbf{R}_{\rm dom}(S)
=
\mathbf{R}\bigl(\mathcal{B}^{\star}(S)\bigr).
\]

**Key.** \(S\): the set of globs under consideration (a rock, a planet, a star, or a lab sample). \(\mathcal{B}\subset S\): a coherent bound subset. \(G(\mathcal{B})\): gravity sourced by that glob’s residual angular momentum. \(\mathcal{B}^{\star}(S)\): the subset whose \(G\) is largest. \(G_{\rm eff}(S)\): what a probe of \(S\) couples to. \(\mathbf{R}_{\rm dom}(S)\): center of gravity of that dominant glob. No graviton. No DR duty cycle.

Gravity particular to one glob, which is the reading adopted for “specific gravity of the glob”:

\[
\sigma^{(g)}
=
\frac{m^{(g)}}{V^{(g)}}
\propto
\frac{\lvert L^{(g)}_{\rm res}\rvert}{V^{(g)}}.
\]

**Key.** \(\sigma^{(g)}\): residual-mass density of generation \(g\). \(m^{(g)}\): mass of that glob. \(V^{(g)}\): its volume. \(L^{(g)}_{\rm res}\): residual angular momentum of its stopped rebound. Proportionality is the live thesis (spatial compression from residual \(L\), strength set by local bound-field density). This \(\sigma^{(g)}\) is not the dimensionless laboratory specific gravity \(\rho/\rho_{\rm water}\).

Floor form of the residual, from the 26 July note, kept as the bottom of the same tower:

\[

=
\ell_0
\cdot
\mathcal{C}_{\rm colour}
\cdot
(\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{n}_{\rm stop})
\cdot
\mathcal{F}_{\rm TEP},
\qquad
\mathcal{C}_{\rm colour}
=
\frac{\lvert F_{\rm colour}\rvert}{\lvert V\rvert}

\mathcal{F}_{\rm 3q}.
\]

**Key.** \(\mathbf{L}_{\rm bounce}\): residual angular momentum of a stopped floor bounce. \(\ell_0\): unit angular-momentum scale of one bounce quantum. \(\mathcal{C}_{\rm colour}\): colour constraint. \(\mathbf{r}\): lever arm of the stop. \(\mathbf{n}_{\rm stop}\): direction of the interrupted rebound. \(\mathcal{F}_{\rm TEP}\): topological-exclusion factor (1 if the line is allowed to stop, 0 if the bounce completes). \(F_{\rm colour}\): colour force at the friction floor. \(V\): volume of the constrained region. \(\mathcal{F}_{\rm 3q}=3/4\): associator lock for a stable three-quark configuration. Higher-generation \(L^{(g)}_{\rm res}\) is the coherent sum of such stops that still belong to one \(\mathbf{R}^{(g)}\), not a new force.
