## Minimal Mathematical Sketch
Introduce a fifth coordinate σ (chosen to avoid the retired symbol τ). Signature remains Lorentzian on the 4D base; σ is taken non-compact and non-propagating for the universal field.

**Line element (toy form, time-only interaction)**:
\[
ds^2 = -N(t,\mathbf{x},\sigma)^2\, c^2\, dt^2 + g_{ij}(t,\mathbf{x})\, dx^i dx^j
\]
No \( d\sigma^2 \) term in the dynamical metric seen by the universal field; σ enters only as a parameter of the lapse \( N \).

**Key to variables and functions**:
- \( ds \): proper interval (length).
- \( N(t,\mathbf{x},\sigma) \): lapse function (dimensionless). Encodes the interaction of σ with time. \( N = 1 \) recovers ordinary 4D proper time when σ is ignored.
- \( c \): speed of light in vacuum (\( \approx 2.998 \times 10^8 \) m s⁻¹).
- \( t \): coordinate time.
- \( \mathbf{x} \): spatial coordinates \( (x,y,z) \).
- \( g_{ij} \): spatial metric (signature \( + \)).
- \( \sigma \): fifth coordinate. Dimension of time or of length/\( c \) according to convention; here treated as a time-like parameter that does not support a kinetic term for the universal field.
- No \( g_{\sigma\mu} \) cross terms for the universal field; any such term is set to zero by the “only interacts with time” postulate.

**Proper time for a comoving clock**:
\[
d\tau_{\rm proper} = N(t,\mathbf{x},\sigma)\, dt
\]
**Key**:
- \( d\tau_{\rm proper} \): proper time interval measured by a clock at fixed spatial coordinates.
- All other symbols as above.

## Mediation Through Time (no innate field coupling)
The universal field (colour + inverted-colour EM/electroweak on the floor, residual-L gravity as spatial compression from stopped rebounds) has no direct charge under σ. Observable effects appear only because dynamical quantities are rates with respect to proper time.

**Example — frequency of a bound bounce**:
\[
\omega = \frac{d\phi}{d\tau_{\rm proper}} = \frac{1}{N} \frac{d\phi}{dt}
\]
**Key**:
- \( \omega \): angular frequency of the phase advance (rad / proper time).
- \( \phi \): phase variable of the bounce / clockwork (dimensionless or rad).
- \( N \): lapse as above.
- A change in σ that raises \( N \) redshifts every frequency without altering the intrinsic coupling constants of the field.

**Residual angular momentum (gravity source)**:
Residual \( L \) is obtained from the stopped rebound of bound EM. The stop occurs at the Present. If the proper-time rate is modulated by \( N(\sigma) \), the residual \( L \) scales as
\[
L_{\rm residual} \propto \frac{1}{N(\sigma)}
\]
(or a power fixed by the precise definition of the stop). Spatial compression therefore changes even though the field itself has no σ-charge.

**Key**:
- \( L_{\rm residual} \): residual angular momentum after the rebound is stopped at the Present (units of action, \( \hbar \)).
- \( N(\sigma) \): lapse evaluated at the relevant σ.
- Proportionality is schematic; the exact power depends on whether energy or action is held fixed.
