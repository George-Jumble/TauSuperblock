## 0. Verdict

**They are not the same object, and the wall does not use the 7 Mpc radius.**

The Tau/Tav radius was a metric circle: a fifth length \(\tau\approx 7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\), substrate of the cylinder, count of globbing levels, and the length in \(\rho_\Lambda=\hbar c/\tau^4\). That package stays rejected.

A conformal wall, in the notes that still stand, is a boundary of a 4D patch: future infinity \(\mathscr{I}^+\) of a womb, or the high-Weyl complement of a void. It has no circumference to set to 7 Mpc. Catalogue voids are tens of megaparsecs. That is survey size, not this radius, and those walls are not hard now.

Putting 7 Mpc back in as the wall’s circle restores the retired hoop.

## 2. What the 7 Mpc radius was

From the 2 October summary, retired as substrate:

- metric \(S^1_\tau\), \(\tau\approx 7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) or \(7.5\,\mathrm{Mpc}\);
- axial stack of the cylinder \(S^1_\phi\times\mathbb{R}_s\);
- \(n_{\rm hier}=\log_7(\tau/\lambda_{313.1})\approx 45.8\);
- Casimir \(\rho_\Lambda=\hbar c/\tau^4\);
- gravitic node \(\max\mathrm{TW}^G\) at that radius.

Forbidden as an unbound fifth dimension (2026-09-14). Optional bound-lattice hook only. The 19 September Cartan note had also called \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) a kernel scale \(\tau_{\rm web}\) and, in the same breath, the circle. The live split is the two-sentence rule: catalogue or kernel scale is not a metric hoop. This session does not reopen the hoop.

Computed this morning: \(\hbar c/\tau^4\) at \(7\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) is short of \(\rho_\Lambda\) by \(3.93\times 10^{109}\). The radius was not doing the vacuum-density job even before it was retired.

## 3. What a conformal wall is

20 September void notes, still the standing description:

- A void is an underdense region in one connected 4-manifold. Light from the walls crosses it now. Typical catalogue sizes \(10\)–\(10^{2}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\). Survey data, not a theorem of F7.
- Isolation, when it happens, is the future event horizon \(r_{\rm eh}=c/H_\Lambda\), observer-dependent, not a sharp skin at the present void radius.
- \(\mathscr{I}^+\) of that patch is spacelike future infinity. Dimension 3. Not a circle.
- Walls carry the Weyl. Interior trends toward \(C\to 0\). Walls are not a Casimir boundary of circumference \(7\,\mathrm{Mpc}\).

The fractal-tree note: a void of a parent can mother a child while the parent’s walls still exist, out of future contact with that interior observer. The wall is still in the same 4-geometry. It is not the Tau circle renamed.
