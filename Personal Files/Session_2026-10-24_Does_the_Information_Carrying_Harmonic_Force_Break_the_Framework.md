# Session 2026-10-24: Does the Information-Carrying Harmonic Force Break the Framework?

**Framework**: Tau-Superblock (TSB / Superblock) — live 4D frame. Residual-L matches laboratory \(G\). UPS stamps and the harmonic are locked kinematic. Tau package hard-rejected.  
**Question**: Will the proposal break the framework or create mathematical or observational impossibilities?  
**Answer**: Does not core-break. Conflicts with the kinematic cut and adds free parameters. No immediate mathematical impossibility; observational problems only if the force is unscreened.

## Plain-language summary

No, it does not break the framework in the core sense. Residual-L matching to laboratory \(G\) can still be kept if the new force is screened or set to zero on laboratory scales. The Tau package stays rejected. The shared geometric cascade continues to exist.  

What it does do is conflict with the locked kinematic-only status of the UPS stamps and the harmonic. It adds at least one free coupling function that is not fixed by any locked operator. Mathematically the construction remains writable; there is no immediate inconsistency inside the existing equations. Observational impossibilities appear only if the force is strong enough, on the scales where we already have tight bounds, to produce non-local signaling, equivalence-principle violations, or residual effects that are not seen. Those problems are free-parameter problems, not structural collapses.
