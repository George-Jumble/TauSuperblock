## 2. Paper 1 — Cogitate (Nature 2025): IIT vs GNWT as two halves of one clockwork

### 2.1 What the experiment actually did

Preregistered adversarial collaboration, \(n=256\) humans, four stimulus categories (faces, objects, letters, false fonts), three orientations, three durations (\(0.5,1.0,1.5\,\mathrm{s}\)), task-relevant and task-irrelevant blocks. Modalities: fMRI (\(n=120\)), MEG (\(n=102\)), iEEG (\(n=34\)). Predictions locked in advance by Tononi / Koch (IIT) and Dehaene (GNWT) with a theory-neutral consortium.

### 2.2 What each theory needed

| Theory | Localisation of the NCC | Binding mechanism they needed to see |
|--------|-------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| IIT | Posterior hot zone (occipital + ventral temporal) | Sustained, content-specific *short-range* synchrony *inside* posterior cortex for the whole stimulus duration |
| GNWT | Prefrontal workspace ignition | Late all-or-none ignition, including an *offset* ignition when the content leaves the workspace; gamma-band broadcast PFC \(\leftrightarrow\) sensory |

### 2.3 What was found (query result)

1. Category information is decodable from visual, ventrotemporal, *and* inferior frontal cortex.
2. Orientation information is robust only in posterior cortex; adding PFC does not improve decoding.
3. Duration is tracked by *sustained* high-gamma in occipital and lateral temporal cortex, not by PFC.
4. PFC responses are transient onsets. There is no reliable offset ignition (Bayes factors favour the null against the GNWT offset model).
5. There is content-specific coupling between frontal and early visual areas, but it is brief and lives in alpha–beta, not in the predicted gamma band.
6. There is *no* sustained gamma synchrony inside posterior cortex. That is a direct hit on IIT’s claim that network connectivity *specifies* the experience for as long as the experience lasts.

Both theories take partial hits. IIT keeps posterior sufficiency for some content dimensions and loses the sustained-synchrony specification. GNWT keeps a brief frontal–sensory handshake and loses ignition-at-offset plus orientation-in-PFC.

### 2.4 TSB reading

Cogitate is an experiment about *where binding lives* and *how long the bound state is held*. TSB does not put those two jobs in two cortical lobes.

- **Holding a content for a duration** is a *bound* bounce: the rebound toward Absolute Future is stopped at Present for as long as the envelope remains occupied. The neural analogue of “stopped at Present” is a *sustained local high-gamma envelope* — exactly what Cogitate finds in posterior sensory cortex. That is residual \(L\), not a workspace token.
- **Making a content available to other modules** is a \(\Phi_7\) scrape: a brief, low-frequency, content-specific handshake across a phase wall. That is what Cogitate finds between inferior frontal and early visual areas. It is not a second ignition at offset; offset is \(\Phi_8\) reset, which *clears* the envelope rather than re-broadcasting it.
- **Absence of sustained posterior gamma synchrony** is expected if IIT’s “connectivity specifies experience” is read as a *standing* bound-state graph. In TSB the bound graph is the residual-\(L\) occupancy of nested envelopes; the *specification* of the experience is the \(\Phi_7\) scrape pattern across those envelopes, which is allowed to be sparse, brief, and not gamma. Gamma is a local clock of one envelope, not the inter-envelope word.
- **Absence of PFC offset ignition** is expected if GNWT’s “leaving the workspace” is read as a second broadcast. In TSB leaving is a reset, not a broadcast. Resets do not re-excite the workspace; they open the foot (see the 2026-09-20 neutrino braid: the odd one-set that must open the \(\Phi_7\) foot).

So Cogitate’s double negative — no sustained posterior gamma, no PFC offset ignition — is the signature of a *two-stroke* clock (scrape then reset) rather than of a failed localisation argument. The papers treat IIT and GNWT as competitors for one anatomical prize. TSB treats them as the bound stroke and the scrape stroke of one protocol.

## 3. Paper 2 — Nested Observer Windows (Neuroscience of Consciousness 2024): the hierarchy Cogitate does not supply

### 3.1 What NOW actually says (query result)

An *observer window* is a spatially and temporally bounded integrator. The mind is a nested mosaic: each tile is itself a mosaic. Three operational rules:

1. Cross-frequency coupling moves information *between* scales (vertical).
2. Zero-phase-lag synchrony defines the *interior* of one window (binding inside a tile).
3. Non-zero-phase-lag coherence moves information *across* windows at the same scale (lateral).

Unitary consciousness sits at the apex in the default presentation, but the paper explicitly leaves three options open:

- **Apex**: only the top window is conscious.
- **Emergent hierarchical**: every window above a threshold is conscious.
- **Panpsychic hierarchical**: every window is conscious; \(\Phi\)-ratios stay roughly constant across levels.

Binding-by-synchrony (Singer–Gray) is accepted as the intra-window glue. Peak frequency of that synchrony *is* the window’s processing speed and therefore its phenomenal now.

## 4. Paper 3 — Science cluster: the gate and the structure-lock

### 4.1 Fang et al. (Science 2025) — thalamic gate

Simultaneous human sEEG from thalamus and prefrontal cortex during conscious detection. Result: consciousness-related activity in the *intralaminar and medial* nuclei (imTha) is earlier and stronger than in ventral nuclei or PFC; directed information flow at onset is imTha \(\to\) PFC. The high-order thalamus *gates* the thalamofrontal loop.

**TSB reading.** A gate that licenses content into reportable Present is the neural analogue of the \(\Phi_7\to\Phi_8\) seam, not of a cortical workspace and not of a posterior hot zone. The Mirron boundary operator is the cosmological version of the same job: it decides whether a bounce is allowed to stop (bound, residual \(L\), a now) or must complete (unbound, light-like). imTha playing that role for perception is consistent with the 2024 Nature Communications result that *central-thalamus* stimulation restores hierarchical cortical organisation under anaesthesia. Hierarchy returns when the gate is driven. That is a scrape/reset restoration, not a new cortical theory.

GNWT can absorb this by putting the gate *under* the workspace. IIT can absorb it only by treating thalamus as an enabler of posterior \(\Phi\), which Cogitate already weakened. TSB absorbs it as the seam itself.

### 4.2 Demertzi et al. (Science Advances 2019) — dynamic coordination patterns

Conscious and unconscious brains visit a repertoire of coordination patterns. Two poles:

- **Pattern 1 (conscious-supporting)**: long-distance positive *and* negative correlations, high modularity, *low* similarity to anatomical connectivity.
- **Pattern 4 (unconscious-supporting)**: weak interregional coordination, low efficiency, *high* similarity to anatomical connectivity.

Loss of consciousness is not “less activity.” It is *collapse of the functional pattern onto the structural skeleton*. The same motif is in the 2024 Nature Communications macaque study (activity under anaesthesia “increasingly constrained by brain structure across scales”) and in the 2025 Communications Biology eigenmode paper (reduced consciousness \(\leftrightarrow\) *stabilized* cortical dynamics).

**TSB reading.** Structure-locked dynamics = bound sector with no residual exploration = TEP fully enforced and no scrape. The functional pattern that *departs* from anatomy is the unbound / scrape sector: completed or half-completed bounces that are not slaves of the existing envelope graph. Consciousness in this dictionary is the brain’s ability to keep running \(\Phi_7\) scrapes that are *not* already written as anatomy. Anaesthesia, sleep, and DoC are regimes in which only the already-bound graph still ticks.

This is the same contrast as bound EM vs unbound EM, lifted one biological level:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{bound / anatomy-locked} & \text{unbound / explorative}\\
\hline
\text{stopped rebound, residual }L & \text{completed P}\leftrightarrow\text{F bounce}\\
\text{Pattern 4} & \text{Pattern 1}\\

**Complete key.** “Anatomy-locked” means functional coupling statistically indistinguishable from the structural connectome. “Explorative” means functional coupling that visits anti-correlations and long-range modules the connectome does not dictate. Residual \(L\) still exists in both; what changes is whether new envelopes can be opened.
