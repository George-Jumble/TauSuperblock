## 2. What we have

Prime geometry remains **one time + three space**.

\(\mathcal{P}\to x\to\mathcal{F}\) is three **points** on a single causal line (origin, here, reset). That is a three-point function of one \(t\), not a metric with three time axes.

The photon is atemporal in proper time (\(\mathrm{d}\tau_\gamma=0\)). That is *fewer* clocks, not more.

Eight phases are a discrete word on \(\mathbb{O}\), not \(t_1,t_2,t_3\).

Six domains are six copies of 3+1, each with one \(t\). That is multiplicity of worlds, not 3D time in one world.
