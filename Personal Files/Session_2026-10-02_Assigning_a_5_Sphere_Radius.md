## 1. Answer

The construct does not produce a radius. The unit CR 5-sphere is defined by

\[
|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2+|z_3|^2=1,
\]

so its embedding radius is the pure number \(1\). A physical radius \(R\) is a rescaling one puts in by hand:

\[
S^5_R=\bigl\{z\in\mathbb{C}^3:\ |z|=R\bigr\},\qquad ds^2_R=R^2\,ds^2_{\mathrm{unit}}.
\]

Until \(R\) is chosen from somewhere else, \(S^5_R\) is the same incidence chart as \(S^5\). Choosing \(R\) does not by itself recover \(\rho_\Lambda\). The dark-energy refusal of this date stands.

## 2. Protocols, in the order they are allowed

### Protocol A — leave it dimensionless

Keep the unit sphere. Hopf phase \(\vartheta\) stays a redundancy. No length enters. This is the disposition already parked. No new postulate.

### Protocol B — borrow a length the live frame already uses

The only length locked to the inserted floor is the Compton length of that floor:

\[
\lambda_{m_0}=\frac{\hbar c}{\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}}.
\]

With \(\hbar c=197.3269718\,\mathrm{MeV\,fm}\) and the printed floor \(\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\),

\[
\lambda_{m_0}=0.630236\,\mathrm{fm}.
\]

Setting \(R:=\lambda_{m_0}\) is an assignment, not a derivation. It glues an incidence chart to the nucleon-third scale. It does not follow from the Hopf map. It must not be fed back to “derive” \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Domain-cross path length is already \(\lambda_{m_0}\), not \(\ell_P\); using it here adds nothing to that protocol.

The Planck length \(\ell_P=\sqrt{\hbar G/c^3}\) is available as a unit and is not preferred. Assigning \(R:=\ell_P\) is the same kind of postulate, one step further from the live floor.

### Protocol C — borrow a cosmological length as a label only

The Hubble length \(c/H_0\) is an observed scale, not an output of \(S^5\):

| \(H_0\) (\(\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}\)) | \(c/H_0\) (\(\mathrm{Mpc}\)) | \(c/H_0\) (\(\mathrm{Gly}\)) |
|---|---|---|
| \(67.4\) | \(4447.96\) | \(14.507\) |
| \(70.0\) | \(4282.75\) | \(13.968\) |
| \(73.0\) | \(4106.75\) | \(13.394\) |

Setting \(R:=c/H_0\) names the chart after an input. It does not explain \(H_0\). Writing \(\rho_\Lambda=\hbar c/R^4\) afterward is circular: the radius was taken from the expansion one was trying to recover.

### Protocol D — retired, not a protocol

\[
R:=\tau\approx 7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}
\]

is the withdrawn Kaluza–Klein circle. It is not assigned. With it stay retired: \(n_{\mathrm{hier}}\), \(G_5\), \(\Lambda_\Omega=7/R_\tau\), and \(\rho_\Lambda=\hbar c/\tau^4\).

### Protocol E — curvature radius of a 5D Einstein equation

A curvature radius is defined only after a metric and a field equation exist, for example a round Einstein 5-manifold with

\[
\mathrm{Ric}=\frac{4}{R^2}g.
\]

The live frame has no 5D Einstein equation and no product \(M_4\times S^5\). Opening one is a new postulate, not a step in this construct. Not adopted.

## 3. Fibre radius is not a second radius

On the round \(S^5_R\), the Hopf fibre is a great circle of radius \(R\). After the quotient, the Fubini–Study metric on \(\mathbb{CP}^2\) is fixed by the same \(R\), up to the conventional factor between the round metric and the quotient metric. There are not two independent radii to solve for. Quotienting removes \(\vartheta\); it does not measure it.

- \(\mathrm{Vol}(S^5_R)=R^5\pi^3\), and \(\pi^3\approx 31.006277\) stays a pure number. Volume is not \(\Omega_\Lambda\).
- No Kaluza–Klein tower is licensed. The phase remains redundancy unless a separate postulate says fields propagate on the fibre.
- Colour \(\mathrm{SU}(3)\), the five hidden domains, residual \(L\), and TEP are unchanged.
- The construct stays on the back burner.
