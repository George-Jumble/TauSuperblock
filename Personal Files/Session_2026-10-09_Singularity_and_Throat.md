## 0. Verdict

**Not in the Kerr chart.** The curvature singularity is the ring, the boundary of the disk. The disk center, the passage called the throat, is regular. A Superblock renaming can put the Mirron on that passage. It does not move the Kerr curvature blow-up.

---

## 1. Complete key

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ring | \(r=0\), \(\theta=\pi/2\). Kretschmann scalar diverges. The singularity. |
| throat | Passage through the disk \(r=0\), \(\theta\neq\pi/2\), into the \(r<0\) extension. Regular except at the ring. |
| \(\rho^2=r^2+a^2\cos^2\theta\) | Vanishes only on the ring. At the disk center \(\rho=a\), finite. |
| \(a\) | Spin parameter. Embedding radius of the ring. |
| Mirron | Boundary operator. Live locus is the seam, not required to sit on the chart zero. |

---

## 2. Chart fact

The Kerr curvature invariant diverges where \(\rho\to 0\). That is \(r=0\) and \(\cos\theta=0\) together. At \(r=0\), \(\theta=0\), \(\rho=a\neq 0\) for a rotating hole. The center is the regular passage. The singularity is the edge of that passage, the ring, intrinsic dimension \(1\), sitting in the equatorial plane.

“Throat” in the extension means that passage. Putting the singularity in the throat identifies the regular point with its boundary.

---

## 3. What can still be said

The live physical locus is the apparent seam, not the chart zero. A Mirron can be read as acting at the passage without moving \(\rho=0\). That is a renaming of the engine. It is not a relocation of the Kerr singularity. \(a=0\) collapses the ring to the Schwarzschild set, which is the idealization already marked as measure zero.
