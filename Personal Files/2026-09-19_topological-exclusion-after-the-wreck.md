# How today’s wreck affects the topological exclusion principle

**Session date:** 19 September 2026  
**Question:** What is left of the topological exclusion principle (TEP) after the metric Tau circle, ordinary KK Casimir, and “π/2 kills CTCs” claims are refused?  
**Short answer:** TEP as a *Superblock selection rule* can stay. TEP as a theorem about windings on a 10 Mpc metric \(S^1\) cannot. The old demand is unchanged: exclusion is real only when curvature, geodesics, and causality are actually controlled.

## 1. What TEP was being asked to do

In the Superblock notes TEP is not one theorem. It is a bundle of jobs:

- keep the PK / Planck–Kerr engine from forming a singularity at the refresh,
- keep the same engine from being a closed timelike curve,
- keep domains from occupying the same phase slot,
- keep non-resonant modes from propagating (confinement-like language),
- in merger drafts, lean on Tau-cylinder windings for strong-CP and “exclusion-enforced bounces.”

The morning review already split the last inch of that bundle:

> A \(\pi/2\) phase rotation does not, by itself, kill CTCs.  
> “Topological exclusion prevents singularities” requires finite curvature invariants and extendible causal geodesics.

Today’s wreck does not add a new objection. It removes one *place* TEP was allowed to live.

---

## 2. What TEP loses

Anything that treated exclusion as a fact about a compact metric circle of radius \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\):

- windings \(n\in\mathbb{Z}\) around that \(S^1\) as the reason strong-CP is solved,
- Casimir mode sums on that cylinder as the reason a floor exists and a singularity cannot form,
- “the circle has no point at \(r=0\)” as a 4D regularity proof,
- KK harmonic absence as proof that singular configurations are missing,
- Gatlin-style non-computable / halting subgraphs on that \(S^1\) as the exclusion mechanism.

If gravity does not wrap that circle, those sentences are about an object TSB no longer uses. They do not transfer by vocabulary.

The Tau-cylinder *formalization* of TEP is therefore off the TSB ledger until someone rewrites it on internal phase space. Gatlin’s circle can keep its own TEP, on his ledger, with the same lab-gravity and Casimir problems already listed.

---

## 3. What TEP can still be

A Superblock-native TEP is a statement about *which configurations of the internal geometry are allowed*.

Allowed homes, none of them a 10 Mpc spacetime circle:

**A. Phase exclusion.**  
The discrete clock (7-fold / 8-phase / CT identification) has no slot for a configuration that would be a 4D singularity or a closed causal loop. Two domains cannot share a phase address. A photon cannot pull back across \(e_i\). That is the same constitutive idea as “no shared \(U(1)\).”

**B. Bounce exclusion.**  
The PK / Mirron refresh is a map

\[
(g,K,\Psi)_-
\;\longrightarrow\;
(g,K,\Psi)_+
\]

that never hits a set \(\Sigma_{\rm bounce}\) on which an invariant \(I\) diverges. TEP is the claim that \(\Sigma_{\rm bounce}\) is empty in the internal space, so the 4D metric never needs a point with \(I=\infty\).

**C. Causal exclusion.**  
There exists a function \(t(x)\) on each domain, and on the embedding, with \(\nabla t\) timelike, so the PK engine’s “half-cycle” is a covering of a line, not a loop in spacetime. Phase may be periodic. Spacetime need not be.

Those three are still available. They are also still *postulates* until the maps are written.

---

## 4. What TEP is not allowed to mean after today

- Not “gravity is regular because extra-dimensional Gauss’s law smears the source over 10 Mpc.”
- Not “CTCs die because \(\Delta\phi=\pi/2\).”
- Not “singularities are excluded because Casimir pressure cannot die on the circle.”
- Not “confinement is exclusion of non-KK modes of \(\tau\).”
- Not a substitute for Israel–Stewart relaxation, for \(\mathcal K_{ij}\), or for \(B_F\).

Exclusion is a restriction on the configuration space. It is not a stress tensor, not a constitutive prefactor, and not a survey line at \(k=1/7\).

---

## 5. The bar that has not moved

To keep TEP as physics rather than a name, three results are still required. They were required this morning. They are required now.

**Regularity**

\[
I[g^{(i)}(x)]<\infty
\quad\text{for all }x\text{ in the domain},
\]

including through a refresh. If the proof only shows that an internal phase never takes a forbidden integer, that is not yet a statement about \(I\).

**Geodesic completeness (or controlled incompleteness)**

Causal geodesics \(\gamma\) either extend through the would-be bounce or end on a boundary whose matching conditions are specified. “Excluded” is not an ending.

**Stable causality**

A global time function on the 4D domain, not only orthogonality in phase space. Periodic \(\phi\) plus a time function \(t\) is allowed. Compact time is not.

Until those three exist, TEP is an axiom of the PK engine and of domain addressing. That is a legitimate axiom. It is not a derived no-singularity theorem, and it is not rescued or destroyed by Gatlin’s circle.

## 6. Practical effect on the research program

| Use of TEP | After today |
|---|---|
| Domain addressing / no shared Maxwell slot | Keep, constitutive |
| PK refresh does not hit \(I=\infty\) | Keep as target; prove on \(g^{(i)}\) |
| CTC control | Keep as target; prove a time function |
| Strong-CP via Tau windings | Drop from TSB, or re-derive without the circle |
| Bounce “because of the cylinder” | Drop |
| Warp / exclusion-enforced bubbles from the cylinder | Drop until a 4D kernel version exists |
| Confinement as non-resonant exclusion in the Mirron cell | Keep as a *cell* hypothesis, not a KK hypothesis |

TEP shrinks to a Superblock selection rule on phases, embeddings, and the refresh map. That is still a principle. It is no longer a 5D theorem. The unification does not stand or fall with it today; the kernel paper and the cell paper do not need a finished TEP. The bounce / aeon paper does.
