## Today they are not disconnected

A void is an underdense region in one connected 4-manifold. Light from the walls crosses the interior. Typical voids are \(10\)–\(10^2\,\mathrm{Mpc}\). The present particle horizon is thousands of megaparsecs. The void is inside our past light cone and we are inside its. Catalogue “disconnection” at \(\sim 7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) was conformal-exhaustion language, not a horizon cut.

**Key.** Particle horizon: how far light has travelled since the bang. Event horizon: how far light can ever travel in the future. Voids sit well inside the present particle horizon.

---

## What does isolate them: the future event horizon

Once the interior is \(\Lambda\)-dominated,

\[
r_{\rm eh}=\frac{c}{H_\Lambda}=c\sqrt{\frac{3}{\Lambda}}.
\]

An observer at the void centre cannot receive signals emitted today from events that remain outside that sphere. Walls that comoving-recede beyond \(r_{\rm eh}\) fall out of *future* causal contact. That is de Sitter isolation. It is not a sharp skin at the present void radius.

This is the same geometry as Penrose’s spacelike \(\mathscr{I}^+\). The rehearsal picture gets stronger, not weaker: the interior is preparing a private future boundary. The walls are still in the same spacetime; they are sliding off that observer’s event horizon.

**Key.** \(r_{\rm eh}\): cosmological event-horizon radius. Isolation is in the future, observer-dependent, and set by \(\Lambda\), not by F7.

## What this does not license

- Treating a void as another aeon or another bang.
- Firing the Mirron at void maturity.
- A derived \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) causal cutoff.
- Hidden domains as the meaning of “disconnected.” Those copies share the Prime chart; their causal story is the kernel, not a void wall.

Voids are causally connected now and can become future-isolated de Sitter patches; that isolation is \(\mathscr{I}^+_{\rm void}\) for an interior observer, while the walls remain in the same 4-geometry until global rest-mass fade.
