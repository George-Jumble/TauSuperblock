## 0. Object under audit

The 5D Riemann-sphere analogue is the CR 5-sphere

\[
S^5=\bigl\{(z_1,z_2,z_3)\in\mathbb{C}^3:\ |z_1|^2+|z_2|^2+|z_3|^2=1\bigr\},
\]

total space of the Hopf fibration

\[
S^1\longrightarrow S^5\xrightarrow{\pi}\mathbb{CP}^2,\qquad \pi(z)=\mathbb{C}\cdot z.
\]

It contains the ordinary Riemann sphere as the coded sub-fibration over a line \(\mathbb{CP}^1\subset\mathbb{CP}^2\):

\[
S^1\longrightarrow S^3\longrightarrow S^2\simeq\mathbb{CP}^1.
\]

Held fixed from the predecessor and from the live frame: this is incidence geometry. It is not a metric bulk, not a Kaluza–Klein circle, not a source of \(\rho_\Lambda\), and not a derivation of \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\). The Hopf phase is not \(\tau\).

## 1. Verdict

The implications are bookkeeping implications. The 5D sphere organises families of ordinary Riemann spheres (skies of events, null-ray labels). It does not add a direction in which a field propagates, does not gravitate, does not pay or waive TEP, and does not count the six domains.

One external coincidence is real and is refused as an identification: the CR automorphism group of \(S^5\subset\mathbb{C}^3\) contains \(\mathrm{SU}(3)\). That is not colour \(\mathrm{SU}(3)\).

## 2. Implications that do follow

### 2.1 Events stay 4-dimensional; the sky of one event stays \(S^2\)

A point of the external arena determines a Riemann sphere of null directions. In the Hopf picture that sphere is a complex line in the base \(\mathbb{CP}^2\), pulled back to an \(S^3\) in \(S^5\). Raising the construction from \(S^3\to S^2\) to \(S^5\to\mathbb{CP}^2\) enlarges the family of skies. It does not enlarge the event manifold.

Consequence for the lock “unbound is the photon conformal sphere only”: the lock is unchanged. The unbound completed bounce is still charted on \(S^2\). \(S^5\) is the family chart, not a second unbound sector.

### 2.2 The fifth coordinate is redundancy, so it has no spectrum

Along the fibre, \(z\sim e^{i\vartheta}z\) is the same point of \(\mathbb{CP}^2\). Any quantity read off the analogue must be invariant under

\[
\vartheta\mapsto\vartheta+\alpha,\qquad\alpha\in\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}.
\]

Consequence: there is no Kaluza–Klein tower \(p_\vartheta=n\hbar/R\) to write down, because no radius \(R\) has been introduced and the phase is quotiented out. The retired compacton mass \(1/\tau\) is not hiding in this fibre.

### 2.3 It matches the null-ray space already latent, locally as CR data

The space \(N(\mathbb{R}^{1,3})\) of unparametrized null rays is five-real-dimensional. In twistor language the projective null locus

\[
\mathrm{PN}=\bigl\{Z\in\mathbb{CP}^3:\ Z\cdot\bar Z=0\bigr\}
\]

is a five-real-dimensional CR manifold, and the flat model of that CR structure is the CR 5-sphere (equivalently the Heisenberg group of CR dimension 2). Implication, kept local: \(S^5\) may be used as the flat model chart of ray space. Global identification \(S^5=\mathrm{PN}\) is not claimed. \(\mathbb{CP}^3\) remains complex dimension 3, real dimension 6; the fifth dimension is the null slice, not projective twistor space itself.

A spacetime event still corresponds to a \(\mathbb{CP}^1\) of twistors, i.e. an ordinary Riemann sphere. That is the same statement as §2.1.

### 2.4 CR dimension 2 is the screen, not a new axis

A null ray has a two-real-dimensional screen space (the Sachs screen). CR dimension 2 is that screen, complexified. The dimension count on \(S^5\) is therefore: screen (2) + celestial directions of the model sphere, organised so that the Hopf phase is quotiented when one passes to \(\mathbb{CP}^2\). Nothing in the count is a propagation axis orthogonal to \(\mathbb{R}^{1,3}\).

### 2.5 Bound sector is untouched, so gravity is untouched

Residual-\(L\) gravity is the angular momentum left when a bound rebound is stopped at the Present. An unbound completed bounce between Absolute Past and Absolute Future leaves no residual \(L\) and pays no TEP cost. The 5D analogue charts unbound ray data. Implication: it does not source effective \(G\), does not shift the dominant-glob rule, and does not alter the degree-of-constraint reading (constraint = mass/gravity of the bound glob).

### 2.6 Phase 8 and the Mirron group are not replaced

The Mirron remains in \(\mathrm{Conf}(S^3)\simeq\mathrm{SO}(4,1)\). \(S^5\) is not \(S^3\). \(\mathrm{SO}(4,1)\) is still the linear model of that conformal group, not a license to propagate in \(\mathbb{R}^{1,4}\). The algebraic pinch \(e_k^2\to\Phi_8\) remains a 4D phase-8 twistor reflection. Implication: the 5D sphere does not become the reset seam and does not become the Mirron world-tube \(\Sigma\).

### 2.7 Domain cross is not a geodesic on \(S^5\)

The 2026-09-29 protocol stands:

\[
E_\times(a\to b)=\theta_{ab}\,\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}.
\]

Adjacent octahedron hop \(\theta=\pi/2\) gives \(491.816\,\mathrm{MeV}\) at the printed floor; antipodal \(\theta=\pi\) gives \(983.633\,\mathrm{MeV}\); unbound light has \(E_\times=0\). Those angles live on the 4D octahedron wall \(W_{ab}\). They are not central angles of the round \(S^5\). Moving the protocol onto \(S^5\) would be a new postulate.

### 2.8 Topology does not supply a 4D instanton

\[
\pi_1(S^5)=\pi_2(S^5)=\pi_3(S^5)=\pi_4(S^5)=0,\qquad\pi_5(S^5)=\mathbb{Z}.
\]

Maps from a 4-dimensional event space into \(S^5\) are null-homotopic. There is no instanton charge in \(\pi_3\) of the kind carried by \(S^3\), and no new winding to identify with baryon number, with the Mirron, or with the phase-8 reset. The Hopf projection itself is a generator of a higher homotopy of the base, not a 4D topological charge available to the live frame.

## 3. Implications that do not follow

1. No metric product \(M_4\times S^5\), \(M_4\times S^1\), or \(M_4\times\mathbb{R}_5\).
2. No restoration of \(\tau\approx 7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\), \(n_{\mathrm{hier}}\), \(G_5\), \(\Lambda_\Omega=7/R_\tau\), \(f_{\mathrm{void}}=127/343\), or Scale Reflection.
3. No dark energy. Finite volume of the unit \(S^5\) is a dimensionless normalisation. Writing \(\rho_\Lambda=\hbar c/R^4\) requires a radius the construction does not have. Forbidden mode stays forbidden.
4. No new value and no new derivation of \(\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Floor remains the inserted nucleon-third protocol.
5. The integer 5 does not identify the five hidden domains with five coordinates. Domains are co-located copies on internal \(S^3\), seen in Prime through the averaged kernel. \(\dim_{\mathbb{R}}S^5=5\) is a count of incidence labels.
6. \(\mathbb{CP}^2\) does not count domains either. The Grassmannian of lines in \(\mathbb{CP}^2\) is dual \(\mathbb{CP}^2\), real dimension 4, not 6.
7. Division-algebra ladder is unchanged: \(S^2,S^4,S^8\). Five remains off that ladder.
8. Shadow ratio \(5.401514\) under ansatz A is not a volume ratio of \(S^5\), not a Hopf invariant, and not re-derived here.
9. Dynamic Refresh scope is unchanged: binding floor only (quarks, gluons, colour, emergent EM flux). \(S^5\) is not a refresh of higher globs and not a localizer of an isolated centre of mass.

## 4. The temptation, refused as a lock

\(\mathrm{SU}(3)\) acts on \(\mathbb{C}^3\) and preserves \(S^5\). The CR automorphism group of the 5-sphere therefore contains \(\mathrm{SU}(3)\). Colour in the live frame is the bound-sector force among the six-phase cloud, with associator locking \(3/4\) in a three-quark configuration, and with domain-cross cost \(E_\times\) on the octahedron wall.

These are not the same \(\mathrm{SU}(3)\).

- Hopf \(\mathrm{SU}(3)\) rotates the homogeneous coordinates \((z_1,z_2,z_3)\) of an incidence chart.
- Colour \(\mathrm{SU}(3)\) acts on the bound colour line that pays TEP when forced through a reset overlap.

Sliding them together would smuggle a derivation of colour out of a choice of model sphere. Analogue only, if mentioned at all. Not a lock. The same refusal applies to reading the three complex coordinates as the three valence lines of the nucleon, and to reading the Hopf phase as electric charge.

## 5. What may be done with it

Permitted, non-load-bearing:

- Keep unbound photons on \(S^2\simeq\mathbb{CP}^1\).
- Use \(S^5\to\mathbb{CP}^2\) when a family of those spheres must be written in one chart, with \(\vartheta\) quotiented.
- Use the CR 5-sphere as the flat model of \(N(\mathbb{R}^{1,3})\) or of \(\mathrm{PN}\), without the global identification.
- Leave the Mirron in \(\mathrm{Conf}(S^3)\), the floor at the printed \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\), and domain cross on \(W_{ab}\).

Not permitted by this audit: any sentence in which a measured mass, a branching ratio, \(\Omega_\Lambda\), or \(\Omega_{\mathrm{DM}}/\Omega_b\) is computed from \(\mathrm{Vol}(S^5)\) or from a winding on \(S^5\).

## 7. One-line answer

The 5D Riemann sphere implies a chart for families of ordinary celestial spheres and a flat model for null-ray space; it implies nothing about propagation, gravity, the mass floor, dark energy, colour, or the count of domains.
