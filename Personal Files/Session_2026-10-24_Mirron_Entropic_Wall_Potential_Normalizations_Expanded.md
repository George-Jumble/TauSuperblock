## 1. Power-law dependence on the empty-space fraction

\[
V_{\rm wall}(x)
\;=\;
V_0
\Bigl(
\frac{\rho_{\rm empty}(x)}{\rho_{\rm empty}(x)+\rho_{\rm bound}(x)}
\Bigr)^{p}
\]

**Analytical.** \(p\) is a free exponent. \(p=1\) recovers linear weighting; \(p>1\) suppresses the potential inside partially bound regions more strongly; \(p<1\) allows a longer tail. \(V_0\) is fixed by matching observed acceleration, not by locked structure.

**Plain language.** The wall push grows only where space is emptier. A higher power keeps the push near true voids and kills it quickly as soon as bound matter appears; a lower power lets a weaker push leak into half-empty regions. Overall strength is still chosen to fit the data.

## 2. Exponential screening set by local bound density

\[
V_{\rm wall}(x)
\;=\;
V_0
\exp\Bigl(
-\frac{\rho_{\rm bound}(x)}{\rho_*}
\Bigr)
\]

**Analytical.** Maximal in pure voids; exponentially killed once local bound density exceeds the free scale \(\rho_*\). Screening length independent of the dominant-glob rule.

**Plain language.** Inside galaxies or clusters the entropic potential switches off exponentially fast. In great voids it stays at full strength. How quickly it switches off is a free choice and does not change laboratory gravity.

## 3. Hierarchical-depth modulation

\[
V_{\rm wall}^{(g)}
\;=\;
V_0
\cdot
f_{\rm empty}
\cdot
\bigl(1+\gamma\,g\bigr)^{-q}
\]

**Analytical.** Larger (deeper) globs suppress the potential by a power of generation index. Effect negligible on Solar-System/galactic scales; free to operate on largest voids. No new operator on residual-\(L\).

**Plain language.** The bigger the bound structure you are inside, the weaker the void-push. Two free numbers set how rapidly the weakening occurs with size. Laboratory tests continue to see only ordinary residual-\(L\) gravity.

## 4. Unbound-energy reservoir normalization

\[
V_{\rm wall}
\;=\;
\alpha_{\rm ent}
\int_{\rm unbound}
E_{\rm completed\,bounce}\,dV
\]

**Analytical.** Proportional to total energy in completed null bounces and unbound Mirron segments. Reservoir not evaluated by locked structure, so \(\alpha_{\rm ent}\) is a fit parameter. Respects bound/unbound split.

**Plain language.** Finished light-like bounces pool their energy at the causal edge. A free fraction of that pool acts as the void-attracted push. The fraction is chosen to match cosmology; it is not calculated from the floor or laboratory gravity.

## 5. Light-cone volume dilution

\[
V_{\rm wall}(\mathcal{L})

V_0

\frac{1}{1+\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{L})/\ell_{\rm wall}^3}
