# Session 2026-10-24: Explanation of the Z2 Index and Domain Partners

**Date**: 2026-10-24  
**Framework**: Tau-Superblock (TSB / Superblock) — live 4D frame. Six co-located domains, antipodal matter/antimatter pairs, domain-cross energy \(E_\times=\theta\Delta_{\rm Tav}\) (antipodal \(\theta=\pi\)). 8-phase G2 clockwork locked. Residual-L matches laboratory \(G\).  
**Context**: Clarification of the non-locking 16-phase cover suggested as a doubling of the 8-phase clock.  
**Status**: Explanatory. The 16-phase remains non-locking.

## Plain-language summary

A \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) index is simply a binary label, +1 or −1, that can be attached to each of the eight G2 slots. Attaching it doubles the number of distinct phase stamps from 8 to 16 without changing the underlying geometry.  

Domain partners are the antipodal pairs among the six co-located domains. Each domain has one partner that sits opposite it (matter versus antimatter). Crossing to the partner costs the antipodal domain-cross energy \(\pi\Delta_{\rm Tav}\). The \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) index can be used to keep track of which member of such a pair a given stamp belongs to, or to keep track of bound versus unbound sectors, while everything remains inside the same single 4D manifold and the same shared geometric cascade.

## Analytical explanation

**Key to symbols**  
- \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) index: discrete binary label \(\varepsilon=\pm 1\).  
- Domain partner: the antipodal co-located domain \(D_{\bar a}\) of a given domain \(D_a\).  
- Antipodal domain-cross energy: \(E_\times^{\rm ant}=\pi\Delta_{\rm Tav}\).  
- 8-phase G2 slot: one of the eight discrete phases of the locked clockwork.  

The 16-phase cover is obtained by the product  
\[
\{\text{G2 slots}\}\times\mathbb{Z}_2.
\]  
One natural assignment of the \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) factor is  
\[
\varepsilon=\begin{cases}
+1 & \text{domain}\\
-1 & \text{antipodal partner}
\end{cases}
\]  
or, equivalently,  
\[
\varepsilon=\begin{cases}
+1 & \text{bound sector}\\
-1 & \text{unbound sector}.
\end{cases}
\]  
In either reading the geometric cascade, residual-L accounting, and the locked 8-phase advance remain unchanged. The extra index is only bookkeeping.

Domain partners themselves are already present in the live frame: the six domains form three antipodal pairs, and the energy cost of an antipodal hop is the recorded value \(\pi\Delta_{\rm Tav}\). The \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) index merely labels which member of a pair is being stamped.
