\[

a^{-4},

Distance to the wall shrinks as the shell approaches the conformal boundary:
\[

=
D_0
\left(1-\frac{a}{a_{\rm wall}}\right)

D_{\rm wall}
\propto

**Variable key:**

- \(n\): free dispersion index.  
- \(D_0\): reference distance.

Force therefore falls both because \(D_{\rm wall}\) decreases and because \(f_{\rm dispersion}\) decreases.

3\,\frac{\ddot{a}}{a}
\quad\text{(late-time, matter-free limit)}.
\]
Substituting the force as a pressure-like or acceleration term requires an auxiliary conversion (energy density of the shell \(\to\) acceleration of the metric). The simplest phenomenological bridge is
\[
\Lambda_{\rm eff}(a)
=
\beta\,
\frac{|\mathbf{F}_{\rm ent}(a)|}{E_{\rm shell}}
=
\beta\,\alpha\,
\frac{D_{\rm wall}(a)\,f_{\rm dispersion}(a)}{E_{\rm shell}},

where \(\beta\) is another free constant of proportionality and \(E_{\rm shell}\) is the total unbound energy on the shell.

**Variable key:**

- \(\beta\): free conversion constant.  
- \(E_{\rm shell}\): total energy of the unbound photonic shell (unevaluated by locks).
