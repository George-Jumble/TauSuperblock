# Session 2026-09-14 — Absorption: photon becomes bound; re-emission is a lower-energy unbound photon

**Statement:** If a photon is absorbed (not reflected) it is bound. Energy exchange requires the bound mass to emit a lower-energy photon.

---

## 1. Complete symbol key

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| \(E_\gamma\) | Energy of the incoming unbound photon |
| \(E_{\gamma'}\) | Energy of the outgoing unbound photon |
| \(\Delta E_B\) | Energy kept by the bound system |
| \(L\) | Residual angular momentum of a stopped bounce |
| \(K(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{F})\) | Completed (unbound) generator |
| \(K(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{N})\) | Generator stopped at Present |
| \(\Phi_7\) | Scrape / mirror letter (reflection channel if elastic) |
| \(m_B\) | Inertial mass of the bound glob after the deposit |

---

## 2. Two channels

**Reflection.** The generator is not stopped. It stays \(K(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{F})\). \(L=0\) on the photon. Frequency can change kinematically (moving mirror) but the photon never becomes part of the mass.

**Absorption.** The generator is stopped at Present on a bound glob:

\[
K(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{F})
\;\longrightarrow\;
K(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{N}).
\]

**Key:** that is “becomes bound.” Residual \(L\) is written on the glob. That deposit *is* inertia:

\[
\Delta E_B
=
c^2\Delta m_B
=
\text{energy stored as residual }L.
\]

The process is not finished as a closed energy account unless the unbound remainder leaves:

\[
E_\gamma
=
\Delta E_B
+
E_{\gamma'}.
\]

**Key:** \(E_{\gamma'}\le E_\gamma\). Equality only if \(\Delta E_B=0\) (no absorption; that is reflection / elastic scatter).

So: absorption \(\Rightarrow\) bound deposit \(\Rightarrow\) if anything is still unbound, it is a **lower-energy** photon. Heat, fluorescence, Compton recoil, and later thermal emission are the same split at different delays.

---

## 3. What this is not

This is **local** bound bookkeeping. It does not set \(t_{00}\) and does not compute \(\rho_\Lambda\).

- Completed photons that never hit a glob stay on \(\mathscr{I}\) and belong to \(V_\infty\).  
- Absorbed photons leave \(V_\infty\) and feed \(m_B\).  
- The cosmological leftover fraction \(t_{00}\) is still the measure of modes that were *never* stopped. Absorption explains how a mode *exits* that set. It does not give the size of the set.

---

## 4. Clockwork reading

\(\Phi_7=C\) as a *mirror* is the reflection channel. Absorption is \(\Phi_7\) plus a stop: the scrape writes \(L\) instead of returning the same generator. \(\Phi_8\) then ticks; the emitted \(\gamma'\) is a new unbound word with smaller \(E\).
