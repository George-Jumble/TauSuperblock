# Session 2026-09-14 — Why the 313.1 MeV floor became unpalatable to the observables

**Question:** What in the theory changed so that \(\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) no longer sits cleanly on data?

**Answer in one line:** the floor is still usable as **quark dressing / stopped-gluon inertia**. It became unpalatable when it was asked to be a **glueball, a survey-fiber KK mode, and a derived output of \(\tau\)** at the same time — after this thread split those objects.

## 2. What the floor was originally tied to

Early TSB used one number for several jobs:

- topological exclusion at \(\Phi_7\) / PK-Mirron scrape  
- constituent-quark scale (\(\sim 300\)–\(350\,\mathrm{MeV}\))  
- “glueball”  
- dimuon analysis anchor \(M_0=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\)  
- lower end of \(n_{\mathrm{hier}}\) against \(\tau\sim 10\,\mathrm{Mpc}\)

Only the first two are the same kind of object (an IR dressing energy). The rest are different observables.

## 3. Theory changes in this thread that broke the map

| Change | Effect on 313.1 MeV |
|--------|---------------------|
| Dropped \(R_5(t)\) | No radion that runs from \(\ell_{\mathrm{Pl}}\) through QCD to \(\tau\). Cannot slide the same circle from MeV to Mpc. |

Nothing abolished the geometric sentence “stopped bounce has a smallest deposit.” What died is the claim that **one** number is simultaneously the glueball, the KK mode of \(\tau\), and a peak in current catalogs.

## 4. Observables, one by one

**PDG / spectroscopy.**  
No established elementary or glueball line at \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Neighbours: \(\pi^0\) \(135\), \(\pi^\pm\) \(140\), \(K\) \(494\), \(\eta\) \(548\), \(\rho\) \(775\). A new \(0^{++}\) at 313 MeV is unpalatable because the glueball window is already occupied near \(1.6\,\mathrm{GeV}\).

**Constituent quarks.**  
Still palatable: \(m_{u,d}^{\mathrm{const}}\sim 300\)–\(350\,\mathrm{MeV}\) is the energy a quark carries in a hadron, mostly gluon dressing. That is \(E_g=\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}\) transmitted to the quark. It is a model scale, not a narrow resonance.

**Lattice glueball.**  
Unpalatable as \(M_{0^{++}}=\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}\). Palatable as \(M_{0^{++}}\simeq 5E_g\) if one allows that bookkeeping.

**ATLAS/CMS exploratory 7-fold.**  
Engine scores were capped exploratory tools. Trigger-aware nulls account for residue-2 under dimuon selection. Those scans do **not** establish a 313.1 MeV particle. Using \(M_0\) as a ruler is not an observation of the floor.

**Large-scale structure.**  
\(\tau\) comb is a different observable. Linking it to 313 MeV only through \(n_{\mathrm{hier}}\) does not make 313 MeV appear in \(P(k)\).

## 5. What did *not* change

\(\Phi_7\) scrape, PK-Mirron, residual \(L\), and “gravity from stopped rebound” are still the geometric language. The floor can stay as

\[
E_g
=
\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}
=
E_{q\leftarrow g}
=
313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}
\]

**Key:** inertial gluon quantum / quark dressing only.

It becomes unpalatable the moment it is also required to be \(M_{0^{++}}\), \(\hbar c/\tau\), or a Clay output of an index that does not produce MeV.

---

## 6. Practical rule

Keep one observable role for the number: **IR dressing**.  
Retire three roles: glueball mass, survey KK quantum, derived child of \(\tau\).

If a later \(\Phi_7\) matrix produces a dimensionless \(\hat\Delta\) times a named \(E_{\mathrm{PK}}\), compare to \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) after the fact. Until then the floor is an input with one phenomenological home (constituent inertia), not a mismatch with every catalog at once.
