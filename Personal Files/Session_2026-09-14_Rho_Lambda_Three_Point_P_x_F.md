## 2. What “bound by three points” means

Not a box with three walls. A **completed generator** is a chain

\[
\mathcal{P}
\;\longrightarrow\;
x
\;\longrightarrow\;
\mathcal{F}.
\]

**Key:** origin; where we stand; reset collapse.

If the chain is broken at \(x\) (absorption), that generator leaves \(V_\infty\) and feeds \(\Delta E_B\). If the chain stays intact, it belongs to the vacuum account.

So \(\rho_\Lambda\) at \(x\) is the energy of all intact chains through \(x\):

\[
\rho_\Lambda(x)
=
T_{00}(\mathcal{P},x,\mathcal{F})
=
U^4\,
t_{00}(\mathcal{P},x,\mathcal{F}).
\]

**Key:** same factorization as before; the regulator is now *named*: only generators with both ends \(\mathcal{P}\) and \(\mathcal{F}\) and an interior point \(x\).

## 3. How the three loci sit on the photon envelope

- \(\mathcal{P}\): packed origin; all \(\mathcal{R}\) overlapping; start of \(f_+\) on the conformal sphere.  
- \(x\): Prime slice; \(\mathcal{R}_x\) is the sky at that event; \(t_x\) runs from \(\alpha_{\mathrm{res}}\) toward \(\Omega_{\mathrm{res}}\).  
- \(\mathcal{F}\): reset boundary; expanded waveform collapses; \(\mathscr{I}\) of this expansion event.

The open circle is still \(\bigcup_t\mathcal{R}_{\gamma(t)}\). \(\rho_\Lambda\) is not that union. It is the **stress of the union evaluated at \(x\)**, counting only chains that also reach \(\mathcal{F}\).

## 4. What this does for \(t_{00}\)

The missing measure is no longer “some contour on the sky.” It is the three-point rule:

\[
t_{00}(\mathcal{P},x,\mathcal{F})
=
N\bigl[f_+\big|_{\gamma_{\mathcal{P}x\mathcal{F}}}\bigr].
\]

**Key:** \(N\) = dimensionless norm of \(f_+\) restricted to completed chains through \(x\).

If \(N\) depends on \(x\) only through the existence of the chain (same for every \(x\) between \(\alpha_{\mathrm{res}}\) and \(\Omega_{\mathrm{res}}\)), then \(\rho_\Lambda\) is constant on the expansion event and \(w=-1\).

If \(N\) runs with \(t_x\) (how far the waveform has opened), \(w\neq-1\).

That is a sharp test of the three-point idea: constancy in \(t_x\) or not.

## 5. What this does not do

It does not output \(5.71\times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}\). It names the regulator: completed \(\mathcal{P}\)-\(x\)-\(\mathcal{F}\) chains only. Computing \(N\) is still the remaining evaluation. Absorption (previous note) is the process that *removes* a chain from this set.
