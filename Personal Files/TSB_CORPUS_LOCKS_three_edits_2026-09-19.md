## Lock 1 — Names of lengths

This session already used \(\ell_C\) for the **web coherence** of the 4D kernel \(\mathcal{K}_{ij}\). That is a megaparsec-class length.

The Compton length of the mother mass is a femtometre. It is not \(\ell_C\).

\[
\lambda_{m_0}
=
\frac{\hbar}{m_0 c}
=
\frac{\hbar c}{m_0 c^{2}}
\approx
0.6302\,\mathrm{fm},
\qquad
m_0=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]

**Key.** \(\lambda_{m_0}\): Compton wavelength of \(m_0\). \(m_0\): infrared mass-gap anchor, \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\). \(\hbar\): reduced Planck constant. \(c\): speed of light in the Prime chart. \(\ell_C\): web-coherence length of \(\mathcal{K}_{ij}\) (Mpc-class). \(\tau_{\rm web}\): catalogue / kernel IR scale, \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\). Do not write \(\ell_C=\hbar/(m_0 c)\).

Retired symbol in this corpus: \(\ell_C\) as Compton.

## Lock 2 — “Circle” is phase, not metric \(S^1\) as IR pole of \(K\)

\(K\) (also written \(\mathcal{K}_{ij}\)) is a **4D averaging kernel**. Its infrared end is an IR pole or an IR scale. It is not a fifth-dimensional metric circle.

Say **IR pole of \(K\)** or **IR scale \(\tau_{\rm web}\)**.

Say **circle** only for an internal phase with \(\varphi\sim\varphi+2\pi\).

The sentence that is out:

> “The circle is now the IR pole of \(K\).”

That sentence puts a metric \(S^1\) back in by vocabulary.

The sentences that replace it:

> The IR scale of the 4D kernel \(K\) is \(\tau_{\rm web}\).  
> Internal domain locks are phases \(\varphi_i\sim\varphi_i+2\pi\). Those phases are a circle in the clock sense. They are not the metric fifth dimension and they are not the IR pole of \(K\).

**Key.** \(K=\mathcal{K}_{ij}\): 4D multi-domain averaging kernel. \(\tau_{\rm web}\): IR scale of that kernel, catalogue value \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\). \(\varphi\): internal phase, period \(2\pi\). \(S_5\): 5D Einstein–Hilbert action on a metric \(\mathbb{R}^{3}\times S^{1}\). \(K\) is not \(S_5\).

Historical corpus (Gatlin \(\mathbb{R}^{3}\times S^{1}_{\tau}\), Casimir of a metric circle, KK tower) is archived as *prior*. It is not the modified framework of this date.

## Lock 3 — Exponent 7 and \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) stay two sentences

Coxeter exponent \(7\) is **clock algebra**. It is an integer in \(\{1,7,11,13,17,19,23,29\}\). It labels a frozen eigenplane of the E₈ Coxeter element. It licenses internal phases \(\varphi\sim\varphi+2\pi\) of order 7.

\(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) is a **catalogue / kernel scale**. It is the listed IR scale \(\tau_{\rm web}\) of \(\mathcal{K}_{ij}\), and the listed web-coherence length \(\ell_C\) in the same class.

Two sentences. If they become one sentence, megaparsecs have been minted from an exponent. That is the Given→Derived slide. It is out.

Not permitted:

> “The Tav circle is frozen exponent 7, hence \(\tau=7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\).”

Permitted, as two sentences:

> The frozen Coxeter exponent is \(7\). That is clock algebra.  
> The catalogue IR scale of \(K\) is \(\tau_{\rm web}=7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\). That is a kernel / web scale.

The integer match is noted. It is not a derivation.

## Compatibility clause for one operator \(K\)

One operator \(K\) with a UV end at \(m_0\) (through \(\lambda_{m_0}\)) and an IR end at \(\tau_{\rm web}\) is **compatible only as a working hypothesis**, and only if both hold:

1. \(K\) is not \(S_5\) on a metric circle.
2. \(m_0\) is not derived from \(\tau\) or from \(\tau_{\rm web}\).

If either fails, the two-pole language is withdrawn. Do not write that the 1/7 harmonic “pins” \(m_0\). \(m_0\) is an independent geometric floor in this corpus.
