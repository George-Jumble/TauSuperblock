## 2. What the floor *is* after this morning

Before today the live frame already treated \(m_0=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) as an **input** (09-21 lock), not as a Clay output and not as a glueball. This morning named the observable that warrants the input.

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}
\;:=\;
\frac{M_n}{3}
=313.188\,\mathrm{MeV}
\;\simeq\;
313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}
\quad\text{(corpus rounding)}.
\]

**Key.** \(M_n\) measured; \(3\) valence occupancy of the nucleon.

Two layers, do not fuse them:

| Layer | Statement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Existence | Bound colour bounce has a smallest positive deposit | TEP + bound vs unbound. Unchanged. |
| Value | That deposit equals the nucleon-third | New protocol. Observable, not a Vălean or Planck–Kerr theorem. |
| Length | \(r_{\mathrm{M}}=\hbar c/\Delta\) | Definition. Follows the value. |
| Current up | \(2.16\,\mathrm{MeV}\) sits *inside* the third | Higgs remainder. Not the floor. |

So the mass-floor in the theory is no longer “a free MeV parameter we happened to like” and is also no longer “a first-principles output of Euler sums.” It is the IR energy assigned to one valence line of the lightest stable three-quark state.

## 4. What *does* change

### 4.1 Status of the number

The sentence “the theory predicts \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\)” is no longer allowed.

Allowed sentence:

> TEP requires a positive floor. The floor is read from the nucleon as \(M_n/3\). Every later formula that used \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) is using that observable.

That is a demotion of uniqueness and a promotion of honesty. The 09-21 stack already called \(m_0\) an input. This session specifies *which* input.

### 4.2 Optional two-symbol split

If dressing-only and constituent-share are kept distinct:

\[
E_g
=
E_{q\leftarrow g}
\simeq
\frac{M_n}{3}-m_u^{\mathrm{cur}}
=
311.03\,\mathrm{MeV},
\]

\[
m_u^{\mathrm{const,A}}
=

313.19\,\mathrm{MeV}.

**Effect on the theory.** Formulas that mean “inertia of one stopped gluon quantum” should use \(E_g\). Formulas that mean “IR mass of one valence line in the nucleon” should use \(\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}=M_n/3\). Until a calculation needs the \(2\,\mathrm{MeV}\) split, keep one printed symbol \(\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) and remember the split exists.

Magnetic-moment constituent mass \(\approx 336\,\mathrm{MeV}\) is **not** adopted as the floor. Using it would move every linear quantity by \(7\%\) and would break the nucleon mass budget.

### 4.4 What can no longer be claimed downstream

- “Weight-6 Vălean identity produces the mass gap.” Out, as a derivation of the MeV number.
- “Planck–Kerr average \(M_{\mathrm{Pl}}/(6\langle\gamma\rangle_6\sqrt{N_{\mathrm{bubble}}})\) predicts \(313.1\).” Still out.
- “Current up mass is the energy input of the floor.” Out.
- “The floor is parameter-free.” Out, if that means “no IR observable was used.” In, if that means “no continuous *extra* parameter beyond \(M_n\) and the valence count.”

The last reading is the useful one. The theory’s free IR datum is now a measured nucleon mass plus the integer \(3\). That is leaner than a floating \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) with no home, and leaner than a megaparsec circle.

### 4.5 What becomes easier

Once the floor is the nucleon-third, several old tensions relax rather than tighten.

- Why MeV rather than eV or GeV: because nucleons are tabulated in MeV.
- Why not a narrow PDG resonance at \(313.1\): the floor is a share of a bound state, not a pole.
- Why it sits near constituent-quark lore: Estimator A *is* that lore.
- Why it is close to, but not equal to, \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}\) and \(\sqrt{\sigma}\): those are other IR operators. No need to force equality.
- Why ATLAS/CMS \(M_0=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) was an analysis ruler, not a discovery: the ruler is the nucleon-third, not a new particle.
