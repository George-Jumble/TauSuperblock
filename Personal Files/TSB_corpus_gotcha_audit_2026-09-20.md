# Corpus audit — other gotchas of the lab-gravity type; claims vs data

**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Question:** Are there other obvious gotchas like lab gravity vs the 7 Mpc cell? Does the corpus claim things experiment has already shown to be incorrect?  
**Locks remain in force.**

Distinction used here:

- **Gotcha:** category error, Given→Derived slide, or a “detection” that does not survive its own method.
- **Contradicted by data:** a specific, sharp prediction that independent published measurements rule out.
- **Unconfirmed:** in-house or Zenodo-only; not the same as false.

The whole framework is not a dead letter. Several *trophy sentences* are.

---

## A. Same class as the lab-gravity slide (retire or demote)

### A1. JADES “8.1σ spike at \(k=0.142857\)” as a detection

November 2025 Zenodo titles say first detection and “the fifth dimension is measured.” A public `JADES_DR4_8.1_SPIKE.py` in the tau-cosmology repo **adds** a Gaussian at \(k=0.142857\) before plotting. A later note in the same family says the mode “lies just beyond the present \(k\)-reach.” Official JADES DR4 papers do not report a monochromatic \(0.\overline{142857}\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}\) line. The modified corpus already recorded JADES/DESI **nulls** on the comb.

Status: not a community detection. The 8.1σ sentence is a trophy. Treat as withdrawn. Not the same as “JWST disproved a 7 Mpc web scale”; it disproves the *detection claim*.

### A2. Cymatics at 142.857 Hz; any table-top tone of the cell

Same dimensional class as the retired 21.83 GHz resonator. A frequency in hertz is not the image of \(\tau_{\rm web}\). Out.

### A3. CMS “12σ” \(n_{\mu}\bmod 7\) (and ALICE “Naked Binary Leak”)

A multiplicity histogram with mean \(\langle n_{\mu}\rangle\approx 2.43\) is peaked at \(n=2\). Residue 2 is then huge by Poisson/NB arithmetic, not by topology. Calling that 12σ 7-fold structure is the collider analogue of the lab-gravity slide. CMS and ALICE have not published these as new physics. Keep as **predictions** if you must; do not keep as results.

### A4. Planck \(D_\ell\) residual FFT “clear 7-fold”

No Planck collaboration paper reports a 7-fold residual comb. An FFT of residuals will always show peaks. In-house. Demote to prediction / look-elsewhere analysis, not “detected.”

### A5. “Zero continuous parameters” / “\(m_0\) from Tav resonance”

After the locks, \(m_0\), \(\tau_{\rm web}\), the freeze \(\{1,7\}\), the linear map \(\beta_i=(m_i-11)/30\), and the equal-density ansatz are all choices or independent floors. June 2026 status-update language that \(m_0\) “arises from Planck–Kerr seed geometry and Tav resonance” is the Given→Derived slide the locks forbade. Strike it wherever it still sits in older PDFs.

### A6. Casimir of a metric circle of radius \(\tau_{\rm web}\) equals \(\rho_\Lambda\)

Usual \(1/R^4\) Casimir of a megaparsec circle is not the observed vacuum density unless a Planck dual is smuggled back in. That dual is the same move that killed the resonator. After the locks, baseline \(w=-1\) from metric \(S^1\) Casimir is **archived prior**, not a live derivation.

### A7. “Yang–Mills mass gap solved”

\(m_0\sim 313\,\mathrm{MeV}\) sits in the QCD ballpark (\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}\) is hundreds of MeV). That is not a Clay-problem proof, not a lattice theorem, and not an experimental confirmation. Keep as a proposed IR anchor. Do not keep as a solved prize.

## B. Claims that data have *not* killed, but that are in tension or mixed

### B1. \(w\equiv -1\) as the whole dark-energy story

DESI DR2 BAO + CMB prefers evolving dark energy at about \(3\sigma\), up to \(\sim 4\sigma\) with some supernova compilations, and closer to \(\Lambda\) with others. The original Tau sentence “\(w=-1\) exact, full story” is under tension. The modified split (baseline plus centrifugal drift of \(\{\beta_i\}\)) was written to live with that. It is **not** a confirmation of the drift formula.

### B2. \(H_0=69.8\) “resolves the Hubble tension”

Planck-like early \(H_0\) remains \(\sim 67\); SH0ES remains \(\sim 73\). A compromise number is not a resolution. Unresolved.

### B3. Quasar / radio dipole excess (Secrest et al.)

Controversial; not established as new physics, not refuted. Domain-velocity language is an interpretation, not a measurement.

### B4. Galaxy correlation length \(r_0\sim 5\)–\(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\)

Real. Identifying it with a compact fifth dimension or with frozen exponent 7 is interpretation. The number was already in the catalogue before the theory.

## C. What experiment has *not* done

No refereed instrument paper has the sentence “Tau-Superblock is false.” Euclid DR1, Rubin, CMB-S4, and a clean DESI+SN consensus on \(w(z)\) are still the live falsifiers for the **catalogue scale** and for DE drift. LHCb / Belle II have not reported a 56-tick echo. Neutron EDM has not tested a geometric strong-CP claim.

So: the corpus is not observationally dead. It is observationally **unconfirmed**, and several of its loudest empirical sentences do not survive their own method.
