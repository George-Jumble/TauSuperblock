## 0. Result in one block

## 3. The veto, stated as a map

Write a candidate refresh as an operation \(\mathcal{R}\) applied once per tick to the cluster state.

Two families:

\[
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{pin}}\!:\;
\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}(\mathbf{R})\;\chi
\;\longmapsto\;
\delta(\mathbf{R}-\mathbf{R}_{\ast})\;\chi'
\qquad\text{(classical pin)}
\]

\[
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{ok}}\!:\;
\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}(\mathbf{R})\;\chi
\;\longmapsto\;
e^{i\theta}\,\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}(\mathbf{R})\;\chi''
\qquad\text{(no which-path tag)}
\]

**Key.** \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{pin}}\): replaces the packet amplitude with a spike at one classical address \(\mathbf{R}_{\ast}\). \(\chi'\): whatever internals survive that pin. \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{ok}}\): may change bound internals to \(\chi''\) and may multiply \(\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}\) by a *global* phase \(\theta\), the same on every path. It may not condition on “which peak of \(\lvert\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}\rvert^{2}\).”

Talbot–Lau fringes need a stable relative phase between the two supports of \(\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}\) for the whole flight. One \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{pin}}\) during that flight is already a which-path measurement. Many pins are worse.

Count ticks if someone still wants Planck-period refresh:

\[
N_{\mathrm{ticks}}
=
\frac{T_{\mathrm{flight}}}{t_P}
\sim
\frac{10^{-3}}{5\times 10^{-44}}
\sim
10^{40}.
\]

**Key.** \(10^{40}\) independent classical pins of \(\mathbf{R}\) during one Pedalino flight is not a small correction. It is the destruction of the experiment. So Layer A’s \(\Delta t_{\mathrm{DR}}\approx t_P\), read as a COM pin, is incompatible with the data by an absurd margin.

Even a “slow” refresh at \(1\,\mathrm{MHz}\) would fire \(\sim 10^{3}\) times during the flight. Still fatal if each tick pins \(\mathbf{R}\).

The veto is not “no clock may tick.” The veto is “the clock may not write \(\mathbf{R}\) to a point.”

## 4. What refresh is still allowed to be

### 4.1 Refresh of bound occupancy (\(\chi\) only)

Metallic bonds, electronic state, nuclear envelopes, colour-dressing — all of that lives in \(\chi\). A protocol that

- scrapes \(\Phi_7\) on a bound line,
- resets \(\Phi_8\) on a blocked target (TEP),
- stops a rebound and stores residual \(L\),

can fire inside \(\chi\) as long as it does **not** correlate with the transverse packet address.

That is Layer B, trimmed: “particles in active manifestation” refers to **bound lines**, not to the packet coordinate. The droplet can be a duty cycle of stopped bounces. The address of the droplet cannot be a duty cycle of classical points.

### 4.2 Unitary / common-phase refresh of COM

A global phase on \(\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}\), the same for every path, is invisible to the fringe. So is any unitary that does not distinguish left from right. If someone wants to keep “the 8-phase engine runs during the flight,” the engine’s action on \(\mathbf{R}\) must be of that kind: a drive, not a collapse.

In live language: isolated COM is an **unbound coordinate**. Unbound coordinates complete Past \(\leftrightarrow\) Future. They are not stopped at Present. \(\Phi_8\) as *stop* does not fire on them. That is already how the photon was treated. Pedalino puts isolated massive COM in the same bin.

### 4.3 What “matter is not persistent” may still mean

Allowed reading: bound mass is occupancy of stopped envelopes. Those envelopes can appear, lock, and clear. The inventory \(\Psi_{\mathrm{DR}}(s)\) is the set of *currently stopped* lines on slice \(s\).

Forbidden reading: the object as a whole is a stroboscopic classical point, re-drawn \(10^{40}\) times per millisecond, and the COM of that drawing is the lab position.

Persistence of matter, after Pedalino:

| What | Persistent during isolated flight? |
|------|-------------------------------------|
| Phase of \(\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}\) | **Yes. Required.** |
| Classical point-value of \(\mathbf{R}\) | **No. There isn’t one.** |
| Bound envelopes \(\chi\) | May flicker, if flicker is path-blind |
| Residual \(L\) on internals | May be rewritten by scrape/reset |
| Residual \(L\) on COM | Should stay \(\approx 0\) (unbound packet) |
