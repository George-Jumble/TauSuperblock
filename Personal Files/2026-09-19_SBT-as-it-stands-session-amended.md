## 1. What SBT is now

A **multi-domain constitutive theory**.

Six 3+1D domains sit on an internal supersphere with staggered embedding velocities. Each domain has its own metric and its own Maxwell field. Photons stay on that domain’s null cone. Other-domain stress reaches us only through a 4D embedding kernel \(\mathcal K_{ij}\). That kernel is stochastic / impulse physics, not a graviton wrapping an extra circle, and not a superluminal field inside our domain (GW170817).

Matter in another domain or another harmonic phase is the dark-matter candidate. Phase-7 Mirron pressure, with coupling below threshold, is the late-time vacuum-floor candidate. The electromagnetic vacuum is a constitutive problem for an 8-phase Mirron cell: produce \(B_F\), then \(\Phi\), then \(\xi\), without hiding \(\alpha_{\rm fs}\) in \(r_M\).

Time in the architecture is a helix: the domain label \(i\) is cyclic; the tick \(n\) is never identified with \(n+6\). Phase is a circle. Spacetime time is the axis. There is no compact-time CTC in the theory as amended.

Local light and local gravity are 4D. Extra structure is internal (supersphere, phases, cell). There is no laboratory-accessible fifth spacetime direction.
