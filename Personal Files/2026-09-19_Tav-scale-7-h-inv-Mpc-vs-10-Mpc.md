# Tav scale: \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) is already \(\sim 10\,\mathrm{Mpc}\)

**Session date:** 19 September 2026  
**Question:** “Isn’t the Tav metric 7 Mpc?”  
**Short answer:** The TSB notes quote \(\tau=7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\). That is a cosmology unit, not a different length from \(\sim 10\,\mathrm{Mpc}\). For \(h\sim 0.7\), they are the same radius. Changing 10 to 7 does not revive a metric \(S^1\).

## 1. What the notes actually wrote

The gravity and cosmology notes do **not** say “Tav = 7 Mpc” as a bare laboratory length. They say

\[
\tau = 7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}.
\]

That is the standard large-scale-structure convention. Distances are quoted in \(h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) so the number stays stable if \(H_0\) is revised.

The conversion is elementary:

\[
7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}
=\frac{7}{h}\,\mathrm{Mpc}.
\]

| Assumed \(h\) | Physical \(\tau\) |
|---|---|
| \(0.67\) (Planck-like) | \(10.4\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) |
| \(0.70\) | \(10.0\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) |
| \(0.73\) (local \(H_0\)-like) | \(9.6\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) |

So the reviewer’s “\(\sim 10\,\mathrm{Mpc}\)” is the \(h\sim 0.7\) evaluation of the same TSB number, not a competing Tav radius.

---

## 2. Even a bare 7 Mpc does not help

Suppose the theory is rewritten to drop the \(h^{-1}\) and freeze

\[
\tau=7\,\mathrm{Mpc}
\]

with no Hubble factor. Then

\[
7\,\mathrm{Mpc}
=7\times 3.0857\times 10^{22}\,\mathrm{m}
\approx 2.16\times 10^{23}\,\mathrm{m}.
\]

A single flat extra dimension is bounded at

\[
R\lesssim 30\,\mu\mathrm{m}=3\times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}.
\]

Ratio:

\[
\frac{2.16\times 10^{23}}{3\times 10^{-5}}\approx 7\times 10^{27}.
\]

At \(10\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) the ratio is \(\sim 10^{28}\). Either way the discrepancy is thirty orders of ten, not a factor of \(10/7\).

The first KK energy is likewise unchanged at any useful precision:

\[
\frac{\hbar c}{7\,\mathrm{Mpc}}\sim 9\times 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{eV},
\qquad
\frac{\hbar c}{10\,\mathrm{Mpc}}\sim 6\times 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{eV}.
\]

Both are an almost continuous tower of gravitational modes, not a particle threshold.

---

## 3. Why cosmology papers say \(7h^{-1}\) in the first place

Filament widths, inter-filament gaps, and “distance to nearest web particle” are measured from galaxy catalogues. Those catalogues convert redshift to distance with an assumed \(H_0\). Quoting \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) means:

> if you used \(H_0=70\), the gap is 10 Mpc; if you used \(H_0=67\), it is 10.4 Mpc; the *catalogue number* stays 7.

That is why the cosmic-web coincidence was written as \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\). It is a statistical scale in expanding-universe units. It is not, by itself, evidence that a fifth metric dimension has that circumference.

---

## 4. The decision is not 7 versus 10. It is metric versus internal.

The phenomenological fork does not move when \(\tau\) is quoted as 7, \(7h^{-1}\), or 10.

**If Tav is a metric \(S^1\)** that gravity can wrap, laboratory inverse-square-law tests already forbid a radius anywhere near megaparsecs. You then need an explicit warp, brane localization, or decoupling mechanism that hides the KK tower below \(\sim 30\,\mu\mathrm{m}\) while still letting a 10 Mpc coherence scale appear in the cosmic web. That mechanism is not optional wording. It is a new piece of the action.

**If Tav is an internal phase / resonance / topological coordinate**, then \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) can be a *projected coherence length* in 4D — a scale at which domain or phase correlations show up in the web — without being the circumference of a graviton-accessible circle. In that reading the displayed 5D Einstein–Hilbert measure \(\int d^5x\sqrt{-g_5}R_5\) with an ordinary macroscopic \(S^1\) is the misleading part, not the number 7.

Those are different geometries. They are not different values of the same geometry.

---

## 5. Recommended wording in TSB drafts

Use one locked sentence and stop mixing units:

> TSB takes the Tav coherence scale to be \(\tau=7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\approx 10\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) for \(h\approx 0.7\). This is *not* proposed as the radius of a laboratory-accessible extra dimension. It is the 4D projection of an internal phase / topological scale. The metric-circle interpretation is excluded by inverse-square-law tests unless an explicit decoupling mechanism is derived.

If a draft instead insists that Tav *is* a metric fifth dimension of 7 Mpc, the next required calculation is the localization mechanism, not another web-statistic coincidence.
