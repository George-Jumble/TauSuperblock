## 2. The arithmetic

\[
\frac{M_p}{3}=312.757\,\mathrm{MeV},
\qquad
\frac{M_n}{3}=313.188\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]

**Key.** \(M_p\): proton. \(M_n\): neutron. Factor \(3\): valence count, bookkeeping. The old print \(313.1\) matches the neutron, rounded, not the proton. Proton third rounds to \(312.8\).

Current-quark average of the proton’s valence content:

\[
\langle m\rangle_{uud}
=
\frac{2m_u^{\rm cur}+m_d^{\rm cur}}{3}
=
3.007\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]

**Key.** Two current ups and one current down. This is the Higgs remainder averaged. It is not the dressing and not the floor.

Dressing share of the same content, whole proton minus those current masses, then divided by three: \(309.75\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Already computed. Still an insertion.
