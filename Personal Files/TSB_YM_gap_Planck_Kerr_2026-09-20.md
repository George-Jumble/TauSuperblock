# Yang–Mills gap of the Planck–Kerr engine — attempt

**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Clay statement.** Compact simple \(G\), continuum QFT on \(\mathbb{R}^4\), Hamiltonian bounded below by \(\Delta>0\) above the vacuum.  
**This note.** What the live engine gives, and why that is not yet the Clay theorem.

---

## 1. What the engine is

Shared extremal Kerr ring, \(R_{\rm int}=\ell_{\rm Pl}\), six faces, Hopf fibre \(L_{\rm Hopf}=2\pi\ell_{\rm Pl}\). Occupancy discrete. Isometries of \(S^3\) broken on purpose (no light \(SU(2)\times SU(2)\)).

A Wilson loop around a Hopf fibre is a \(U(1)\) holonomy. Six faces can carry a discrete connection. That is a **6-site gauge quantum mechanics**, not \(SU(N)\) Yang–Mills on \(\mathbb{R}^4\).

**Key.** Engine: Planck–Kerr ring + six faces. Toy Hilbert space: finite. Clay Hilbert space: infinite, continuum 4D.

---

## 2. Gap of the toy (real, and not Clay)

Finite lattice, compact structure group, any reasonable Hamiltonian \(H\) bounded below. Spectrum is discrete. The gap above the ground state is positive because the space is finite-dimensional (or compact).

Order of magnitude if the only length is the fibre:

\[
\Delta_{\rm toy}
\sim
\frac{\hbar c}{L_{\rm Hopf}}
=
\frac{\hbar c}{2\pi\ell_{\rm Pl}}
=
\frac{M_{\rm Pl}c^2}{2\pi}
\approx
1.9\times 10^{18}\,\mathrm{GeV}.
\]

That is a Planckian gap. It is not \(m_0=313\,\mathrm{MeV}\). It is also not 4D YM.

**Key.** \(\Delta_{\rm toy}\): gap of the six-face ring mechanics. Planckian.

---

## 3. What would have to be added

To reach Clay you need all four:

1. A continuum limit of faces / of the 4D chart in which the engine sits, with a compact simple \(G\) (for QCD, \(SU(3)\)).
2. Existence of the Wightman / Osterwalder–Schrader theory at that limit.
3. A mass gap \(\Delta>0\) that survives the limit.
4. A formula that returns \(m_0\) if that is the intended IR floor — which requires a hierarchy \(\Delta_{\rm toy}\to m_0\), i.e. \(10^{19}\) in mass, not supplied by F7.

None of 1–4 is in the live frame. Emergent QCD from Planck–Kerr remains a slogan until those exist.

A Wilson-area law on Prime, \(\langle W(C)\rangle\sim e^{-\sigma\mathrm{Area}(C)}\), with string tension \(\sigma\sim m_0^2/(\hbar c)\), would *name* the gap after the floor. It would not derive it. The kernel’s Yukawa already *assumes* a mass \(m_0\). Using \(K\) to prove the gap is circular.

## 4. Honest map

| Object | Gap | Theory |
|---|---|---|
| six-face ring QM | \(\sim M_{\rm Pl}/2\pi\) | proved by finiteness |
| 4D continuum YM | Clay \(\Delta>0\) | open |
| Superblock floor \(m_0\) | \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) | input |

The engine does not derive the YM gap. It supplies a Planckian toy gap and a hoped-for IR name \(m_0\). Connecting them is the hierarchy problem plus Clay, not a one-line identity.

---

## 5. Decision

Do not write “Planck–Kerr solves Yang–Mills.” Do not write \(\Delta_{\rm YM}=m_0\) as a theorem. Leave \(m_0\) the floor. Leave Clay open.
