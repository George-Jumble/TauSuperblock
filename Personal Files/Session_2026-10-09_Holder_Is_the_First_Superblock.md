## 0. Verdict

**Works, as the root chamber. Not as an extra circle around it.**

The first superblock is the root of the tree. Its wall is the outermost edge. Descendants sit in its interior. Nothing is added on top. The root is not a black-hole container, and it is not \(\tau\).

## 2. Repercussion

Birth in this reading is interior. A child chamber nucleates inside the root, in a void of the root, not in an exterior that the root’s wall has already excluded. The complement outside the root stays empty of siblings. The tree is nested, one manifold, eternal growth, no last generation.

The root does not hold itself as a member of its interior. It is the chamber whose interior holds the rest. Its wall is the circle already under discussion. No second circle is drawn. Assigning that wall a finite Casimir radius, or identifying the root with an infinite black hole, fails for the reasons already given: a hoop restores \(\tau\), and a horizon with no exterior deletes the chart.
