## 2. What the Recent CMS (and earlier ATLAS) 7-Fold Scans Actually Did

The CMS Run2012BC DoubleMuParked analysis (and the parallel ATLAS Open Data 2lep scan) performed a **generic, inclusive** search:

- multiplicity \( n_\mu \bmod 7 \) on the full triggered sample,
- periodogram on the event-ordered multiplicity series,
- periodogram / subharmonic search on the \( p_T \) spectrum.

These are valuable exploratory diagnostics, but they are **not** the same as the targeted B-meson programme.  

In particular:

- The dominant residue-2 peak is an immediate consequence of the dimuon trigger (\( n_\mu \approx 2 \)) and is fully accounted for by a trigger-aware null.
- A uniform 1/7 null is therefore the wrong background model for that analysis — exactly as the report itself warned.
- Even a carefully constructed trigger-aware null on inclusive multiplicity is still not a test of “particular points in the beta meson decay.”
