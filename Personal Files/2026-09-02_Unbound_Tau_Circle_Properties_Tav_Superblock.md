## 2. Two Distinct Circles

The framework uses two geometrically related but physically distinct objects that are easy to conflate:

1. **The bound tau circle \(S^1_\tau\)**

Casimir energy belonging to the vacuum of the infinite manifold is therefore an *unbound* energy and must not be identified with the discrete \(1/7\) cycles of any single bound node.
