## Distinction

- Indexed by discrete generation \(g=0,1,2,\dots\).
- Radii (or time-contrasts) scale geometrically: \(R_g=R_0\lambda^g\).
- Scaling ratio chosen so that the fractal dimension matches the live anomalous dimension \(\alpha=1-\log_7 6\approx 0.079218\) (or its complement \(\log_7 6\)).
- Spheres may be concentric, successive shells, or associated with different hierarchical levels (hadronic, nuclear, atomic, molecular, \ldots).
- They live either as ordinary 4D loci of the globs or as the optional η-spheres of the time-only fifth-sector sketch.
- Branching is the six co-located domains or the seven-phase count, not a planar hexagonal packing.

**Complete key**
- \(R_g\): radius at generation \(g\).
- \(\lambda\): scaling ratio (chosen = 7 to recover \(\alpha\)).
- \(\alpha\): anomalous dimension from the 142857 cycle.

### Overlapping circles grid
- Planar arrangement of congruent circles whose centers lie on a triangular (hexagonal) lattice.
- Radius equals the nearest-neighbor center-to-center distance, so neighboring circles overlap.
- Finite patches: 7 circles (Seed of Life), 19 circles (Flower of Life), then centered hexagonal numbers 1, 7, 19, 37, \ldots.
- Six-fold rotational symmetry; lenses (vesica piscis) at each overlap.
- Purely geometric/decorative pattern; no derivation of \(\alpha\), residual-\(L\) gravity, or the mass floor.

**Complete key**
- Centered hexagonal number for \(n\) rings: \(3n(n-1)+1\).
- No free scaling ratio \(\lambda\) that is tuned to \(\log_7 6\).
