## 0. Verdict

**The interior of the chamber. The side with a clock.**

Low-Weyl names the region where this chamber’s bound fields source the curvature and residual \(L\) does the compressing. It is not a claim that the Weyl tensor vanishes there.

## 2. The split

Inside the wall the occupancy is this chamber’s: six co-located sectors, a clock, bound fields, unbound light on null rays. Einstein’s equation ties the Ricci piece to that stress-energy. Residual \(L\) is the framework account of the compression. Waves and tides, the Weyl piece, still exist here — a gravitational wave inside the chamber is Weyl. “Low” means the complement’s tidal label is not what organizes this side. The clock and the bound fields are.

No ratio \(C/\mathrm{Ricci}\) has been computed. The word is a side of the wall, not a measured amplitude.
