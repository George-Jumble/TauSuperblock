Independent re-analysis of DECam data (including 2020–2021) shows at least three distinct low-amplitude brightenings plus long-term mean-magnitude variations. Phoebe is an ordinary variable star, not microlensing. This resolves tension with OGLE null results that already rule out substantial lunar-/planetary-mass PBH fractions of dark matter.
