## Analysis

The geometric point of simultaneity (UPS \(\Sigma_n\) of tick \(n\), realized at the \(\Phi_8\) seam for the floor) does deliver a discrete update with each tick: the kinematic phase advance \(\Delta\phi_{\rm tick}\) of already-present floor excitations (colour + inverted-colour EM/electroweak).

This is not a dynamical “push” in the sense of an impulse that deposits residual angular momentum or sources a cosmological repulsive term.

- Residual \(L\) arises only when a bound force quantum’s rebound toward Absolute Future is stopped at the Present. The residual is continuous compression proportional to local bound-field density. It is not a discrete kick delivered by the simultaneity map itself.
- The stamp is a phase multiplication / addition on modes that already exist at the floor. It does not generate new residual \(L\), does not act on isolated COM packets or higher globs, and does not accumulate into a dark-energy density.
- Unbound completions (light-like bounces between Absolute Past and Absolute Future) retain no residual \(L\). A phase advance on unbound modes likewise deposits none.
- Promoting the per-tick geometric update to a gentle outward push that sources \(\rho_\Lambda\) would require identifying the stamp (or the UPS map) with a force that the live locks do not assign to it. It would also reintroduce a free parameter to match the observed magnitude and blur the floor-only / residual-\(L\) distinction.

### Open reading (non-lock)

The white-stroke repulsion remains the sign flip of already-present residual \(L\), not a push generated by the simultaneity point.
