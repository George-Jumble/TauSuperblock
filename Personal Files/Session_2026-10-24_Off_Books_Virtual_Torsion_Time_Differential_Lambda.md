## The integration

Couple the virtual-torsion transmission to the time differential by making the transmission factor (or the collected energy) depend on \(\delta t_N\) or \(\nabla t_N\):

\[
E_{\rm push}[\delta t_N]
=
\sum_{a=1}^{6}
E_{\rm reabs}^{(a)}
\cdot
f_{\rm torsion}^{(a)}(\delta t_N,\,\nabla t_N).
\]

**Key.** \(f_{\rm torsion}^{(a)}(\delta t_N,\,\nabla t_N)\): transmission that now depends on the local relative-now contrast or its gradient. The functional form is unfixed.

A minimal phenomenological attachment to an effective lambda is

\[
\Lambda_{\rm eff}
\propto
\frac{E_{\rm push}[\delta t_N]}{V_{\rm ref}}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\Lambda_{\rm eff}
\propto
\bigl|\nabla t_N\bigr|^p
\cdot
\sum_a E_{\rm reabs}^{(a)},
\]

where \(V_{\rm ref}\) is a reference volume and \(p\) is an unfixed power.

**Key.** \(V_{\rm ref}\): arbitrary conversion volume. \(p\): free exponent. Both remain free.

- The time differential \(\delta t_N\) already appeared as a modulator of the unbound wall potential and as a source of a phenomenological \(\Lambda_{\rm thermo}\propto|\nabla t_N|^p\). Inserting the virtual-torsion factor simply multiplies that modulator by a floor energy \(E_{\rm reabs}\).

- Promoting the virtual re-absorption energy itself to a dynamical source of \(\rho_\Lambda\) conflicts with the 2026-10-07 author cut that keeps virtual/Casimir contributions as floor objects.
- If the inertial push deposits residual angular momentum, the effect is already contained in the residual-L account of laboratory \(G\); the torsion label and the time-differential dependence then re-describe an existing mechanism rather than generate a new cosmological term.
- The preferred non-locking carrier remains the gradient of the unbound wall potential \(V_{\rm wall}\). The virtual-torsion \(\times\) time-differential construction can be viewed as one possible micro-physical story for how a fraction of floor energy is modulated into that wall potential, but the story does not evaluate the fraction.

Thus the coupling identifies a formal point at which a derivation might later be attached, yet under present locks the expression stays phenomenological.
