## What protocol step 1 is not

### Not a uniqueness theorem

A uniqueness theorem would have to exhibit:

- a named series \(S\) (Vălean or otherwise),
- a named factor \(f\) (integer, Bernoulli, Eisenstein coefficient, 7-fold clockwork occupancy, …),
- a named map \(\mathrm{Res}: (S,f,r_M)\mapsto\mathbb{R}\) whose output is forced to be \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) and no other infrared number,

and then prove that every other candidate series/factor/scale is excluded.
