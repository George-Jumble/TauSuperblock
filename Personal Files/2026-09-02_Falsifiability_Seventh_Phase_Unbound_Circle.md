## 1. Tests that isolate the seventh phase \(\phi_7\)

\(\phi_7\) is the claim that there exists an *atemporal coupling surface* — not a seventh spacetime — at which:

- bound-node orbits scrape PK mirrors,
- mass is generated (geometric Higgs),
- domain-to-domain currents pay a topological toll,
- the 313.1 MeV floor is charged.

### 1.1 Domain-boundary tolls (cleanest astrophysical \(\phi_7\) test)

**Prediction.** Massless probes are frictionless in void interiors and filament bulk. They pay a positive, statistically distinct “toll” only when the line of sight crosses a \(\sim 7\, h^{-1}\) Mpc conformal wall.

Already flagged in the CHIME × SDSS void cross-match:

- void interiors / filaments: near-zero anomalous DM,
- boundary crossings: mean residual \(\approx +4.4\) DM units,
- periodogram of residuals recovers 7-fold structure.

**Instruments.** CHIME, ASKAP/CRAFT, DSA-2000, SKA-Mid FRB catalogs cross-matched to SDSS / DESI / Euclid void catalogs.

**Pass.** Boundary-only excess persists and grows with sample size; bulk remains consistent with zero after known IGM/host subtraction; residual periodogram keeps the 7-fold ladder.

**Fail.** After host-galaxy and IGM control, boundary residuals are consistent with bulk, *or* the excess tracks baryonic overdensity rather than the conformal-wall catalog, *or* no 7-fold remains in the residual periodogram.

Why this is \(\phi_7\) and not just \(S^1_\tau\): a bound-circle-only model can put structure at 7 Mpc. Only \(\phi_7\) requires a *localized impedance at the wall* for massless probes.

### 1.2 The 313.1 MeV exclusion floor (collider \(\phi_7\) test)

**Prediction.** The scrape of a PK mirror charges a topological exclusion cost \(m_0 \approx 313.1\) MeV. This is an IR gluon / hadronic-inertia floor, not a fitted QCD parameter. Glueballs appear as \(gg\) composites near \(\sim 1700\) MeV. Heavy-ion “Naked Binary Leak” phase-shift correlates with this anchor.

**Instruments.** ALICE / LHCb / CMS heavy-ion and minimum-bias spectra; lattice QCD \(0^{++}\) glueball; precision threshold scans around \(2m_0\) and \(m_0\).

**Pass.** A persistent spectral or phase-shift feature locked to \(313.1\) MeV (not sliding with \(\sqrt{s}\)); glueball \(0^{++}\) consistent with \(\sim 2 \times 850\) MeV massive-gluon composite at low tension to lattice.

**Fail.** No feature at \(313.1\) MeV in well-controlled heavy-ion / threshold data at the sensitivity where the TAV ENGINE already claimed a signal; or the lightest glueball cannot be read as a two-massive-gluon state without new continuous parameters.

### 1.3 Flavor “temporal echoes” (PK-mirror / \(\phi_7\) current swap)

**Prediction.** \(B^0 \to K^{*0}\mu^+\mu^-\) \(C_9\), \(P_5'\) and related anomalies are geometric tolls of Color \(\to\) EM/Weak flux swapping across \(\phi_7\), not new \(Z'\) particles. Echoes are subensemble-correlated (RGC / 142857 filtering), not a uniform Wilson-coefficient shift.

**Fail.** Full Belle II + LHCb Upgrade II return the anomalies to the SM, *or* the residuals are a single constant Wilson shift with no 7-fold / subensemble structure.

### 1.4 Geometric Higgs, not a separate vacuum expectation value

**Prediction.** The compact circumference acting through \(\phi_7\) *is* the Higgs. Consequences already written in the Tau prediction sheet:

- trilinear shift \(\delta\lambda_{hhh}/\lambda_{hhh}^{\mathrm{SM}} \approx +0.65\%\),

**Fail.** \(\lambda_{hhh}\) is SM-like to well below \(0.65\%\) *and* a large \(\bar\theta\) axion is required by \(d_n\); or LFV appears at a rate that bypasses the halting filter. (Note the internal tension: some Tau sheets also quote an axion mass. A confirmed QCD axion *and* a purely geometric \(\phi_7\) Higgs cannot both be the strong-CP solution. That is itself a decision-level test.)

## 2. Tests that isolate the unbound circle \(S^1_\infty\)

\(S^1_\infty\) is the claim that vacuum energy is an *unbound* Casimir of the infinite manifold, *not* the 1/7 harmonic energy of a 7 Mpc cell. Bound nodes are gravitational cells. They do not set \(\rho_\Lambda\).

### 2.1 Split dark energy: static Casimir backbone vs rotational drift

**Prediction (locked by the 20 Aug 2026 note + DESI evaluation).**

- Backbone from \(S^1_\infty\): \(w \equiv -1\) exactly at leading order.
- Apparent DESI “weakening” from six-domain supersphere rotation, not from a dynamical scalar.

Tau prediction sheet (pre-registered): Euclid DR1 \(w_0 = -1.000 \pm 0.003\), \(w_a = 0.000 \pm 0.01\) for the *Casimir piece*.

The Superblock reading of DESI is that a *fitted* \(w_0 > -1\) or \(w_a \neq 0\) can appear when rotation is ignored, but the correct split reconstruction must recover a static \(w=-1\) backbone plus a geometric centrifugal term.

**Instruments.** Euclid DR1 (Oct 2026), DESI DR2+, Roman, CMB-S4 cross-check.

**Pass.** After explicitly separating a constant Casimir piece from a supersphere-rotation piece, the Casimir piece stays at \(w=-1\) inside \(\pm 0.003\); the residual matches the predicted rotational decay, not a thawing/freezing quintessence trajectory.

**Fail.** The entire dark-energy sector is a single dynamical fluid whose \(w(z)\) cannot be split that way; *or* the constant piece is forced away from \(-1\) at \(> 3\sigma\); *or* \(w(z)\) develops 7-fold oscillations locked to \(k_\tau\) (that would mean vacuum energy *is* the bound circle, contradicting the unbound claim).

This last clause is the sharpest \(S^1_\infty\) discriminator.

### 2.2 Laboratory and cavity Casimir vs 7 Mpc scaling

**Prediction.** Standard parallel-plate Casimir \(\propto 1/a^4\) is the local 4D shadow. TSB corrections come from \(\phi_7\) disclosure of an *unbound* vacuum, not from a cavity sized to \(\tau = 7\, h^{-1}\) Mpc. Therefore:

Existing TSB Casimir session logs (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21241034) are the place to freeze a numbered residual before the next precision Casimir run (MEMS, spherical-plate, critical-Casimir analogues).

**Pass.** Residuals match the pre-registered TSB correction and do *not* contain a \(k_\tau\) harmonic.

**Fail.** A 7 Mpc (or \(1/7\)) harmonic is required to fit laboratory Casimir data; or TSB residuals are absent at the claimed sensitivity.

### 2.3 Massless vs massive probes of the same sky

**Prediction.** Massless probes (photons, gravitons) can couple through \(\phi_7\) to \(S^1_\infty\) and therefore feel the global vacuum. Massive probes see only local bound nodes.

**Consequence.** GW propagation (LISA / PTA) can show a vacuum-tied damping or phase that electromagnetic large-scale structure does *not* reproduce as a 7 Mpc standing wave in the same line of sight.

**Pass.** A vacuum-sector GW residual exists that is *not* a \(k_\tau\) baryon-acoustic or clustering harmonic.

**Fail.** Every GW residual that is claimed for Tav is identical to the electromagnetic 7-fold clustering signal. That collapses \(S^1_\infty\) back into \(S^1_\tau\).

### 2.4 Infinite KK tower / non-computable prefix

This is the hardest claim to kill cleanly. The computable prefix is the first six harmonics. Everything beyond is “partially non-computable.”

**Practical proxy.** Euclid / Rubin / CMB-S4 should see the first six \(k_n = n/\tau\) spikes and then a *suppression*, not an infinite comb of equal-strength lines.

**Pass.** Harmonic ladder detectable at \(n = 1\ldots 6\), then \(1/n^2\) (or stronger) collapse.

**Fail.** Either no ladder at all (kills bound circle too), or a long equal-amplitude comb past \(n=6\) with no computability cutoff (kills the Tav “prefix” story that distinguishes \(S^1_\infty\) from a plain compact \(S^1_\tau\)).

### 2.5 What would kill \(S^1_\infty\) even if \(\phi_7\) survives

- Vacuum energy tracks bound-circle harmonics.
- Casimir in the lab requires a 7 Mpc scale.
- \(w(z)\) is a single dynamical scalar with no static \(-1\) backbone.
