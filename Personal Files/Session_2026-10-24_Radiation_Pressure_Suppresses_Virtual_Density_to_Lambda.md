## The assumption

Radiation pressure is assumed to supply the mechanism that reduces the bare virtual/Casimir density to the observed value:

\[
\rho_{\rm eff}
=
\rho_{\rm Cas}
-
f(P_{\rm rad})
\quad\text{or}\quad
\rho_{\rm eff}
=
\rho_{\rm Cas}\times
\Bigl(\frac{P_{\rm rad}}{P_{\rm ref}}\Bigr)^p.
\]

**Key.** \(f(P_{\rm rad})\) or the power-law factor: a function of radiation pressure that cancels or suppresses the bare density. \(P_{\rm ref}\): a reference pressure (unfixed unless identified with a locked scale). \(p\): an exponent (unfixed).

## Possible concrete readings inside the corpus

1. **Balance against the unbound shell**  
   The Mirron/Entropic wall potential is already the reservoir of completed unbound (radiative) bounces. One may set the effective density equal to the difference
   \[
   \rho_{\rm eff}
   =
   \rho_{\rm Cas}
   -
   \frac{E_{\rm shell}}{V_{\rm horizon}},
   \]
   where \(E_{\rm shell}\) is the rough photonic reservoir estimated in earlier notes (\(\sim 10^{66}\)–\(10^{67}\,\mathrm{J}\)).  
   **Key.** \(E_{\rm shell}\): total unbound energy inside the cosmic event horizon. \(V_{\rm horizon}\): corresponding volume.  
   The difference can be tuned to \(\rho_\Lambda\) only by adjusting the fraction of \(E_{\rm shell}\) that is allowed to cancel \(\rho_{\rm Cas}\), or by choosing which cutoff enters \(\rho_{\rm Cas}\). The cancellation is not automatic.

2. **Redshifting**  
   Radiation pressure scales as \(a^{-4}\) while a pure vacuum term does not. At late times radiation is negligible, so it cannot suppress a constant bare \(\rho_{\rm Cas}\) today. Suppression would have to occur at a specific epoch (e.g., the EM–vacuum crossover examined in earlier notes) and then freeze. That freeze-out introduces a new scale that is not fixed by residual-L or the floor protocol.

3. **Local screening**  
   Radiation pressure could screen the virtual contribution inside bound regions while leaving a residual in voids. This is compatible with residual-L matching laboratory \(G\) (the screening would be an additional unbound effect), but the residual density in the voids is again set by the efficiency of the screening, which is free.

In all three readings a free efficiency, fraction, or epoch remains. Radiation pressure organizes a cancellation or a dilution; it does not compute the numerical value \(5\times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}\) from locked quantities.
