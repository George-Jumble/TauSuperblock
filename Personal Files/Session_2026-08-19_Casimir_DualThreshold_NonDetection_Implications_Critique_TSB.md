- The prediction was made **operational and falsifiable**. A concrete statistical test was defined, a null-hypothesis envelope was constructed, and the gate was enforced even when it produced a null result. That is the correct scientific posture.  
- The earlier pathology (near-100 % false-positive rates on pure decay nulls) was diagnosed and corrected. The current dual-threshold design is a real improvement in methodological hygiene.

These are genuine positives for the research programme, independent of whether the physical claim is true.

## 3. Critique and Points of Vulnerability

1. **Sensitivity of the primary detector is currently zero on the pure planted control at the operating gate.**  
   A non-detection is only as informative as the test’s power. The secondary matched filter recovers planted signals and also returned null on the real data, which strengthens the negative result. Still, the primary detector’s lack of power at the chosen threshold means the theory cannot yet claim that “if the effect were present at any appreciable strength we would have seen it.” That claim requires a power analysis under realistic signal amplitudes and the same trend-removal pipeline.

2. **The 7-cycle signature may be under-motivated or overly flexible.**  
   A period of exactly 7 (or the 142857-related cycle) is a strong, distinctive claim. If it is a rigid derivation from the theory’s axioms (G₂ 8-phase clockwork + Topological Exclusion + order-6 orbital shear), the non-detection is costly. If the period can be adjusted, or if “approximate seven-phase structure” is later accepted, the theory loses the virtue of specificity that made the original claim interesting. Post-hoc period adjustment after a failed test is a classic theory-protecting move and should be resisted.

3. **Risk of residual-feature promotion.**  
   After a primary signature fails, it is tempting to treat secondary residuals (anisotropy, weak hysteresis on one material, discrete steps on organics) as the new evidence. Those features were already visible in the less-controlled falsification battery and are more easily produced by ordinary dielectric physics. Without a new, pre-registered, theory-derived prediction that specifically forecasts those residuals with quantitative amplitude and location, they do not carry the same confirmatory weight.

4. **Scope limitation.**  
   The test was performed on one class of data (Matsubara-frequency dielectric responses from a particular magnetic-fluid / Casimir-related set). A theory that aims at a general time-symmetric breakdown mechanism must eventually face other domains (ATLAS 7-fold, DESI BAO imprint, lattice IR floor, etc.). A single clean negative in one domain does not refute the whole structure, but repeated negatives in the domains where the theory makes its sharpest predictions would.

5. **Calibration vs. discovery tension.**  
   The very strictness that makes the current protocol trustworthy also makes discovery harder. That is the correct trade-off if the goal is reliable knowledge. It becomes a problem only if the response to repeated nulls is to loosen gates or redefine success rather than to revise or restrict the theoretical claims.
