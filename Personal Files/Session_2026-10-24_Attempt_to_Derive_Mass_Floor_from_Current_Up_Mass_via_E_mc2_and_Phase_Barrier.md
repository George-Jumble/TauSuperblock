# Session 2026-10-24: Attempt to Derive the Mass Floor from the Current Up-Quark Mass via E=mc² and Phase-Barrier Crossing

**Date**: 2026-10-24  
**Framework**: Tau-Superblock (TSB / Superblock). Printed working floor is the neutron-dressing protocol 309.3 MeV (insertion, not a derivation). Current up-quark mass is the external PDG input \(m_u^{\rm cur}=2.16\pm 0.07\) MeV. TEP supplies only the inequality \(\Delta>0\). Phase-boundary transit energy is \(E_\times=\theta\Delta_{\rm Tav}\).  
**Proposal**: Use the current mass of an up quark, perform \(E=mc^2\), and identify the result as the energy produced by crossing the phase barrier, thereby deriving the mass floor.  
**Answer**: No. Circular and does not derive the floor.

## Plain-language summary

No. Taking the current mass of the up quark and writing \(E=mc^2\) simply returns the rest energy of that current mass (2.16 MeV). That number is not the mass floor.  

Identifying the same energy with the cost of crossing a phase barrier does not help: the transit energy is already written as \(E_\times=\theta\Delta_{\rm Tav}\), which contains the floor on the right-hand side. Using the current mass to “derive” the floor therefore loops back on itself. TEP still supplies only the inequality \(\Delta>0\); the numerical value remains an insertion.

## Analytical statement

**Key to symbols**  
- \(m_u^{\rm cur}\): current up-quark mass = 2.16 MeV.  
- \(E=mc^2\): rest energy.  
- \(\Delta_{\rm Tav}\): printed floor (309.3 MeV).  
- \(E_\times=\theta\Delta_{\rm Tav}\): domain-cross / phase-barrier energy.  

\[
E(m_u^{\rm cur})=m_u^{\rm cur}c^2=2.16\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]  
This is not equal to the floor. Setting  
\[
E_\times=m_u^{\rm cur}c^2
\]  
forces \(\theta\Delta_{\rm Tav}=2.16\) MeV and therefore solves for the floor in terms of itself, which is circular. No dimensionless clock function is fixed independently of the floor, and no named operator that can miss is supplied.
