## 12. Cosmology investigation (in progress) — closed sub-points [ruling] unless tagged

### 12a. Binding-burst tail [ruling]
It tails off as the Mirron fabric decompresses (matter thinning plus expanding space); it does not end at ~10⁻⁵ s. The released energy is vacuum-like (w = −1). It holds, then hands itself back at matter–radiation equality (z_eq ≈ 3420). Its size is set by conditions at equality, not as a fixed share of the binding push. At hand-back the held energy returns to Φ7.
- Best natural rule explored, X2: ρ_v = ρ_mρ_r/(ρ_m + ρ_r) at z_eq, f_EDE = 0.111 [estimate; not ruled as the amplitude rule].
- H0 ≈ 69.2 / 69.5 (calibrations 0.622 / 0.852 to Poulin et al. 2025) [estimate].
- Hand-back with w ≈ 1/2 decay (71.7 / 72.9) was suggested as a route toward H0 ~ 73 — **not ruled** [open].
- Completeness of the hand-back must be ~1.2×10⁻¹² [estimate; open].

### 12b. Redshift as dispersion [proposal]
The Superblock dispersion wave replaces standard cosmological redshift (it is not additive to it). 1 + z = a_obs/a_emit is preserved; this is the mechanism of expansion redshift, not a tired-light alternative. The wave continues to the conformal wall even if the originating gauge boson is absorbed. It passes the classic tests; no H0 relief alone (68.65) [estimate].
