## What “sum the energies” can mean

Because the domains are co-located, there is no spatial volume that belongs to one domain and not the others. A sum is therefore a sum over internal labels, not a sum of six independent cosmic energy densities. Four concrete operators already exist:

1. **Domain-cross energies**  
   \[
   E_{\rm cross}^{\rm tot}
   =
   \sum_{a<b}
   E_\times(a\to b)
   =
   \sum_{a<b}
   \theta_{ab}\,\Delta_{\rm Tav}.
   \]  
   **Key.** The sum runs over the 15 unordered pairs (or 30 directed hops). On the octahedron there are 12 adjacent (\(\theta=\pi/2\)) and 3 antipodal (\(\theta=\pi\)) pairs. The total is a fixed multiple of \(\Delta_{\rm Tav}\) (order \(10\,\mathrm{GeV}\) at the working print). This is an internal protocol cost, not a cosmological density.

2. **Residual-L / binding energies**  
   Each domain may be assigned the residual-L energy of the globs it participates in. The sum is then the total hierarchical binding energy. By the dominant-glob rule the effective \(G\) of any set is already that of the largest coherent bound set; summing the six labels does not create a new gravitational source or a new value of \(G\).

3. **Unbound wall contribution**  
   The Mirron/Entropic wall potential is already a sum over completed unbound bounces, independent of domain label. Partitioning that sum into six domain bins and re-adding them returns the same \(V_{\rm wall}\). No new scale appears.

4. **Laboratory Casimir**  
   As in the preceding note,
   \[
   E_{\rm Cas}^{\rm tot}
   =
   \sum_{a=1}^6
   E_{\rm Cas}^{(a)}.
   \]  
   The result remains a floor quantity. Conversion to a density still requires an unfixed reference volume, and dynamical use as \(\rho_\Lambda\) conflicts with the 2026-10-07 author cut.

## Does the sum produce lambda?

No derivation is obtained for any of the four operators.

- Domain-cross total is \(\mathcal{O}(10\,\mathrm{GeV})\) per set of hops—an atomic/nuclear scale, not a cosmological energy density.
- Residual-L sum is already the hierarchical account of laboratory \(G\); it does not source \(\rho_\Lambda\).
- Unbound wall sum is the existing non-locking thermodynamic candidate; re-binning by domain does not evaluate its magnitude.
- Casimir sum is excluded as a source by the author cut and still needs a free cutoff to approach \(\rho_\Lambda\).

A purely formal total can be written

\[
E_{\rm tot}
=
E_{\rm cross}^{\rm tot}
+
E_{\rm residual\text{-}L}^{\rm tot}
+
V_{\rm wall}
+
E_{\rm Cas}^{\rm tot},
\]

but each term is either already accounted for, internally fixed at the wrong scale, or forbidden as a dynamical source of lambda. No combination derives the observed acceleration.
