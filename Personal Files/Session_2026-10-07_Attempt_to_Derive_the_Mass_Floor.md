Those ingredients give:

\[
E_{\rm violation}\ge\Delta,\qquad\Delta>0,
\]

and dimensionless labels \(1,2,3,4,5,6\). **Key.** \(E_{\rm violation}\): overlap cost. \(\Delta\): the unknown quantum. An inequality plus a finite group has no MeV in it. \(\hbar\) and \(c\) turn a mass into a length only after a mass is supplied. They do not choose the mass.

- Planck average \(M_{\rm Pl}/(6\langle\gamma\rangle_6\sqrt{N})\). Uses \(G\), which this cut leaves alone, and \(N\) was never measured into \(313.1\). Retired.

- Casimir of a wall. Refused this morning. Matching length for \(\rho_\Lambda\) is \(0.086\,\mathrm{mm}\), not a quark mass.
