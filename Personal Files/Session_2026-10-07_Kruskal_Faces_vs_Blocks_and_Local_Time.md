## 0. Verdict

**The refusal is about axes, not about forbidding six copies.** One metric has four Kruskal regions. Six faces sit on the ring angle of one shared engine. Those are different cuts. Calling each face a Kruskal block multiplies 4-geometries in the wrong place.

The interaction the corpus writes between a domain and its antimatter conjugate is an internal hop, paid as domain-cross work, read through the kernel. It is not a closed timelike curve. A time increment per interaction is the right kind of bookkeeping to avoid the same event. If that increment is real, the passage is not a CTC. The notes do not derive the increment, and they do not identify the hop with \(r<0\).

## 3. The two axes

### 3.1 Causal blocks of one metric

Start with Schwarzschild, because Kruskal is simplest there. Boyer–Lindquist / Schwarzschild coordinates break at \(r=2M\). Kruskal–Szekeres \((T,X)\) covers the maximal analytic vacuum extension. One manifold. Four regions:

| Block | What it is | In a collapse |
|-------|------------|----------------|
| I | Our exterior | Kept |
| II | Future interior, black | Kept if the star collapses |
| III | Other exterior, joined by a non-traversable throat | Cut off. Not another universe |
| IV | Past interior, white | Cut off. Eternal idealization |

Horizons are the lines \(T=\pm X\). The singularity \(r=0\) is a spacelike hyperbola. I and III are two outsides of one vacuum solution. Nothing in that diagram is a second Superblock domain.

Kerr adds structure and does not change the moral. Incomplete at \(r_\pm=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-a^2}\). Inner horizon \(r_-\) is a Cauchy horizon; mass inflation; global hyperbolicity dies. The ring is \(r=0\), \(\theta=\pi/2\). The disk \(r=0\), \(\theta\neq\pi/2\) is regular and opens \(r<0\). The Carter diagram at fixed polar angle is a stack of diamonds, black then white then black. Identifications can cut the stack to four sheets. Without them the stack is infinite. CTCs sit in \(r<0\), near the ring. The note’s word for that sheet is “antiverse,” in quotes, meaning the other asymptotic of this vacuum extension. Section 1 already said the analogous Schwarzschild sheet is not a hidden domain. The same refusal covers the Kerr sheet.

### 3.2 Faces on the ring

The Superblock cut is around the shared ring:

\[
\chi\in[0,2\pi),
\qquad
\text{face }i=\text{sector }\varphi_i\to\varphi_{i+1}.
\]

**Key.** \(\chi\): ring angle. Face: a sector of that angle. Colour B–W–B–W–B–W is painted on these sectors. Six domains are six co-located copies attached to this one ring, or six ways of reading its exterior through the kernel. They are not six diamonds stacked in \((T,X)\).

Crossing a joint therefore does not mean “enter region III” or “enter \(r<0\).” It means the exterior you are reading is now copy \(j\) rather than copy \(i\), via \(K\) or via binding. The metric blocks of copy \(i\) stay the blocks of copy \(i\).

That is the whole refusal. Six copies are allowed. Six extra Kruskal blocks, drawn as the six domains, are the wrong multiplication. One PK seed per domain was the overview’s version of that multiplication: it gives each face its own engine instead of a sector of the shared one.

## 4. Matter / antimatter is not that curve

The corpus interaction of a domain with its antimatter partner is the antipodal hop on the octahedron, 29 September:

\[
E_\times(a\to\bar a)=\pi\Delta_{\rm Tav}=983.633\,\mathrm{MeV}
\]

at the printed floor. **Key.** \(E_\times\): work to drag one bound line from \(D_a\) to its conjugate \(D_{\bar a}\). \(\pi\): antipodal internal angle. \(\Delta_{\rm Tav}=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\): inserted floor. The two copies share external events. The wall is internal. This hop is not a jump I\(\to\)III and not a loop in \(r<0\).

So “the CTC has always been the way the envelopes interact” is not what the notes say. The notes say kernel / binding / domain-cross. The CTC is a region of one vacuum extension, flagged and not ridden.

## 5. Local time increment

The charge: each interaction increments local time in each block, so the next cycle is not the same local time.

If that increment is nonzero,

\[
(t_i,x)\;\longrightarrow\;(t_i+\Delta t_i,\,x'),
\qquad \Delta t_i\neq 0,
\]

the end event is not the start event. **Key.** \(t_i\): local time of envelope \(i\). \(\Delta t_i\): advance booked on that hop. \(x'\): spatial or internal arrival. A curve through two distinct events is not a closed timelike curve. The bookkeeping that would tame the paradox is the bookkeeping that removes the CTC.

The notes do not derive \(\Delta t_i\). The 21 July taming was a change of octonion phase at the same spatial point, closed on the retired cylinder. The previous written progression of \(\pm T_1\) summed to \(0\). A derived nonzero advance would be a different object from the \(r<0\) curve, and it would still not turn Kruskal III into a domain.
