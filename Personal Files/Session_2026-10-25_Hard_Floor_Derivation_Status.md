**Topic:** Is there any way to derive the hard floor from the corpus as it stands?

## Answer
**No.** As the hard corpus stands, there is no derivation of the TEP floor from the existing elements.

---

## Current Status of the Floor
The TEP floor is protocol step 1:
\[
\Delta=\frac{\hbar c}{r_M}=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV},
\]
the Compton dual of the Mirron radius \(r_M\approx 0.630\,\mathrm{fm}\).

It is an input fixed by that geometric radius. It is not an output of the residual-\(L\) gravity, the light-cone hard-wall structure, the incidence relation, the shear/Weyl evaluation, the monodromy unwind, or the hierarchical rule.

---

## Why the Present Corpus Does Not Derive It
- The gravitational core uses \(r_M\) as the scale of the KK cylinder and Mirron, but the matching that recovers laboratory \(G\) fixes only the product \(\kappa\langle L_{\rm res}\rangle\). It does not determine the absolute energy \(\hbar c/r_M\).
- The optical scalars \(\sigma\) and \(\Psi_0\) scale with \(L_{\rm res}/r^n\); after the bound-sector average they reproduce the Newtonian field. No residue of those expressions equals 313.1 MeV.
- The monodromy unwind condition at the light-cone hard-wall \(\mathcal{W}\) sets unbound \(L_{\rm res}\to 0\); it supplies no mass-gap or floor value.
- Prior corpus decisions already exclude the routes that were once considered:
  - Vălean weight-6 identities are retired as TEP generators.
  - Near-miss numerical maps are not IN.
  - The floor is explicitly independent of the light-cone identification and the shear/Weyl results.

No combination of the hard-corpus relations yields \(\Delta\) as a derived quantity. The numerical discrepancy with 309.3 MeV therefore remains open and external to the completed gravitational core.
