## The postulate restated

The geometric immediate (\(t=0\)) point of simultaneity (UPS) cascades, by the recursive midpoint / contraction already latent in earlier UPS language, until it reaches a single universal +1 (privileged observer). The energy associated with that cascade is asked to match the sum of

- the thermodynamic lambda (entropic wall-potential gradient),
- the kinetic energy of domain rotation,
- the energy of revolution of globs around their common center of gravity.

## Exploratory construction

A pairwise simultaneity map on co-located domains contracts, under a completeness assumption, to one UPS per tick:

\[
\text{UPS}_{\rm tick}
=
\lim_{n\to\infty}
\text{midpoint}^{(n)}(\text{events of the tick}).
\]

**Key.** \(\text{midpoint}^{(n)}\): \(n\)-fold application of a binary simultaneity operation defined on the co-located envelope. The limit is the geometric \(t=0\) locus of that tick.

A further cascade over ticks (or over hierarchical globs) yields a universal point:

\[
\text{UPS}_{+1}
=
\lim
\text{cascade}(\text{UPS}_{\rm tick}).
\]

**Key.** \(\text{UPS}_{+1}\): the universal privileged-observer locus. Off-books.

The energy one might associate with the cascade is not supplied by the stamp itself (the stamp remains kinematic). Possible non-locking carriers are:

- the modulation of the unbound reservoir already resident on the wall,
  \[
  \delta V_{\rm wall}
  \propto
  g(\delta t_N,\,\text{cascade depth})\,E_{\rm unbound},
  \]
- or a phenomenological conversion of the relative-time contrast into an effective stress-energy that contributes to \(\Lambda_{\rm thermo}\).

Domain rotation energy scales with the envelope velocities \(\beta_i\) of the six domains; revolution energy scales with the residual-L binding of each glob about the dominant center of gravity. Both are already accounted for by the hierarchical residual-L sector and do not require a new source.

## Can the numbers match?

No derivation is obtained.  

- The UPS stamp is kinematic: it advances the phase of already-present floor excitations by \(\Delta\phi_{\rm tick}\). It does not deposit residual \(L\) and, by the 2026-10-18 cuts, does not source \(\rho_\Lambda\).
- Any energy attributed to the cascade must therefore be re-interpreted as a modulation of the existing unbound wall potential or as a free conversion factor multiplying \(\delta t_N\). Both leave the coefficient fitted to the observed acceleration.
- Domain rotation and revolution around the common center of \(G\) are already residual-L phenomena; their energy is set by the bound-sector density and the dominant-glob rule. Adding them to the lambda budget does not create a new derivation; it merely enlarges the phenomenological target that the fitted \(\Lambda_{\rm thermo}\) is asked to meet.
- The universal +1 remains an auxiliary label. Promoting it to a dynamical energy reservoir would re-introduce a preferred frame and would require an explicit operator (not supplied) that converts the cascade into stress-energy without violating the kinematic character of Φ₈ or the residual-L account of laboratory \(G\).

Thus the postulate can be written, but the energy match stays fitted. It does not evaluate the unbound reservoir, does not fix the conversion constants, and does not derive \(\rho_\Lambda\).
