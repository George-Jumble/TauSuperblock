# Session Log: Macroscopic Kerr-Ring Black → White Loop
**Date:** 2026-10-25  
**Project:** Tau-Superblock Universe  
**Topic:** Examine the Kerr ring black → white loop in terms of a macroscopic Kerr ring

---

## Scale and Location
A macroscopic Kerr ring lies at astrophysical radius \(R_{\rm ring}\gg r_M\). Every such ring remains inside some local Tau cell whose causal boundary is the light-cone hard-wall \(\mathcal{W}\) of that cell’s \(T_{\rm priv}\) locality.

The macroscopic ring is not a Mirron and does not replace the microscopic KK cylinder. Residual-\(L\) originates only at the Planck-scale Mirrons (\(\Sigma_{\mathrm{M}}\), \(r_M\approx 0.630\,\mathrm{fm}\)).

---

## Black → White Transition at Macroscopic Radius
- **In-going (black) phase**: trajectories reaching the macroscopic ring follow classical Kerr geometry. They do not lock a new species of residual angular momentum. Any \(L_{\rm res}\) they carry was already set by microscopic Mirrons.
- **Out-going (white) phase**: the ring may reverse radial momentum for a subset of generators. Those generators remain ordinary null rays of the cell; they terminate on the same light-cone hard-wall \(\mathcal{W}\) and satisfy the unbound unwind
  \[
  \phi_{\rm mon}\to 0\quad\Rightarrow\quad L_{\rm res}\to 0,\qquad
  \sigma\big|_{\mathcal{W}}^{\rm unbound}=0,\qquad
  \Psi_0\big|_{\mathcal{W}}^{\rm unbound}=0.
  \]

No additional monodromy is generated at \(R_{\rm ring}\). The incidence relation, shear, and Weyl expressions derived for microscopic residual-\(L\) are unchanged.

---

## Hierarchical Gravity
The macroscopic ring may dominate the local mass \(M_{\rm eff}\) appearing in
\[
\kappa\langle L_{\rm res}\rangle = \frac{G M_{\rm eff}}{c^2}.
\]
This identifies which glob’s \(T_{\rm priv}\) is dominant; it does not alter the microscopic origin of \(\langle L_{\rm res}\rangle\) or the value of \(\kappa\). The hierarchical rule and laboratory \(G\) remain intact.

---

## Causal Structure
- All null generators associated with the macroscopic ring lie inside the light cone of the enclosing \(T_{\rm priv}\) and end on \(\mathcal{W}\).
- The ring does not create a second causal boundary. The only hard-wall is the light-cone wall of the cell.
- Nested cells continue to nest by inclusion of light cones.

---

## Points of Care
- The corpus supplies no mechanism by which the macroscopic ring itself refreshes or locks residual-\(L\). Black → white behaviour at \(R_{\rm ring}\) is a classical or semi-classical reflection, not a Mirron-type dynamic refresh.
- Trapped null rays, once they escape, are still required to reach \(\mathcal{W}\). The light-cone hard-wall remains the ultimate causal cutoff.
- The hard floor \(\Delta=\hbar c/r_M=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) is unaffected.

---

## Exclusions
- No promotion of the macroscopic ring to a cell-defining or universal hard-wall.
- No new global monodromy.
- No re-introduction of rejected 7 Mpc phenomenology.
- No change to the bound/unbound split enforced at \(\mathcal{W}\).

## Verdict
A macroscopic Kerr-ring black → white loop is causally allowed inside a cell but does not participate in residual-\(L\) locking or in the definition of the hard-wall. It can dominate \(M_{\rm eff}\) and therefore the local hierarchical \(G_{\rm eff}\), yet all null generators still terminate on the light-cone wall with the standard unbound unwind. The microscopic Mirron remains the sole engine of the residual-\(L\) source. The hard corpus is preserved.

---

## Plain-Language Summary
A large Kerr ring (the kind that could sit inside a real black hole) can bounce some light rays from incoming to outgoing, but it does not create the residual spin that makes gravity. That spin still comes only from the tiny Mirron rings. All the light, whether it bounced off the big ring or not, still ends at the outer light-cone wall of the cell and loses any leftover spin there. Ordinary gravity continues to work exactly as before.
