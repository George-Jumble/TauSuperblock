# Session 2026-10-24: Can a Constant Be Derived for Diminishment of Residue on the Dominant Light-Cone of a \(T_{\rm priv}\)?

**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Query:** Given the hard wall (causal separation) of universal expansion, and the fact that some galaxies we see from the early universe have had time to expand to 2x the universal hard-wall since the time of the emission of the residual radiation we are detecting, can we derive a constant for the diminishment of the residue left on the dominant light-cone of any given \(T_{\rm priv}\) as it is causally separated from other points in the local 4D manifold?  
**Context:** Hard wall = conformal causal edge of occupancy. Each \(T_{\rm priv}\) owns its own light-cone; cross-domain / hierarchical link weakens with null volume or depth (prior coefficients unfixed). Residual-\(L\) matches laboratory \(G\) via dominant-glob rule. UPS kinematic. Tau package rejected. External cosmology: particle horizon \(\approx 46\,\mathrm{Gly}\); high-\(z\) sources have proper distances that have grown by a factor of order 2 relative to emission-era scales and now lie outside future event-horizon contact.  
**Status:** No constant derived. A diminishment function can be written and phenomenologically referenced to the horizon growth; prefactors remain free.

## Observational context (external input)

Particle horizon \(\approx 46\,\mathrm{Gly}\). Light from a \(z\sim 14\) galaxy has traveled \(\sim 13.5\,\mathrm{Gyr}\) while the source’s present proper distance is \(\sim 30{-}34\,\mathrm{Gly}\). Future signals from that locus do not reach us. The factor \(\sim 2\times\) is a rough description of that growth relative to the emission-era causal scale; it is an external calibration point, not a Superblock derivation.

## Formal diminishment

\[
R_{\rm residue}(\mathcal{L})
\;=\;
R_0
\cdot
f\Bigl(
\frac{d_{\rm proper}}{d_{\rm wall}},\,
\frac{\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{L})}{\ell_{\rm wall}^3}
\Bigr).
\]

Minimal forms consistent with prior weakening language:
\[
f
\;=\;
\Bigl(1+\alpha\frac{d_{\rm proper}}{d_{\rm wall}}\Bigr)^{-1}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\exp\bigl(-\beta\,\Delta\tau_{\rm hier}\bigr).
\]

**Key.** The horizon-scale observation may set a reference value of the argument (e.g. \(d_{\rm proper}/d_{\rm wall}\sim 2\)) but leaves \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) free.

---

## Why no derivation

- Locked structure supplies residual-\(L\) inside bound globs and a kinematic UPS stamp. It does not evaluate an optical-depth coefficient.
- The hard wall is the causal boundary; dilution toward it is not fixed by the dominant-glob rule, the G2 floor clock, or the protocol floor.
- Choosing \(\alpha\) or \(\beta\) so that \(f\) takes any particular value at \(d_{\rm proper}/d_{\rm wall}\sim 2\) is a fit to the external horizon scale, not an output.
- Residual-\(L\) (laboratory \(G\)) continues to be set by the dominant glob’s stopped rebounds; the cone-residue factor is a large-scale matching coefficient, screened inside bound systems.
