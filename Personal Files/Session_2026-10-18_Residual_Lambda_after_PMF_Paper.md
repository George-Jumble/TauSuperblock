The PMF model reduces the tension from \(\sim5\sigma\) to \(\approx2.7\sigma\) in the SH0ES-calibrated combination. It does not eliminate it.

The late-time dark-energy sector remains a cosmological constant (\(w=-1\)). PMFs act only on recombination (sound horizon).

## Residual Lambda

In flat cosmology \(\Omega_\Lambda=1-\Omega_m\).

- ΛCDM (Planck+DESI): \(\Omega_\Lambda\approx0.6934\)
- bΛCDM (Planck+DESI+SH0ES-calibrated): \(\Omega_\Lambda\approx0.7083\)

The shift in \(\Omega_\Lambda\) is \(\approx+0.015\). This is a correlated byproduct of the higher \(H_0\) and lower \(\Omega_m\) permitted by the shorter sound horizon; it is not an independent adjustment of the dark-energy density.

### Residual tension in \(H_0\)
\[
\Delta H_0\approx73.0-69.93=3.07\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}
\]
(about \(2.7\sigma\) given the quoted errors).  

To close the remaining gap while keeping the PMF-recombination solution fixed, an additional late-time contribution (or a further modification of the expansion history) would still be required. In terms of an effective cosmological-constant density the residual is model-dependent, but the fractional shortfall in \(H_0\) is
\[
\frac{\Delta H_0}{H_0^\mathrm{SH0ES}}\approx0.042
\]
(4.2 %). Because \(H_0^2\propto\rho_\mathrm{crit}\) and \(\Omega_\Lambda\) is already near 0.7, a pure late-time \(\Delta\rho_\Lambda\) large enough to supply the missing \(\Delta H_0\) would be of order a few percent of the present \(\rho_\Lambda\), subject to the usual geometric degeneracies with \(\Omega_m\) and the sound horizon.

No unique “residual \(\lambda\)” is fixed by the paper; the PMF solution leaves a \(\approx2.7\sigma\) Hubble residual that any additional dark-energy adjustment would have to absorb.

## Conclusion

Assuming the PMF paper is accurate, bΛCDM raises \(H_0\) to \(\approx69.9\) km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ and shifts \(\Omega_\Lambda\) upward by \(\approx0.015\) to \(\approx0.708\). A residual Hubble tension of \(\approx3\) km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ (\(\approx2.7\sigma\), or \(\approx4\%\)) remains relative to SH0ES. That residual is the quantity still to be addressed by any additional late-time dark-energy (or other expansion-history) contribution. The paper does not itself compute or require a specific residual \(\lambda\); it leaves the gap for further physics while keeping \(w=-1\).
