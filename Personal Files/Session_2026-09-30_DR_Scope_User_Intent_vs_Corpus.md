Your “ticks are UPS instantiation” *is* what those notes were written to be. What the corpus did *not* do is put a hard fence between that tick and DR. \(\Phi_8\) was used as both “reset of a bound colour line” and “Dynamic Refresh tick” and, in the photon notes, “frame rewrite.” Those three jobs need three names. You just supplied the fence:
