## 0. Verdict

**The current mass can be an anchor. It is not the strong binding energy.**

Laboratory QCD puts the strong binding in the dressing, not in \(m_u^{\rm cur}\). The current mass is the Higgs remainder, already present in the Lagrangian of a free quark. Anchoring \(\Delta\) on it prints a few MeV, not \(309.3\). Anchoring on the phase-boundary transit is circular: the transit work was defined as \(\theta\Delta\).

## 2. What the current mass is

A current mass is the mass term in the QCD Lagrangian, \(m_q\bar q q\), fixed by the Higgs vacuum expectation value and the Yukawa coupling. It is there for a quark that is not inside a hadron. Lattice and sum-rule determinations of \(m_u^{\rm cur}\) do not require the nucleon mass.

The nucleon budget runs the other way from the proposal:

\[
M_n
=
m_u^{\rm cur}+2m_d^{\rm cur}
+
E_{\rm dress}^{(n)}.
\]

**Key.** First three terms: current masses, about \(11.6\,\mathrm{MeV}\) in the neutron. \(E_{\rm dress}^{(n)}=928.0\,\mathrm{MeV}\): everything else — gluon field, quark kinetic energy, condensate, trace anomaly. That remainder is the strong binding. The current mass is the small piece that is not generated by the binding.

“In the process of being bound” describes a quark that already has \(m^{\rm cur}\) and is acquiring dressing. It does not identify \(m^{\rm cur}\) with the added strong mass.

## 3. If the current mass is the anchor anyway

Allowed as an external number, because it is not defined as \(M_n/3\). A clock derivation would still need \(f\) written first:

\[
\Delta_{\rm clock}=f\,m_u^{\rm cur}.
\]

**Key.** \(m_u^{\rm cur}=2.16\,\mathrm{MeV}\). \(f\): clock function, unwritten. With \(f=1\), the floor prints \(2.16\,\mathrm{MeV}\). That is a different protocol from \(309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}\). It is the Higgs remainder taken as the quantum. It is not the per-line colour dressing. No clock \(f\) is on hand that turns \(2.16\) into \(309.3\) without being fitted to that ratio, \(309.3/2.16\approx 143\).

## 4. Transit of the phase boundary

The 29 September derivation defined the transit work from the floor:

\[
E_\times(a\to b)=\theta_{ab}\,\Delta.
\]

**Key.** \(E_\times\): work to drag one bound line across the internal angle. \(\theta_{ab}\): that angle. \(\Delta\): the floor. Adjacent hop \(491.8\,\mathrm{MeV}\) and antipodal hop \(983.6\,\mathrm{MeV}\) were \(313.1\) times \(\pi/2\) and \(\pi\). They are not an independent measure of \(\Delta\). Solving for the floor gives \(\Delta=E_\times/\theta\), which is the definition read backwards.

An independent transit energy would be a measured excitation at the scrape that was not computed from \(\Delta\). None is in the notes. Setting that energy equal to \(m_u^{\rm cur}\) is a new protocol, and it prints a few MeV, as above.
