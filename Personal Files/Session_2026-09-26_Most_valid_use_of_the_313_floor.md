# Session 2026-09-26 — Most valid way to use the inserted 313.1 MeV floor

**Question:** Of “use the nucleon” and “share equally among three valence lines,” which is the most valid way to use the number?  
**Short answer:** Using the nucleon is the strong choice. Equal share is the weak choice. The most valid *use* of the resulting number is as the equal-share IR energy of one valence line in the nucleon — the bookkeeping quantum of bound colour dressing — not as a particle, a pole, or a derived theorem.

## 2. The two choices are not on a par

| Choice | Strength | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Use the nucleon | Strong | Lightest stable three-quark state. Mass known to parts in \(10^{10}\). It is the actual object whose inertia is almost all colour dressing. |
| Share equally among three valence lines | Weak | Convenient bookkeeping. Proton is \(uud\), neutron is \(udd\). Current masses differ. Kinetic energy, confinement, hyperfine, and EM self-energy are not split 1:1:1. |

So if the question is which *protocol step* is more valid: **use the nucleon**. That is data. Equal share is a modelling decision that turns one measured mass into a per-line quantum.

A more valid share, if one wants to tighten the insertion without pretending it is a derivation:

\[
E_{\mathrm{dress}}^{(n)}
=
M_n-m_u^{\mathrm{cur}}-2m_d^{\mathrm{cur}}
=928.01\,\mathrm{MeV},
\qquad
\frac{E_{\mathrm{dress}}^{(n)}}{3}
=309.34\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]

That still assumes equal *dressing*, not equal *total* mass. It is better physics and it is no longer the printed \(313.1\). Keep \(313.1\) as the crude equal-share of the whole nucleon. Do not upgrade the digits until a calculation needs the current-mass split.

Do not replace “use the nucleon” by “use the current up quark.” That was never a valid insertion. Do not replace it by “use \(\mu_p\) to get \(336\,\mathrm{MeV}\).” That is a different operator (magnetic moment), not the mass budget.

## 3. Most valid use of the number, once inserted

Ranked.

**1. Allowed and primary — per-line IR inertia in the nucleon.**  
One valence line in \(n\) or \(p\) carries about \(313\,\mathrm{MeV}\) of inertia under equal share. Almost all of that is colour dressing. This is the home of \(\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}=E_g=E_{q\leftarrow g}\) in the 14 September lock.

**2. Allowed and secondary — TEP cost scale.**  
TEP says a forced bound \(\Phi_7\to\Phi_8\) overlap costs at least one dressing quantum. Using the nucleon-third as that quantum is a naming, not a proof. Valid as long as one does not say TEP computed the digits.

**3. Allowed and definitional — Compton clock.**  
\(\lambda_{m_0}=\hbar c/\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}=0.6302\,\mathrm{fm}\) is what that energy *is*, as a length. Valid. Not an extra observable.

**4. Allowed as bookkeeping — C0 packing quantum.**  
\(n_{\mathrm{M}}=\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}/\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}\) counts engines if each engine stores one dressing quantum of vacuum energy. Valid only as bookkeeping. Not a derivation of \(\Lambda\).

**5. Allowed as a ruler — analysis origin \(M_0\).**  
Dimuon scans may start a periodogram at \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\). That does not make a resonance.

**Not valid uses**

- A new PDG particle or \(0^{++}\) glueball at \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\).
- The current up mass, or an energy input *of* the current up field.
- Output of a named Vălean weight-6 identity.
- KK quantum of a megaparsec circle.
- Magnetic-moment constituent mass \(\approx 336\,\mathrm{MeV}\) treated as the same object.
- A parameter-free prediction.

The most valid way to use \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) is as the equal-share IR energy of one valence line in the nucleon, inserted as the quantum of bound colour dressing. “Use the nucleon” is the data. “Share equally” is the bookkeeping.
