# Aeon-enveloping twistor on phase 8 — 4D, not KK

**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Question:** can a Tau-*like* big circle exist as an aeon-enveloping twistor on the 8th phase (absolute past / absolute future bounce) in four dimensions?  
**Answer:** yes, if the circle is time or null. No, if it is a fifth spacelike axis.

---

## 1. Two different cylinders

**Tau (dropped).** Spacelike \(S^1_\tau\), radius \(7\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\), metric extra direction, Casimir \(\rho\sim 1/\tau^4\). That is five-dimensional kinematics even when people draw it as a picture.

**This object.** An identification of absolute past with absolute future along a twistor (a null geodesic / light-ray), ticked on phase 8. The manifold stays four-dimensional.

**Key.** Twistor, here: a light ray, or the \(\mathbb{CP}^1\) of rays through a point. Phase 8: Mirron reset tick. Bounce: future-directed ray reflected to past-directed, or \(\mathscr{I}^+\) paired with \(\mathscr{I}^-\).

## 2. Where a 4D “big circle” is allowed

Conformal compactification of a 4-world already has \(\mathscr{I}^\pm\).

- Minkowski: \(\mathscr{I}^\pm\) are lightlike.
- de Sitter (closed slicing): spacetime topology \(\mathbb{R}\times S^3\). That *is* a cylinder, and it is four-dimensional. Spatial slices are \(S^3\); the axis of the cylinder is time. No fifth coordinate.
- Identify or reflect the two ends (bang \(\leftrightarrow\mathscr{I}^+\), or \(\mathscr{I}^-\leftrightarrow\mathscr{I}^+\)) and conformal time becomes a segment with a bounce, or an \(S^1\) if you identify rather than reflect.

A metric timelike \(S^1\) (clocks that return to the same event) is a closed timelike curve. Allowed as a 4-geometry (Gödel-type), poisonous as physics. Do not do that.

A **bounce** is different: the ray hits a mirror and reverses conformal orientation. That is a map, not a CTC. Phase 8 is the tick of that map. Mirron language already had this: Absolute Future arrested, rebound toward Absolute Past, present as the cut.

**Key.** \(\mathbb{R}\times S^3\): Einstein / closed-dS cylinder, 4D. CTC: metric time loop — off. Bounce: reflection on the conformal boundary — on.

## 3. Twistor envelope

In 4D twistor theory a point of spacetime is a Riemann sphere of light rays. An aeon, conformally, is a region whose null boundary is generated by twistors.

An **aeon-enveloping twistor** is then a congruence of generators of that boundary — the light that skins the aeon. Phase 8 fires when those generators hit the mirror and the occupancy is rewritten (parent \(\to\) child, or present refresh).

No extra dimension is used. Twistor space is \(\mathbb{CP}^3\), which is not a fifth spacetime axis; it is the space of rays of the same 4-world.

Hopf, from the last note, sits naturally here: \(S^3\to S^2\) with \(S^1\) fibres. Those fibres are the natural circles *inside* \(S^3_{\rm int}\) or inside a closed spatial slice. They can carry phase 8 as a fibre coordinate without being KK electromagnetism and without being \(7\,\mathrm{Mpc}\).

**Key.** Enveloping congruence: null generators of the aeon boundary. \(\mathbb{CP}^3\): space of rays, not a 5th dimension.
