# Session 2026-10-18: Plausibility of the Casimir–Entropic Proportionality Hypothesis

**Date**: 2026-10-18  
**Query**: Is the proportionality hypothesis plausible?

## Hypothesis under examination

The Casimir potential is proportional to the dark energy of the entropic force (Mirron/Entropic wall potential):
\[
V_{\rm Cas}\propto\rho_{\rm ent}\qquad\text{or}\qquad\rho_{\rm Cas}\propto\rho_{\rm ent}.
\]

Terminology reminder:
- Laboratory Casimir: floor Lifshitz pressure; author cut refuses it as source of \(G\) or \(\rho_\Lambda\).
- Mirron/Entropic wall potential: collective unbound-Mirron potential at the conformal hard-wall; gradient is the entropic force. Non-locking; strength fitted.

## Plausibility assessment

### Mathematically possible
Yes. One can always introduce a free factor \(\kappa\) or a wall cutoff \(\ell_{\rm wall}\) such that
\[
\rho_{\rm ent}=\kappa\,\rho_{\rm Cas}(\ell_{\rm wall})
\]
holds by construction after fitting. No contradiction with the residual-\(L\) equations or the 4D arena arises if the proportionality is purely kinematic and laboratory Casimir is not the energy source.

### Physically natural / derived?
No, not from the locked structure.
- Residual-\(L\), the 142857 cycle, the floor protocol (309.3 MeV), and the kinematic \(\Phi_8\) stamp supply no relation that fixes \(\kappa\) or \(\ell_{\rm wall}\).
- Laboratory Casimir densities at typical experimental separations differ from the observed \(\rho_\Lambda\sim5\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}\) by \(10^{20}\)–\(10^{30}\) or more. The pure-Casimir matching length is \(\sim0.086\) mm. Any proportionality therefore requires either that specific length or an equivalent suppression factor of enormous size. Neither is supplied by existing locks.
- A dynamical reading (Casimir energy converted into the Mirron wall potential that sources \(\rho_\Lambda\)) conflicts with the 2026-10-07 author cut.

### Empirical / theoretical precedent
Proportionality between a vacuum-energy expression and the cosmological term has been explored in the literature, but it invariably reintroduces a free scale or cutoff to bridge the hierarchy. No standard result predicts that the laboratory Casimir potential (or a simple multiple of it) equals the observed dark-energy density without such tuning. Inside Superblock the same gap remains.

### Conditions that would raise plausibility
The hypothesis becomes more plausible if a distinctive, parameter-free signature appears, for example:
- a wall cutoff \(\ell_{\rm wall}\) fixed by Mirron spacing or by the 142857 clock that automatically yields the observed \(\rho_\Lambda\) within uncertainties;
- a measurable correlation between void expansion rate and a Casimir-scale observable that matches the proportionality without additional fitting;
- a screening or hierarchical suppression factor already present in residual-\(L\) globbing that evaluates to the required \(10^{20}\)–\(10^{30}\).

None of these is currently exhibited.

## Equations (complete key)

Observed dark-energy density:
\[
\rho_\Lambda\sim5\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}
\]
- External input.

Laboratory Casimir energy-density scale:
\[
\rho_{\rm Cas}\sim\frac{\hbar c}{\ell^4}
\]
- \(\ell\): separation.
- At \(\ell\sim0.086\) mm, \(\rho_{\rm Cas}\sim\rho_\Lambda\); elsewhere the ratio is huge.

Proportionality (non-locking):
\[
\rho_{\rm ent}=\kappa\,\rho_{\rm Cas}(\ell_{\rm wall})
\]
- \(\kappa\): free factor.
- \(\ell_{\rm wall}\): free or unfixed cutoff.
- Equality after choice of \(\kappa\) or \(\ell_{\rm wall}\) is fitting, not derivation.

Mirron/Entropic wall potential (independent definition):
\[
V_{\rm wall}=\sum_i V_i(R_{\rm wall};\mathbf{x}_i)
\]
- Collective Mirron potential; gradient is entropic force.

## Conclusion

The proportionality hypothesis is mathematically possible as a fitted scaling relation and does not immediately break residual-\(L\) matching to laboratory \(G\) or the 4D frame, provided laboratory Casimir is not the dynamical source. It is not plausible as a derived or natural consequence of the locked Superblock structure: the required suppression or matching scale is not supplied by residual \(L\), the clock, or the floor protocol, and a dynamical identification conflicts with the author cut. Plausibility would increase only if a parameter-free signature fixing \(\kappa\) or \(\ell_{\rm wall}\) is exhibited. Until then the connection remains an open, non-locking fitting hypothesis of low theoretical compulsion.
