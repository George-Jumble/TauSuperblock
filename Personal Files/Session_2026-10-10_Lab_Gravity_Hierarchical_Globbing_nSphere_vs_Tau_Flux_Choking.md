# Session 2026-10-10: Can Lab-Gravity, Hierarchical Globbing and Nested n-Spheres Replicate Tau-Circle Flux Choking for Hot Filaments and Cold Voids?

**Date**: 2026-10-10  
**Project**: Tau-Superblock Universe (main SC frame)  
**Status**: Exploratory. Tau metric package remains hard-rejected. Nested n-spheres remain an off-books construction. No derivation of observed temperatures or of \(\rho_\Lambda\).

## Question
Using laboratory gravity (residual-\(L\)), hierarchical globbing, and the n-sphere, can the flux-choking and flux-opening formerly attributed to the Tau circle be replicated, contributing to hot filaments and cold voids as a result of the fractal nature of expanding space?

## Complete key
- Residual \(L\): angular momentum left by a rebound stopped at the Present; source of laboratory \(G\) via spatial compression. No graviton.
- Hierarchical globbing: successive generations (hadronic, nuclear, atomic, molecular/condensed, \ldots). Effective \(G\) belongs to the dominant glob’s centre of gravity. Children become internals of the parent.
- Nested n-spheres: off-books family \(R_g=R_0\lambda^g\) with \(\lambda=7\), branching \(N=6\), so that \(d_f=\log_7 6=1-\alpha\) with \(\alpha\approx 0.079218\). Overlap by containment. Not a metric fifth dimension.
- Flux choking: residual-\(L\) pinch of a local gap, \(\rho_{\rm ch}=\rho(L/L_{\rm ch})^4\). Node mass is an input; choke is the output.
- Fractal expanding space: the hierarchical tree of superblocks itself, growing without a Tau circle.
- Hot filaments / cold voids: observed cosmic-web temperature and density contrast. Not derived here.
- Tau circle: hard-rejected metric package (\(\tau\), \(n_{\rm hier}\), \(\rho_\Lambda=\hbar c/\tau^4\)).

## What the retired Tau circle was supposed to do
The Tau cylinder supplied a hierarchical scale count and a Casimir-like density that could be differentially choked or opened according to local occupancy, producing contrasts between overdense and underdense regions. That package is hard-rejected.

## Live ingredients that remain
1. Residual-\(L\) gravity matches laboratory \(G\). Compression is stronger where the density of bound fields is higher (positive-feedback spatial compression).
2. Hierarchical globbing organises that compression by generation. The dominant glob sets the effective centre of gravity.
3. Nested n-spheres (off-books) supply a geometric scaffolding whose logarithmic scaling reproduces the live anomalous dimension \(\alpha\) by choice of ratio. They are successive shells, overlapping by containment, not a planar grid and not a restoration of \(\tau\).
4. Expanding space is the growth of the fractal tree of superblocks. No additional circle is required.

## Phenomenological replication
In overdense regions (filaments, nodes) the local density of bound lines is higher, so residual-\(L\) compression is stronger. Any local mode gap (Casimir-like or otherwise) that is sensitive to that compression experiences a larger fractional closure \(L\to L_{\rm ch}\). The associated density scales as
\[
\rho_{\rm ch}
=
\rho\left(\frac{L}{L_{\rm ch}}\right)^4.
\]
**Complete key**
- \(\rho\): unchoked density scale.
- \(L\): unchoked gap.
- \(L_{\rm ch}<L\): gap after residual-\(L\) pinch.
- \(\rho_{\rm ch}\): density after the pinch.

In underdense regions (voids) the bound-line density is lower, residual-\(L\) compression is weaker, and the same gaps remain closer to their unchoked size (relative “opening”).  

Because the nested spheres scale geometrically across generations, the choking/opening contrast is fractal: it appears at every hierarchical level with the same logarithmic ratio that defines \(\alpha\). The expanding space is precisely this growing fractal tree; new globs form, new shells appear, and the differential residual-\(L\) compression continues to operate. No metric Tau circle is used.

## Limits (unchanged from prior notes)
- For a \(10^{15}\,M_\odot\) node at \(1\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) acting on the Higgs-depth gap the fractional closure is \(\sim 10^{-44}\). The effect is undetectable at that scale and does not select the node mass. Node mass remains an input.
- The construction does not derive the observed temperatures of filaments or voids, nor does it replace standard shock-heating and adiabatic cooling.
- It does not recover \(\rho_\Lambda\) and does not restore \(\tau\), \(n_{\rm hier}\), or any Casimir identification with a 7 Mpc hoop.
- Nested n-spheres remain off-books; they are a geometric locus for the hierarchical contrasts, not a new dynamical field.

## Verdict
Yes, as a phenomenological analogue. Laboratory residual-\(L\) gravity acting on the hierarchical nested spheres of the expanding fractal tree produces differential choking in overdense regions and relative opening in underdense regions. The fractal scaling is supplied by the same ratio that yields the live \(\alpha\). This replicates the intended contrast (hotter/denser filaments versus cooler/emptier voids) without the retired Tau circle. The replication is not a derivation of the observed web temperatures and does not alter any core lock.
