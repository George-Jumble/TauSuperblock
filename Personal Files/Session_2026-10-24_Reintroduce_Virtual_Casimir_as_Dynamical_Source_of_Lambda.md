## What re-introduction means

Lift the author cut and allow the virtual/Casimir sector to contribute dynamically to the stress-energy that sources accelerated expansion:

\[
\rho_{\rm eff}
=
\rho_{\rm residual\text{-}L}
+
\kappa\,\rho_{\rm Cas}
+
\cdots
\]

**Key.** \(\rho_{\rm residual\text{-}L}\): the hierarchical residual-L contribution that already matches laboratory \(G\) on glob scales. \(\kappa\rho_{\rm Cas}\): the newly admitted dynamical piece. \(\kappa\) must be extremely small (\(\sim 10^{-120}\) in Planck units, or an equivalent cutoff) if \(\rho_{\rm Cas}\) is computed at any ordinary microscopic scale.

## Does it break the framework?

### Observational contradictions
None that are immediate.

- Laboratory gravity continues to be carried by residual-L. Provided the virtual contribution is either screened on laboratory scales or is the same constant term already absorbed into the cosmological constant, local tests (equivalence principle, inverse-square law, Cavendish-type measurements) remain satisfied.
- Cosmological observations already accommodate a small positive \(\rho_\Lambda\). Adding a suppressed Casimir piece does not contradict supernova distances, BAO, or CMB peaks; it merely relocates the fine-tuning into \(\kappa\).
- No new particle or force is required that would have been seen at the LHC or in precision electroweak data.

### Mathematical impossibilities
None.

- The mode sum is finite once a cutoff or boundary condition is stated. No division by zero, no sign indefiniteness that cannot be regulated, and no violation of the dominant-energy condition if the effective equation of state is arranged to be \(w\approx -1\).
- Co-location of the six domains, the kinematic character of the UPS stamp, and the bound/unbound split can all be retained. The virtual contribution simply becomes one more term in the unbound or floor stress-energy.

### What it does cost
- It re-introduces the cosmological-constant problem in its standard form: why is \(\kappa\rho_{\rm Cas}\) equal to the observed density rather than \(10^{120}\) times larger? The framework gains no explanatory power.
- At least one new free parameter (\(\kappa\), or an equivalent cutoff length) appears. Earlier off-books notes already showed that matching requires such a parameter; lifting the cut does not remove it.
- The construction conflicts with the explicit 2026-10-07 author cut. That cut is not a mathematical necessity; it is a scope decision that keeps residual-L as the sole account of laboratory \(G\) and refuses vacuum-fluctuation sourcing of \(\rho_\Lambda\).
- It does not derive the numerical value of \(\rho_\Lambda\) from the clock, the floor protocol, or the residual-L matching condition.

Comparison with earlier lock-removal analysis (2026-10-18): removing the Casimir-source cut is mathematically possible and does not break laboratory physics, but adds free parameters and does not derive \(\rho_\Lambda\). Removing the residual-L direction lock or restoring metric \(\tau\) would be core-breaking. The present proposal belongs to the first class.
