## 1. Simple-language explanation for outsiders

Imagine the universe is not a single explosion that started from nothing at a single moment (the ordinary Big Bang picture). Instead, picture an ever-growing family tree of “rooms” or chambers. Each room expands. When a room gets large enough, its outer boundary becomes a permanent horizon: light and ordinary signals from the outside can no longer reach the inside of a new child room that forms in an isolated void of the parent. The parent and child are still part of the same four-dimensional universe; they are just causally sealed from each other after the isolation.

The “crossover” is the quiet joining surface where the geometry of the parent’s future boundary is matched, through a scale-stretching (conformal) rule, onto the child’s beginning. The matching rule is simple: the stretch factor of one side times the stretch factor of the other side equals \(-1\). Light cones line up. No singularity is required. The process that does the matching is a local reflection performed by a tiny Planck-scale Kerr-type mirror (the Mirron) that lives at the phase-8 reset seam of an eight-slot internal clock. That same mirror also sources the six co-located “copies” of ordinary space-time that sit on top of one another and differ only by an internal frame.

Gravity, in this picture, is not a force carried by particles. It is the leftover spin that remains when a bound colour or electromagnetic “bounce” is stopped at the present moment instead of completing its trip all the way to the absolute future. Unbound light completes the trip and leaves no leftover spin. The tendency of bound material to clump is therefore automatic: more bound material means more leftover spin means stronger spatial compression.

There is no global beginning singularity that everyone shares. There can be a first room (a “mother of mothers”) as a pure ontological statement, but it is not derived, and it is not an infinite black hole. Every later room is born the same way: isolation of a void, a non-zero secular current, and a new six-phase occupancy stabilized by the Mirron reflection. The ordinary Big Bang is replaced by this local, hierarchical, conformal crossover.
