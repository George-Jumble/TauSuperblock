   The stamp stays kinematic. Slices do not source residual \(L\), do not source \(\rho_\Lambda\), and do not convert floor virtuals into a dynamical wall potential. Any thermodynamic reading remains fitted.

4. **Observational contact.**  
   None new. Preferred-frame or global-simultaneity signals continue to be constrained by the same laboratory and cosmological tests that already bound the dominant-glob rule.
