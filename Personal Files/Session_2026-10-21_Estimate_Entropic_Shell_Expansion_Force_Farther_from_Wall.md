# Session 2026-10-21: Estimate of Entropic-Shell Expansion — Force Greater Farther from the Wall, Dropping as Atemporal Photonic Energy Disperses along the Expanding Wall

**Date**: 2026-10-21  
**Query**: so, estimate the expansion of the entropic shell, using the axiom of the further away the wall the greater the force, which drops off as the atemporal photonic energy shell disperses along the expanding wall.  
**Context**: Non-locking Mirron/Entropic wall potential. Wall = locus of completed unbound bounces. Photon = atemporal null, \(d\tau=0\), \(L_{\rm res}=0\). Residual-L matches laboratory \(G\) (bound sector only).

## Axiom

The entropic force is greater the farther the photonic shell is from the wall, and drops off as the atemporal photonic energy shell disperses along the expanding wall.

\[
|\mathbf{F}_{\rm ent}|
\propto
D_{\rm wall}
\times
f_{\rm dispersion},
\]
where \(D_{\rm wall}\) is the proper (or conformal) distance from the shell to the wall, and \(f_{\rm dispersion}\) decreases as the shell expands and its energy dilutes.

**Variable key:**  
- \(\mathbf{F}_{\rm ent}\): entropic force on the shell.  
- \(D_{\rm wall}\): distance from the current photonic shell to the hard wall (locus of completed bounces).  
- \(f_{\rm dispersion}\): dimensionless dilution factor of the unbound photonic energy density (\(0<f\le1\)).  
- Shell: expanding null congruence of unbound photons.  
- Wall: locus of completed unbound bounces (\(L_{\rm res}=0\)).

## Scaling model

Assume a thin spherical (or conformal) shell of unbound photonic energy expanding toward the wall.

Distance to wall:
\[
D_{\rm wall}(t)
=
R_{\rm wall}(t)
-
R_{\rm shell}(t).
\]
**Variable key:**  
- \(R_{\rm wall}\): proper radius of the hard wall (grows with the chamber).  
- \(R_{\rm shell}\): proper radius of the photonic shell.  
- \(t\): coordinate time in the 4D chamber.

Dispersion: energy density of the shell falls by geometric dilution. For a null shell in an expanding background,
\[
\rho_{\rm photon}
\propto
\frac{E_{\rm tot}}{4\pi R_{\rm shell}^2\,\Delta R}
\times
a^{-2}
\quad\text{(or steeper if conformal stretch is included)},
\]
so
\[
f_{\rm dispersion}
=
\left(\frac{R_0}{R_{\rm shell}}\right)^n,
\]
with \(n=2\) for pure area dilution of a thin shell, or \(n=3\)–\(4\) once volume and redshift factors are counted.

**Variable key:**  
- \(\rho_{\rm photon}\): energy density on the shell.  
- \(E_{\rm tot}\): total unbound energy in the shell (conserved until deposition).  
- \(\Delta R\): radial thickness of the shell.  
- \(a\): scale factor.  
- \(R_0\): reference radius.  
- \(n\): dilution index (\(n=2\) thin-shell area; higher if redshift included).

Force law under the axiom:
\[
F_{\rm ent}
=
\alpha\,
D_{\rm wall}
\times
\left(\frac{R_0}{R_{\rm shell}}\right)^n.
\]
**Variable key:**  
- \(\alpha\): fitted strength constant (energy per length squared, or force per length). Not derived from locks.  
- All other symbols as above.

## Expansion estimate

The shell is null, so its coordinate expansion is fixed by the background geometry plus any residual focusing. The entropic force supplies an additional radial acceleration to the shell’s effective radius only in the non-locking reading:

\[
\frac{d^2 R_{\rm shell}}{dt^2}
=
\frac{F_{\rm ent}}{M_{\rm eff}}
=
\frac{\alpha}{M_{\rm eff}}
\bigl(R_{\rm wall}-R_{\rm shell}\bigr)
\left(\frac{R_0}{R_{\rm shell}}\right)^n.
\]
**Variable key:**  
- \(M_{\rm eff}\): effective inertial mass of the shell (unbound energy \(E_{\rm tot}/c^2\); may be set to 1 in geometric units).  
- Acceleration is toward the wall when \(D_{\rm wall}>0\).

Qualitative behaviour:
- Far from the wall (\(D_{\rm wall}\) large, \(R_{\rm shell}\) small): force is large, shell is driven outward rapidly.  
- As \(R_{\rm shell}\) grows, dispersion factor \((R_0/R_{\rm shell})^n\) falls, force weakens.  
- Near the wall (\(D_{\rm wall}\to0\)): force drops to zero by the distance factor as well; remaining energy is deposited on the locus of completed bounces.

Order-of-magnitude scaling (fitted \(\alpha\)):
\[
\dot{R}_{\rm shell}
\sim
\sqrt{
\frac{\alpha}{M_{\rm eff}}
R_{\rm wall}
R_0^n
}
\times
R_{\rm shell}^{1-n/2}
\]
(early, when \(R_{\rm shell}\ll R_{\rm wall}\)).  
For \(n=2\) the velocity scales as \(1/R_{\rm shell}^0\) (roughly constant early push); for \(n=4\) it falls as \(1/R_{\rm shell}\).

No locked value of \(\alpha\), \(R_{\rm wall}\), or \(E_{\rm tot}\) exists, so the absolute expansion rate relative to observed \(H_0\) or \(\rho_\Lambda\) remains a fit. The functional form (force larger farther from the wall, dropping by dispersion) is fixed by the stated axiom.

## Conclusion

Under the stated axiom the entropic shell expands most rapidly when it is farthest from the wall and its energy is least diluted. The force drops both because the distance \(D_{\rm wall}\) shrinks and because the photonic energy disperses along the expanding wall (\(f_{\rm dispersion}\to0\)). The functional form is fixed; the absolute expansion rate is set by the free strength \(\alpha\) and is not derived from existing locks. Residual-\(L\) matching to laboratory \(G\), the bound/unbound split, the wall definition, the Casimir cut, and Tau rejection are all preserved. The construction remains non-locking.
