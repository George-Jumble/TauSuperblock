## Integration

Local points of simultaneity differ from one another and from the universal POS. Those differences are collected (“globbed”) by hierarchical binding into a single contrast relative to the privileged observer:

\[
\Delta t_{\rm glob}
=
\mathcal{G}\bigl(\{t_{\rm sim}^{(i)}\}\bigr)
-
t_{\rm POS},
\]

where \(\mathcal{G}\) denotes the globbing (averaging or dominant-glob) operation already used for residual-L centers of gravity.

**Key.** \(t_{\rm sim}^{(i)}\): local point of simultaneity associated with glob or domain \(i\). \(t_{\rm POS}\): universal privileged-observer POS. \(\Delta t_{\rm glob}\): net time differential after globbing.

The virtual torsion supplies the coupling that converts this differential into an inertial push:

\[
\mathbf{F}_{\rm push}
=
\kappa\,
\Delta t_{\rm glob}\,
\boldsymbol{\tau}_{\rm virt},
\]

or, in energy form,

\[
E_{\rm push}
=
\kappa\,
|\Delta t_{\rm glob}|\,
E_{\rm virt}.
\]

**Key.** \(\boldsymbol{\tau}_{\rm virt}\): virtual-torsion vector (auxiliary). \(\kappa\): unfixed coupling. \(E_{\rm virt}\): virtual-sector energy available for re-absorption or transmission. \(\mathbf{F}_{\rm push}\): resulting push directed relative to the universal POS.

This push can be read as an additional contribution to the gradient of the wall potential, thereby furnishing a candidate term for the thermodynamic lambda:

\[
\Lambda_{\rm thermo}
\propto
\kappa\,
|\Delta t_{\rm glob}|\,
\rho_{\rm virt}.
\]

**Key.** \(\rho_{\rm virt}\): virtual energy density (floor scale). The proportionality constant and \(\kappa\) remain free.

## Is this a derivation?

No.  

- The time differential \(\Delta t_{\rm glob}\) is a kinematic contrast among simultaneity loci. By the existing UPS cuts the stamp itself does not deposit residual \(L\) or source \(\rho_\Lambda\).
- The virtual torsion and \(E_{\rm virt}\) belong to the floor sector. The author cut refuses that sector as a dynamical source of lambda. Using it here re-introduces the refused sourcing unless \(\kappa\) is regarded as a purely phenomenological fitting parameter.
- Globbing is already the operation that assigns effective \(G\) to the dominant center of gravity. Applying the same operation to simultaneity differences does not evaluate a new energy density; it merely organizes an existing contrast.
- No locked quantity (clock function, floor protocol, residual-L matching condition, domain angle) fixes \(\kappa\) or the conversion from \(\Delta t_{\rm glob}\) into an energy density equal to the observed \(\rho_\Lambda\).

The construction therefore supplies a possible point at which a derivation could later be attempted, once an explicit operator for \(\kappa\) and a non-floor carrier for the energy are written. At present it remains a fitted phenomenological expression.
