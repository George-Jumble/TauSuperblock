# Superblock-portion failures and overclaims only

**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Scope:** William / Superblock / TSB-side. Gatlin’s metric \(S^1\), resonator, JADES 8.1σ, Casimir-of-circle, KK-as-DM are excluded. Merger slides listed only where a Superblock note adopted them.

Three grades:

- **Dead as result** — method does not support the sentence.
- **Overclaim** — real program, written as a theorem or a PASS.
- **Choice written as geometry** — convention, not forced.

## Dead as result

1. **Collider 7-fold as a detection.** CMS \(n_\mu\bmod 7\) “12σ” (mean multiplicity \(\approx 2.4\) peaks at 2). ALICE “Naked Binary Leak” at \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Neither is a collaboration paper. The histogram is arithmetic, not topology.

2. **Planck \(D_\ell\) residual FFT as “clear 7-fold detected.”** June 2026 TSB status update. No Planck paper. FFT of residuals always has peaks. Look-elsewhere not paid.

3. **“Yang–Mills mass gap solved.”** \(m_0\approx 313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) is a QCD-scale number. It is not a Clay proof, not a lattice theorem, not an existence-and-gap paper in the sense of the prize.

4. **SPARC / halo AIC as confirmation.** Cored Mirron / softened-Kerr profiles beat NFW on AIC in-house. Burkert and cored isothermal already do that. Extra flexibility is not a unique Superblock detection.

5. **Status-update PASS list.** Glueball “0.2σ,” halo “lowest AIC,” Planck “detected,” ALICE “anomalous phase-shift” — written as tests passed. They are in-house readings.

## Overclaim (program treated as theorem)

6. **\(m_0=313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\) as parameter-free geometric output of Planck–Kerr / Mirron.** After the locks it must not come from \(\tau\). Then the Superblock derivation has to stand alone. Until that derivation is a closed calculation from the Mirron geometry with no target-seeking, \(m_0\) is an IR floor, not a theorem.

7. **Emergent QCD from Planck–Kerr geometry.** Glueball as two \(\sim 850\,\mathrm{MeV}\) gluons adding to \(\sim 1700\,\mathrm{MeV}\) matches lattice \(0^{++}\) in the ballpark. Ballpark is not a spectrum.

8. **Six hidden domains as the dark-matter budget.** Shadow gravity through \(K\) is a mechanism. \(\sum_{i=2}^{6}\gamma_i\approx 5.40\) vs \(\Omega_{\mathrm{DM}}/\Omega_b\approx 5.2\) is an equal-density ansatz plus a leftover. Not a measurement of five other spacetimes.

9. **Conformal / twistor averaging \(\to\) Newtonian \(G\) plus cored halos.** The kernel is defined. The reduction to Poisson in the far zone is sketched. It is not a published GR limit with controlled error.

10. **Geometric strong-CP solution.** A claim. Neutron EDM has not tested it. Not a failure of data; a failure of “solved.”

11. **Fermion/boson reversal, sterile leakage, domain dipole as results.** Predictions in the notes. Status language treats some as already seen (dipole, “BOOM”). They are not.

12. **DESI drift as centrifugal walk of \(\{\beta_i\}\).** Superblock-side DE hypothesis after the Casimir split. DESI does not confirm that formula.

## Choice written as geometry

13. **Freeze of Coxeter exponents \(\{1,7\}\).** \(W(E_8)\) has eight exponents. Freezing those two is a choice. Other freezes exist.

14. **\(\beta_i=(m_i-11)/30\).** Linear offset from the slowest moving plane. Not the unique map from \(\sigma(c)\). A sine map, a chord map, or a different zero plane would give different \(\gamma_i\).

15. **Equal-density ansatz** for the six domains. Needed to turn \(\gamma_i\) into an \(\Omega\) ratio. Not given by E₈.

16. **Weight-6 spectral kernel as if identical to the order-7 clock.** Six moving planes vs seven phase slots. Two integers. Writing them as one object is a slide *inside* Superblock.

17. **“Zero continuous parameters.”** Even without \(\tau\), Superblock still has \(m_0\), the freeze, the \(\beta\) map, the density ansatz, and the kernel shape. Strike the phrase.
