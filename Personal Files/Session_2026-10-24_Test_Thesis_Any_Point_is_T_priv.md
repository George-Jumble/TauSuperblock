## Thesis restated

**Thesis.** Any given point \(p\) in space and time is \(T_{\rm priv}\), in the sense that a hierarchical cascade can be back-traced to (or from) that point to produce a point of simultaneity.

In symbols:

\[
\forall\, p\in\mathcal{M}^{3+1}\,,
\quad
\exists\,
\bigl(\mathcal{G}_0(p)\subset\mathcal{G}_1(p)\subset\cdots\subset\mathcal{G}_N\bigr)
\quad\text{such that}\quad
T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}
=
\text{POS}\bigl(\mathbf{R}_{\rm dom}(p),\,\text{UPS stamp}\bigr).
\]

**Key.** \(\mathbf{R}_{\rm dom}(p)\): center of gravity of the dominant (largest coherent) glob containing \(p\). \(\text{POS}(\cdot)\): the geometric point-of-simultaneity construction already latent in the UPS language (midpoint / contraction of co-located events, or the \(\Phi_8\) stamp relative to that center).

## Mathematical reading

The hierarchy of binding is a partial order by containment of coherent bound sets (globs). Every event \(p\) that carries any bound occupancy lies in at least one glob; the chain of nested globs containing \(p\) is well-defined once the coherence criterion (shared residual \(L\), one COM) is fixed.

Back-tracing is the map

\[
p
\;\mapsto\;
\bigl\{\mathcal{G}_k(p)\bigr\}_{k=0}^{N}
\;\mapsto\;
\mathbf{R}_{\rm dom}(p)
\;\mapsto\;
T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}.
\]

**Key.** The final arrow is the existing geometric UPS construction applied to the dominant center: the stamp remains kinematic (\(\Delta\phi_{\rm tick}\) only). No new operator is introduced.

Equivalently, one may invert the earlier cascade:

\[
T_{\rm priv}^{(p)}
=
\mathcal{G}^{-1}\bigl(\text{local labels of }p;\,\{\mathbf{R}^{(g)}\}\bigr).
\]

Both directions are set-theoretic once the containment relation is given. No continuous free parameter or metric fifth dimension is required.

## Repercussions

1. **Relational rather than absolute privileged observer.**  
   The earlier off-books “universal +1” is recovered only as the special case in which the cascade is continued all the way to a single root chamber. For every other event the relevant \(T_{\rm priv}\) is that of its own dominant containing glob. This matches the already-locked statement that effective \(G\) (and therefore the reference for residual-\(L\) compression) belongs to the dominant glob’s center of gravity.

2. **No new energy source.**  
   Because the stamp remains kinematic, the construction does not deposit residual \(L\), does not source \(\rho_\Lambda\), and does not convert virtual/Casimir floor objects into a dynamical cosmological term. Any subsequent coupling to a thermodynamic wall potential remains fitted, exactly as in the preceding off-books notes.

3. **Compatibility with inverted globbing.**  
   The thesis is the statement that the inverse image of the globbing map exists for every base point. It therefore sits comfortably beside the 2026-10-24 inverted-\(T_{\rm priv}\) exploration: the arrow may run either way; the hierarchy supplies the partial order in both cases.

4. **Observational contact.**  
   None new. Laboratory tests of \(G\), equivalence-principle bounds, and the absence of a preferred-frame signal continue to constrain the dominant-glob rule exactly as before. The thesis does not predict a unique cosmic rest frame independent of the hierarchical containment.

The thesis is **consistent** with the live Superblock frame.
