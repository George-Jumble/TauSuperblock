# Dark matter and the vacuum floor are not Tau-circle loads in Superblock

**Session date:** 19 September 2026  
**Claim under review:** Superblock dark matter is other-domain / other-phase matter on the supersphere. The late-time vacuum is Phase-7 Mirron emission that almost couples to a prime domain. Gatlin’s non-computable series is a separate Tau construct.  
**Short answer:** Those three Superblock assignments do **not** put dark matter or dark energy on the metric Tau circle. The circle problem remains only where drafts still *also* keep \(S_5\) compactification, \(G_4=G_5/(2\pi\tau)\), or “ultralight KK modes.” Separate the ledgers.

## 1. Two ledgers, not one theory

**Gatlin Tau ledger (public 2025–2026 KK papers)**

- \(\tau\) is a compact metric \(S^1\).
- \(G_4=G_5/(2\pi\tau)\).
- Dark matter = ultralight Kaluza–Klein modes of that circle.
- Dark energy = graviton Casimir energy of that circle.
- Infinite KK tower = “non-computable” harmonic series.

**Superblock / TSB ledger (your statement today)**

- Dark matter = ordinary matter in another harmonic phase or another domain of the supersphere.
- Vacuum floor = Phase-7 Mirrons radiating at a coupling just below the threshold to join a prime domain.
- Non-computable series = Gatlin’s construct, motivated by quantum uncertainty / partial computability, not a TSB requirement that the circle carry \(\Omega_{\rm DM}\) and \(\Omega_\Lambda\).

Those are different load-bearing structures. The micron-bound critique attaches to the first ledger. It does not automatically attach to the second.

The confusion exists because some unification drafts *imported* the first ledger’s jobs into papers that also carry Superblock language. That import is the thing to stop, not the Superblock mechanisms themselves.

## 2. Other-domain matter is a hidden-sector theory, not a KK tower

If domain \(j\neq\) ours contains ordinary stress-energy, and after averaging we only see its gravitational trace,

\[
G_{\mu\nu}^{\rm (eff)}
=8\pi G\left(
T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm (ours)}
+\sum_{j\neq{\rm ours}}\bigl\langle T_{\mu\nu}^{(j)}\bigr\rangle
\right),
\]

then \(\sum_{j\neq{\rm ours}}\langle T_{\mu\nu}^{(j)}\rangle\) *is* dark matter in our instruments: it sources curvature, it does not source our photons if the electromagnetic constitutive tensor does not mix domains.

**Key:** \(\langle T_{\mu\nu}^{(j)}\rangle\) is the twistor- or time-averaged stress of domain \(j\) as it appears in our 4D equations. No circle radius enters. No KK mass \(n\hbar c/\tau\) enters.

That is the same structural idea as mirror matter or a dark sector, with the Superblock-specific claim that the other sector is a co-located 3+1D domain on the supersphere rather than a separate gauge copy invented by hand.

What this does *not* do:

- it does not need ultralight modes of a 10 Mpc circle,
- it does not need \(G_4=G_5/(2\pi\tau)\),
- it does not put \(\Omega_{\rm DM}\) on Tav.

What it still owes:

- why electromagnetism, and most non-gravitational forces, do not mix domains, while the averaged impulse kernel does,
- why the effective density is \(\rho_{\rm DM}/\rho_b\approx 5\), not an order-one accident of six domains,
- why the effective fluid is cold (or has the right sound speed) on galactic and CMB scales,
- why domain matter clusters in a way that matches SPARC / lensing / bullet-cluster-type offsets, or why it does not,
- a statement of whether other-domain baryons can annihilate or radiate into our photons at any loop order.

Those are ordinary hidden-sector calculations. They are not Tau-circle calculations.

## 3. Phase-7 Mirron emission is a constitutive vacuum, not graviton Casimir

Your vacuum sentence, written as physics rather than narrative:

Phase-7 Mirrons produce a stress that almost, but not quite, enters a prime-domain Maxwell / impulse channel.

A usable constitutive form is

\[
T_{\mu\nu}^{(7)}
=(\rho_7+p_7)u_\mu u_\nu+p_7 g_{\mu\nu}
+\varepsilon_{7\to p}\,\mathcal{S}_{\mu\nu},
\]

\[
0<\varepsilon_{7\to p}\ll 1,
\]

with the uncoupled piece behaving as a near-vacuum,

\[
p_7\approx-\rho_7
\quad\text{or}\quad
w_7\approx-1+\delta(\varepsilon_{7\to p}),
\]

and \(\mathcal{S}_{\mu\nu}\) the piece that would become ordinary radiation or matter if the coupling crossed threshold.

**Key:** \(\varepsilon_{7\to p}\) is the mixing amplitude into a prime domain; \(\mathcal{S}_{\mu\nu}\) is the operator that *would* source our photons or our baryons if \(\varepsilon_{7\to p}\) were order one; \(\delta\) is the leak away from \(w=-1\).

That is *not*

\[
\rho_{\rm Cas}\sim\frac{C}{\tau^4},\qquad \tau=7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}.
\]

The \(1/\tau^4\) disaster belongs to ordinary KK Casimir on a metric circle. Phase-7 Mirron pressure lives in the cell / domain constitutive tensor. Its scale has to come from \(n_M^{(7)}\), \(r_M\), and \(\varepsilon_{7\to p}\), not from the cosmic-web length.

What this still owes:

- a derivation of \(\rho_7\sim 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{eV}^4\), or whatever floor you claim, from Mirron microphysics,
- why \(\varepsilon_{7\to p}\) stays below threshold for cosmological time (the same lock-or-tune issue as nucleation \(\Gamma=3Hn_M\)),
- the associated stress-energy: if Phase-7 radiates, what is the back-reaction on \(\rho_7\),
- whether “almost couples” produces photon or fifth-force leakage inside existing bounds,
- a clear sentence that this floor is *not* the Casimir energy of a 10 Mpc \(S^1\).

Keep the word “Casimir” only if a mode sum on a cavity is actually performed. Otherwise say “Phase-7 vacuum pressure.” Borrowed words are how the two ledgers got glued.

## 4. Gatlin’s non-computable series can stay Gatlin’s

A partially non-computable 142857 / halting structure as a selection rule on resonances is a Tau-side proposal. Reading it as a metaphor for quantum uncertainty is a philosophical gloss, not a derivation of \(\Omega_{\rm DM}\) or \(\Omega_\Lambda\).

TSB does not need that series to carry dark matter or the vacuum. If a draft wants it as an information-theoretic comment on the Resonance Graph, label it as such. If a draft uses the infinite KK tower of a metric \(S^1\) as the literal non-computable object, that draft has put the load back on the circle.

Authorship hygiene, already in your preregistration instinct:

- Gatlin: Tav topology, 142857 clock, RGC, claimed survey harmonics, metric-KK packaging.
- Brown / Superblock: six domains, Mirron / PK engine, Phase-7 fluid, twistor average, stochastic impulse gravity, other-domain matter.

Do not let unification language assign Superblock jobs to the circle, or circle jobs to Mirrons, without an equation that says so.

## 5. Does this evade the micron bound?

**Yes**, if all of the following are true at once:

1. Dark matter is \(\langle T_{\mu\nu}^{(j)}\rangle\) from other domains / phases, not KK modes of \(\tau\).
2. The vacuum floor is Phase-7 Mirron stress, not \(\rho\sim 1/\tau^4\).
3. The gravitational kernel \(\mathcal K\) that couples domains is four-dimensional. \(\phi\) or Tav is a phase label in that kernel, not a spatial circle gravity wraps.
4. Drafts that still contain \(S_5\) with \(y\in[0,2\pi\tau]\) and \(G_4=G_5/(2\pi\tau)\) are marked as Tau-KK, not as TSB gravity.

**No**, if a unification paper still says “macroscopic 5D Kaluza–Klein substrate of radius \(\tau\)” *and* “other-domain dark matter” in the same breath without saying which object sources \(\Omega_{\rm DM}\). Two mechanisms written side by side put the load on both until one is retired.

The circle problem was never “Superblock cannot have dark matter.” It was “a metric \(S^1\) of 10 Mpc cannot be the gravitational extra dimension, and some merged drafts asked it to be.”
