## Brief Recap of Penrose Twistor Theory (with Complete Variable Keys)

Twistor theory (Roger Penrose) provides a holomorphic, complex-geometric foundation for massless fields, conformal geometry, and aspects of quantum gravity/GR. It encodes spacetime points and null geodesics (light rays) via twistors.

**Fundamental Twistor**:
A twistor is a 4-component complex object
\[
Z^\alpha = (\omega^A, \pi_{A'}), \quad \alpha = 0,1,2,3; \quad A=0,1; \quad A'=0',1'
\]

**Complete Key**:
- \( Z^\alpha \): Twistor (projective class [Z] defines a point in projective twistor space PT ≈ ℂP³ minus a line at infinity in some contexts).
- \( \omega^A \): Undotted spinor part (related to "position" or holomorphic coordinate in twistor space).
- \( \pi_{A'} \): Dotted spinor part (related to "momentum" or direction of null geodesic).
- Indices A, A': Spinor indices transforming under SL(2,ℂ) (double cover of Lorentz group SO(1,3)).

**Incidence Relation** (relates twistor to spacetime point x^{AA'}):
\[
\omega^A = i x^{AA'} \pi_{A'}
\]

**Complete Key**:
- \( x^{AA'} \): Spacetime coordinate in spinor notation (x^μ ↔ x^{AA'} via Pauli matrices or Infeld-van der Waerden symbols; null vectors when det(x)=0).
- i: Imaginary unit (ensures reality conditions for real Minkowski spacetime).
- This equation defines a complex line (ℂP¹) in twistor space corresponding to a spacetime point; or conversely, a null geodesic (light ray) in spacetime corresponds to a point in twistor space.
- For null geodesics (massless particles like photons): the twistor satisfies Z^α \bar{Z}_α = 0 (null twistor condition in some normalizations).

**Projective Twistor Space**:
\[
\mathbb{PT} = \{ Z^\alpha \mid Z^\alpha \neq 0 \} / \mathbb{C}^*
\]

**Complete Key**:
- ℂ*: Complex scaling (homogeneity).
- Physical fields (massless helicity h) are represented by holomorphic functions or cohomology classes on regions of PT (e.g., via contour integrals or twistor transform).

**Conformal Rescaling (key for CCC and gravity)**:
\[
g_{ab} \to \Omega^2 g_{ab}
\]

**Complete Key**:
- \( g_{ab} \): Spacetime metric.
- \( \Omega \): Conformal factor (smooth, positive; →0 at future timelike infinity I⁺ in FLRW aeons).
- Preserves null geodesics and conformal structure (angles, light cones). Massless fields (photons, gravitons in linearized theory) are conformally invariant.

**Penrose Conformal Cyclic Cosmology (CCC) Key Features**:
