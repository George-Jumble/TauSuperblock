# Session 2026-10-24: Current Mass and Constituent Mass of the Up Quark

**Date**: 2026-10-24  
**Framework**: Tau-Superblock (TSB / Superblock). Printed working mass floor is the neutron dressing protocol 309.3 MeV (not a derivation). PDG values are external observational inputs.  
**Question**: Current mass of an up quark? Constituent mass?

## Plain-language summary

The current (bare, running) mass of the up quark is \(2.16\pm 0.07\) MeV in the \(\overline{\rm MS}\) scheme at 2 GeV (PDG 2026).  

The constituent mass is model-dependent. In the simple non-relativistic quark model it is conventionally taken near 336 MeV. An equal-share accounting of the nucleon mass gives approximately 313 MeV (or the live printed floor 309.3 MeV if the neutron dressing protocol is used). Neither number is a unique experimental observable.
