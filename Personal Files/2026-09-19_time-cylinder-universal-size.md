# A universal time cylinder, with the 7 Mpc hypothesis ignored

**Session date:** 19 September 2026  
**Proposal:** Restore a 5th-dimensional relationship by a time cylinder of universal size. Do not use \(7h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}\).  
**Status:** Exploratory. Not framework law.  
**Short answer:** A *metric* compact time circle of any universal size makes closed timelike curves. That is not a safe 5th dimension. A 5th-dimensional relationship can come back only as (i) an internal phase clock, or (ii) a *spacelike* extra direction whose universal radius is small enough for Cavendish and colliders.

## 1. “Time cylinder” is several geometries

Ignore 7 Mpc. The phrase still splits.

**A. Compact time:** identify \(t\sim t+T\) at every spatial point. Universal size means one \(T\) everywhere. The extra “circle” is time. Every worldline that sits still in space and waits a time \(T\) is a closed timelike curve.

**B. Two times:** signature \(3+2\) (three space, two time), compact or not. A second time coordinate, not just a periodic label on the first.

**C. Ordinary KK cylinder with a clock interpretation:** spacetime \(\mathbb{R}^{3,1}\times S^1_{\rm space}\), extra coordinate spacelike, radius \(R_5\) the same at every 4D point. Time *passage* winds fields around that circle. People call that a cylinder; it is not compact time.

**D. Internal time cylinder:** \(\phi\sim\phi+2\pi\) is a phase with a universal period in the PK / Mirron clock. No extra metric direction. The “cylinder” is the phase space of the engine.

A and B are 5th-*time* relationships. C is a 5th-*space* relationship dressed in clock language. D is not a 5th spacetime dimension.

---

## 2. Compact time of universal size is the worst option

If the metric identifies

\[
(t,\mathbf{x})\;\sim\;(t+T,\mathbf{x})
\]

then the curve \(\mathbf{x}=\mathrm{const}\), \(t\) running through one period, is timelike and closed. That is true for

- Planck-scale \(T\),
- nuclear-scale \(T\),
- laboratory \(T\),
- cosmological \(T\).

The *size* does not save you. A small CTC is still a CTC. Chronology-protection arguments then have to kill or decouple that identification, which means it is not doing gravitational 5D work.

Known relativistic cylinders that compactify a time-like direction (Misner space and relatives) are time-machine geometries. They are studied as pathologies, not as vacuum engines.

So: “time cylinder, universal size, 5th dimension in the metric” is not a repair of the 7 Mpc model. It is a different, harder causality problem.

---

## 3. A second time dimension is a different theory

Signature \(3+2\) is a research subject (two-time physics). It is not a drop-in replacement for \(S_5\) on \(\mathbb{R}^3\times S^1\).

Typical costs:

- extra first-class constraints to kill ghosts,
- a compact time-like circle still makes CTCs unless the second time is gauge rather than metric,
- laboratory gravity and Maxwell still have to come out 3+1 with one \(c\).

If the second time is only a gauge parameter, you are back at D: an internal clock. If it is metric, you owe a 3+2 action and a proof that 4D causality survives. That is a new theory, not Tau-with-the-radius-erased.

## 4. Where a 5th-dimensional relationship *can* return

Drop 7 Mpc. Two legal restorations remain.

**Internal phase (D).**  
Universal size here means the clock period is the same law at every point: one \(\Delta t_\tau\) or one discrete phase step, not a position-dependent radius. That is already Superblock-native. It gives you a 5th *relationship* (phase advances as time passes) without a 5th metric direction. Cavendish never sees it. GW170817 never sees it. It does not produce \(G_4=G_5/(2\pi R)\).

**Spacelike extra circle of small universal radius (C).**  
This is ordinary Kaluza–Klein / ADD / UED. The 5th coordinate is space. Time passage can still wind modes around it. Laboratory bounds then fix the *size*:

| \(R_5\) | Status |
|---|---|
| \(\lesssim 30\,\mu\mathrm{m}\) (one flat extra dimension) | inverse-square-law window |
| TeV\(^{-1}\) | collider / UED window |
| Planck | invisible as a radius; 5D GR at the Planck scale |
| \(10\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) | ignored in this note; already excluded |

If the goal is “have a real 5D Einstein–Hilbert factor again,” this is the only metric option that is not dead on arrival. It will not print a cosmic-web length by itself. It will not be Gatlin’s \(\tau\). It is a standard extra dimension with a standard bound.

Warping can change the bound, but then you must write \(A(y)\), not call the extra coordinate “time.”

## 5. “Universal size” is the easy part

KK already assumes a radius that is the same at every 4D point unless you promote it to a radion field. Universality does not choose time versus space and does not choose a number. It only says: no position-dependent \(\tau_{\rm web}\) dressed up as compactification.

Once 7 Mpc is ignored, you must pick a number from geometry (\(r_M\), Planck length, a cell scale) or from an experiment. If the number is not supplied, “universal time cylinder” is a topology with an unset modulus. The modulus is a free parameter until the cell or the action fixes it.

## 6. What this does to TEP, \(\mathcal K\), and the Superblock frame

- TEP does **not** get a free proof from compact time. Compact time is the thing TEP was supposed to forbid.
- \(\mathcal K_{ij}\) does not become 5D because a clock is periodic.
- Other-domain matter and Phase-7 pressure do not need this cylinder.
- \(B_F\) does not need this cylinder.
- A small spacelike \(S^1\) *would* put KK modes back into the gravitational kernel. Then you are no longer in the “stochastic 4D kernel, internal phases only” theory. That is a fork. Choose it on purpose.

Fork, written plainly:

> **Fork T.** 5th relationship is internal time/phase. Metric is 4D per domain. No compact \(t\).  
> **Fork S.** 5th relationship is a small spacelike circle of universal radius \(R_5\) inside a lab-allowed window. Metric is 5D. Clock language is optional poetry.  
> **Fork X.** Compact metric time of period \(T\). CTCs. Not recommended.

Do not mix T and X. A periodic phase plus a time function is T. A periodic time coordinate is X.
