**P2. Topological Exclusion asymmetry**

**P3. Discrete occupation thresholds (weak)**

**P4. Absence of hierarchical / gravitational signatures**

- Scale-dependent modulations versus separation that rely on \(n_{\rm hier}\).

## 3. Operational Falsification Protocol

### 3.1 Primary null hypothesis \(H_0\)
The dielectric (or force) response is consistent with a smooth Lifshitz / continuum vacuum spectrum plus ordinary material and experimental artefacts. No residual order-6 phase modulation, no TEP-generated directional asymmetry, and no discrete occupation thresholds beyond those expected from material physics.

### 3.2 Test statistics (pre-registered)

| Test | Statistic | Gate (suggested starting values) | Interpretation |
|------|-----------|----------------------------------|----------------|
| T1. Pure phase modulation | Periodogram power at frequencies corresponding to the order-6 cycle (or its first few harmonics) after linear/quadratic trend removal | Power-ratio > 0.40 *and* matched-filter score > 0.08, with max FPR ≤ 0.05 on pure-decay nulls | Residual 7-related modulation present |
| T2. Directional asymmetry | Signed difference of up-sweep versus down-sweep response (or forward/reverse time series) normalised by variance | Asymmetry amplitude > 2.5\(\sigma\) under label-permutation null | TEP / reset-seam asymmetry present |
| T3. Discrete occupation thresholds | Change-point count (PELT or binary segmentation) after trend removal, compared with material-only nulls | Excess steps significant at \(p < 0.01\) after multiple-testing correction | Weak TEP thresholds present |
| T4. Hierarchical contamination check | Explicit search for hierarchical envelope or \(\Delta n\)-scaled features | Any significant hierarchical signal → framework inconsistency (bound physics leaking into unbound sector) | Must be null |

### 3.3 Calibration and power requirements

- All gates must be calibrated on pure synthetic nulls (smooth Lifshitz + realistic noise + material dispersion) so that the false-positive rate is controlled at the stated level.  
- Power must be quantified for residual modulation amplitudes of order \(10^{-2}\)–\(10^{-3}\) relative to the continuum background (the range expected from pure group-theoretic factors).  
- A non-detection is informative only when power against the residual amplitudes exceeds 80 %.

### 3.4 Material and dataset rules

- Multiple independent dielectric series (or force curves) must be examined.  
- A material-differentiation gate is retained: the residual phase modulation, if present, should not be confined to a single anomalous material.  
- Pre-registration of the exact frequency bins, trend-removal method, and null-generation procedure is mandatory before unblinding.
