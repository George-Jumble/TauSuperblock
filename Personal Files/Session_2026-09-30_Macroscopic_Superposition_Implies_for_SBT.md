## 0. Result in one block

The experiment constrains **one** SBT reading and leaves the live theory almost untouched.

It kills any version in which *every massive object is being classically rewritten, every tick, including its center of mass*. That is the old “continuous Dynamic Refresh of matter” slogan if it is read as a COM-classifying map.

It does **not** kill:

- six co-located domains,
- \(\Phi_7\) scrape / \(\Phi_8\) reset as a *bound-sector* protocol,
- residual-\(L\) gravity of *stopped* rebounds,
- TEP on reset overlap,
- Mirron as a boundary operator on a bound envelope.

Reason: what interfered is the **unbound center-of-mass coordinate** of an isolated cluster in vacuum. The metallic binding inside the cluster was never put into a Schrödinger-cat state. SBT’s stop is a property of a *bound* bounce, not a mass threshold.

It also does **not** test Penrose objective reduction. A naive \(t=\hbar/E_G\) for this mass and grating-scale separation is decades, not milliseconds. Pedalino’s bite is on **CSL-type** universal localization, not on gravitational self-energy collapse, and not on TEP.

## 2. What was actually superposed

Not “a rock is in two places as a bound object.”

A sodium nanoparticle is a **bound metal** (ions + electrons, residual \(L\) on atomic and metallic envelopes) whose **COM** was prepared in a near-field Talbot–Lau superposition and reconstructed as interference fringes. The wave that interferes is

\[
\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}(x,t),
\qquad
\lambda_{\mathrm{dB}}=\frac{h}{mv}\sim 16\,\mathrm{fm}.
\]

**Key.** \(\psi_{\mathrm{COM}}\): COM wavefunction. \(x\): transverse lab coordinate. Internal vibrational, electronic, and metallic-binding degrees of freedom are traced or stay unentangled enough that they do not wash the fringe. That is an experimental achievement about isolation, not a proof that the *bound internals* were cats.

Two SBT sectors are therefore in play at once:

| Sector | In the cluster | Superposed? |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| Bound internals (Na–Na metallic envelopes, electrons) | yes | **not tested** |
| Unbound COM in vacuum | the beam coordinate | **yes, \(\mu=15.5\)** |

SBT already has this split: bound EM is stopped at Present; unbound motion completes. An interferometer is a machine for keeping one coordinate unbound.

## 3. What is killed

### 3.1 Continuous classical Dynamic Refresh of the COM

Unified Tav-Superblock V2.0 and older SBT prose treated matter as a continuous Dynamic Refresh driven by octonionic clockwork. If that sentence means

> every massive worldline, including the COM of an isolated object, is overwritten to a classical point every \(\Phi_8\) tick,

then Pedalino falsifies it. A classical rewrite of COM once per tick (or even once per microsecond) destroys Talbot–Lau fringes. The cluster flew for \(\sim 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}\) at \(v\sim 160\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) over a compact grating baseline and still interfered.

**Live amendment, forced by the data:** Dynamic Refresh, if the phrase is kept, is a protocol on *bound envelopes*, not a COM-classifying map on isolated objects. Photons were already the exception (unbound completed bounce). Pedalino adds: the COM of a \(10^5\,\mathrm{u}\) metal in vacuum is in the same exception class.

### 3.2 “Macroscopic \(\Rightarrow\) already classical”

Any SBT slogan that used “macroscopic” as a synonym for “has residual \(L\) / has been stopped” is too coarse. \(7000\) atoms is macroscopic for a chemist and still quantum for a COM. The live criterion is **bound vs unbound**, not atom-count.

### 3.3 Universal CSL-like collapse as an SBT mechanism

Pedalino’s own supplement uses the fringes to cut CSL parameter space (visibility \(\ge 78\%\) of the quantum prediction at \(m=172\,\mathrm{kDa}\), \(v=160\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)). If someone had planned to *implement* TEP or \(\Phi_8\) as a mass-proportional spontaneous localization of every nucleon, that implementation is now tighter. TEP as written is **not** CSL: TEP fires on reset-overlap of bound colour lines, not on free COM.

## 4. What is not touched

### 4.1 Residual-\(L\) gravity

Gravity in the live frame is spatial compression from residual angular momentum of *stopped* rebounds, belonging to the largest coherent bound set. The COM of an isolated cluster in a beam has almost no stopped rebound on that coordinate. Seeing COM interference does not measure \(G\), does not measure residual \(L\), and does not test whether bound globs source effective \(G\). Non-result.

### 4.2 Six domains and \(\mathcal K_{ij}\)

Nothing in a one-domain vacuum interferometer addresses co-located domains or the embedding kernel. Non-result.

### 4.3 TEP

TEP is an exclusion on two \(\Phi_8\) resets sharing a local twistor \(H^1\), with cost \(E_{\mathrm{violation}}\ge\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}\) and domain-cross work \(E_{\times}=\theta_{ab}\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}\). Two arms of a Talbot–Lau interferometer are not two domain resets. Non-result.

### 4.4 Penrose OR / Orch OR

Naive gravitational self-energy for this object:

\[
E_G^{\mathrm{(sep)}}\sim\frac{Gm^{2}}{d},
\qquad
t_{\mathrm{OR}}\sim\frac{\hbar}{E_G^{\mathrm{(sep)}}}\sim 10^{12}\,\mathrm{s}.
\]

**Key.** \(G\): Newton constant. \(d=133\,\mathrm{nm}\). \(t_{\mathrm{OR}}\) using separation \(d\) is millennia. Using radius \(R\sim 4\,\mathrm{nm}\) still gives \(t_{\mathrm{OR}}\sim 10^{11}\,\mathrm{s}\). Flight time is milliseconds. Pedalino cannot see Penrose collapse even if Penrose collapse is true. Do not cite this paper as support for, or as a refutation of, Wiest / Hameroff / Orch OR. Different scale, different environment (vacuum metal vs warm wet tubulin).

### 4.5 \(\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}\) and the nucleon-third protocol

A \(172\,\mathrm{kDa}\) COM rest energy is \(\sim 1.6\times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{MeV}\). That number has no protocol relation to \(313.1\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Do not write a hierarchy stretch from the cluster to the floor and call it tested.

## 5. What is sharpened (the actual implication)

SBT’s bound/unbound cut must be drawn on **degrees of freedom**, not on objects.

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{bound coordinate} & \text{unbound coordinate}\\
\hline
\text{stopped rebound, residual }L & \text{completed P}\leftrightarrow\text{F motion of that coordinate}\\
\text{atomic / metallic / colour envelopes} & \text{COM of an isolated packet in vacuum}\\
\text{pays }E_{\times}, E_{\mathrm{violation}} & E_{\times}=0\text{ on that coordinate}\\
\text{may be refreshed / reset} & \text{must stay coherent if isolated}\\
\text{Pedalino silent} & \text{Pedalino requires this}
\end{array}
\]

A sodium cluster is bound *inside* and unbound *as a packet*. That is allowed in live SBT. It was easy to blur in older refresh language. The experiment makes the blur expensive.

The same cut already existed for light: the photon is unbound; the bound charge that emitted it is not. Pedalino is the massive analogue of that sentence.

## 6. What would actually threaten live SBT

Not this paper. Threats would be:

1. Interference of a coordinate that SBT has already classified as bound (e.g. a colour line inside a nucleon kept in a spatial cat for times \(\gg \hbar/\Delta_{\mathrm{Tav}}\) without paying TEP). That experiment does not exist.
2. A demonstrated, mass-proportional, universal collapse that fires on isolated COM at rates that kill Talbot–Lau and that SBT had claimed as \(\Phi_8\). Pedalino moves the other way: isolated COM did **not** collapse.
3. A gravitational measurement showing that a COM superposition sources a different \(G\) than a bound glob of the same mass. Not done.
