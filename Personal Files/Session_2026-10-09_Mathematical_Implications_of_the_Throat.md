## 2. What the passage is

On the disk, \(\rho=a|\cos\theta|\neq 0\). Curvature invariants that blow up as \(\rho^{-6}\) stay finite. The metric in Kerr–Schild form is smooth through \(r=0\) at \(\theta\neq\pi/2\). Continuing the coordinate gives a region \(r<0\) on the other side of the same analytic extension. That is the throat: one manifold, two sides of a disk, boundary singular.

Implications that are chart facts:

- The passage is not a second asymptotic end by itself. Another exterior appears further along the ladder, after another pair of horizons, if the vacuum extension is kept maximal.
- Identifications can cut that ladder. Without them it is infinite. An infinite ladder is not a count of aeons.
- For \(r<0\), \(g_{\phi\phi}\) can be negative. Those are closed timelike curves in the extension. The live bounce is a reflection, not a license for those curves.
- \(a\to 0\) shrinks the disk. The passage disappears and the Schwarzschild singularity is the whole \(r=0\) set. Exact \(a=0\) remains an idealization.

## 3. Reading the white stroke there

Possible, and not core-breaking:

\[
\theta_{\rm exp}\;\longrightarrow\;-\theta_{\rm exp}
\]

evaluated on congruences that meet the disk. **Key.** \(\theta_{\rm exp}\): expansion scalar. Negative on infall, positive on the outgoing stroke. The arrow is the Mirron continuation plus a discrete \(G_2\) step. One engine.

Repercussions. The recovery sentence that refused “white locus = throat” was protecting two false identifications: a disconnected void at the center, and mass flowing into the ring as the cosmological expansion. Those stay refused. The weaker reading — the sign flip is read at the passage — is open. Slot 7 couples as the internal holding channel, the Higgs fabric. It is not a spatial mouth on the far side of the disk. Making it a mouth would add a sheet the \(3+1\) core does not have.

No energy falls out of this. A release \(E_{\rm rel}\) needs an anchor that is not \(\Delta\). The floor protocol remains \(309.3\,\mathrm{MeV}\). The throat does not derive it.
